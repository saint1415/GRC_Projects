## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how the workforce uses bank systems, devices, and data, and how staff spot and stop payment fraud.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Bank workforce members (directors, officers, employees, contractors, and temporary staff) at the six branches and the operations center. Covers all systems and data, including systems that service providers operate for the bank. It applies to customer information and all other bank information.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Director | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and annually; tracks training |
| IT Manager (ISO) | Awareness content, reminders, and phishing simulations |
| Deposit Operations Manager | Wire-fraud and callback training content for branch and wire-room staff |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Bank systems are for bank business. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not involve customer data or create risk. (PL-4)
4.2 Every workforce member must sign this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4(1))
4.3 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year. The ISO runs quarterly phishing simulations and sends monthly reminders. (AT-2; PR.AT-01; III.C.2)
4.4 **Role-based fraud training.** Branch managers, universal bankers, tellers, and wire-room staff must complete business email compromise and wire-fraud training at hire and every year, covering the callback standard, red flags (changed beneficiary instructions, urgency, new email domains), and how to escalate. (AT-3; PR.AT-02; III.C.1.a)
4.5 **Never trust contact details in a payment request.** Verify every emailed or phoned payment request, or change of payment instructions, by calling the phone number already on file (POL-02 4.8). If a customer or colleague pressures you to skip the callback, stop and escalate to your manager.
4.6 Lock your screen when you step away. Position teller screens so customers cannot read other customers' information. (AC-11)
4.7 Do not install software or browser extensions, or connect USB storage, unless IT approves it. (CM-11)
4.8 Do not open unexpected attachments or links. Report suspicious messages with the Report Phishing button.
4.9 Report suspected fraud within 15 minutes and anything else suspicious immediately (POL-03 4.2).
4.10 **Generative AI:** use only tools on the approved list. Never enter customer information, SAR information, or loan files into a tool that is not approved (POL-04 4.8). (PL-4)
4.11 Look up customer accounts only when your job requires it. Access to the core is logged and reviewed. (AU-6)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions procedure (POL-01 section 4.7). Sanctions range from retraining to termination, depending on intent and harm. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), phishing simulation results, and monthly callback sampling.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.6. No exception may waive the callback in 4.5.
