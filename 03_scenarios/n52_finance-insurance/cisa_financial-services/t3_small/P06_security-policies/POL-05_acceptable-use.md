## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how workforce members use company devices, accounts, data, and AI tools, so everyday work does not put card data or merchant funds at risk.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (owners, employees, and contractors), whether in the Florida office or working remotely, and all company devices, accounts, and approved tools.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| IT Manager | Owns this policy and the approved-tools list |
| HR Manager | Collects acknowledgments; runs training records |
| Managers | Make sure their staff follow this policy |
| All workforce | Read, acknowledge, and follow this policy |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Workforce members must read and acknowledge this policy at hire and every year. (PL-4; GV.PO-01; PCI DSS 12.2.1, 12.6.3)

4.2 Workforce members must **never** type, paste, or store card numbers in email, chat, tickets, documents, or AI tools. A merchant who sends a card number must be told to delete it and use the virtual terminal or secure upload, and the message must be reported under POL-03 4.9. (PL-4; PR.AT-01; PCI DSS 12.2.1)

4.3 Workforce members must complete security awareness training at hire and every year. Training must cover phishing and social engineering, card data handling, and generative AI rules. Staff must take part in the quarterly phishing exercises. Developers must complete secure coding training every year. (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02; PCI DSS 6.2.2, 12.6.1, 12.6.3, 12.6.3.1, 12.6.3.2; 16 CFR 314.4(e)(1))

4.4 Only company-managed laptops may be used for work. Personal devices may reach email and chat only through the approved mobile app with MFA, and may never reach the CDE, the cloud console, or the repository. (AC-19; AC-20; PR.PS-05; PCI DSS 12.2.1)

4.5 Workforce members must not disable, bypass, or tamper with endpoint protection, disk encryption, or other security tools. Only IT may approve an exception, and only for a limited time. (SI-3; PR.PS-05; PCI DSS 5.3.5)

4.6 Only software from the approved list may be installed. Browser extensions on administrator laptops need IT approval. (CM-11; PR.PS-05)

4.7 **Generative AI.** Only tools on the approved-tools list may be used for work. The approved enterprise AI assistant does not train on company data. Restricted and Confidential information must never be entered into any AI tool that is not approved, including public chatbots. That covers card numbers, merchant names with transaction details, and security configurations. (PL-4; AC-20; GV.PO-01; 16 CFR 314.4(e))

4.8 Workforce members must report lost or stolen devices, suspicious emails or calls, and suspected incidents immediately, under POL-03. (IR-6; RS.MA-02)

4.9 Company systems are monitored for security purposes. Users have no expectation of privacy on company systems, and a notice to this effect is shown at sign-in to the bastion and the cloud console. (AC-8; PL-4)

4.10 **Social engineering.** Staff must never change a merchant's funding bank account, contact details, or users because of a phone or email request alone. They must follow the call-back and step-up steps in POL-02 4.12. (AT-2; PR.AT-01; 16 CFR 314.4(c)(1))

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement in POL-01 section 4.14. Compliance is checked through acknowledgment records, training completion, phishing results, and the annual control assessment (P07).

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.13. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the majority owner and CEO for High risk), and expire within 12 months.
