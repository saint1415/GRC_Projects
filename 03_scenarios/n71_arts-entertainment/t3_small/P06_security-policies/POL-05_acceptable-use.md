## 1. Purpose
Set the rules for using company devices, accounts, and data, so that everyday work at the box office, the bars, and the office does not put patrons or the company at risk.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company employees, owners, and temporary staff; the marketing agency; the integrator; and event-day workers from staffing contractors who use company devices (scanners, POS handhelds, radios).

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR and Payroll Specialist | Collects acknowledgments; tracks training |
| Department heads | Make sure their staff and contractors follow the policy |
| Food and Beverage Manager | Card reader inspections and bar staff training |
| IT Manager | Keeps the approved tools list |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company devices and accounts are for company business. Every user must read and acknowledge this policy at hire and annually. (PL-4; AC-8; GV.PO-01; PCI 12.2)
4.2 Payment devices and box office workstations must be used only for ticket sales and payments. No email, web browsing, or personal use on them. (PL-4; PCI 12.2)
4.3 Every workforce member must complete security awareness training at hire and annually. Box office staff must also complete phone-order card handling training, and office staff must take part in phishing exercises at least quarterly. (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02; PCI 5.4; 12.6)
4.4 Bar and stand leads must inspect every card reader before doors on each event day using the inspection checklist, and report any reader that looks altered or is missing. (PL-4; AT-3; PR.AT-02; PCI 9.5)
4.5 Users must not install software, browser extensions, scripts, tags, or pixels on company devices or pages without IT approval through change control (POL-01 4.12). (PL-4; PR.AT-01; PCI 6.4.3)
4.6 Users must never share passwords, MFA codes, or badges, and must lock their screen when stepping away. (PL-4; PR.AT-01)
4.7 Requests to change payment details for an artist, promoter, or vendor must be confirmed by a phone call to a number already on file, never to a number in the request. (PL-4; AT-2; PR.AT-01)
4.8 Only AI tools on the approved tools list may be used with company data, and no Restricted data (POL-04) may be entered into public AI tools. (PL-4; GV.PO-01)
4.9 Report anything suspicious at once under POL-03. (PL-4; PR.AT-01)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.10. For contractors, violations can lead to removal from company work. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), training records, and reader inspection logs.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the majority owner for High risk), and expire within 12 months.
