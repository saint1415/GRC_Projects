## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how the workforce uses firm systems, devices, and data.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members using firm laptops, smartphones, kiosks, SaaS accounts, and data.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR and Compliance Manager | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and annually; tracks role training |
| IT Manager | Security awareness content, reminders, phishing exercises, and the approved AI tools list |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Firm systems are for firm business. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not involve candidate or associate data or create risk. (PL-4)
4.2 Every workforce member must sign this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4(1))
4.3 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year. The IT Manager sends monthly reminders and runs at least quarterly phishing exercises. (AT-2; PR.AT-01)
4.4 **Role training.** Payroll staff are trained each year on bank-change fraud and fake sign-in pages; Onboarding and Compliance Specialists on I-9 records handling and the E-Verify rules (plus the E-Verify Tutorial and refreshers); recruiters on FCRA and I-9 record limits. (AT-3; PR.AT-02; 8 CFR 274a.2(g)(1)(iii))
4.5 **Payroll sign-in.** Sign in to the payroll platform only from the firm's single sign-on page or a saved bookmark, never from a link in an email or text. (AT-2)
4.6 Never share a password or MFA approval, including E-Verify credentials. Never approve an MFA prompt you did not start. (IA-5; E-Verify MOU Art. II.A.15)
4.7 Lock your screen when you step away. Do not leave candidate records open on a lobby kiosk. (AC-11)
4.8 Do not install software, browser extensions, or connect USB storage unless IT approves it. (CM-11)
4.9 Report lost devices, suspicious messages, and unexpected bank-change requests to IT immediately (POL-03 4.2).
4.10 **Generative AI:** use only tools on the approved list. Never paste or upload SSNs, I-9 documents, consumer reports, bank data, or resumes into a tool that is not approved. The ATS AI features may be used only as set up in P10 (screening scores are a sort aid; every applicant who meets the minimum requirements gets a human review). (PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement (POL-01 section 4.7). Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), phishing exercise results, and training records.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.6. They must be written, risk-rated, approved at the risk acceptance level in POL-01 section 4.4 (IT Manager for Low, COO for Moderate, President for High and Very High), and expire within 12 months.
