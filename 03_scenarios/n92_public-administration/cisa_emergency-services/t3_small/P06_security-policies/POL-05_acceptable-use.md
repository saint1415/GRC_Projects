## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how the workforce uses company systems, devices, and data, in the office, at the dispatch console, and in the ambulance.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (owners, employees, contractors, students on clinical rotations, and ride-along observers) at headquarters, Station 2, and in company ambulances. Covers all systems and data, including systems that business associates operate for the company. It applies to electronic protected health information (ePHI), dispatch data, and all other company information.

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use (for example, checking personal email on a break) is allowed on office PCs if it does not involve patient data or create risk. Dispatch consoles and mobile data computers must not be used for personal browsing or email. (PL-4; 164.310(b))
4.2 Every workforce member must sign this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4(1))
4.3 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year. The IT Manager sends monthly security reminders and runs at least quarterly phishing exercises. (AT-2; PR.AT-01; 164.308(a)(5)(ii)(A))
4.4 Dispatchers must complete manual dispatch training at hire and drill it at least quarterly. (AT-3; PR.AT-02)
4.5 Lock your screen when you step away from an office PC. Do not leave a tablet unattended outside the ambulance. Position screens so patients and bystanders cannot read them. (AC-11; 164.310(c))
4.6 Do not install software, browser extensions, or connect USB storage unless IT approves it. Never plug personal phones into dispatch consoles or mobile data computers. (CM-11)
4.7 Do not open unexpected attachments or links. Report suspicious messages with the Report Phishing button. (AT-2)
4.8 Report lost or stolen devices, misdirected patient records, and anything suspicious immediately (POL-03 4.2).
4.9 **Generative AI:** use only tools on the approved list. Never paste, type, or dictate patient or caller information into a tool that is not approved. The AI call triage module may be used only as approved in P10 (shadow mode today). (PL-4)
4.10 Look up patient records and call history only when your job requires it. CAD and ePCR access is logged and reviewed monthly. (AU-6)
4.11 Do not photograph patients, scenes, or screens with personal phones. (PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions procedure (POL-01 section 4.7). Sanctions range from retraining to termination, depending on intent and harm. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07) and the access reviews in this policy set.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.6. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the majority owner for High risk), and expire within 12 months.
