## 1. Purpose
Make sure the company detects, contains, reports, and recovers from security incidents quickly and lawfully, keeps dispatching ambulances while it does, and meets breach notification deadlines.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (owners, employees, contractors, students on clinical rotations, and ride-along observers) at headquarters, Station 2, and in company ambulances. Covers all systems and data, including systems that business associates operate for the company. It applies to electronic protected health information (ePHI), dispatch data, and all other company information.

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 The company must maintain an incident response plan and runbooks for its most likely incidents, starting with a CAD outage from ransomware (P08). (IR-8; RS.MA-01)
4.2 Workforce members must report any suspected incident **immediately**, and within 1 hour at most, by calling the IT Manager's incident line or telling the on-duty dispatch supervisor. Examples: phishing clicks, lost tablets, misdirected patient records, unusual CAD or console behavior. Good-faith reporting is never sanctioned. (IR-6; RS.MA-02; 164.308(a)(6)(ii))
4.3 **Dispatch comes first.** If CAD or the consoles are unreliable, the on-duty dispatch supervisor must switch to manual dispatch at once, without waiting for IT, and notify the county PSAP. (CP-2; 164.308(a)(7)(ii)(C))
4.4 Every incident must be logged, categorized, and tracked to closure in the ticketing system under the Security category. (IR-5)
4.5 The Privacy Officer must decide whether an incident is a reportable breach under 45 CFR 164.402, using the four-factor risk assessment, and document the decision. (IR-6)
4.6 Notifications to individuals, HHS, the media, the Florida Department of Legal Affairs, the county, and law enforcement must meet the deadlines in the P08 notification matrix. Legal counsel must confirm each notification. (IR-6; RS.CO-02; RS.CO-03)
4.7 No ransom may be paid without approval from the majority owner, legal counsel, and the cyber insurer, and an OFAC sanctions check. (IR-4)
4.8 The incident response plan and manual dispatch mode must be tested at least annually by tabletop exercise and drill, and after any major incident. (IR-3)
4.9 Lessons learned must be documented within 30 days of closing an incident and added to the risk register. (IR-4; ID.IM)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions procedure (POL-01 section 4.7). Sanctions range from retraining to termination, depending on intent and harm. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07) and the access reviews in this policy set.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.6. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the majority owner for High risk), and expire within 12 months.
