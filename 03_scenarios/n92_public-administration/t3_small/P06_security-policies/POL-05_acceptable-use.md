## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how the workforce uses company systems, devices, and agency data.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members, wherever they work, including at home and at agency sites.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Manager | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and annually; tracks training and certifications |
| IT Manager | Security awareness content, reminders, and phishing exercises |
| Managers | Make sure their staff complete role-based training |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not involve agency data or create risk. (PL-4)
4.2 Every workforce member signs this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4)
4.3 **Training.** Security awareness training is required at hire (before any system access) and every year. Staff in designated positions (POL-01 4.5) also complete CJIS training before accessing CJI and FTI disclosure awareness training before accessing FTI, and recertify every year. Engineers, administrators, and support staff complete role-based training each year. (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02)
4.4 **Agency data stays in the platform.** Do not copy agency data into email, chat, personal notes, spreadsheets, or tickets. When an agency user sends a screenshot or file with agency data, move the work to the in-platform secure support channel and report it under POL-03 4.2. (PL-4)
4.5 **Remote work.** Work with agency data only on a company laptop, in a private space where screens cannot be seen or overheard, and from inside the United States. Do not print agency data at home. Lock the screen whenever you step away. (PE-17; AC-11)
4.6 Do not use personal devices or personal accounts for company or agency data. Company email on a phone is allowed only through the managed app. (AC-20)
4.7 Do not install software or browser extensions, or connect USB storage, unless IT approves it. (CM-11)
4.8 Do not open unexpected attachments or links. Report suspicious messages with the Report Phishing button. (AT-2)
4.9 **Generative AI.** Use only AI tools on the approved list. Never paste agency data, source code, or credentials into a tool that is not approved. (PL-4)
4.10 Look at agency records only when a ticket or task requires it. Access is logged and reviewed. (AU-6)
4.11 Every sign-in screen for systems that hold agency data shows the warning banner. Using the system means accepting monitoring. (AC-8)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.8. Compliance is checked through training records, access log reviews, and the annual control assessment (P07).

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7.
