## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how workforce members use company systems, devices, customer building systems, and customer information.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members and subcontractors, on company devices, customer equipment, and any personal device used for company work.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Manager | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and annually; tracks training |
| IT Manager | Awareness content, role-based training, reminders, and phishing exercises |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company and customer systems are for company business. Limited personal use of company laptops (for example personal email on a break) is allowed if it involves no customer information and creates no risk. Customer building systems and GSA systems may be used only for assigned work. (PL-4)
4.2 Every workforce member must sign this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4(1))
4.3 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year, with insider threat and social engineering content. The IT Manager runs at least quarterly phishing exercises. (AT-2; PR.AT-01)
4.4 **Role-based training** is required before access and annually: BAS technicians and ROC operators (OT security, remote access rules, vendor call-back), administrators (privileged use), and anyone handling CUI. Federal site staff also complete GSA's annual IT security training. (AT-3; PR.AT-02)
4.5 Lock your screen when you step away. Keep drawings and door schedules out of view of building visitors. (AC-11)
4.6 Do not install software, browser extensions, or remote-support tools, and do not connect USB storage, unless IT approves it. On customer engineering workstations, use only company-issued, scanned USB drives. (CM-11; MP-7)
4.7 Do not use personal devices or accounts for customer information, except the CMMS app under the device protection policy. Do not forward drawings or schedules to personal email. (AC-20)
4.8 **Never give remote access or passwords over the phone or email.** Anyone claiming to be a vendor, integrator, or customer IT person must be verified by calling back a number from the contract file. (AT-2)
4.9 Report lost or stolen devices, lost PIV cards or badges, misdirected drawings, and anything suspicious immediately (POL-03 section 4.2).
4.10 **Generative AI and AI features:** use only tools on the approved list. Never paste or upload drawings, door schedules, cardholder data, or CUI into a tool that is not approved. Do not turn on AI or biometric features in customer systems (for example face recognition or video analytics) without an approved P10 assessment. (PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement (POL-01 section 4.7). Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), training records, and phishing exercise results.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.6. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the majority owner for High risk), and expire within 12 months.
