## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how the workforce and students use college systems, devices, and data.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members: employees, full-time and adjunct faculty, contractors, and student workers. Statements 4.1 to 4.9 apply to the workforce. Statement 4.10 also applies to students using the 5 campus labs, and is repeated in the student handbook.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Manager | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and each year; tracks training completion |
| IT Director | Security awareness content, monthly reminders, and phishing exercises |
| Registrar | Annual FERPA training content |
| Lab Coordinator | Lab rules of use and the isolated practice lab |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 College systems are for college business. Limited personal use, such as checking personal email on a break, is allowed if it involves no student data and creates no risk. (PL-4)

4.2 Every workforce member must sign this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4(1))

4.3 **Training.** Security awareness and FERPA training are required at hire and every year. The training content must be updated each year to cover the risks in the current risk assessment, including aid-themed phishing, refund fraud, and safe handling of aid data. The IT Director must send monthly security reminders and run phishing exercises at least quarterly. Staff who fail two exercises in a row must complete refresher training. (AT-2; PR.AT-01; 314.4(e)(1))

4.4 Lock your screen when you step away. Position screens so students and visitors cannot read them at the front desk and in the financial aid office. (AC-11)

4.5 Do not install software or browser extensions, or connect USB storage, unless IT approves it. (CM-11)

4.6 Do not open unexpected attachments or links, especially messages about aid documents, refunds, or password resets. Report suspicious messages with the Report Phishing button. (AT-2)

4.7 Report lost or stolen devices, misdirected student data, and anything suspicious to IT immediately (POL-03 4.2).

4.8 **Generative AI.**
- Use only AI tools on the approved list (P10).
- Never paste or upload student records, financial aid data, or other Restricted information (POL-04) into a tool that is not approved.
- Scores from the early-alert model (AI-001) may be used only by advisors in the approved pilot, and only as described in P10.
- The admissions CRM's applicant scoring feature (AI-002) must stay turned off.

(PL-4; CM-7)

4.9 Look up a student's record only when your job requires it. Access to the SIS and FAMS is logged, and exports and unusual access are reviewed monthly. (AU-6; 34 CFR 99.31(a)(1)(ii))

4.10 **Labs and special-purpose workstations.**
- Students and faculty must not use lab computers to reach staff systems or to store Restricted information.
- Administrator exercises in IT courses may be done only in the isolated practice lab, which has no connection to the staff network.
- The 2 SAIG workstations in the financial aid office may be used only for SAIG transmissions and Department of Education websites. No email or general web browsing is allowed on them.

(PL-4; AC-8; CM-7)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement (POL-01 section 4.10). Sanctions range from retraining to termination, depending on intent and harm. Student violations of 4.10 are handled under the student code of conduct. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07) and training records.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7. They must be written, risk-rated, approved at the level in POL-01 4.4, and expire within 12 months.
