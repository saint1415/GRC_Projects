## 1. Purpose
Set up the Cris Santos Company cybersecurity program, assign who is accountable, and give every other security policy its authority. The program protects the confidentiality, integrity and availability of terminal information and systems, and keeps the company in compliance with the U.S. Coast Guard cyber rule (33 CFR Part 101, Subpart F).

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (owners, employees, temporary staff and contractors), plus longshore labor and vendor technicians whenever they use company IT or OT. Covers every system and data set at the Florida terminal, including the cloud tenant, SaaS services, crane and yard equipment controllers (OT), and systems that vendors operate or support for the company.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 The company must maintain a cybersecurity program documented in this policy set, the System Security Plan (P02) and, once approved, the Cybersecurity Plan required by 33 CFR 101.630. (PM-1; GV.PO-01)
4.2 The General Manager must designate in writing, by name and title, a Cybersecurity Officer and an alternate. The CySO must be reachable by the Coast Guard 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, through a published contact rota. (PM-2; GV.RR-02; 101.620(b)(3))
4.3 A cybersecurity risk assessment must be performed at least annually, sooner after a change in ownership or a major change, using NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1. It forms part of the Cybersecurity Assessment (101.650(e)(1)). Risks must be tracked in the risk register with an owner and a treatment. (RA-3; PM-9)
4.4 **Risk acceptance authority:** the IT Manager may accept Low and Very Low risks; the General Manager, Moderate; the majority owner, High and Very High, only temporarily and with a dated treatment plan. A High risk that could cause injury, a transportation security incident or loss of control of cargo-handling equipment may not be accepted; it must be treated. (PM-9; GV.RM-01)
4.5 Security policies must be reviewed at least annually and updated after major changes, incidents or changes to the Coast Guard rule. (PL-1; GV.PO-02)
4.6 **Exceptions** to any security policy must be requested in writing, risk-rated, approved per 4.4, recorded in the risk register, and limited to 12 months or less. A waiver or equivalence for a Subpart F requirement can only come from the Coast Guard (101.665). (PL-1)
4.7 **Sanctions.** Workforce members who fail to comply with security policies must be sanctioned in proportion to intent and harm: retraining, written warning, suspension or termination. HR must document each sanction. (PS-8; GV.RR-04)
4.8 **Suppliers.** Cybersecurity capability must be an evaluation criterion when buying IT or OT systems and services. New and renewed contracts with IT and OT vendors and service providers must require them to notify the CySO without delay of cybersecurity vulnerabilities and reportable cyber incidents affecting company systems. (SA-4; SA-9; SR-8; GV.SC-05; 101.650(f)(1)-(2))
4.9 Security controls must be assessed at least annually by an assessor independent of the controls (P07). After the Cybersecurity Plan is approved, the Plan must be audited annually (101.630(f)). (CA-2; GV.OV-01)
4.10 **Records.** Records of cybersecurity training, drills, exercises, threats, reportable cyber incidents and Plan audits must be created and kept for at least 2 years, protected against unauthorized deletion, destruction or amendment, and made available to the Coast Guard on request. Policies, assessments and the risk register are kept for at least 2 years after they are replaced. (SI-12; 101.640; 33 CFR 105.225)
4.11 The CySO must keep a register of the legal, regulatory and contractual security requirements that apply to the terminal, including carrier terminal services agreements, and review it at least annually. (GV.OC-03)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement in POL-01 section 4.7. Sanctions range from retraining to termination of employment or of a contract, depending on intent and harm. For longshore labor, the company may refuse further access to its systems and refer the matter to the hiring hall. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), the Cybersecurity Assessment (33 CFR 101.650(e)(1)) and the reviews in this policy set.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.6. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the majority owner for High risk), and expire within 12 months. Where a Subpart F measure is not technically feasible, the compensating control must be documented for the Cybersecurity Plan.
