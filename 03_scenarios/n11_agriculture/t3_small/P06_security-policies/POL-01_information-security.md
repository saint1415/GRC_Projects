## 1. Purpose
Set up the Cris Santos Company information security program, assign who is accountable, and give every other security policy its authority. The program protects the farm's crops, workers, records, and personal data by keeping irrigation and farm systems safe, accurate, and available.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (owners, year-round and seasonal employees including H-2A workers, and contractors such as the MSP and the irrigation integrator when they work on farm systems) at the Home Block and the North Block. Covers all farm systems and data, including the irrigation, pump, fertigation, and cooler operational technology (OT) and the systems vendors operate for the farm.

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 The farm must maintain an information security program based on NIST CSF 2.0, with NIST SP 800-82 Rev. 3 applied to irrigation and pump OT. The program is documented in this policy set and the System Security Plan. (PM-1; GV.PO-01)
4.2 The Operations and Technology Manager is the designated security lead. The designation must be in writing and must state the time allowed for the role. (PM-2; GV.RR-02)
4.3 A cybersecurity risk assessment must be performed every July before the season, and after major changes, using NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1. Risks must be tracked in the risk register with an owner and a treatment. (RA-3; PM-9; ID.RA-05)
4.4 Risk acceptance authority: the security lead may accept Low risks; the Farm Manager, Moderate; the majority owner and General Manager, High and Very High. A risk that could injure a worker must not be accepted at High without a dated treatment plan. (PM-9; GV.RM-02)
4.5 Security controls must be assessed at least annually (P07). Every weakness must be tracked on the POA&M with an owner and date, and the POA&M must be reviewed with the majority owner each quarter. (CA-2; CA-5; GV.OV-01)
4.6 **Suppliers.** Before the farm buys or connects any system that can touch irrigation, farm records, or personal data, the security lead must complete the supplier checklist. Contracts with suppliers that access farm systems (the integrator, MSP, equipment dealer, and SaaS vendors) must include security terms: named accounts, MFA, access only when approved, incident notice, and return or deletion of farm data at exit. (SA-4; SA-9; SR-2; GV.SC-05)
4.7 Critical suppliers must be reviewed each year, using a SOC 2 report where one exists or a questionnaire where it does not. (SR-6; GV.SC-07)
4.8 Exceptions to any security policy must be requested in writing, risk-rated, approved under 4.4, recorded in the risk register, and limited to 12 months or less. (PL-1)
4.9 **Consequences.** Workforce members who break security policies face consequences in proportion to intent and harm: retraining, written warning, suspension, or termination. Good-faith reporting of a mistake is never punished. (PS-8; GV.RR-04)
4.10 Policies must be reviewed at least annually and after major changes or incidents. (PL-1; GV.PO-02)
4.11 Records must be kept for at least the periods the law sets: Produce Safety records for at least 2 years (21 CFR 112.164(a)(1)); H-2A earnings records for at least 3 years after the certification date (20 CFR 655.122(j)(4)); security documentation (assessments, risk register, incident records) for at least 3 years. (SI-12)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.9. Consequences range from retraining to termination, depending on intent and harm, and are applied the same way to every worker regardless of visa status. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07) and the access reviews in this policy set.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.8. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the majority owner and General Manager for High risk), and expire within 12 months.
