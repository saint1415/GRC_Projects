## 1. Purpose
Set up the Cris Santos Company information security program, assign who is accountable, and give every other security policy its authority. The program protects CUI entrusted to the company by its defense customers, keeps the plant running, and keeps the company eligible for defense work.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (owners, employees, temporary workers, and contractors) at the Florida plant and working remotely. Covers all company systems and data, with added rules for the CUI Engineering Enclave (CEE) defined in the SSP (P02), printed CUI on the shop floor, and systems that service providers operate for the company. It applies to controlled unclassified information (CUI), including controlled technical information and export-controlled technical data, federal contract information (FCI), and all other company information.

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 The company must maintain an information security program that meets NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 for every system that processes, stores, or transmits CUI, as DFARS 252.204-7012(b) requires, and that supports CMMC Level 2 certification. The program is documented in this policy set, the SSP, and the POA&M. (PM-1; GV.PO-01)
4.2 The IT Manager is the designated security program lead. The designation must be in writing. (PM-2; GV.RR-02)
4.3 CUI may be processed, stored, or transmitted only inside the CUI Engineering Enclave or on printed media handled under POL-04. Any change to the enclave boundary must be approved by the IT Manager and reflected in the SSP before use. (PL-2; 3.12.4)
4.4 A risk assessment must be performed at least annually and after major changes, using NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1. Risks must be tracked in the risk register with an owner and treatment. (RA-3; PM-9; 3.11.1)
4.5 Risk acceptance authority: the IT Manager may accept Low risks; the Vice President of Operations, Moderate; the President, High and Very High. A known failure to meet a DFARS 252.204-7012 duty or an unauthorized export may not be accepted. (PM-9; GV.RM-01)
4.6 Security policies must be reviewed at least annually and updated after major changes, incidents, or assessment findings. (PL-1; GV.PO-02)
4.7 Exceptions to any security policy must be requested in writing, risk-rated, approved per 4.5, recorded in the risk register, and limited to 12 months or less. (PL-1)
4.8 **Sanctions.** Workforce members who fail to comply with security policies must be sanctioned in proportion to intent and harm: retraining, written warning, suspension, or termination. HR must document each sanction. (PS-8; GV.RR-04)
4.9 The company must assess its security requirements against SP 800-171A objectives at least annually, keep a POA&M for every open deficiency, and review the POA&M monthly. The SPRS score must reflect the current assessment and must be updated within 30 days of a material change. The President affirms compliance in SPRS only on evidence. (CA-2; CA-5; 3.12.1; 3.12.2; 252.204-7019; 32 CFR 170.22)
4.10 **Service providers and subcontractors.** Before a cloud service provider stores, processes, or transmits CUI, it must meet the FedRAMP Moderate baseline or equivalent (DFARS 252.204-7012(b)(2)(ii)(D)). Before any subcontractor or outside processor receives CUI, the purchase order must include DFARS 252.204-7012 and, where required, 252.204-7020 and 252.204-7021, and the Contracts Manager must confirm the supplier's SPRS and CMMC status. No flowdown, no CUI. (SA-9; SR-3; GV.SC-05; 252.204-7012(m))
4.11 Security documentation (SSP, POA&M, assessments, policies, incident records) must be retained for at least 6 years. (PL-1)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement in POL-01 section 4.8. Sanctions range from retraining to termination, depending on intent and harm. A suspected unauthorized release of ITAR or EAR technical data is also referred to the Contracts Manager (Empowered Official) for a voluntary disclosure decision. Compliance is checked through the annual self-assessment against SP 800-171A objectives, the control assessment (P07), and the reviews in this policy set.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the President for High risk), recorded in the risk register, and expire within 12 months. An exception never removes a DFARS 252.204-7012 duty or permits an unauthorized export.
