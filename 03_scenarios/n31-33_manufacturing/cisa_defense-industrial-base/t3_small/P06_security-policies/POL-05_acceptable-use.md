## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how the workforce uses company systems, devices, and data, including CUI and generative AI tools.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (owners, employees, temporary workers, and contractors) at the Florida plant and working remotely. Covers all company systems and data, with added rules for the CUI Engineering Enclave (CEE) defined in the SSP (P02), printed CUI on the shop floor, and systems that service providers operate for the company. It applies to controlled unclassified information (CUI), including controlled technical information and export-controlled technical data, federal contract information (FCI), and all other company information.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|

| IT Manager | Security awareness content, role-based training, and phishing exercises |

| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use of corporate systems is allowed if it does not involve CUI or create risk. Enclave systems are for work only. (PL-4)
4.2 Every workforce member must sign this policy before receiving access and again each year. Existing staff must sign by 2026-10-31. (PL-4)
4.3 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year. It must cover CUI marking and handling, export control basics, phishing, and insider threat indicators and reporting. Administrators, engineers who mark CUI, and quality staff who distribute drawings also complete role-based training each year. (AT-2; AT-2(2); AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02; 3.2.1 to 3.2.3)
4.4 Lock your screen when you step away. Do not leave printed drawings unattended where visitors pass. (AC-11; 3.1.10)
4.5 Do not install software, browser extensions, or connect removable storage unless IT approves it. (CM-11; 3.4.9)
4.6 Do not open unexpected attachments or links. Report suspicious messages with the Report Phishing button, and report any sign-in prompt you did not expect. (AT-2)
4.7 Report lost or stolen devices, CUI sent to the wrong place, and anything suspicious to IT immediately (POL-03 4.2).
4.8 **Generative AI.** Use only tools on the approved list kept by the IT Manager and the Director of Engineering (P10). Never paste, upload, or describe CUI, export-controlled data, or customer drawings in any public AI chatbot or AI service outside the enclave. The enclave suite's AI assistant may be used with CUI only by users enrolled in the approved pilot, and its output must be checked by a qualified person before use. AI output is never the source for a dimension, tolerance, or NC program. (PL-4; AC-20; 3.1.20)
4.9 Enclave systems display a banner: use is monitored and CUI rules apply. Using the system means accepting these conditions. (AC-8; 3.1.9)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement in POL-01 section 4.8. Sanctions range from retraining to termination, depending on intent and harm. A suspected unauthorized release of ITAR or EAR technical data is also referred to the Contracts Manager (Empowered Official) for a voluntary disclosure decision. Compliance is checked through the annual self-assessment against SP 800-171A objectives, the control assessment (P07), and the reviews in this policy set.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the President for High risk), recorded in the risk register, and expire within 12 months. An exception never removes a DFARS 252.204-7012 duty or permits an unauthorized export.
