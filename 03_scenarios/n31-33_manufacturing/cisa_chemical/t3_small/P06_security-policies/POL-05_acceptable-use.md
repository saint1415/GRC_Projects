## 1. Purpose
Set the rules for using company systems, OT workstations, and data, and the training every user must complete.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members and contractors, including DCS integrator staff, on any company system, OT workstation, or company data, on site or remote.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All users | Follow these rules; sign the acknowledgment at hire and yearly |
| HR Manager | Collects acknowledgments; tracks training completion |
| IT Manager | Runs awareness training and phishing exercises; keeps the approved-tools list |
| Plant Manager | Ensures operators and maintenance staff complete OT training |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for business use. Limited personal use of office systems is allowed if it does not interfere with work or security. **OT workstations are for operating and maintaining the process only.** No email, web browsing, or personal use. (PL-4; CM-7)
4.2 Users must sign this policy at hire and every year. Integrator staff must sign the OT rules of behavior before their first session. (PL-4)
4.3 **Training.** All 162 employees must complete security awareness training at hire and every year, including phishing and payment-fraud awareness. Operators, I&E technicians, the Controls Engineer, and integrator staff must also complete OT security training every year, covering removable media, remote access rules, and when to suspect a cyber cause in a process upset. Quarterly phishing exercises run for office staff. (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02; 27.230(a)(11))
4.4 Users must not install software on any company device. On OT workstations, only the Controls Engineer may install vendor-qualified software, under MOC. (CM-11; PR.PS-05)
4.5 Users must not connect personal devices, phones, or chargers to OT workstations or OT network ports. (MP-7)
4.6 Users must report lost devices, suspicious messages, and unexpected HMI or DCS behavior at once (POL-03 4.2). (SI-8)
4.7 Changes to bank details, payment instructions, or shipping addresses for hydrogen peroxide orders must be confirmed by a phone call to a known number. (PL-4)
4.8 **Generative AI tools.** Only AI tools on the IT Manager's approved-tools list may be used for company work, and only with the data classes the list allows. Formulations, master recipes, process data, DCS or SIS configurations, legacy CVI, and employee PII must **never** be entered in public generative AI tools. AI-001 recommendations are advisory only (P10). (PL-4; GV.PO-01)
4.9 OT workstations show a login banner stating that use is monitored and restricted to authorized operation. (AC-8)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement in POL-01 section 4.7. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), training records, and monitoring.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.6.
