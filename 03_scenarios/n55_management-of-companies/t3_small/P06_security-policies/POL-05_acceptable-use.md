## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how the workforce uses group systems, devices, data, and AI tools.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members (owners, managers, employees, contractors, and temporary staff) of Cris Santos Company, LLC and each subsidiary, at every site and when working remotely. Covers all systems and data the group owns or uses, including the Shared Corporate Services Platform, each subsidiary's own systems, and systems that service providers run for the group.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Director | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and annually; tracks training |
| IT Manager | Security awareness content, reminders, phishing simulations, and the AI approved-tools list |
| Subsidiary Presidents | Make sure their staff complete training and follow these rules |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Group systems are for group business. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not involve Restricted data or create risk. (PL-4)
4.2 Every workforce member must sign this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4(1))
4.3 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year, and when new tools such as the AI assistant are introduced. The IT Manager sends monthly reminders and runs phishing simulations at least quarterly. Payables, treasury, HR, and Finance staff must also complete payment fraud and social engineering training each year. (AT-2; AT-2(3); PR.AT-01; 314.4(e)(1))
4.4 **Payments.** Never change a vendor's or employee's bank details based on an email, text, or call alone. Call the vendor or employee back on a number already on file and get a second approver. Report any such request as a possible incident. (AT-2(3))
4.5 Lock your screen when you step away. Do not install software, browser extensions, or USB storage unless IT approves it. (AC-11; CM-11)
4.6 Use only group-managed devices for Restricted data. Report lost or stolen devices immediately (POL-03 4.3). (AC-19)
4.7 Do not open unexpected attachments or links. Approve an MFA prompt only when you just started a sign-in yourself. Report suspicious messages with the Report Phishing button. (AT-2)
4.8 Do not use personal email or personal cloud storage for group work.
4.9 **Generative AI.**
   - Use only AI tools on the approved list. Today that is the group AI assistant, for enrolled pilot users.
   - Do not enter Restricted data into any AI tool except the group assistant, and only for the approved purposes in P10.
   - **Do not use AI output to make or support decisions about hiring, firing, discipline, pay, or credit.**
   - Check AI output before you rely on it or send it outside the group. You are responsible for anything you send.
   - Report AI output that shows information you should not be able to see.

   (PL-4; GV.PO-01)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement (POL-01 section 4.8). Sanctions range from retraining to termination, depending on intent and harm. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07) and the access reviews in this policy set.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the right level under POL-01 4.5, and expire within 12 months.
