## 1. Purpose
Set up the Cris Santos Company information security program, assign who is accountable, and give every other security policy its authority. The program protects customer devices and data in the company's care, customer records, payment activity, and company systems.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (employees, managers, temporary staff, and contractors) at Stores A to D and the Depot. Covers all company systems and data, customer devices and the data on them while they are in the company's custody, and systems that vendors operate for the company.

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 The company must maintain an information security program documented in this policy set and the System Security Plan, using NIST CSF 2.0 as its benchmark. (PM-1; GV.PO-01)
4.2 The IT Manager is the designated Information Security Lead. The designation must be in writing and acknowledged. (PM-2; GV.RR-02; PCI DSS 12.1.3)
4.3 A risk assessment must be performed at least annually and after major changes (a new site, service, or system), using NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1. Risks must be tracked in the risk register with an owner and a treatment. (RA-3; PM-9)
4.4 Risk acceptance authority: the IT Manager may accept Low risks; the General Manager, Moderate; the majority owner, High and Very High. A High risk to customer device data may not be accepted without a dated treatment plan. (PM-9; GV.RM-01)
4.5 Security policies must be reviewed at least annually and after major changes or incidents, and published to all staff. (PL-1; GV.PO-02; PCI DSS 12.1.1, 12.1.2)
4.6 Exceptions to any security policy must be requested in writing, risk-rated, approved per 4.4, recorded in the risk register, and limited to 12 months or less. (PL-1)
4.7 **Sanctions.** Workforce members who break security or privacy policies must be sanctioned in proportion to intent and harm: retraining, written warning, suspension, or termination. Viewing, copying, keeping, or sharing a customer's personal data without a repair need is serious misconduct and is grounds for termination. HR and the Operations Manager must document each sanction. (PS-8; GV.RR-04)
4.8 **Suppliers.** No vendor may receive, store, or process customer personal data, or support card payments, until it has passed a security review and signed contract terms covering security, data use (including no training of AI models on company data), deletion, and breach notice to the company within 72 hours or less. The Controller keeps a vendor inventory and checks each critical vendor's assurance report (SOC 2 or PCI DSS AOC) every year. (SA-4; SA-9; GV.SC-05; PCI DSS 12.8.1-12.8.5)
4.9 Security controls must be assessed at least annually (P07) and after major changes. (CA-2; ID.IM-01)
4.10 Security documentation (policies, assessments, risk decisions) must be kept for at least 3 years; incident records and any written no-notice determination under Fla. Stat. 501.171(4)(c) for at least 5 years. (SI-12)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions rule in POL-01 section 4.7. Sanctions range from retraining to termination, depending on intent and harm. Misuse of a customer's device data is treated as serious misconduct. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), the access reviews in POL-02, and the log reviews in POL-03.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.6. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the majority owner for High risk), and expire within 12 months.
