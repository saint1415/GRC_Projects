## 1. Purpose
Make sure the hospital detects, contains, reports, and recovers from security incidents quickly and lawfully, keeps patients safe while systems are down, and meets breach notification deadlines.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members of Cris Santos Company, LLC: employees, contracted clinicians, agency staff, students, volunteers, and vendor and MSP personnel. Covers all systems and data, including medical devices, building OT, and systems that business associates operate for the hospital.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| IT Manager | Incident commander for security incidents; coordinates the MSP and forensic firm |
| CEO | Engages counsel and the cyber insurer; approves external communications; with the Director of Nursing and the ED physician on duty, decides ambulance diversion |
| Director of Nursing | Activates downtime procedures; clinical safety lead during the incident |
| Quality and Compliance Manager (Privacy Officer) | Breach risk assessment and notification decisions |
| Facilities Manager (Emergency Preparedness Coordinator) | Activates the emergency plan and incident command when the CEO directs; county emergency management liaison |
| All workforce | Report suspected incidents immediately |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 The hospital must maintain an incident response plan and runbooks for its most likely incidents, starting with ransomware that forces EHR downtime (P08). The plan must be an annex to the emergency preparedness plan. (IR-8; RS.MA-01; 42 CFR 485.625(a)(2))
4.2 Workforce members, including contracted clinicians and agency staff, must report any suspected incident **immediately**, and within 1 hour at most, by calling the 24x7 incident line or telling the charge nurse. Examples: phishing clicks, lost devices, misdirected PHI, unusual system or device behavior. Good-faith reporting is never sanctioned. (IR-6; RS.MA-02; 164.308(a)(6)(ii))
4.3 Every incident must be logged, categorized, and tracked to closure in the ticketing system under the Security category. (IR-5)
4.4 The Privacy Officer must decide whether an incident is a reportable breach under 45 CFR 164.402, using the four-factor risk assessment, and document the decision. (IR-6)
4.5 Notifications to individuals, HHS, the media, the Florida Department of Legal Affairs, and law enforcement must meet the deadlines in the P08 notification matrix. Legal counsel must confirm each notification. (IR-6; RS.CO-02; RS.CO-03)
4.6 No ransom may be paid without approval from the governing body, legal counsel, and the cyber insurer, and an OFAC sanctions check. (IR-4)
4.7 **Patient safety first.** When an incident takes away the EHR, laboratory, imaging, or phones, the Director of Nursing must activate downtime procedures at once. Ambulance diversion may be requested from county EMS only by the CEO (or delegate), the Director of Nursing, and the ED physician on duty together. Diversion never stops the medical screening and stabilization of anyone who arrives at the hospital. (IR-4; CP-2; 42 CFR 489.24)
4.8 The incident response plan must be tested at least annually by a facilitated tabletop exercise with clinical leaders, and after any major incident. The tabletop may be filed as the additional exercise of the emergency preparedness program. (IR-3; ID.IM-02; 42 CFR 485.625(d)(2)(ii))
4.9 Lessons learned must be documented within 30 days of closing an incident, added to the risk register, and filed as an after-action report in the emergency preparedness binder. (IR-4; 42 CFR 485.625(d)(2)(iii))

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement (POL-01 4.8). Sanctions range from retraining to termination or removal from the schedule, depending on intent and harm. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07) and the access reviews in this policy set.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.6. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the governing body for High and Very High risk), and expire within 12 months.
