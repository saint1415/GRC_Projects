## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce may use company systems, data, devices, and AI tools.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members and anyone else given access to company systems or data, at all sites and when working remotely.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Director | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and annually |
| Compliance and Privacy Officer | Security and privacy training content; tracks completion |
| Security Manager | Monthly reminders and quarterly phishing simulations |
| Chief Medical Officer | Clinical AI use rules and the recording-consent process for the AI scribe |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use is allowed if it involves no patient data and creates no risk. (PL-4; 164.310(b))
4.2 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4(1))
4.3 Security and privacy training is required at hire and every year. Monthly security reminders and at least quarterly phishing simulations are sent to all workforce. Staff in specialized roles (IT administrators, CBO finance staff, ASC staff, AI tool users) must complete role-based training. (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02; 164.308(a)(5)(ii)(A))
4.4 Lock your screen when you step away, and position screens so patients and visitors cannot read them. (AC-11; 164.310(c))
4.5 Do not install software or browser extensions, or connect USB storage, unless IT approves it. (CM-11)
4.6 Only company-managed devices, or personal phones enrolled under the mobile app protection rules, may access company systems. (AC-19)
4.7 Report lost or stolen devices, misdirected PHI, suspicious messages, and anything unusual immediately (POL-03 4.2).
4.8 **AI tools.** Use only AI tools on the approved list (STD-05). Never paste, upload, or dictate patient information into a tool that is not approved. Outputs from approved tools must be reviewed by a qualified person before they enter a medical record or go to a patient or payer. (PL-4)
4.9 **Recording visits.** Providers using the AI scribe must obtain and document the consent of every party to the conversation before recording starts, as Florida law requires (Fla. Stat. 934.03(2)(d)), and must stop recording if anyone objects. (PT-4; PL-4)
4.10 Look up patient records only when your job requires it. EHR access is logged and analyzed, and inappropriate access leads to sanctions. (AU-6)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the HIPAA sanctions procedure (POL-01 section 4.8). Compliance is checked through the annual independent assessment (P07), EHR access analytics, and AI scribe consent audits.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7.
