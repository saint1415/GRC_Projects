## 1. Purpose
Set up the Cris Santos Company information security program required by the FTC Safeguards Rule, assign who is accountable, and give every other security policy its authority. The program protects the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of customer information, escrowed and closing funds, and all other company information.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members: owners, employees, and the affiliated sales associates who work under the Broker of Record's license as independent contractors. Covers both offices, all company systems and data, and systems that service providers operate for the company. It applies to customer information under 16 CFR 314.2(d) and, by company decision, to all client, tenant, and applicant information handled on the same systems.

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 The company must maintain a written information security program made up of this policy set (POL-01 to POL-05), the System Security Plan (P02), the risk register (P01), and the POA&M (P07). The program must contain the elements in 16 CFR 314.4. (PM-1; GV.PO-01; 314.3(a))
4.2 **Qualified Individual.** The IT Manager is the Qualified Individual. The majority owner must sign a designation letter stating the Qualified Individual's authority, reporting line, and budget. If any duty is delegated to a service provider, the company keeps responsibility and the IT Manager oversees that provider. (PM-2; GV.RR-02; 314.4(a))
4.3 **Risk assessment.** A written risk assessment must be performed each August, and after a major change or a significant incident, using NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1. It must include criteria for rating risks, criteria for assessing confidentiality, integrity, and availability, and how each risk will be mitigated or accepted. Risks are tracked in the risk register with an owner and a treatment. (RA-3; PM-9; 314.4(b))
4.4 **Risk acceptance authority.** The IT Manager may accept Low and Very Low risks; the COO, Moderate; the majority owner, High and Very High, only temporarily and with a dated treatment plan. A High risk to clients' closing funds or to escrowed funds may not be accepted without a treatment plan due within 90 days. (PM-9; GV.RM-01; 314.4(b)(1)(iii))
4.5 Security policies must be reviewed at least annually and updated after major changes or incidents. (PL-1; GV.PO-02)
4.6 **Exceptions** to any security policy must be requested in writing, risk-rated, approved under 4.4, recorded in the risk register, and limited to 12 months or less. An exception to MFA (POL-02 4.3) or to encryption (POL-04 4.2) is valid only with the Qualified Individual's written approval of an equivalent or compensating control, as 314.4(c)(3) and (c)(5) require. (PL-1)
4.7 **Sanctions.** Employees who violate a security policy face sanctions in proportion to intent and harm: retraining, written warning, suspension, or termination. For contractor sales associates, sanctions range from retraining and loss of system access to ending the affiliation with the brokerage. HR (employees) or the Sales Managers (contractors) must document each sanction. (PS-8; GV.RR-04)
4.8 **Service providers.** Before a service provider receives access to customer information, the company must assess its safeguards and sign a contract that requires it to maintain them. The IT Manager keeps an inventory of these providers and reviews the highest-risk ones each year, using their SOC 2 reports where available (P09). (SA-9; GV.SC-05; 314.4(f))
4.9 **Testing.** Key controls must be assessed at least annually (P07). Absent effective continuous monitoring, the company must run a penetration test each year and vulnerability assessments at least every six months, and after major changes. (CA-2; 314.4(d))
4.10 **Annual report.** The Qualified Individual must report in writing to the majority owner at least once a year. The report covers the program's overall status and compliance with the Safeguards Rule, and material matters: risk assessment, risk decisions, service provider arrangements, test results, security events and responses, and recommended changes. The first report is due 2026-12-15. (PM-9; GV.OV-01; 314.4(i))
4.11 The program must be evaluated and adjusted after testing and monitoring results, material changes to operations (for example, a new closing platform), risk assessments, and incidents. (PM-9; 314.4(g))
4.12 Security policies, risk assessments, assessment results, incident records, and annual reports must be retained for at least 5 years. (SI-12)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under section 4.7. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), quarterly access reviews (POL-02), and the monthly sampling of wire verification records (POL-03 4.9).

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow section 4.6. They must be written, risk-rated, approved under 4.4, and expire within 12 months.
