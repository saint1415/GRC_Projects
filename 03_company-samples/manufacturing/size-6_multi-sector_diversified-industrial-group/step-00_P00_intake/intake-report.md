## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the group's own records before any assessment work began on 2026-05-04. It covers the legal entities, the systems that hold device software, signing keys, PHI, Federal contract information and client confidential data in each division and in corporate shared services, the suppliers that touch them, and the rules that may bind each entity. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Items are grouped by division: the title of each row starts with Group, Medical Devices, Distribution or Testing. Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (interviews, document reviews, samples, tests) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analyses (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Entities and scale.** The holding company is an SEC registrant with three wholly owned subsidiaries; corporate shared services sit in the parent (EV-001). The workforce is 45,000: Medical Devices about 24,000, Distribution about 13,000, Testing about 3,500, corporate about 4,500 (EV-002). FY2025 revenue was about $18.0 billion: Medical Devices $10.8 billion, Distribution $6.3 billion, Testing $0.9 billion (EV-003). The FY2025 Form 10-K carries the Item 106 disclosure; the disclosure committee charter lists no written materiality factors for product incidents (EV-004). Risk acceptance authority is set by level, and patient-safety risks rated High must be treated (EV-005). The board risk committee receives quarterly cyber and product security reports (EV-006). Internal audit reports to the board audit committee; no Distribution assessment report was provided (EV-007).

**Policies and common controls.** Group policies POL-01 to POL-05 (2025 edition) are mapped to CSF 2.0, with logging, cloud guardrail, change control and retention standards (EV-008). The Distribution supplement v2025 was last aligned in 2025-05 and does not address Federal contract information, allows site accounts on handhelds, leaves visitor logs to each site, and has its own 3-level severity scale; the Testing supplement v2025 has barrier rules and no AI tool rule (EV-009). The Distribution security and compliance lead position was vacant for 7 months across 2025 and 2026 (EV-002). The common control catalog lists 89 controls; inheritance matrices exist for Medical Devices (2025) and Testing's 5 federated laboratories (2026), and no Distribution matrix was provided (EV-010).

**Group identity and access.** 45,000 workforce identities use MFA; administrators and key custodians use hardware keys (EV-011). Identity governance runs joiner-mover-leaver workflows and quarterly certification; PLM holds 164 contract manufacturer and supplier guest accounts, 38 with no expiry date, outside the certification campaigns (EV-012, EV-014). Privileged access is just in time through PAM with session recording, including MES and automation vendor sessions (EV-013). Endpoints lock after 10 minutes and laptops are encrypted; remote access goes through the zero-trust service (EV-015).

**Group security operations.** The SOC runs 24x7. MES at Plants D and E, distribution center automation and the 4 acquired laboratories are not in the SIEM data source list, and no rule covers Medical Devices access to Testing repositories (EV-016). The incident response plan v5 was approved 2025-02; its site lists do not include Plants D and E or the acquired laboratories, and the SOC, the PSIRT and Distribution use different severity scales (EV-017). EDR covers IT endpoints, servers, cloud workloads, build agents and MES servers at Plants A to C (EV-018). Scans run weekly, with composition analysis in every build (EV-019).

**Group cloud platform and network.** Provider A hosts the landing zone, the DCC, the Testing client portal and the DEMS build agents; provider B hosts DCC disaster recovery and the immutable backup vault (EV-020, EV-023). Hub firewalls deny by default between division accounts, and the findings vault account has no route to Medical Devices accounts (EV-021). Keys are per account, with per-client keys in the findings vault (EV-022). External endpoints accept TLS 1.2 or higher (EV-024), and logs go to a write-only archive (EV-025).

**Workforce, vendors, AI, notification, trade and facilities.** Training completion is 98%, with quarterly phishing exercises (EV-026). Vendors are tiered and reviewed each year; the 2025 supply agreement template carries SBOM and vulnerability notice clauses (EV-028); SOC 2 reports are on file for both cloud providers and the identity, PLM, source hosting and LIMS vendors (EV-029). AI found: AI-001, predictive maintenance, a coding assistant pilot, demand forecasting, an ordering portal chatbot, AI-assisted fuzzing, the Testing drafting tool (since 2026-02), an enterprise assistant pilot and a proposed applicant screening tool; the Group AI Standard was adopted 2026-03 (EV-030). The single notification matrix is a draft and no cross-division exercise is recorded (EV-031). Screening for export control happens at onboarding (EV-032). Federal contracts are held only by Distribution (EV-033).

**Medical Devices.** About 215,000 devices are installed: IX-4 about 95,000, IX-3 about 42,000 (cleared 2018), PM-7 about 60,000, US-2 about 18,000 (EV-037). The SPDF procedure and the cyber device checklist date from 2023 (EV-038). The CVD policy is published, 97% of 2026 reports were acknowledged within 2 business days, and the division has been an ISAO member since 2023; PSIRT cases are linked to complaint records by hand (EV-039). SBOMs are generated in every build for IX-4, PM-7, US-2 and the DCC, none for IX-3, and 31% of third-party components have no supplier SBOM entry or support end date (EV-040). Security maintenance releases are quarterly for IX-4 and PM-7 (EV-041). The IX-3 operating system component reaches end of support in June 2027, which the IX-3 guide does not state (EV-042); IX-3 updates go by USB, and 58% of units run the latest security release (EV-043). Current products are signed in HSMs with two-person approval; IX-3 is signed with a software key on the Plant D build server, with a passphrase held by 4 administrators (EV-044). Plants D and E share one network between MES, test stations and office systems, their 19 test stations use shared logins, and their MES backups stay on local disk (EV-048, EV-049, EV-050). IX-3 changes at Plant D are logged outside the group change board (EV-051), and contract manufacturer access to PLM was last reviewed in 2024 (EV-052). The DCC serves about 1,300 hospitals under BAAs with PHI of about 9 million patients; the BAA terms register covers 81% (EV-053). About 14% of hospitals have not configured federation, and support staff hold standing read access to all tenants' PHI (EV-055). AI-001 has open bias findings (EV-060).

**Distribution.** The VA and DoD contracts include FAR 52.204-21 and 52.204-25, and the DoD contract also DFARS 252.204-7012; the next option is due 2027-03-31 (EV-061). No self-assessment, SPRS entry or list of systems holding Federal contract information was provided (EV-062). Distribution is a distributor and an initial importer for 6 foreign manufacturers' lines and does not relabel (EV-063). The complaint intake form has no cybersecurity prompt, and importer complaints are reviewed weekly by hand (EV-064). The ERP runs in one colocation data center with no replica (EV-066). Handhelds sign in with site accounts, and the federal order module is restricted to the federal sales role (EV-067). The Section 889 representation relies on supplier attestations (EV-069).

**Testing.** 4 of 9 laboratories run a local directory outside SYS-G1 with MFA for remote access only (EV-071). A collaboration space used for client test material has 212 Medical Devices engineers as members (EV-072). The LIMS is project-based and the findings vault uses per-client keys (EV-073). NDAs cover about 950 clients; templates prohibit PHI and have no AI consent terms (EV-074). Accreditation to ISO/IEC 17025 is current (EV-075). 3 submissions in 2026 contained patient identifiers (EV-076). Testing ran premarket penetration tests for US-2 and the DCC with no independence statements on file (EV-077). The AI drafting pilot has no change record or client consent clause (EV-078).
