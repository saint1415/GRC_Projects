## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce uses group systems and information.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members in every division and in corporate shared services, on any device used for group work, including plant, warehouse, and laboratory equipment.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow these rules; complete training; report incidents |
| Managers | Make sure staff complete training and acknowledgments |
| Group HR | Tracks acknowledgments and training completion |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Workforce members must access patient, customer, client, and federal contract information only as needed for their job. Browsing records out of curiosity is prohibited. (PL-4; AC-8)

4.2 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy and their division supplement at hire and every year. Testing staff and Medical Devices engineers must also acknowledge the information barrier rules. (PL-4; PR.AT-01)

4.3 Every workforce member must complete security awareness training at hire and every year, with quarterly phishing exercises. (AT-2; PR.AT-01; 164.308(a)(5)(ii)(A))

4.4 Engineers, release managers, and key custodians must complete secure development and custody training before receiving pipeline or signing access. (AT-3; PR.AT-02)

4.5 Suspected incidents must be reported to the group SOC within 1 hour (POL-03 4.1). (IR-6)

4.6 Group information may be used only on group-managed devices and approved services. It must not be sent to personal email or stored on personal devices or personal cloud storage. (AC-19; AC-20; 52.204-21(b)(1)(iii))

4.7 USB storage devices are blocked on engineering and plant systems except for approved, signed field service update media. (AC-19; PL-4)

4.8 **Generative AI.** Only AI tools on the group approved-tools list may be used for group work. Source code, vulnerability details, signing material, PHI, client data, and Federal contract information must never be entered into public AI tools. (PL-4; PL-4(1))

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
