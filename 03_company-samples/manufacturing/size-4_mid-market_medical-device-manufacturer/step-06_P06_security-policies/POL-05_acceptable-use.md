## 1. Purpose
Set the rules every workforce member follows when using company systems, the plant floor, and AI tools.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members and contractors, on campus, on the plant floor, at hospitals, and working remotely.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Director | Collects the annual acknowledgment; applies sanctions with the Compliance and Privacy Officer |
| Security Manager | Maintains the approved software and AI tools lists; monitors use |
| Managers and plant supervisors | Make sure their teams follow this policy |
| All workforce | Follow the rules below |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for authorized business use. Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy at hire and each year. (PL-4; GV.RR-04; 164.310(b))
4.2 Report phishing, lost devices, and anything unusual about a company system or a company device at a hospital immediately to the service desk security line. (AT-2; IR-6; PR.AT-01)
4.3 **Plant floor.** Do not connect personal devices or personal USB media to plant equipment or the OT network. Use only company-issued, scanned media. Operators must use their own login once badge login is deployed, and must never share it. (PL-4; CM-7; PR.PS-05)
4.4 Install only software from the approved catalog. Do not disable EDR, allowlisting, or encryption. (CM-11; PR.PS-05)
4.5 Lock screens when away. Use only company-managed, encrypted laptops for company work. (SC-28; PL-4)
4.6 Send PHI and Confidential data only through approved, encrypted channels to approved recipients. (SC-8; PR.DS-02)
4.7 Remote access to company systems requires a company device and MFA. Field service engineers must connect to hospital networks only as each hospital allows. (AC-17; PR.AA-03)
4.8 **AI tools.** Use only AI tools on the approved list (STD-05). Never enter source code, PHI, keys, credentials, or unfixed vulnerability details into public AI tools. Review AI output before relying on it, and put AI-assisted code through the same review as any other code. (PL-4; PR.AT-01)
4.9 Company systems are monitored for security. Users should have no expectation of privacy in their use of company systems, as permitted by law. (PL-4; AU-6)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.11. Blocking controls (public AI domains, USB control on stations) back up this policy.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.10.
