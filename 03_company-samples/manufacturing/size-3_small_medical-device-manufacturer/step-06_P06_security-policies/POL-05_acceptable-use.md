## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how the workforce uses company systems, devices, source code, and data, including AI tools.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members and contractors, on company devices and on any personal device used for company work (email and chat only).

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Manager | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and annually; tracks training |
| IT Manager | Security awareness content, reminders, phishing exercises, and the approved software list |
| VP Engineering | The approved AI tools list for engineering (with the IT Manager) |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not involve company data or create risk. (PL-4; 164.310(b))
4.2 Every workforce member must sign this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4(1))
4.3 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year. The IT Manager runs at least quarterly phishing exercises. Engineers and support staff with production access also complete annual secure development and HIPAA training. (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02; 164.308(a)(5)(ii)(A))
4.4 Lock your screen when you step away. At hospitals, never leave a laptop unattended. (AC-11)
4.5 Install only software on the approved list. Do not connect personal USB storage to company laptops or to MES and test stations. (CM-11)
4.6 Do not open unexpected attachments or links. Report suspicious messages with the Report Phishing button. (AT-2)
4.7 Report lost or stolen devices, misdirected PHI, unusual device behavior, and messages from security researchers to the incident line immediately (POL-03 4.2).
4.8 **Generative AI.** Use only AI tools on the approved list (P10). Never paste or upload source code, design files, vulnerability details, or PHI into a public AI tool or a personal account. The approved enterprise coding assistant may be used for company code only under its contract terms (no training on company data). AI-generated code is reviewed and tested like any other code. (PL-4; AC-20)
4.9 Look up patient data in the device cloud only when a hospital support ticket requires it. Access is logged and reviewed. (AU-6)
4.10 Do not change device settings, test limits, or production software outside the change control process. (CM-3)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions rule in POL-01 section 4.8. Sanctions range from retraining to termination, depending on intent and harm. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07) and web filtering reports.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7. They must be written, risk-rated, approved at the level set in POL-01 4.4, and expire within 12 months.
