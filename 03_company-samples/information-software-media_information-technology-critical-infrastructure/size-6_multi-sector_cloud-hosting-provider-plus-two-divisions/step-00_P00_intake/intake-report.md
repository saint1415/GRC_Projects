## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the group's own records before any assessment work began on 2026-05-04. It covers the legal entities, the systems that hold or can reach customer, agency, client, merchant and consumer data in each division and in corporate shared services, the suppliers and affiliates that touch them, and the rules that may bind each entity. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Items are grouped by division: the title of each row starts with Group, Cloud Hosting, Managed IT or Payment Processing. Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (interviews, documents, searches, tests) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analyses (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Entities and scale.** The holding company is an SEC registrant (large accelerated filer); the three divisions are separate legal subsidiaries and corporate shared services sit in the parent. The register lists state money transmitter licenses for bill pay and no bank charter, New York license, broker-dealer or adviser registration, insurance license or 42 CFR Part 2 program (EV-001). The workforce is 45,000 employees in 46 states plus about 1,400 contractors: Cloud Hosting about 21,000, Managed IT about 12,000, Payment Processing about 7,000, corporate about 5,000; G1 operations staff are U.S. persons (EV-002). FY2025 revenue was about $18.0 billion: Cloud Hosting $10.2 billion, Managed IT $4.8 billion, Payment Processing $3.0 billion in net revenue (EV-003). The Form 10-K Item 106 disclosure describes board risk committee oversight, and the disclosure committee charter covers Form 8-K Item 1.05 (EV-004, EV-005).

**Group identity and endpoints.** Every workforce identity uses single sign-on with MFA; administrators and HCP privileged roles use phishing-resistant hardware keys; the Managed IT legacy identity tenant federates in with push MFA and 12-hour sessions (EV-006). Joiner-mover-leaver workflows run from HR events, with a 4-hour disable target; legacy-tenant identities sit outside these workflows and are disabled the next business day (EV-007). The Q1 2026 certification did not include partner-operator grants, legacy-tenant accounts or RMM technician accounts (EV-008). Privileged access is just in time through PAM with recording; partner-operator access is not brokered by PAM (EV-009). Managed laptops are encrypted and enrolled in EDR (EV-010).

**Group security operations.** EDR covers endpoints and all division workloads, including the CDE and the enclave (EV-011). The SIEM ingests HCP, CDE and G1 security logs; there is no rule on run-command volume across tenants, and RMM audit logs are not a source (EV-012). The AI triage service, enabled 2025-11, auto-closed about 64% of alerts in Q1 2026 and can run 7 containment actions without a human in every division; alert context, including G1 metadata, goes to a third-party model service; no accuracy validation by division and no division approval of unattended actions were provided (EV-013). Playbooks were exercised each quarter, with no cross-division exercise; Managed IT handles single-client RMM incidents in its own ticketing (EV-014). The 2026 incident response plan does not define FedRAMP, DFARS or bank notification incidents in one place and does not describe the AI triage actions (EV-015). Notice procedures are kept by division, and no single group matrix lists all the notice duties (EV-016). Remediation targets are CVSS-based; network devices and BMCs are scanned monthly (EV-017).

**Policies and common controls.** The 2025 group policies are aligned to NIST CSF 2.0, with group standards (logging retention 1 year searchable and 7 years archived) and division supplements (EV-018). The common control catalog lists 83 controls; inheritance is documented for Cloud Hosting and Payment Processing (group services only), and no record was provided for Managed IT (EV-019).

**Group landing zone, keys, backups and logs.** Division accounts on SL-1 inherit guardrails, and the hub denies traffic between divisions by default (EV-020). Keys are HSM-backed, with a separate G1 domain (EV-021). The backup vault at external provider X is immutable with a separate backup identity (EV-022). The log archive is write-once (EV-023). External endpoints accept TLS 1.2 or higher (EV-024).

**Workforce, vendors, AI, risk and legal.** Awareness training shows 97% completion, with quarterly phishing exercises (EV-025). Background checks are recorded for all hires; partner operators are not under the HCP personnel terms (EV-026). Two follow-the-sun support vendors that see tickets and logs are screened against sanctions lists only (EV-027). SOC 2 reports are on file for the identity, SIEM, EDR and RMM vendors; the RMM contract has no incident notice clock (EV-028). Ten AI uses were found, three of them in use before the Group AI Standard was adopted on 2026-04-20 (EV-029, EV-030). Internal audit reports to the board audit committee and has planned the common control assessment for July and August (EV-032). No intercompany agreement binds Cloud Hosting or Managed IT to Payment Processing safeguards terms, and the partner-operator federation has no interconnection terms (EV-033). Group legal treats Payment Processing as a financial institution under 16 CFR 314.2(h), and counsel treats hosting for banks as a covered service (EV-034).

**Cloud Hosting.** About 38,000 customers, about 290,000 console identities, about 260 banking organizations and about 900 health care customers under BAAs; about 4,100 tenants operated by Managed IT; 72-hour customer notice and 99.99% and 99.9% SLAs (EV-036). Bank-designated contacts cover 182 of about 260 bank customers (EV-037). BAA notice terms are not in a register (EV-038). G1 holds a legacy Moderate authorization (2023-03-15), now Rev5 Class C; the package is documents and spreadsheets; the boundary list and third-party list do not include SYS-G1, SYS-G2, the AI triage service or the model service (EV-039). The 2026 annual assessment was completed on 2026-03-20; the next starts 2027-02-08 (EV-040). G1 uses legacy monthly deliverables and a POA&M with deviation requests (EV-041). Agency incident procedures are on file for 21 of 27 agencies; two staff hold DIBNet certificates; DIBNet and imaging steps are not in the G1 runbook (EV-042). The Security Inbox has been listed since 2026-01 and the Secure Configuration Guide was published in 2026-02 (EV-043). Two G1 services have no recorded module status (EV-044). The G1 incident procedure has no PAIN step, and the coordinator rota has one person overnight (EV-045). The partner-operator role covers about 4,100 tenants for about 1,900 engineers, with standing access and 12-hour tokens not bound to a device (EV-047). The HCP logs every request and run-command execution (EV-048). Change records have no FedRAMP significant change field; releases and guest-agent packages are signed with HSM keys (EV-049). Fix validation is not recorded for network devices and BMCs (EV-050). Failover tests covered single-zone and single-region loss (EV-052). About 61% of non-root customer administrators have MFA (EV-056). Supply chain procedures have no FAR reporting step (EV-057).

**Managed IT.** About 2,900 clients and about 380,000 systems under the RMM; about 190 banks, about 210 health care clients under BAAs (10 to 60 days), about 150 DIB clients, 11 federal civilian contracts and 9 DoD subcontracts; 48-hour client notice; no ESP responsibility matrix (EV-058). Bank contacts cover 121 of about 190 banks (EV-059). The legacy tenant accepts push MFA and 12-hour sessions; migration is due 2027-03-31 (EV-060). One RMM tenant serves all clients and internal uses; one approver can send a script to many clients; agents sit on 140 settlement-support servers and the enclave management servers; logs are kept 90 days and not forwarded (EV-061). The supplement was last aligned in 2024-05 and allows push MFA, 12-hour sessions, single-approver scripts and 90-day log retention (EV-062). The 2024-02-12 self-assessment shows 96 of 110 requirements fully met, and no C3PAO is engaged (EV-063). One person holds a DIBNet certificate, and the enclave procedure has no prime notice step (EV-064). FCI sits in the general consulting workspace (EV-065). The runbook agent has run 12 approved runbooks unattended since 2026-03 (EV-068).

**Payment Processing.** About 210,000 merchants, about 2,300 billers and about 14 million bill payers; sponsor banks A and B require notice within 24 hours; per-license notice duties are not mapped (EV-069). The Qualified Individual was designated in 2023 and reported to the boards in 2026-03 (EV-070). The two affiliates are not on the TPSP list, and the responsibility matrix covers group services only (EV-071). The 2026-03 scope confirmation lists the 140 connected-to servers without their RMM path; segmentation tests ran in 2025-09 and 2026-03 (EV-072). RMM accounts hold local administrator rights on those servers, and RMM actions are not among the reviewed log sources (EV-073). Merchant portal MFA is optional (EV-074). A SOC 1 Type 2 report covers settlement (EV-075). The division plan's card network procedure was last tested in 2024 (EV-076). No subgroup testing or decline explanation process is documented for the models (EV-079). The 2026 Report on Compliance, signed on 2026-04-30, arrived after the window with no open items (EV-080).
