## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce uses group systems and the customer and client environments it can reach.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members in every division and in corporate shared services, on any device used for group work.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow these rules; complete training; report incidents |
| Managers | Make sure staff complete training and acknowledgments |
| Group HR | Tracks acknowledgments and training completion |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Workforce members must access customer, client, agency, merchant, and consumer information only as their job requires, and only through approved, logged paths (support tooling, PAM, the client access broker). (PL-4)

4.2 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy and their division supplement at hire and every year. (PL-4; PR.AT-01)

4.3 Every workforce member must complete security awareness training at hire and every year, including adversary-in-the-middle phishing and MFA fatigue attacks. The group runs quarterly phishing exercises. (AT-2; AT-2(3); PR.AT-01; 16 CFR 314.4(e)(1))

4.4 Role-based training is required before access is granted: privileged administrators, partner operators, RMM technicians, CUI enclave administrators (CUI handling), and CDE staff (PCI DSS). (AT-3; PR.AT-02)

4.5 Suspected incidents must be reported to the group SOC within 1 hour (POL-03 4.1). (IR-6)

4.6 Restricted information may be used only on group-managed devices and approved services. It must not be sent to personal email or stored on personal devices. (AC-19; AC-20)

4.7 **Generative AI.** Only AI tools on the group approved-tools list may be used for group work. Credentials, customer or client data, CUI, account data, and G1 information must never be entered into a tool that is not approved for that class of data. (PL-4; PL-4(1))

4.8 Credentials and secrets must never be pasted into tickets, notes, chat, or documentation fields (POL-02 4.11). (IA-5)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.7.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.11.
