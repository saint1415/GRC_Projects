## 1. Purpose
Set the rules for how workforce members use company systems, devices, data, and AI tools.

## 2. Scope
All employees, contractors, and supplier staff with company accounts, on any device that reaches company systems.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Chief Human Resources Officer | Policy owner; training records and acknowledgments |
| Managers | Make sure their staff complete training and follow this policy |
| CISO | Approves the approved AI tools list (STD-05.3) with the AI governance committee |

## 4. Policy statements
Each statement is testable and tagged with its SP 800-53 control(s) and CSF 2.0 subcategory. `policy-control-map.csv` traces each statement to its regulatory driver and shows whether Internal Audit tested it in 2026 (P07).

4.1 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy at hire and annually. (PL-4; GV.RR-04)
4.2 Every workforce member must complete security awareness training at hire and annually, including phishing and social engineering exercises; staff with privileged or release roles must complete role-based training annually. (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01)
4.3 Company systems may be reached only from managed devices; G1 administration and provider tooling must never be reached from personal devices (STD-05.2). (AC-19; AC-20; PR.AA-05)
4.4 Remote work from outside the United States requires prior approval, and access to G1 or customer content from outside the United States is prohibited. (AC-17; AC-20; PR.AA-05)
4.5 Only AI tools on the approved list (STD-05.3) may be used with Internal or higher information; customer content must never be entered into an AI tool unless the tool is approved for it. (AC-20; SI-12; GV.OV-01)
4.6 Workforce members must report suspected incidents, lost devices, and phishing immediately through the security reporting channel. (IR-6; RS.MA-01)
4.7 Workforce members must not disable or bypass security controls, share credentials, or approve their own changes. (PL-4; AC-5; GV.RR-04)
4.8 Workforce members with access to G1 or customer content must sign access agreements before access and re-sign annually. (PS-6; GV.RR-04)

## 5. Standards and procedures under this policy
Standards set measurable requirements (approved by the CISO); procedures give step-by-step instructions (approved by the owning director). Both sit below this policy in the hierarchy and cannot contradict it.
- STD-05.1 Security Awareness and Training Standard
- STD-05.2 External Systems and Personal Devices Standard
- STD-05.3 Approved AI Tools List

## 6. Compliance and enforcement
Compliance is monitored through continuous control monitoring, the annual Internal Audit assessment (P07), the FedRAMP independent assessment, and access certifications. Violations are handled under the sanctions procedure (PRC-01.1, POL-01 statement 4.6), from retraining to termination of employment or contract, in proportion to intent and harm.

## 7. Exceptions
Exceptions to this policy follow the process in POL-01 section 7 and `policy-hierarchy.md` section 5 (PRC-01.2).
