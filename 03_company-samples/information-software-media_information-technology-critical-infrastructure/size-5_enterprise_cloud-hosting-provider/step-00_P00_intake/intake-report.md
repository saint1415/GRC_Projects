## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the company's own records before the enterprise BIA, risk analysis and gap analysis began on 2026-06-01, and before Internal Audit's control assessment of HCP-G began on 2026-07-13. It covers the organization (the six commercial regions, the Government region G1, the edge, the three service lines and the acquired managed services business AQ-1), the systems that hold customer content, federal data and keys or that can change them, the vendors that touch them, and the rules that may bind the company. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (BIA interviews and the G1 contingency test, risk workshops, gap analysis samples and the Class D delta review, Internal Audit tests, the AI portfolio review) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

At this size the sources are enterprise systems of record: the workforce identity platform and HR system, the CMDB and provisioning system, the network policy repository, the SIEM, SOC case platform and vulnerability platform, the key management service, the fleet automation and release systems, the third-party risk register and accounts payable vendor master, the contract repository, the FedRAMP compliance group's records, board and disclosure committee records, Internal Audit and SOX workpapers, and the SEC filings.

Each register row names the owning organization's system of record and owner. The [asset inventory](asset-inventory.csv) reconciles the CMDB with the provisioning system, the service inventories for FR-1 and FR-2, the EDR console and the AI governance committee register; the [vendor register](vendor-register.csv) reconciles the third-party risk register with the accounts payable vendor master and the contract repository.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analysis (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Identity and access.** Employee and contractor accounts are federated through SSO, each with a FIDO2 security key, and AQ-1 staff are not in this directory (EV-001). FIDO2 keys are required for every SSO application, remote access and privileged access; lockout follows 5 failed attempts in 15 minutes (EV-002). Elevation to production and G1 roles is just in time against an approval ticket, and sessions are recorded (EV-003). G1 roles can be granted only to identities with the U.S.-person attribute; about 300 identities hold them, and privileged roles are reviewed quarterly (EV-004). The G1 console has about 26,000 identities, most federated from agency identity providers, and blocks console access without MFA (EV-005). HR lists 12,000 employees and about 1,400 contractors; employee terminations reach identity governance the same day, while contractor end dates are entered from supplier email notices (EV-006). AQ-1 staff, including about 180 technicians with RMM roles, sign in to a legacy directory with app-based MFA and are not enrolled in PAM (EV-007). The legacy RMM tool manages about 9,500 servers of 140 SL-3 customers, has an AI script generator enabled, and sends logs to the SIEM as a daily batch (EV-008).

**Hosts, endpoints and network.** EDR reports on about 15,000 corporate endpoints, on host management servers in every region (312 in G1) and on PAM bastions (EV-009). The inventory lists about 240,000 commercial hosts and about 9,000 G1 hosts; about 1,900 R3 hosts run a hypervisor release whose vendor support ends 2027-03-31 (EV-010). Baselines are kept as code with daily drift detection (EV-011). Host software and guest-agent packages are signature-verified before install; one BMC firmware model is installed after a hash comparison only (EV-012). Internet, tenant, control and management domains are separated, the G1 enclave is reached from the corporate jump zone only through PAM bastions, encrypted management-plane flows are not inspected, and one registrar holds all company domains with registry lock enabled (EV-013). Scans run weekly for hosts, containers and network devices and every 14 days for BMCs, and the KEV feed is matched daily (EV-014). Corporate endpoints, mobile devices and email follow managed policies (EV-015).

**Logging, SOC, keys, backup and recovery.** The SIEM lists 212 G1 log sources; the G1 archive is write-once; legacy RMM logs arrive as a daily batch (EV-016). The SOC runs 24x7 with about 140 staff and tracks incidents through all phases (EV-017). The AI triage module auto-closes low-score alert classes and, for defined classes, isolates hosts or suspends customer accounts without prior analyst approval; suspension is disabled in G1 (EV-018). Release signing keys are held in HSMs; quorum is required for key export but not for signing requests from the build system (EV-019). All FR-1 services and 50 of 52 FR-2 services use modules with active CMVP validations; 2 G1 services use a library update stream whose validation is pending (EV-020). Control plane state is backed up to write-once regional vaults with cross-region copies and an encrypted copy in the out-of-band vault on Cloud provider X (EV-021). The R1 to R6 control plane recovery tests in 2026-05 recovered within their target times, and the vault restore took 1.2 hours (EV-022). The HCP-G contingency plan is at v5 and the configuration management plan at v6 (EV-023).

**Release path and change.** Host channel releases need two approvers from teams other than the author. Guest-agent channel releases need one approver, and the approver group includes the authoring team. Fleet automation can deploy to every host and every enrolled guest, and runs SL-3 jobs on about 48,500 customer servers (EV-024). Build provenance is generated but not checked by the signing service, and build runners are shared across teams (EV-025). Each G1 change record carries a risk rating, a security impact field and a significant-change field (EV-026).

**Governance, prior assurance and disclosure.** POL-01 to POL-05, standards and procedures sit in the policy portal with owners, review dates, an exception register and attestation rates (EV-027). The 2025 enterprise risk assessment rolled into the ERM register under NIST IR 8286 (EV-028). FR-1 (64 services) and FR-2 (52 services) are active certifications on the Agency path, with ATO letters from 21 agencies on SL-1 and 50 government customers on SL-2; packages are held as documents and spreadsheets, and continuous monitoring follows the legacy monthly process (EV-029). The 2026-03 independent assessment of FR-1 and FR-2 left no open High findings (EV-030). The rule tracker lists each 2026 ruleset with its maintain date; no Security Decision Record, Ongoing Certification Report, Quarterly Review or trust center exists yet (EV-031). SOX testing covers billing, metering and ERP, and the 2026 interim SOX sample records late terminations of AQ-1 legacy directory accounts (EV-032). SL-1 and SL-2 have 2025 SOC 2 Type 2 reports and ISO/IEC 27001 certificates; SL-3 has neither (EV-033). The risk and technology committee receives quarterly cyber reporting, and the board approved the risk appetite in 2026-02 (EV-034). The materiality worksheet has no lines for SLA credits, customer churn or federal certification impact; its last recorded exercise is the 2026-02 tabletop, and AQ-1 reporting lines do not appear in it (EV-035). The FY2025 Form 10-K includes Item 1C (EV-036). Incident response runbooks include DFARS and bank notice procedures; the DFARS 90-day media preservation step has not been exercised, and business associate notices have been exercised for single-customer cases only (EV-037). Training, phishing simulations and acknowledgments are recorded (EV-038). Ten common control providers publish their controls; the parameter register has no values for 31 Class D enhancement parameters (EV-039).

**Third parties and contracts.** The third-party register lists about 2,600 vendors, 310 tier 1 or tier 2, with 26 tier-1 SOC report reviews past their annual date and no review on file for the legacy RMM vendor (EV-040, EV-041). The customer agreement sets security incident notice within 72 hours of confirmation and SLA credits up to 30% of monthly fees; the bank addendum is signed by 196 of 210 banking organizations; the DFARS addendum accepts paragraphs (c) to (g); the BAA template is used with about 1,100 health care customers (EV-042). The legacy RMM vendor contract has no security or incident notice terms; R1 has one contracted fuel supplier and one on spot terms; one HSM manufacturer and one telephony provider serve all regions (EV-043). Vendor assurance on file includes Cloud provider X's FedRAMP authorization and SOC 2 report and the colocation providers' SOC 2 reports (EV-044). The CRM holds about 41,000 customers and a bank-designated incident contact for 188 of about 210 banking organizations (EV-045). Tier-1 and tier-2 vendors are screened for countries of concern and covered persons at contract (EV-046).

**Facilities and hardware.** The company runs 18 commercial data centers, G1-A and G1-B, and 41 PoPs at 5 colocation providers; the G1 facility survey records no wireless networks, output devices or collaborative computing devices in the data halls (EV-047). Badge and biometric access, quarterly access reviews, visitor records and escort logs are kept (EV-048). Generators hold 72 hours of fuel, and the R1 hurricane drill ran in 2025-09 (EV-049). G1 receiving checks deliveries for damage and mismatch; the procedure has no tamper-evidence step for server deliveries (EV-050). Drives are sanitized on site and destroyed with certificates (EV-051).

**Business volume and structure.** FY2025 revenue was about $4.8 billion, about $13.2 million per calendar day; R1 carries about 24% of SL-1 revenue (EV-052). AQ-1, acquired 2025-07, serves 140 SL-3 customers; migration of its servers to fleet automation is due 2027-03-31, and the deal approval process has no security step (EV-053). Capacity is planned per region with a full-zone reserve (EV-054).

**AI.** The AI governance committee register lists 15 use cases. Review dates are recorded for 8; AI-002 (pilot) and AI-008 were awaiting review; no review is recorded for AI-005, AI-010, AI-012, AI-013 and AI-014 (EV-055).

**Obligations and other records.** Counsel's applicability memo covers each candidate rule (EV-056), and the HIPAA program document lists the HIPAA-eligible SL-1 services (EV-057). The results are in [`obligations-register.csv`](obligations-register.csv). The 2026 penetration test covered the G1 console, API and management plane (EV-058). Threat intelligence records campaigns against provider tooling, software supply chains and edge devices (EV-059). The secure configuration guide recommends but does not require MFA for SL-1 tenant administrators, and the status page runs on Cloud provider X (EV-060). The cyber insurance policy requires insurer approval before any ransom payment (EV-061). About 400 control plane APIs exist, 97% on the central policy engine, and storage control services fail open on authorization cache expiry (EV-062). The G1 control plane authorizes every API call through its policy engine and requires a customer-approved request for operator access to tenant resources (EV-063). Internal accounts are created with guardrails and scanned daily (EV-064). The Class D program charter and plan set the sequence to a Rev5 Class D application (EV-065). Meter records are validated at collection and reconciled to invoices monthly (EV-066).
