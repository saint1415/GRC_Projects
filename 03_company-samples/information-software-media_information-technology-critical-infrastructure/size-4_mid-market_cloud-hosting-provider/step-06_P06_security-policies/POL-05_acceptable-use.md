## 1. Purpose
Set the rules every employee and contractor must follow when using company systems and customer data, and require the training that makes the rules work.

## 2. Scope
All employees and contractors, on company laptops, NOC workstations, phones with company apps, and any company or customer system.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Director of Corporate IT | Endpoint management, device controls, approved software list |
| HR Director | Rules of behavior acknowledgments, training records, sanctions with managers |
| Director of Security | Training content, phishing tests, AI tool approvals with the AI review group |
| Managers | Make sure their staff complete training and follow the rules |
| All workforce | Follow this policy; report incidents and lost devices at once |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 **Rules of behavior.** Every workforce member must sign the rules of behavior and the confidentiality agreement at hire and every year before keeping access. (PL-4; PS-6; GV.RR-04; C-IT-R01)
4.2 **Customer data.** Customer data may be accessed only to deliver the contracted service, through approved tools, and never copied to personal devices, personal accounts, or unapproved services. (PL-4; AC-21; PR.DS-01; C-IT-R01)
4.3 **Company devices only for administration.** Only company-managed laptops may reach PAM, cloud consoles, the RMM tool, or the code repository. Phones may hold email and chat only under device management. (AC-20; AC-19; PR.AA-05; C-IT-R01)
4.4 **Software and media.** Staff must not install unapproved software on company devices. Removable media is blocked except approved encrypted devices for data center diagnostics. (CM-11; MP-7; PR.PS-05; C-IT-R01)
4.5 **Device protection.** Company laptops must keep full-disk encryption, EDR, and screen lock on. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the NOC within 1 hour. (SC-28; AC-11; PR.DS-01; C-IT-R01)
4.6 **AI tools.** Staff may use only AI tools approved under STD-10. Federal data, customer data, credentials, and source code for the government partition must never be entered into an AI tool that is not approved for that data. AI output must be reviewed by a person before it is sent to a customer, merged into code, or used in a hiring decision. (PL-4; SA-9; GV.RR-04; C-IT-R01)
4.7 **Communications.** Staff must not discuss incidents, customers, or agency relationships on social media or with the press. Only the Director of Communications speaks for the company. (PL-4(1); AC-22; GV.RR-04; C-IT-R01)
4.8 **Security awareness.** All workforce must complete security awareness training at hire and yearly, including phishing, social engineering of support staff, and insider threat content. Quarterly phishing tests are run. (AT-2; AT-2(2); AT-2(3); PR.AT-01; C-IT-R01)
4.9 **Role-based training.** Every engineer with a privileged role (all 214) and every NOC agent must complete role-based training yearly, covering PAM, change control, spillage, and the customer account recovery rules. (AT-3; PR.AT-02; C-IT-R01)
4.10 **Training records.** Training completion must be recorded and kept for 5 years. Access is suspended for anyone more than 30 days overdue. (AT-4; PR.AT-01; C-IT-R01)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Completion rates and phishing results are reported to the CTO quarterly. Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.15.

## 6. Exceptions
Through POL-01 section 4.8.
