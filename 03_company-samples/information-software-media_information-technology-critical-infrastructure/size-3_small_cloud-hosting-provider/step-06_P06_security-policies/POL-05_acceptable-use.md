## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how workforce members use company systems, devices, and information, including the administrative access many staff have to customer environments.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members and contractors, on any device or network used for company work, including laptops, NOC workstations, phones, home networks, and customer sites.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Manager | Collects acknowledgments at hire and every year; keeps training records |
| IT Manager | Runs awareness training and phishing exercises; keeps the approved-tools list |
| Managers | Make sure new privileged staff finish role-based training before getting access |
| Workforce | Follow these rules and report anything suspicious |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Every workforce member must read and acknowledge this policy and the confidentiality agreement at hire and every year after. (PL-4; PS-6)

4.2 **Devices.**
- Company data and administrative work must stay on company-managed laptops and NOC workstations.
- Personal phones may be used only for email and chat through the managed apps. They must never be used for administrative access.
(AC-19; AC-20)

4.3 **Training.**
- All staff must complete security awareness training each year, including insider threat and social engineering content.
- All staff take part in quarterly phishing exercises.
- Privileged administrators (platform, managed services, software, NOC, and IT staff) must complete role-based training within 30 days of getting the role, and every year after.
(AT-2; AT-2(2); AT-2(3); AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02)

4.4 USB and other removable storage are blocked on company laptops. Exceptions need IT approval under POL-01 section 4.6. (MP-7)

4.5 **Remote work.**
- Use only the company laptop.
- Keep the screen out of view of others and lock it when away.
- Do not use shared or public computers for company work.
(PE-17)

4.6 **Social media and outside sites.** Do not post customer names, infrastructure details, network diagrams, or incident information on social media or public sites. Do not speak for the company about incidents. The COO approves public statements. (PL-4(1))

4.7 Report suspected incidents, lost devices, or lost security keys to the NOC immediately, within 15 minutes (POL-03 section 4.2).

4.8 **AI tools.**
- Use only AI tools on the approved-tools list kept by the IT Manager.
- Never paste customer data, credentials, tenant metadata, or source code into public AI tools.
(PL-4; PR.PS-05)

4.9 **Prohibited:**
- sharing accounts or security keys
- disabling or bypassing EDR, the bastion host, or MFA
- accessing customer environments without a ticket
- running RMM scripts outside the approval rules in POL-02 section 4.9
- installing unapproved software
(PL-4; AC-6)

4.10 **Monitoring notice.** Company systems are monitored for security, including AI-assisted analysis of security logs (P10). Users have no expectation of privacy on company systems, except as the law provides. (PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement in POL-01 section 4.7. Sanctions range from retraining to termination, depending on intent and harm. Compliance is checked through acknowledgment records, training records, and the annual control assessment (P07).

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.6. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the right authority, and expire within 12 months.
