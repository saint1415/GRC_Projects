## 1. Purpose
Set the rules every user of group IT and OT must follow, and the training they must complete.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members, registered longshore workers who use VMTs, crane cabs or HMIs, and vendor technicians and integrators who use group systems.

## 3. Policy statements
3.1 Users must acknowledge this policy at hire or registration and each year. Longshore workers acknowledge it at the hiring hall training session. (PL-4; GV.RR-04)

3.2 **Training.** Everyone with access to IT or OT must complete annual cybersecurity training covering the recognition of threats and incidents, techniques used to circumvent security, and how to report to the CySO, and, once each Cybersecurity Plan is approved, its relevant provisions within 60 days. Everyone whose duties include using OT must also complete OT-specific training. New personnel must be trained within 5 days of gaining access and no later than 30 days after hire. (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02; 101.650(d)(1), (d)(4))

3.3 A person who must use IT or OT before training must be accompanied or monitored by a trained person, and the supervision must be recorded on the shift log. (AT-2; 101.650(d)(3))

3.4 Only hardware, firmware and software on the group approved lists may be installed or connected. Users must not connect personal USB drives, laptops or phones to group IT or OT, including HMIs, gate servers, scale PCs and building controllers. Maintenance must use group-issued laptops. (CM-11; MP-7; 101.650(b)(1); 101.650(i)(2))

3.5 Personal devices may reach only email and collaboration through the group's managed app, and never FCI, CUI or SSI. (AC-20; 52.204-21(b)(1)(iii))

3.6 **Payment changes.** No one may change a supplier's, tenant's or closing agent's bank details, or release a payment to new details, on the strength of an email or a call the requester started. Changes must be verified through a known phone number and the vendor portal. (SI-8; PR.AT-01)

3.7 **Generative AI.** Restricted data must not be entered into AI tools that are not approved in the group AI inventory. Approved tools must not train on group inputs. (PL-4; P10)

3.8 Users must report lost devices, suspicious messages and unusual system or equipment behavior immediately (POL-03 4.3).

## 4. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.8. Training completion is tracked in SYS-G5, including longshore attendance passed from the hiring halls.

## 5. Exceptions and related documents
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.12. Related: POL-01 to POL-04; Group AI Standard (P10).
