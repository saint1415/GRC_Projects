At the Sole Proprietorship tier the business keeps **one** consolidated policy, because one person writes, follows, and checks it (`01_company-sizes/tier-project-scaling.csv`, t1 P06). This file is kept so the folder has the standard policy set names. It holds no separate rules.

**Where the incident response rules are:**

- POL-01 section 10 (Incident response): runbook and printed contact sheet (10.1), same-day incident log with time of discovery (10.2), report clocks under FAR 52.204-25(d), FAR 52.204-23(c), and the BPA (10.3), Florida notice check (10.4), no extortion payment without counsel and an OFAC check (10.5), and the annual walkthrough (10.6).
- POL-01 6.3: failed receiving checks go to the quarantine shelf and the P08 runbook.
- The procedure itself is the P08 runbook (`../step-08_P08_incident-response-runbook/ir-runbook.md`). SP 800-53 IR-1 is met by POL-01 as a whole.

Traceability for every statement is in `policy-control-map.csv`.
