At the Sole Proprietorship tier the business keeps **one** consolidated policy, because one person writes, follows, and checks it (`01_company-sizes/tier-project-scaling.csv`, t1 P06). This file is kept so the folder has the standard policy set names. It holds no separate rules.

**Where the access control rules are:**

- POL-01 section 7 (Access control): unique accounts (7.1), MFA by authenticator app (7.2), unique passphrases and no default passwords (7.3), quarterly account review (7.4), standard daily laptop account (7.5), device lock (7.6), sealed emergency access (7.7), and monthly sign-in review (7.8).
- POL-01 6.7: owner-started, watched remote support sessions.
- POL-01 9.6: physical access to the garage, the stock cabinet, keys, codes, and the entry log.
- FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(i), (ii), (v), (vi), (viii), and (ix) are carried by these rules. SP 800-53 AC-1 and IA-1 (policy and procedures) are met by POL-01 as a whole.
