## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce uses group systems, information, and products in group custody.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members in every division and in corporate shared services, including temporary agency workers, on any device used for group work.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow these rules; complete training; report incidents |
| Managers | Make sure staff complete training and acknowledgments |
| Group HR | Tracks acknowledgments and training completion |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Workforce members must use group information only as needed for their job. Browsing customer, consumer, seller, or client records out of curiosity is prohibited. (PL-4; AC-8)

4.2 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy and their division supplement at hire and every year. (PL-4; PR.AT-01)

4.3 Every workforce member must complete security awareness training at hire and every year, including phishing and social engineering. The group runs quarterly phishing exercises. (AT-2; PR.AT-01; 3.2.1, 3.2.3; PCI DSS 12.6.3.1)

4.4 **Role-based training** must be completed before access: CUI handling for FFE users; anti-counterfeit and tamper inspection for buyers, receiving staff, and integration technicians; payment handling for contact center agents. (AT-3; SR-11(1); PR.AT-02; 3.2.2)

4.5 Suspected incidents, including suspect products, must be reported to the group SOC within 1 hour (POL-03 4.1). (IR-6)

4.6 Group information may be used only on group-managed devices and approved services. It must not be sent to personal email or stored on personal devices. CUI must never be handled on mobile devices or at alternate work sites. (AC-19; AC-20; 3.1.18, 3.10.6)

4.7 **Generative AI.** Only AI tools on the group approved-tools list may be used for group work. CUI, FCI, cardholder data, and personal information must never be entered into public AI tools. (PL-4; PL-4(1))

4.8 **Card payments by phone.** Contact center agents must use the keypad entry or automatic pause, must never write down card numbers or security codes, and must never read them back aloud. (PL-4; PCI DSS 3.3.1)

4.9 **Supplier payment changes.** Staff must never change supplier or client bank details based on an email or phone request; changes must come through the supplier portal with MFA and be verified by call-back to a number already on file. (PL-4; AT-2)
