## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how the workforce uses company systems, devices, and data, and how everyone helps keep counterfeit products and payment fraud out of the business.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (owners, employees, temporary warehouse staff, and contractors) and all company systems, devices, and data.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Manager | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and annually; tracks training |
| IT Manager | Awareness content, reminders, phishing exercises, and the approved AI tools list |
| Department managers | Make sure role-based training is done before access |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use (for example, checking personal email on a break) is allowed if it does not involve company data or create risk. (PL-4)
4.2 Every workforce member must sign this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4)
4.3 **Training.** Security awareness training is required at hire and every year, with quarterly short modules on current threats, business email compromise, CUI, and insider threats, and at least quarterly phishing exercises. Role-based training is required before access for lab technicians (CUI handling), receiving staff and buyers (anti-counterfeit and Section 889), and administrators. (AT-2; AT-3; SR-11(1); PR.AT-01; SP 800-171 3.2.1 to 3.2.3)
4.4 Lock your screen when you step away. (AC-11)
4.5 Do not install software or browser extensions, or connect USB storage, unless IT approves it. (CM-11; MP-7)
4.6 **Payments and bank details.** Never act on a request to change bank details or to pay a new account because of an email, chat, or call alone. Call the supplier back at the number already in the ERP, not the number in the message, and get a second approver. (AT-2)
4.7 Do not open unexpected attachments or links. Report suspicious messages with the Report Phishing button. Report suspect products, broken seals, or mismatched serial labels to your manager at once (POL-03 4.2). (AT-2; IR-6)
4.8 **External systems.** Do not use personal email, personal cloud storage, or file-sharing sites for company data. (AC-20; SP 800-171 3.1.20; FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(iii))
4.9 **Phones.** Company email on phones is allowed only through the managed app with MFA. CUI must never be viewed, photographed, or stored on a phone. (AC-19; SP 800-171 3.1.18, 3.1.19)
4.10 **Generative AI:** use only tools on the approved list (P10). Never paste CUI, FCI, customer pricing, supplier bank details, or employee information into a tool that is not approved for that data. Treat AI output as a draft that you must check. (PL-4)
4.11 **Visitors and drivers.** Escort visitors at all times. Drivers wait in the driver area and do not enter the warehouse or lab. Challenge anyone without a badge. (PE-3; SP 800-171 3.10.3)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement (POL-01 section 4.7). Sanctions range from retraining to termination, depending on intent and harm. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), training records, and phishing exercise results.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.6. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the Chief Executive Officer for High risk), and expire within 12 months.
