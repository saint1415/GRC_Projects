## 1. Purpose
Set the rules every workforce member follows when using company systems, data, facilities, and AI tools.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members, including temporary warehouse staff and contractors, on any device or location used for company work.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Director | Owns this policy; acknowledgments, training records, sanctions |
| Security Manager | Technical enforcement (web filtering, device control) and training content |
| Managers | Make sure their staff complete training and follow these rules |
| All workforce | Read, acknowledge, and follow this policy |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Workforce members must acknowledge this policy at hire and each year. (PL-4)
4.2 Company systems are for business use; limited personal use is allowed if it does not create risk. Activity on company systems may be monitored. (PL-4)
4.3 Workforce members must complete annual awareness training, including insider threat indicators and how to report them, and take part in phishing simulations. Enclave users complete annual CUI training; administrators, receiving staff, buyers, and Integration Center leads complete role-based training, including anti-counterfeit training. (AT-2; AT-2(2); AT-3; SR-11(1); PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02)
4.4 Company data must not be stored in personal email, personal cloud storage, or unapproved apps. Personal devices may reach only email and chat through the company's app protection. (AC-20)
4.5 Never approve an MFA prompt you did not start; report it at once. (AT-2)
4.6 **Payment and bank changes.** Supplier bank-detail changes and urgent payment requests must be verified by calling the supplier at the number already in the ERP, never a number in the request. The call-back must be recorded in the change ticket. (AT-2; AC-5)
4.7 **Product claims.** Product specifications and compatibility statements given to customers must match the OEM's published data. (PL-4; FTC Act Section 5)
4.8 **AI tools.** Only AI tools on the approved-tools list (P10) may be used. CUI and FCI must never be entered into any AI tool, and Confidential data only into the approved enterprise assistant. Workforce members must check AI output before relying on it. (PL-4; AC-20)
4.9 **Remote and alternate work sites.** Remote staff must work on company devices, lock screens, keep paper to a minimum, and never print or download CUI. CUI is accessed only inside enclave virtual desktop sessions. (PE-17; SP 800-171 3.10.6)
4.10 **Visitors and drivers.** Visitors must sign in, wear a badge, and be escorted. Truck drivers must check in electronically and wait in the driver area. No visitor may enter the FIL cage without a log entry and an escort, and visitors may not walk Integration Center benches unescorted. (PE-3; PE-8; PR.AA-06)
4.11 Badges must not be shared, and tailgating must be challenged or reported. (PE-3; PR.AA-06)
4.12 **Sanctions.** Workforce members who fail to comply with security policies must be sanctioned in proportion to intent and harm, from retraining to termination. HR must document each sanction. (PS-8; GV.RR-04)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Compliance is checked through training completion, phishing results, web filter and device control reports, visitor log reviews, and the annual assessment (P07). Violations are handled under section 4.12.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.6.
