## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how the workforce, including contractor sales associates, uses company systems, devices, and data.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members: owners, employees, and the affiliated sales associates who work under the Broker of Record's license as independent contractors. Applies to company devices and to personal devices whenever they access company email, the transaction platform, or other company systems.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Manager | Collects signed acknowledgments from employees at hire and annually; tracks employee training |
| Sales Managers | Collect signed acknowledgments from contractor sales associates at affiliation and annually; track their training |
| IT Manager | Security awareness content, monthly reminders, phishing exercises, and the approved AI tools list |
| All workforce members | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use of company devices is allowed if it does not involve client data or create risk. (PL-4)
4.2 Every workforce member, including every contractor sales associate, must sign this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4)
4.3 **Training.** Security awareness training is required at hire or affiliation and every year, for employees and contractor sales associates alike. It must cover business email compromise and wire fraud. The IT Manager sends monthly reminders and runs phishing exercises at least quarterly. Closing Services staff, the Controller, and transaction coordinators also receive role training on the funds-transfer verification rule (POL-03 4.9). (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02; 314.4(e)(1))
4.4 **Wire instructions.** Never send, forward, or confirm wire instructions by email, text, or phone. Tell clients at the start of every transaction that wire instructions come only through the Closing Communications Portal and must be confirmed by calling the closing team at the number in the contract packet. If a client asks about new or changed instructions, call the incident line at once (POL-03 4.2). (AT-2; PL-4)
4.5 Do not set rules that forward company email to a personal or outside account. Do not use personal email for company business. (PL-4; AC-4)
4.6 Lock your screen when you step away. Keep screens and paper out of view of clients and visitors at open houses and in the office. (AC-11)
4.7 Do not install software or browser extensions on company devices, or connect USB storage, unless IT approves it. (CM-11)
4.8 **Generative AI.** Use only AI tools on the approved list kept by the IT Manager. Never paste or upload client, tenant, or applicant information, contracts, or closing documents into a tool that is not approved. Listing descriptions and marketing drafts that contain no personal information may use approved general tools. AI features that score or rank applicants or buyers may be used only as approved in P10. (PL-4)
4.9 Open client and transaction files only when your work requires it. Access to the transaction platform and closing software is logged and reviewed. (AU-6)
4.10 Report lost or stolen devices, misdirected documents, suspicious messages, and anything unusual to the incident line immediately (POL-03 4.2). Use the Report Phishing button for suspicious email.

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement in POL-01 4.7. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), phishing exercise results, and acknowledgment records.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 4.6. No exception may be granted to 4.4 or 4.5.
