## 1. Purpose
Set the rules every user must follow when using company systems and client information, so that daily habits do not become the way fraudsters get in.

## 2. Scope
All employees and contractor agents, on company devices and on personal devices used for company work. Each user must acknowledge this policy at onboarding and each year.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All users | Follow this policy; report problems immediately |
| Managers and managing brokers | Make sure their staff and agents complete the annual acknowledgment |
| Director of Agent Services | Tracks agent acknowledgments and makes them a condition of system access |
| Security Manager (Qualified Individual) | Owns the policy; reports acknowledgment rates quarterly |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not create risk or breach another policy. (PL-4)

4.2 Users must not share their accounts or MFA devices, approve MFA prompts they did not start, or enter their company password on any page other than the company sign-in page. (PL-4; IA-2)

4.3 **Email rules.** Users must not set rules that forward company email to personal or outside accounts, and must not use personal email for company business. Automatic external forwarding is blocked. (PL-4; AC-20; 314.4(c)(1))

4.4 **Never handle wire instructions by email.** Users must never send, forward, or confirm wire instructions by email, text, or chat. When a client asks about wiring money, the user must tell the client to use only the instructions in the Closing Communications Portal and to call the closing team at the number in the contract packet before sending money. (PL-4; AT-2)

4.5 **Personal devices.** Contractor agents and employees who use personal devices must keep them updated and screen-locked, use only the browser for company systems on laptops, use only the managed mail and document apps on phones, and must not save client documents to personal storage or personal cloud accounts. (AC-19; AC-20; 314.4(c)(1)(i))

4.6 **Report at once.** Users must report suspicious emails, unexpected MFA prompts, lost devices, and any request to change payment details to the incident line immediately (POL-03 4.1). Knowledge of an incident by any employee or agent can start legal notice clocks. (IR-6; 314.4(j)(2))

4.7 **Clean desk.** Paper with Restricted information must not be left unattended and must be locked away at the end of the day. (PL-4; MP-4)

4.8 **AI tools.** Users may use only AI tools on the approved-tools list. Client, customer, applicant, and tenant information must never be entered into any other AI tool. Users must review AI-drafted listing descriptions and advertisements for discriminatory statements before publication (42 U.S.C. 3604(c)), and must not use any AI feature that ranks, screens, or scores people unless it is approved for that purpose (P10). (PL-4; PR.AT-01)

4.9 Users must complete security training each year (POL-01 4.10) and accept phishing simulations. (AT-2; 314.4(e)(1))

4.10 Users must sign the confidentiality agreement (employees) or the agent agreement with its security duties (contractor agents). (PS-6)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Monitoring of company systems is permitted and disclosed in this policy. Violations by employees are handled under POL-01 section 4.8; violations by contractor agents are handled by the Broker of Record and can lead to loss of system access.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7. No exception may be granted to statements 4.3 and 4.4.
