## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce and contractor agents use group systems and information.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members and contractor agents in every division and in corporate shared services, on any device used for group work.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce and contractor agents | Follow these rules; complete training; report incidents |
| Managers and managing brokers | Make sure staff and agents complete training and acknowledgments |
| Group HR | Tracks acknowledgments and training completion |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Workforce members and contractor agents must access customer and client information only as needed for their work. (PL-4; AC-8)

4.2 Every workforce member and contractor agent must acknowledge this policy and their division supplement at onboarding and every year. (PL-4; PR.AT-01)

4.3 Every workforce member and contractor agent must complete security training at onboarding and every year, with content updated for the risks in the risk assessment, including business email compromise. Contractor agents who have not completed training within 30 days of the due date lose access until they do. (AT-2; AT-2(3); PR.AT-01; 314.4(e)(1))

4.4 Staff in Title disbursement, escrow accounting, treasury, and accounts payable must complete role-based payee verification training before they receive access. (AT-3; PR.AT-02)

4.5 Suspected incidents and suspicious payment requests must be reported to the group SOC within 1 hour (POL-03 4.1). (IR-6)

4.6 **Payment instructions.** No one may send, forward, or change wire or payment instructions by email or text message, or act on a payment change received by email, text, or phone without the payee verification in POL-01 4.9. Agents must direct buyers and sellers to the Closing Communications Portal for every instruction. (SC-37; PL-4)

4.7 Restricted information may be used only on group-managed devices or in approved apps with app protection on personal devices. It must not be sent to personal email or stored in personal cloud storage. (AC-19; AC-20)

4.8 **Generative AI.** Only AI tools on the group approved-tools list may be used for group work. Restricted information must never be entered into public AI tools. (PL-4; PL-4(1))

4.9 **Advertising.** Listing descriptions, property advertisements, and new-home marketing, including drafts written with AI tools, must be checked before publication for statements that indicate a preference, limitation, or discrimination based on a protected class (42 U.S.C. 3604(c)). (PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.7. For contractor agents, violations may end the independent contractor agreement.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.12.
