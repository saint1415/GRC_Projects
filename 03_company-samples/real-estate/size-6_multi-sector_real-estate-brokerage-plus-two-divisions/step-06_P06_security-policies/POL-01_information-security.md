## 1. Purpose
Set up one information security program for the whole group, assign who is accountable at group and division level, and give every other group policy and every division supplement its authority. For Home Loans and Title this policy, with POL-02 to POL-05, the common control catalog, and the system security plans, is the written information security program that 16 CFR 314.3(a) requires.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members of Cris Santos Company Holdings, Inc. and its divisions (employees and contractors), the about 52,000 contractor sales associates who work under the brokerage's licenses, all systems and data the group owns or operates, and systems operated for the group by service providers. It covers customer information of Home Loans and Title, brokerage client and tenant data, Homebuilding buyer and homeowner data, payment instructions, and all other group information.

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 The group must maintain one information security program aligned to NIST CSF 2.0 that meets the FTC Safeguards Rule for Home Loans and Title and applies to every division. (PM-1; GV.PO-01; 314.3(a))

4.2 The Group CISO is the Qualified Individual for Home Loans and Title. Each institution's board must designate a senior officer (the President, Mortgage and the President, Title) to direct and oversee the Qualified Individual, and each institution retains responsibility for its own compliance. The senior officers must meet the Qualified Individual at least quarterly and keep minutes. (PM-2; GV.RR-02; 314.4(a)(1)-(2))

4.3 Each division and corporate must complete a written risk assessment at least annually and after major changes, using NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1. Division risks that cross divisions, sit in shared services, or need a group decision must roll up to the group register. (RA-3; PM-9; ID.RA-01; 314.4(b))

4.4 **Risk acceptance authority:** Low, the division security and compliance lead; Moderate, the division president (the Group Chief Risk Officer for group risks); High, the Group Chief Risk Officer with the Group CISO, reported to the board risk committee; Very High, the board risk committee only. Risks to customer funds rated High must be treated, not accepted. (PM-9; GV.RM-01; 314.4(b)(1)(iii))

4.5 **Division supplements.** Each division must maintain a written supplement. A supplement may add stricter requirements but must not weaken group policy. It must be re-aligned within 90 days after a group policy changes, and each division security and compliance lead must attest alignment every year. (PL-1; GV.PO-02)

4.6 **Common controls.** Corporate control providers must maintain the common control catalog. Each division must document, for each of its systems, which controls it inherits and which responsibilities remain with the division, and confirm that documentation every year. (PL-2; CA-2; GV.RR-02)

4.7 **Sanctions.** Workforce members and contractor agents who violate security policies must be sanctioned in proportion to intent and harm, up to termination of employment or of the independent contractor agreement. (PS-8; GV.RR-04)

4.8 **Service providers and affiliates.** No service provider may receive, maintain, process, or access customer information or client data without a security review and a written contract requiring appropriate safeguards and incident notice. This includes the parent company when it provides services to Home Loans and Title, under a written intercompany agreement that requires a program meeting 16 CFR Part 314. Tier 1 providers must be reassessed every year. (SA-9; SA-4; SR-6; GV.SC-05; GV.SC-07; 314.4(a)(3); 314.4(f))

4.9 **Payee verification.** In every division, any new payee, changed bank account, changed wire instruction, or payoff must be verified out of band through SYS-G5 by calling a number already on file (never one supplied in the request), before any payment. Every wire from an escrow or trust account requires two approvers, and the approver cannot be the person who entered the payee. Wire instructions to consumers are delivered only through the Closing Communications Portal. (SC-37; AC-3(2); AC-5; PR.AA-05; 314.3(b))

4.10 **Evaluation.** Group internal audit must assess common controls at least annually and sample each division's controls. Results feed the POA&M and the risk registers, and the program is adjusted in light of them. (CA-2; CA-7; ID.IM-01; 314.4(d)(1); 314.4(g))

4.11 **Annual report.** The Qualified Individual must report in writing at least annually to the boards of Home Loans and Title on the overall status of the program and compliance with 16 CFR Part 314, and on material matters, including the parent's services as a provider and security events by institution. (PM-9; GV.OV-01; 314.4(i))

4.12 **Exceptions** to any group policy or supplement must be requested in writing, risk-rated, approved under 4.4, recorded in the division register, and limited to 12 months or less. An expired exception ends automatically; access that depends on it is disabled. (PL-1)

4.13 Security policies, risk assessments, assessments, and required actions must be retained for 6 years from creation or last effective date, whichever is later. (SI-12)

4.14 **AI systems.** No AI system that uses customer information or client data, or that makes or supports decisions about applicants, borrowers, buyers, tenants, or homeowners, may be deployed or materially changed without registration in the group AI inventory and approval under the Group AI Standard (P10). (RA-3; PL-2; GV.RM-01)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under 4.7. Compliance is checked through the annual common control assessment and division samples (P07), the annual supplement attestations, and quarterly access certifications.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow section 4.12.
