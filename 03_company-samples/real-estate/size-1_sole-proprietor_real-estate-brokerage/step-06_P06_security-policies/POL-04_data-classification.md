- POL-01 section 8 (Data handling and money instructions): three classification levels (8.1), approved storage locations (8.2), encryption (8.3), the callback rule for money instructions (8.4), secure collection of identity documents (8.5), backup (8.6), retention and disposal, including consumer reports (8.7), and 5-year retention of security documentation (8.8).

- SP 800-53 MP-1 and SC-1 are met by POL-01 as a whole.
