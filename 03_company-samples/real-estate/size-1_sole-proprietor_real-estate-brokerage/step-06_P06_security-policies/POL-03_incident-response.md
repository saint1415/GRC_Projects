At the Sole Proprietorship tier the brokerage keeps **one** consolidated policy, because one person writes, follows, and checks it (`01_company-sizes/tier-project-scaling.csv`, t1 P06). This file is kept so the folder has the standard policy set names. It holds no separate rules.

**Where the incident response rules are:**

- POL-01 section 10 (Incident response): runbook and printed contacts including the bank fraud desk (10.1), same-day incident log (10.2), bank first when money may be misdirected (10.3), notification deadlines (10.4), annual walkthrough and lessons learned (10.5).
- The procedure itself is the P08 runbook (`../step-08_P08_incident-response-runbook/ir-runbook.md`) and its notification matrix.
- SP 800-53 IR-1 is met by POL-01 as a whole.

Traceability for every statement is in `policy-control-map.csv`.
