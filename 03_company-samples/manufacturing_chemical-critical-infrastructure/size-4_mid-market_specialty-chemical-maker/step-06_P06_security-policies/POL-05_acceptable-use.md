## 1. Purpose
Set the rules every user agrees to when using company systems, so that everyday behavior does not put people, plants, or information at risk.

## 2. Scope
All employees, contractors, and vendor personnel who use company IT or OT systems, at any site or remotely.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Director | Collects acknowledgments; tracks training for employees and contractors |
| Information Security Manager (CySO) | Training content, including the USCG topics |
| Managers and Shift Supervisors | Make sure their staff and contractors have completed training before system access |
| All users | Follow this policy |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Users must read and accept this policy before getting access, and each year after. (PL-4)
4.2 Users must complete security awareness training before access and every year. Port plant personnel, including contractors, must also complete the USCG cybersecurity training topics within 5 days of system access and no later than 30 days after hire, and every year; OT users get the OT module. Untrained people may use IT or OT only when accompanied or monitored by trained personnel. (AT-2; AT-3; 33 CFR 101.650(d)(1)-(4))
4.3 Company systems are for business use. Limited personal use of office systems is allowed if it does not interfere with work or security. Personal use of OT systems is never allowed. (PL-4)
4.4 Personal phones, USB drives, chargers, and laptops must not be connected to OT systems or control room consoles. (MP-7; SC-41; 33 CFR 101.650(i)(2))
4.5 Users must not try to bypass security controls, share passwords, or use another person's account or driver card. (PL-4; 33 CFR 101.650(d)(1)(iii))
4.6 **Generative AI.** Only AI tools on the approved list (STD-07) may be used for company work. SSI, Restricted information (formulations, recipes, control system details), and personal information must never be entered into public AI tools. AI output is a draft: the user is responsible for checking it. (PL-4; STD-07)
4.7 Users must report suspected phishing, lost devices, unusual control system behavior, and other security concerns immediately to the CySO or the control room. (IR-6; 33 CFR 101.650(d)(1)(iv))
4.8 Hazmat employees must complete security awareness training and, where they handle 50% hydrogen peroxide shipments or implement the DOT security plan, in-depth security training. (AT-3; 49 CFR 172.704(a)(4)-(5))
4.9 The company monitors use of its systems for security purposes. Users have no expectation of privacy on company systems, subject to applicable law. (AC-8)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Checked through acknowledgment and training records, device control reports, and phishing simulations. Violations are handled under POL-01 4.8.

## 6. Exceptions
Under POL-01 4.7.
