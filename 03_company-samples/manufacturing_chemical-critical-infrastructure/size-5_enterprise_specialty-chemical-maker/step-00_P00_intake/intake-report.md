## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the company's own records before the enterprise BIA began on 2026-05-04, before the enterprise risk analysis and gap analysis began on 2026-06-01, and before Internal Audit's control assessment fieldwork began on 2026-07-13. It covers the organization (14 plants, including the three plants acquired in 2025, PLT-12 to PLT-14), the IT and OT systems that run production, safety, shipping and the two external service lines (SL-1 and SL-2), the vendors that touch them, and the rules that may bind the company. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (BIA interviews and recovery tests, risk workshops, gap analysis samples and walk-throughs, Internal Audit tests, and the AI portfolio review) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

At this size the sources are enterprise systems of record across IT, OT and process safety: the identity platform and HR system, the PLT-01 OT directory and DCS configuration, the central OT remote access gateway, the OT backup vault and passive OT monitoring, the SIEM and SOC case system, the MOC and PSM records system, the cloud consoles, the third-party risk register and accounts payable vendor master, the contract repository, the FSO's MTSA records, board and committee records, prior Internal Audit and SOX workpapers, and regulator correspondence.

Each register row names the owning system of record and owner. The [asset inventory](asset-inventory.csv) reconciles the OT asset inventory and passive monitoring asset list with the identity, endpoint and cloud exports; the [vendor register](vendor-register.csv) reconciles the third-party risk register with the accounts payable vendor master and the contract repository. SSI (the Facility Security Plan, Cybersecurity Plan drafts and network maps) was reviewed in the FSO's SSI repository and is described here by name only.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analysis (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Identity and access.** Workforce accounts are federated through SSO with MFA registered; the about 960 workforce members at PLT-12 to PLT-14 sign in through legacy directories that are not federated, and each plant's OT uses its own directory (EV-001). MFA is required for remote, cloud and administrative access, with phishing-resistant authenticators for privileged roles and remote OT access (EV-002). Privileged IT and OT DMZ accounts are vaulted with just-in-time elevation (EV-003). Joiner, mover and leaver events flow from the HR system for IT and gateway accounts, while OT directory accounts are changed by ticket (EV-004). HR holds about 12,000 employees, about 4,600 of them in Florida (EV-005). The PLT-01 OT directory holds about 412 accounts in 5 DCS role levels, with engineer and administrator rights held by 22 people (EV-006). The central remote access gateway serves 11 of 14 plants with named accounts, MFA, supervisor approval and recording, and holds 61 integrator accounts with PLT-01 targets (EV-007). The acquired plants' records show one network segment for IT and OT at each plant, shared DCS and PLC accounts, and 3 legacy integrators using their own always-on remote tools (EV-008). The three plants run their legacy ERP until 2027-03-31, and the 2025 deal checklists had no OT section (EV-009).

**Endpoints, OT assets and network.** EDR reports on 98% of about 14,500 IT endpoints (EV-010). EDR or allowlisting covers 61% of about 2,300 OT workstations and servers; at PLT-01 the 9 Blend Hall 1 operator stations on an unsupported operating system do not run the agent, and USB is disabled on operator stations (EV-011). The OT asset inventory lists the PLT-01 DCS, SIS, terminal systems and 64 PLCs, with firmware fields blank for some terminal and rack PLC records (EV-012). OT DMZs exist at 10 of 14 plants (not PLT-08 or PLT-12 to PLT-14), and network map v2 shows the PLT-01 terminal and rack networks in part (EV-013). The PLT-01 OT DMZ rule base has 212 rules (EV-014). The SD-WAN reaches 40 sites, with edge devices at each (EV-015). Passive OT monitoring runs at 9 of 14 plants; at PLT-01 there is no sensor on the terminal and rack PLC networks; OT logs from PLT-08 and PLT-11 to PLT-14 are not SIEM sources; and no use case is listed for alarm limit changes or SIS keyswitch position (EV-016). KEV matching runs daily, and the compensating control field is blank for most open PLT-01 OT findings older than 30 days (EV-017).

**Security operations, backup and recovery.** The SOC runs 24x7 with OT monitoring analysts and MSSP overflow (EV-018). The OT backup vault takes daily offline and immutable backups for 11 plants; the 2026-03 restore test recovered the PLT-01 ammonia DCS area within 12 hours, and there is no restore record for the Chlor Unit and blend hall areas (EV-019). Comparison of running SIS programs with the approved copies is a manual procedure, last recorded in 2025-11 (EV-020). IT backups are immutable, and tier-1 IT DR tests ran in 2025, with the 2026 test set for 2026-05-16 (EV-021). The PLT-01 OT contingency plan v2 has detailed restore procedures for the ammonia area (EV-022).

**Process safety and emergency response.** MOC is electronic, and MOC form v7 asks a security impact question; the DCS change log and the MOC system are separate records with no reconciliation report on file (EV-023). The 2025 ammonia unit PHA includes control system compromise scenarios, and the 2023 Chlor Unit PHA and the PLT-04 and PLT-05 PHAs do not (EV-024). SIS proof tests are recorded on schedule (EV-025). RMP submissions cover 8 plants; compliance audits are on schedule, with scopes that do not include control system security; the 2025 notification exercises did not simulate loss of the business network (EV-026). Cellular phones, call lists and radios are in place at PLT-01, PLT-04 and PLT-05, and VoIP on the business network is the primary notification path at 9 plants (EV-027). The threshold worksheets give the PLT-01 maximum intended inventories (for example, 78,795 lb of anhydrous ammonia and 16,000 lb of chlorine), and the DCS alarm and setpoint databases are kept outside the process safety information (EV-028).

**MTSA at PLT-01.** The Facility Security Plan is approved, and the terminal transfers base oils from tank barges (EV-029). 94% of the 3,540 PLT-01 employees and contractors with IT or OT access completed the cybersecurity training by 2026-01-12; 212 contractor personnel had not and were flagged for escort or monitoring; all 64 key personnel completed their module (EV-030). The CySO's project file targets plan submission by 2027-03-31; the outline maps all 14 sections and Section 1 is being drafted; the approved list draft covers the DCS, SIS and OT DMZ, with terminal PLC and tank gauging entries in progress; there are no Cybersecurity Assessment work papers yet (EV-031). The CySO was designated in writing on 2025-12-15 (EV-037).

**Governance, prior assurance and disclosure.** The 2025 policy set (POL-01 to POL-05) sits in the policy portal with standards, procedures and an exception register (EV-032). The OT Security Center of Excellence, formed in 2024 with 12 staff, keeps OT standards based on NIST SP 800-82 Rev. 3 (EV-033). The 2025 risk analysis rolled into 8 enterprise risks under NIST IR 8286 Rev. 1, and the board approved the risk appetite in 2026-02 (EV-034, EV-035). Internal Audit's 2025 assessment, the FY2025 SOX ITGC testing and the 2025 OT DMZ penetration test are on file (EV-036). The disclosure committee has no operations or process safety member; the materiality playbook's factors and examples refer to data breaches; the severity-1 trigger list does not name a plant safe-state shutdown; and the minutes record no exercise with an OT scenario (EV-038). The FY2025 10-K includes Item 1C (EV-039). The 2026-03-19 tabletop used an OT ransomware scenario without the disclosure committee (EV-040). Role-based OT training runs at 11 plants, not yet at PLT-12 to PLT-14 (EV-041).

**Third parties and service lines.** The vendor register lists about 2,600 vendors, about 180 with OT or remote access, with assessment records for 4 of the 6 DCS and PLC integrators (EV-042, EV-043). The PLT-01 DCS vendor support contract has no vulnerability or incident notification clause, 2 of the 6 integrator contracts have no security clauses, and the 31 contracts inherited with the acquired plants carry no review record (EV-044). SOC reports are on file for both clouds, both colocation providers, the ERP, HR and payroll and productivity suite vendors (EV-045). SL-1 issued its first SOC 2 Type 2 report for calendar 2025 (EV-046). SL-2 serves about 85 customers without a SOC 2 report, and two large customers have asked for one with Confidentiality and Processing Integrity by 2027 (EV-047).

**Facilities and engineering office.** Badge access and video cover the 14 plants, with TWIC readers at the PLT-01 marine terminal and quarterly reviews (EV-048). Sanitization certificates are on file (EV-049). The PLT-01 engineering office keeps media, tool, spares and receiving records (EV-050).

**Business volume, structure and supply.** Revenue was about $4.8 billion in FY2025, about $13.2 million per calendar day, with PLT-01 at about $910 million a year (EV-051). PLT-01 runs about 260 batches a day from about 1,900 master recipes, and the company ships about $12 million of product a day (EV-052). The company runs 14 plants, 9 distribution centers and 2 R&D centers in 8 states (EV-053). One supplier provides all anhydrous ammonia and one all chlorine ton containers (EV-054).

**AI.** The AI governance committee register lists 8 use cases, 5 with completed review (EV-074). Feature discovery found 2 more outside the register: the ERP demand forecasting feature (AI-009) and the HR SaaS resume ranking feature (AI-011), for 10 at intake; 2025 web proxy logs show visits to public generative AI services (EV-075).

**Obligations.** PLT-01's marine terminal makes it a Part 105 facility (EV-029); the threshold worksheets and RMP submissions set the RMP and PSM scope (EV-026, EV-028); the company offers bulk hazmat covered by the DOT security plan rule (EV-056); it is an SEC registrant (EV-039); its CFATS Top-Screens and tiering are on file, and the statute has lapsed (EV-055); and the employee health plan is a separate covered entity (EV-058). Counsel's view on each candidate rule is in EV-057; the results are in [`obligations-register.csv`](obligations-register.csv).
