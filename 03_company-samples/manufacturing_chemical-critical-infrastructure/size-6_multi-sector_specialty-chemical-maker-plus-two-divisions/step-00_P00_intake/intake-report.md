## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the group's own records before any assessment work began on 2026-05-04. It covers the legal entities, the systems that control chemical processes, store or move hazardous materials, produce shipping papers or hold sensitive data in each division and in corporate shared services, the suppliers that touch them, and the rules that may bind each entity. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Items are grouped by division: the title of each row starts with Group, Specialty Chemicals, Distribution or Hazmat Transport. Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (interviews, document reviews, samples, tests) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analyses (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Entities and scale.** The holding company is an SEC registrant with three operating subsidiaries; corporate shared services sit in the parent (EV-001). The workforce is 45,000: Specialty Chemicals about 24,000, Distribution about 9,500, Hazmat Transport about 6,000 (about 2,700 drivers), corporate about 5,500; about 110 drivers live in California (EV-002). FY2025 revenue was about $18.0 billion: Specialty Chemicals $11.4 billion, Distribution $5.6 billion and Hazmat Transport $1.0 billion from third parties (EV-003). The FY2025 annual report carries the Item 106 disclosure; the disclosure committee's materiality factors do not mention OT or process safety consequences (EV-004). Risk acceptance authority is set by level, and process safety risks rated High must be treated (EV-005). The board risk committee receives the High risk list each quarter (EV-006). Internal audit reports to the board audit committee; the Hazmat Transport assessment records list no inherited group controls (EV-007).

**Policies, OT standard and common controls.** Group policies POL-01 to POL-05 (2025 edition) are aligned to CSF 2.0; the library has no AI standard (EV-008). The 2025 OT security standard (OT DMZ, gateway-only remote access, passive monitoring) is deployed at 9 of 16 plants and Terminal T1; the 7 legacy plants are scheduled to migrate by 2027-12-31 (EV-009). The Hazmat Transport supplement v2023 was last aligned 2023-04 and allows shared accounts, MFA for email only, next-business-day account removal and its own severity scale (EV-010). The common control catalog lists 116 controls; inheritance matrices exist for Specialty Chemicals and Distribution, and none was provided for Hazmat Transport (EV-011).

**Group identity and the OT remote access gateway.** 45,000 workforce identities use MFA; IT administrators and central engineers use phishing-resistant authenticators (EV-012). Identity governance disables corporate accounts within 4 hours of termination; plant OT domains are outside it (EV-013). Privileged access is just in time through PAM (EV-014). The gateway serves 17 sites for 14 integrators and OEMs and the engineering hub. Integrator teams share one account and MFA device per team, no site approval step is configured, sessions are recorded at 9 of 17 sites, and the hub holds standing connections to all 16 plants (EV-015).

**Group security operations.** The SOC runs 24x7 with an OT desk since 2025. OT sensors cover the 9 standard plants and Terminal T1, none of the 7 legacy plants, and DCS event journals are not a data source (EV-016). The incident response plan v2025 has an OT annex; its site lists omit the 5 plants acquired in 2024 and it does not define Coast Guard or DOT reportable incidents (EV-017). EDR covers IT, cloud, OT DMZ servers and the hub workstations (EV-018). ICS advisories are triaged within 2 business days (EV-019).

**Group cloud platform and network.** Provider A hosts the landing zone, the data platform, AI-001, the managed inventory platform, the TMS and the ERP integration services; provider B hosts disaster recovery and the immutable vault; plant and terminal control systems run on premises (EV-020). Guardrails apply to every division account (EV-021). Hub firewalls deny by default and reach plant OT DMZs only through approved conduits (EV-022). Keys are group-managed (EV-023). The vault receives daily cloud backups, nightly plant configuration and recipe copies and hourly TMS backups (EV-024), and logs go to a write-once archive (EV-025).

**Workforce, vendors, AI and notification.** Awareness training completion is 98% or more at every plant; integrator personnel are not enrolled (EV-026). Employees with control system access are screened; no screening records for remote integrator staff were provided (EV-027). 6 of 14 integrator contracts have no security clauses or incident notice terms, and 8 of 14 integrators have no security assessment (EV-028). SOC 2 reports are on file for both cloud providers and the identity, LIMS, telematics and driver records vendors, and none for the telemetry gateway vendor (EV-029). AI found: AI-001 (advisory), predictive maintenance, a formulation assistant pilot, an SDS assistant, demand forecasting, route optimization, driver camera AI, an enterprise assistant pilot with 3,000 users and a SOC triage feature (EV-030). The single notification matrix is a draft and no cross-division exercise is recorded (EV-031). The ERC has a backup site outside Florida (EV-032). Every division's shipping papers come from ERP product data, and the pre-printed fallback covers 500 of about 11,000 products (EV-033). No EAR-controlled technology was found and no List I chemicals are distributed (EV-036); no division holds federal contracts or accepts payment cards (EV-037).

**Specialty Chemicals.** 16 plants in 8 states: Plant C1 is RMP Program 3, 4 ammonia plants are Program 2, and the other 11 hold nothing above a threshold quantity (EV-043). Seven plants keep their legacy CFATS measures (EV-044). Plant C1 can hold 360,000 lb of chlorine on its unloading manifold; its PSI index does not describe the control system network or remote access paths (EV-045). The RMP update was submitted 2024-06-20 and the 2025-05-14 compliance audit findings are closed (EV-046). The 2024 PHA and the Program 2 hazard reviews do not treat control system compromise as a cause, and DCS alarms are credited alongside the SIS (EV-047, EV-048). The MOC form has no cybersecurity question, and recipe pushes from the hub are not routed through the plant MOC screen (EV-049). Safe work practices do not mention remote entry by integrators (EV-050). Plant C1 OT: 412 devices, a DCS one release behind (supported), all engineers with full rights on the 3 EWS, a shared SIS engineering account, 4 managed conduits, unauthenticated controller protocols, no DCS controller firmware integrity check, and tested configuration restores (EV-055 to EV-061). The hub keeps standing connections to all 16 plants and its recipe library is readable by every hub engineer (EV-065). The 7 legacy plants run flat networks with dual-homed historians and HMIs that no longer receive patches; 2 have always-on integrator tools, and 5 have no tested OT backups (EV-066). AI-001 shows recommended setpoints on an advisory interface; its ranges have no documented check against the PSI safe limits (EV-068). The offeror security plan's risk assessment covers physical threats at loading racks (EV-069).

**Distribution.** About 38,000 customers in 30 states, 64 branches and 3 bulk terminals (EV-070). Terminal T1 is an MTSA facility with an approved Facility Security Plan and a Cybersecurity Officer designated in 2025 (EV-071). Its Cybersecurity Plan is not drafted, there is no approved hardware and software list, and the network map is dated 2022 (EV-072). 23 of about 60 regular contractor personnel with OT access have no training record and no escort entries (EV-073). The rack and tank gauging PLCs share a segment with the terminal office, and terminal automation engineers share one administrator role (EV-074). One cyber drill was held 2026-04-22 (EV-075), and 3 of 7 terminal OT vendor contracts have incident notice terms (EV-076). The offeror security plan's risk assessment covers physical threats only (EV-077). The managed inventory service runs about 2,600 gateways (about 640 at utilities); the vendor portal that configures them signs in with a password only, and firmware is pushed outside the release pipeline (EV-078). The 12 largest customers asked for a SOC 2 Type 2 report by the end of 2027, and the telemetry vendor has not been reviewed since 2023 (EV-079).

**Hazmat Transport.** About 1,900 tractors and 2,700 drivers at 28 terminals; electronic shipping papers since 2024; no explosives, radioactive, poisonous-by-inhalation or bulk methane loads (EV-081). The carrier security plan's risk assessment covers physical threats only and was not updated for electronic shipping papers (EV-082). Dispatch changes to consignees and routes raise no alert, and ELD support accounts are shared at 6 of 28 terminals (EV-083). Driver camera AI scores are used in discipline with no recorded review of the video (EV-084). All 28 terminal managers can read testing results for every terminal (EV-085). 41 new drivers were past 90 days without security awareness training (EV-086).
