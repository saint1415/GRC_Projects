At the Sole Proprietorship tier the business keeps **one** consolidated policy, because one person writes, follows, and checks it (`01_company-sizes/tier-project-scaling.csv`, t1 P06). The hazmat transportation security plan (HSP-01) is a separate document only because 49 CFR 172.802 requires a written plan that can be shown to DOT or DHS. This file is kept so the folder has the standard policy set names. It holds no separate rules.

**Where the incident response rules are:**

- POL-01 section 10 (Incident response): runbook and printed contacts (10.1), same-day incident and suspicious-order log (10.2), reporting suspicious hydrogen peroxide orders to the producer and local law enforcement (10.3), notification deadlines (10.4), no ransom without counsel and an OFAC check (10.5), annual walkthrough with a supplier's shipping office and lessons learned (10.6).
- HSP-01 section 7: what counts as a hazmat security incident and the first actions.
- The procedure itself is the P08 runbook (`../step-08_P08_incident-response-runbook/ir-runbook.md`) and its notification matrix.
- SP 800-53 IR-1 is met by POL-01 as a whole.

Traceability for every statement is in `policy-control-map.csv`.
