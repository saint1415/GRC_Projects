At the Sole Proprietorship tier the business keeps **one** consolidated policy, because one person writes, follows, and checks it (`01_company-sizes/tier-project-scaling.csv`, t1 P06). The hazmat transportation security plan (HSP-01) is a separate document only because 49 CFR 172.802 requires a written plan that can be shown to DOT or DHS. This file is kept so the folder has the standard policy set names. It holds no separate rules.

**Where the access control rules are:**

- POL-01 section 7 (Access control): own accounts and no family use of the laptop (7.1), MFA with an authenticator app for email (7.2), passphrases in a password manager (7.3), standard account for daily work (7.4), phone carrier PIN and sealed recovery codes (7.5), monthly sign-in, forwarding-rule, and payee review (7.6), quarterly accounting user review (7.7).
- POL-01 section 6.4: owner-started, watched remote support sessions.
- HSP-01 sections 4.2 and 4.3: who may authorize a hazmat release, and how the accounts that carry pickup authorizations are protected.
- SP 800-53 AC-1 and IA-1 (policy and procedures) are met by POL-01 as a whole.

Traceability for every statement is in `policy-control-map.csv`.
