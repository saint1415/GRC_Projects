## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how workforce members use company systems, devices, customer building systems, and customer information.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members and subcontractors, on company devices, customer equipment, and any personal device used for company work.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Director | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and annually; tracks training |
| Security Manager | Awareness content, phishing exercises, and administrator training |
| OT Security Engineer | Role-based OT security training for Building Technology and ROC staff |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company and customer systems are for company business. Limited personal use of company laptops and phones is allowed if it involves no customer information and creates no risk. Customer building systems and GSA systems may be used only for assigned work. (PL-4)
4.2 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4)
4.3 Do not post photos of customer buildings' equipment rooms, panels, door hardware, or screens, or discuss customer security arrangements, on social media or public sites. (PL-4(1))
4.4 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year, with insider threat and social engineering content. The Security Manager runs monthly phishing exercises. (AT-2; PR.AT-01)
4.5 **Role-based training** is required before access and annually: BAS technicians, security systems technicians, and ROC operators (OT security, remote access rules, vendor call-back, recognizing unknown devices and modems); administrators (privileged use); and anyone handling CUI. Federal site staff also complete GSA's annual IT security training. (AT-3; PR.AT-02)
4.6 Lock your screen when you step away. Keep drawings and door schedules out of view of building visitors. (AC-11)
4.7 Do not install software, browser extensions, or remote-support tools, and do not connect USB storage, unless IT approves it. On customer engineering workstations, use only company-issued, scanned USB drives. Never install a modem or wireless device in a customer panel. (CM-11; MP-7)
4.8 Do not use personal devices or accounts for customer information, except company apps on an enrolled phone. Do not forward drawings or schedules to personal email. (AC-20)
4.9 **Never grant remote access or give out passwords over the phone or email.** Anyone claiming to be a vendor, integrator, or customer IT person must be verified by calling back a number from the contract file. Only named Building Technology managers approve broker sessions. (AT-2)
4.10 Report lost or stolen devices, lost PIV cards or badges, misdirected drawings, and anything suspicious immediately (POL-03 section 4.2).
4.11 **Generative AI and AI features:** use only tools on the approved list. Never paste or upload drawings, door schedules, cardholder data, face images, or CUI into a tool that is not approved. Review every AI output before using it. Do not turn on AI or biometric features in customer systems (for example face recognition or video analytics) without an approved P10 assessment. (PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement (POL-01 section 4.8). Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), training records, and phishing exercise results.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7. They must be written, risk-rated, approved under POL-01 section 4.4, and expire within 12 months.
