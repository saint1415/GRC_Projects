## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce uses group systems, customer building systems, and information, including at customer sites.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members in every division and in corporate shared services, and integrators and subcontractors who use group systems, on any device used for group work and at every customer site.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Group HR director | Rules of behavior acknowledgment and training records |
| Group CISO | Approved-tools list and technical enforcement |
| Supervisors and site managers | Make sure staff follow customer site rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Every user must acknowledge the rules of behavior before access and every year. (PL-4; GV.PO-01)

4.2 Group work must be done on company-managed devices. Security officers use only company-managed phones for guard tour, post orders, and incident reports. (AC-19; PR.AA-03)

4.3 Every workforce member must complete security awareness training at hire and every year. Staff with company email also receive phishing exercises; frontline staff without company email complete an annual short module on their managed phone. (AT-2; PR.AT-01)

4.4 **Role-based training** is required before access and every year for OT technicians and integrators, CUI handlers, consumer report adjudicators, IBOP administrators, and staff with unescorted access to customer buildings that house federal tax information or criminal justice agencies (customer contracts flow down IRS Pub. 1075 and CJISSECPOL v6.1 AT-3 topics). (AT-3; PR.AT-02)

4.5 Personal devices, personal email, and personal cloud storage must not be used for customer, government, or Restricted information. (AC-20; PR.AA-05)

4.6 At customer sites, staff must follow the customer's escort, key, and photography rules. Staff must not photograph security systems except as a work order requires, and must not enter areas holding federal tax information without the escort the customer requires. (PL-4; GV.PO-01)

4.7 **Generative AI.** Only tools on the approved-tools list may be used for work, and only with the data the list allows. Public AI services must not receive Restricted or Confidential information. (PL-4; PL-4(1); GV.PO-01)

4.8 No one may install remote-support or remote-access software on a site engineering workstation, controller, or customer device. (CM-11; AC-17)

4.9 Lost or stolen devices, badges, keys, and PIV cards must be reported to the SOC or ROC within 1 hour. (IR-6; RS.MA-02)

4.10 Video, body camera footage, and access logs may be viewed only for the contracted purpose. (PL-4; AC-21)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.7. Compliance is checked through training records, device management reports, and the P07 assessment (AT-2, AT-3).

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.11.
