At the Micro tier the company keeps three core policies. The workforce use rules that an Acceptable Use Policy would hold are short enough to sit with the access rules staff already read, so they live in **POL-02 Part C. Workforce use rules**, with the AI and biometric feature rule in POL-04:

| Essential rule | Where it lives | Driver |
|---|---|---|
| Use company systems and customer data for contract work only | POL-02 C.1 | PL-4 |
| No customer data, drawings, or CUI in personal accounts, messaging apps, or public AI chatbots | POL-02 C.2 | PL-4; 32 CFR Part 2002 |
| Approved AI tools only; no AI or biometric feature in a customer system without a P10 assessment | POL-04 4.7 | SA-9 |
| Never connect company devices to the GSA Building Systems Network; no customer data on personal devices | POL-02 C.3 | FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(iii) |
| Security training at hire and yearly; customer-required courses; phishing simulations | POL-02 C.4 | AT-2; AT-3 |
| Report suspected incidents at once | POL-02 C.5 and POL-03 4.2 | IR-6 |
| Signed acknowledgment at hire and after each annual update | POL-02 C.6 | PL-4 |

Traceability for these rules is in `policy-control-map.csv` under POL-02 and POL-04.
