## 1. Purpose
Set the rules every workforce member follows when using group devices, accounts, and data.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members and all group devices, including dispatch consoles, crew tablets, terminal kiosks, and building OT consoles.

## 3. Policy statements
3.1 Every workforce member must acknowledge these rules and the SSI handling rules at hire and each year. (PL-4; GV.PO-01)

3.2 Dispatch consoles, PTC administration workstations, terminal control PCs, and building OT consoles are for operational use only: no email, no web browsing, no personal devices, and no removable media unless approved by the system owner. (CM-11; PR.PS-05; SD III.D.1.d)

3.3 Users must not install software. Only software on the approved list may run on operations devices. (CM-11; PR.PS-05)

3.4 Users must report suspected phishing with the report button and must never approve unexpected MFA prompts. (AT-2(3); SI-8; SD III.D.1.a)

3.5 Bank account, remittance, or payment instruction changes received by email or phone must never be acted on without call-back verification to a number already on file. (AT-2(3); PR.AT-01)

3.6 Only generative AI tools on the approved list may be used for group work, and SSI, FCI, and Restricted information must never be entered into a tool that is not approved for that level (P10). (PL-4; GV.PO-01)

3.7 Employees who see something suspicious on rail property, at a terminal, or in a building (tampering, unknown devices, unexpected remote sessions) must report it at once; in rail operations this supports the observe and report training required for security-sensitive employees (49 CFR 1580.115). (AT-2; PR.AT-01)

3.8 Personal use of group email and devices is allowed only if limited, lawful, and not on operations devices. The group may monitor its systems. (PL-4)

## 4. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.7. Compliance is checked through phishing exercises, allowlisting alerts, and training records (P07 AT-2).

## 5. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.12.
