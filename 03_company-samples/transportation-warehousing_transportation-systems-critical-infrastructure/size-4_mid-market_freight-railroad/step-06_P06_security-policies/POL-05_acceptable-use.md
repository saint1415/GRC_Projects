## 1. Purpose
Set the rules every workforce member follows when using company systems, devices, and data, so that everyday use does not open a path into dispatch, PTC, or field systems.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members and contractors who use company systems or devices: office workstations and laptops, dispatch consoles, PTC administration workstations, shop and yard PCs, the 410 crew tablets, company phones, the productivity suite, the TMS and shipper portal, and approved AI tools. Every user acknowledges this policy at hire and every year.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow this policy; report suspected incidents and lost devices at once |
| Managers | Make sure staff complete the acknowledgment and training |
| HR Director | Tracks acknowledgments; applies discipline with the employee's manager |
| Cybersecurity Manager | Owns the policy, the approved-software and approved-AI lists, and awareness training |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use of office systems is allowed if it does not affect work, security, or other users. **Dispatch consoles, PTC administration workstations, and CTC maintenance workstations are for operational use only**: no email, web browsing, personal use, or removable media. (PL-4; CM-7)
4.2 Users must complete security awareness training at hire and every year, and office users take part in quarterly phishing simulations. Security-sensitive employees also complete the TSA-approved security training within 60 days of starting a security-sensitive function and recurrent training every 3 years (49 CFR 1580.115). (AT-2; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02)
4.3 Users must not install software, browser extensions, or remote access tools that are not on the approved-software list. Application allowlisting enforces this on consoles and OT servers. Macros from internet files are blocked. (CM-11; CM-7; PR.PS-05; SD III.D.1.d)
4.4 Users must not connect personal devices, personal USB drives, or personal hotspots to company networks, consoles, field equipment, or locomotives. Company data may be reached from personal phones only through the managed apps with MFA. (AC-19; AC-20)
4.5 **Crew tablets** must be kept locked, used only for train lists, bulletins, and approved apps, and reported lost or stolen to the NOC within 1 hour. (AC-19)
4.6 Users must report suspected phishing with the report button, and must report any suspected incident to the NOC or the MSSP hotline immediately (POL-03 4.2). Users must never try to investigate on their own. (IR-6; RS.MA-02)
4.7 Users must not disclose SSI, Restricted data, network details, or information about security measures to anyone without a need to know, including on social media, in conversations with vendors, or to callers who claim to be from TSA, the FRA, or a Class I railroad without verification through a known number. (AC-3; 1520.9(a))
4.8 **AI tools.** Only AI tools on the approved-AI list may be used for company work, and only with the data classes listed for each tool. Public generative AI tools must not receive SSI, Restricted data, customer data, or infrastructure imagery. AI outputs must be reviewed by a person before use, and AI must never replace a required inspection or a decision that a rule assigns to a qualified person (POL-01 4.15; STD-05). (PL-4; SA-9)
4.9 Users must not try to bypass security controls, including EDR, MFA, the PAM jump host, application allowlisting, DNS filtering, or the locks and seals on signal housings, tower shelters, and onboard PTC equipment. (PL-4; PE-3)
4.10 Company systems display a notice that use is monitored. Users have no expectation of privacy in company systems, except as the law provides. (AC-8)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 statement 4.11. For employees covered by a collective bargaining agreement, the agreement's procedures apply. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), acknowledgment reports, and phishing simulation results.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 statement 4.9.
