## 1. Purpose
Set the rules every employee and contractor follows when using company systems, crew tablets, dispatch consoles, and data, and the training they must complete.

## 2. Scope
All employees and contractors, including train and engine crews, dispatchers, signal maintainers, mechanical and engineering staff, and office staff, at all 64 railroads and acquired railroads from closing.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Chief Human Resources Officer | Owns this policy; acknowledgments and training records |
| Assistant Vice President, Rail Security | TSA security training program content and records |
| Managers and supervisors | Make sure their teams complete training before they work |
| All employees and contractors | Follow this policy; report incidents and suspicious activity |

## 4. Policy statements
Each statement is testable and tagged with its SP 800-53 control(s) and CSF 2.0 subcategory. `policy-control-map.csv` traces each statement to its regulatory driver and shows whether Internal Audit tested it in 2026 (P07).

4.1 Company systems are for authorized business use. Users must not try to bypass security controls, use another person's credentials, or access systems or data outside their role. (PL-4; GV.RR-04)
4.2 Every employee and contractor must acknowledge this policy and the SSI handling rules at hire and every year. (PL-4; PS-6; GV.RR-04)
4.3 Every employee must complete security awareness training at hire and every year, including phishing and insider threat topics. (AT-2; PR.AT-01)
4.4 Security-sensitive employees of the Covered Railroads must complete TSA-approved security training before performing the function or within 60 days under supervision, and recurrent training at least every three years; hazmat employees must complete security awareness and in-depth security training. (AT-3; PR.AT-02)
4.5 Dispatchers, NOC supervisors, and train control staff must complete annual role-based training on cyber incidents and manual operations; console access is suspended after a 30-day grace period if training is overdue. (AT-3; PR.AT-02)
4.6 Only approved software may run on company devices; users may not install software on dispatch consoles, servers, or crew tablets. (CM-11; PR.PS-05)
4.7 Removable storage must not be used on dispatch consoles, OT workstations, or servers except approved, scanned media for vendor-certified updates. (MP-7; PR.PS-05)
4.8 Personal devices must not connect to OT networks or the NOC operations zone; crew tablets must be company-managed. (AC-19; PR.AA-05)
4.9 Only AI tools on the approved list (STD-05.3) may be used for company work, and any new AI use must be registered with the AI governance committee before use. (AC-20; PR.DS-01)
4.10 Employees must report lost devices, suspicious messages, and suspicious activity at once to the SOC or the railroad police communications center. (IR-6; RS.MA-02)

## 6. Compliance and enforcement
Compliance is monitored through training records, acknowledgment reports, device management reports, and the annual Internal Audit assessment (P07). Violations are handled under PRC-01.1 (POL-01 statement 4.7) and collective bargaining agreement procedures.

## 7. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 7 and PRC-01.2. No exception may waive a regulatory training deadline.
