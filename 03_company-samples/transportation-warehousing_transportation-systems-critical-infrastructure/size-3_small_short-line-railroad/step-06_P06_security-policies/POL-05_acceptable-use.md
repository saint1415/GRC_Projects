## 1. Purpose
Set the rules for using company devices, networks, and data, including crew tablets and operations workstations, and for using AI tools.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (owners, employees, and contractors) at Central Yard, North Yard, South Yard, the tower sites, and on trains. Covers all company systems and data, including operational technology (dispatch, radio, wayside, and onboard PTC equipment) and systems that service providers operate for the company.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| IT Manager | Maintains this policy, the training program, and the approved-tools list |
| Department heads | Make sure staff complete training |
| HR Manager | Records policy acknowledgments and training completion |
| All workforce | Follow these rules; acknowledge this policy each year |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company devices and accounts are for company business. Limited personal use of office devices is allowed if it does not affect security or work. **Operations workstations, dispatch consoles, and crew tablets are for operational use only.** (PL-4)
4.2 Every workforce member must read and acknowledge this policy at hire and every year. (PL-4)
4.3 **Training.** All employees complete security awareness training at hire and every year, including how to report a cyber event to dispatch. Office staff also receive quarterly phishing exercises. Dispatchers and IT staff receive role-based training on manual dispatch after a cyber outage and on administrator duties. (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02)
4.4 Crew tablets must stay locked with a PIN, must not be shared, and must be reported lost to dispatch at once. (AC-19)
4.5 Do not connect personal devices, USB drives, or chargers with data lines to dispatch consoles, the PTC workstation, radio equipment, or wayside equipment. (CM-7; PR.PS-05)
4.6 Only IT may install software. Operations systems run only vendor-certified software. (CM-11; PR.PS-05)
4.7 Do not photograph or post images of dispatch screens, tower sites, detector equipment, or SSI on social media. (PL-4)
4.8 **AI tools.** Only tools on the approved-tools list kept by the IT Manager may be used for company work. No Restricted data or SSI in any AI tool that is not approved for it. AI outputs that support safety decisions, such as defect detection results, only supplement required inspections by qualified people and never replace them (49 CFR 213.233(b) allows inspection devices only "to supplement visual inspection"). (PL-4; GV.PO-01)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the discipline procedure in POL-01 section 4.8. Consequences range from retraining to termination, depending on intent and harm; for employees covered by a collective bargaining agreement, the agreement's procedures apply. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07).

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.6. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the majority owner for High risk), and expire within 12 months.
