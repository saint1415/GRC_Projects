## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce uses group systems and information.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members in every division and in corporate shared services, on any device used for group work.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow these rules; complete training; report incidents |
| Managers | Make sure staff complete training and acknowledgments |
| Group HR | Tracks acknowledgments and training completion |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Workforce members must access customer, cardholder, and independent grocer information only as needed for their job. Looking up records out of curiosity is prohibited. (PL-4; AC-8)

4.2 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy and their division supplement at hire and every year. (PL-4; PR.AT-01; PCI DSS 12.2.1)

4.3 Every workforce member must complete security awareness training at hire and every year, including phishing and social engineering. The group runs quarterly phishing exercises. (AT-2; AT-2(3); PR.AT-01; PCI DSS 12.6.1, 12.6.3.1; 16 CFR 314.4(e)(1))

4.4 Role-based training is required before access is granted: secure coding for developers, PIN pad inspection for store staff, payment page rules for anyone who can publish web content or tags, and consumer report handling for credit staff. (AT-3; PR.AT-02; PCI DSS 6.2.2, 9.5.1.3)

4.5 Suspected incidents, including tampered PIN pads and unexpected changes to web pages, must be reported to the group SOC within 1 hour (POL-03 4.1). (IR-6)

4.6 Restricted information may be used only on group-managed devices and approved services. It must not be sent to personal email or stored on personal devices. Card numbers must never be accepted or sent by email, chat, or text message; if a customer sends one, delete it and tell the customer the safe way to pay. (AC-19; AC-20; PCI DSS 3.2.1, 4.2.2)

4.7 **Generative AI.** Only AI tools on the group approved-tools list may be used for group work. Restricted or Confidential information must never be entered into public AI tools. (PL-4; PL-4(1))

4.8 **Payment changes.** Any request to change a supplier's or customer's bank details must be verified by a call to a known number before the change is made. (AT-2; PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
