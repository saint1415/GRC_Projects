## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how the workforce and contractors use company systems, devices, and data, with specific rules for cashiers, office staff, and anyone who can change the website.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (owners, employees, and temporary staff) and contractors with access to company systems, including the marketing contractor. Covers the store, online ordering, and all systems and data, including systems that service providers operate for the company (storefront, payment processor, POS vendor, cloud provider, pricing engine vendor). It applies to cardholder data wherever it could appear, customer and loyalty member data, workforce data, and all other company information.

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use on a break is allowed if it does not involve customer data or create risk. (PL-4)
4.2 Every workforce member and contractor must sign this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4; PCI DSS 12.2)
4.3 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year. Office staff and managers also get quarterly phishing exercises. Cashiers and front-end supervisors also get annual training on PIN pad tampering and substitution. (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02; PCI DSS 12.6; 9.5)
4.4 Never ask a customer to read out, write down, or email a card number. Never type a card number into a register, handheld, email, or note. Card payments are taken only on PIN pads or through the online payment form. (PL-4; PCI DSS 3.2; 4.2)
4.5 Cashiers must inspect self-checkout PIN pads at every opening and lane PIN pads weekly, using the inspection checklist, and report anything unusual to the Store Manager at once. Do not let anyone service or replace a PIN pad without the Store Manager's approval. (PL-4; PCI DSS 9.5)
4.6 Lock your screen when you step away. (AC-11)
4.7 Do not install software or browser extensions, or connect USB storage, unless IT approves it. (CM-11)
4.8 Do not add scripts, tags, pixels, or apps to the storefront, or change the checkout page, outside the approved change process (POL-01 4.13 and 4.14). This applies to the marketing contractor. (PL-4; PCI DSS 6.4.3)
4.9 Do not open unexpected attachments or links. Report suspicious messages with the Report Phishing button. (AT-2; PCI DSS 5.4)
4.10 Keep handhelds locked with a PIN and report a lost or stolen device immediately (POL-03 4.2). (AC-19)
4.11 **Generative AI:** do not enter customer, loyalty, or employee data into AI tools that are not on the approved list kept by the IT Manager (see P10). (PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement in POL-01 section 4.10. Sanctions range from retraining to termination of employment or contract, depending on intent and harm. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), the quarterly access reviews (POL-02 4.6), and the PCI DSS self-assessment each year.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the majority owner for High risk), recorded in the risk register, and expire within 12 months. No exception may allow card numbers to be entered or stored outside the P2PE devices and the processor's payment form.
