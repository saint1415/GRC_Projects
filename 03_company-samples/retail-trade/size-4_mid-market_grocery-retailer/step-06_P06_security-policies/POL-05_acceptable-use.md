## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce may use company systems, data, devices, payment equipment, and AI tools.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members and anyone else given access to company systems or data, at all stores, the DC, the support center, and when working remotely.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Director | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and annually |
| Privacy and Compliance Manager | Security and privacy training content; tracks completion |
| Security Manager | Monthly reminders and phishing simulations |
| Director of Store Operations and Store Managers | Store training on PIN pads, card handling, and reporting |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use of support-center PCs is allowed if it involves no customer data and creates no risk. Registers, store servers, and service-desk PCs used for the virtual terminal are for business use only. (PL-4)
4.2 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4(1); PCI DSS 12.2)
4.3 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year for all workforce, including store staff. Support-center staff receive phishing simulations at least quarterly (monthly for finance staff). Staff in specialized roles (administrators, cash office and finance, catering and service-desk staff, PIN pad inspectors, AI tool users) must complete role-based training. (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02; PCI DSS 12.6, 5.4)
4.4 Lock your screen when you step away. Service-desk and catering staff must not leave the virtual terminal open and unattended. (AC-11)
4.5 Do not install software or browser extensions, or connect USB storage, unless IT approves it. Never plug any device into a register, store server, or network port in a store. (CM-11)
4.6 Only company-managed devices, or personal phones enrolled under the mobile app protection rules, may access company systems. (AC-19)
4.7 Report lost or stolen devices, suspicious messages, unknown devices in a store, customer fraud complaints, and anything unusual immediately (POL-03 4.2).
4.8 **PIN pads.** Store staff must inspect PIN pads on the store's schedule and record each inspection. Never let anyone service, swap, or remove a PIN pad unless the Store Manager has verified the visit with IT. Report any loose overlay, broken seal, extra wire, or unfamiliar device at once. (AT-3; PCI DSS 9.5.1.3)
4.9 **Customer card data.** Never ask customers to read card numbers aloud, write them down, or send them by email, chat, or text. Use the PIN pad or the virtual terminal as POL-04 4.2 allows. (PL-4)
4.10 **AI tools.** Use only AI tools on the approved list (STD-05). Never paste customer, employee, card, or supplier data into a tool that is not approved for that data. Outputs from approved tools must be checked by a person before they reach a customer, a supplier, a job applicant, or a price. (PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement (POL-01 4.8). Compliance is checked through the annual independent assessment (P07), training completion reports, and PIN pad inspection logs.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7.
