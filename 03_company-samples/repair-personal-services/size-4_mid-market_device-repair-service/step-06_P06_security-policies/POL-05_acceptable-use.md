## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce may use company systems, customer devices, data, and AI tools.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members and anyone else given access to company systems, customer devices, or data, at all sites and when working remotely.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Director | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and annually |
| Security Manager | Training content, monthly reminders, and quarterly phishing simulations |
| Store Managers and Depot supervisors | Make sure their staff follow this policy at the bench and the counter |
| Director of Customer Experience | Contact center recording and AI summary rules |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use is allowed if it involves no customer data and creates no risk. (PL-4)
4.2 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy and the customer data confidentiality agreement before receiving access, and again each year. (PL-4(1); PS-6)
4.3 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year, with quarterly phishing simulations. Role-based training is required for technicians and data recovery specialists (customer data access and insider risk), counter and contact center staff (payment terminals, card numbers in calls, release checks), Store Managers (warning signs and incident logging), developers, and administrators. (AT-2; AT-2(2); AT-3; PCI DSS 12.6.1, 9.5.1.3)
4.4 **Customer devices.** Use a customer device only for the repair and tests the ticket allows. Never browse, copy, photograph, or share its content, never connect it to a personal device, and never sign in to the customer's accounts. (PL-4; AC-20)
4.5 Lock your screen when you step away, and position counter screens so customers in the queue cannot read them. (AC-11)
4.6 Do not install software or browser extensions, or connect USB storage, unless IT approves it. Approved encrypted transfer drives are logged per ticket. (CM-11; MP-7)
4.7 Only company-managed devices, or personal phones using the protected mobile app for email, may access company systems. Personal devices must never be used to store customer data. (AC-19; AC-20)
4.8 Report lost devices, missing parcels, suspicious messages, tampered terminals, a colleague misusing customer data, and anything unusual immediately (POL-03 4.2).
4.9 **AI tools.** Use only AI tools on the approved list (STD-05). Never paste or upload customer data, device photos, passcodes, or claim data into a tool that is not approved. AI suggestions (diagnoses, summaries, rankings, forecasts) must be checked by a qualified person before they reach a customer, a partner, or a decision about a person. (PL-4)
4.10 **Recorded calls.** Contact center agents must use the approved recording announcement, which tells callers that calls are recorded and summarized with an AI tool, and must stop recording if any party objects. Outbound calls are not recorded unless every party consents at the start of the call, because Florida law treats interception as lawful when all parties have given prior consent (Fla. Stat. 934.03(2)(d)). (PT-4; PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions rule in POL-01 section 4.8. Compliance is checked through acknowledgment and training reports, bench session reviews, and the annual control assessment (P07).

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7.
