## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce uses group systems, customer devices, and customers' systems.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members in every division and in corporate shared services, on any device used for group work, and on any customer device or customer system they touch.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow these rules; complete training; report incidents |
| Managers and store managers | Make sure staff complete training and acknowledgments |
| Group HR | Tracks acknowledgments and training completion |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Workforce members must access customer records, customer devices, and customers' systems only as needed for the job in front of them. Curiosity is not a reason. (PL-4; AC-8)

4.2 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy and their division supplement at hire and every year. Technicians, in-home technicians, and managed services technicians must also sign the customer data access standard (POL-04 4.6). (PL-4; PS-6; PR.AT-01; PCI DSS 12.1.3)

4.3 Every workforce member must complete security and privacy training at hire and every year. The group runs quarterly phishing exercises. Technicians complete the technician course (device content access, passcodes, sanitization, infected devices); RMM administrators complete privileged access training; store teams complete payment device tampering training. (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02; PCI DSS 12.6, 9.5.1.3; 164.308(a)(5)(ii)(A))

4.4 Suspected incidents, including any suspected misuse of a customer device, must be reported to the group SOC within 1 hour (POL-03 4.1). (IR-6)

4.5 Restricted information may be used only on group-managed devices and approved services. Workforce members must not photograph customer devices' screens or content with personal phones, and must not use personal storage on bench workstations. (AC-19; AC-20; 164.310(b))

4.6 **Generative AI.** Only AI tools on the group approved-tools list may be used for group work. Restricted information, including passcodes, card data, and customer device content, must never be entered into public AI tools. (PL-4; PL-4(1))

4.7 **Customer sites and remote sessions.** In-home and managed services technicians must start a remote session only with the customer's consent shown in the tool, and must end it when the task is done. (PL-4; AC-17)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.7.
