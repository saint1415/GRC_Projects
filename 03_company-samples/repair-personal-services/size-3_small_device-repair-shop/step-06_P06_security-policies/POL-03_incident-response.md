## 1. Purpose
Make sure the company detects, contains, reports, and recovers from security incidents quickly and lawfully, including misuse of customer devices, and meets every legal and contractual notice deadline.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (employees, managers, temporary staff, and contractors) at Stores A to D and the Depot. Covers all company systems and data, customer devices and the data on them while they are in the company's custody, and systems that vendors operate for the company.

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 The company must maintain an incident response plan and runbooks for its most likely incidents, starting with customer data exposure at the repair counter (P08). (IR-8; RS.MA-01; PCI DSS 12.10.1)
4.2 Workforce members must report any suspected incident **immediately**, and within 1 hour at most, to the IT Manager's incident line or their Store Manager. An anonymous line is available for reports about colleagues. Examples: a colleague browsing a customer's photos, a phishing click, a lost device, a tampered payment terminal, card numbers written down, or a missing customer device. Good-faith reporting is never sanctioned. (IR-6; RS.MA-02)
4.3 Every incident must be logged, categorized, and tracked to closure in the help desk under the Security category. (IR-5)
4.4 **Preserve evidence.** Bench PCs, counter tablets, and customer devices involved in a suspected incident must not be reimaged, wiped, or returned until the IT Manager releases them. (IR-4; RS.AN-07)
4.5 The IT Manager, with counsel, must decide whether an incident is a breach under Fla. Stat. 501.171 and the laws of other states where affected customers live, and document the decision. Any no-notice determination under 501.171(4)(c) must be in writing and kept for 5 years. (IR-6)
4.6 Notices must meet the deadlines in the P08 notification matrix, including: the acquirer and Manufacturer A within 24 hours of suspecting an incident (contract terms), and Florida residents and the Department of Legal Affairs no later than 30 days after determination. Counsel must confirm each legal notice. (IR-6; RS.CO-02)
4.7 No ransom or extortion payment may be made without approval from the majority owner, counsel, and the insurer, and an OFAC sanctions check. (IR-4)
4.8 The incident response plan must be tested at least annually by tabletop exercise, and after any major incident. (IR-3; ID.IM-02)
4.9 Lessons learned must be documented within 30 days of closing an incident and added to the risk register. (IR-4; ID.IM-03)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions rule in POL-01 section 4.7. Sanctions range from retraining to termination, depending on intent and harm. Misuse of a customer's device data is treated as serious misconduct. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), the access reviews in POL-02, and the log reviews in POL-03.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.6. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the majority owner for High risk), and expire within 12 months.
