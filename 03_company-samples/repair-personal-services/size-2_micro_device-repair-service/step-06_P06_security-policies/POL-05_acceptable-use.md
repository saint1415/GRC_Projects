At the Micro tier the shop keeps three core policies. The staff use rules that an Acceptable Use Policy would hold are short enough to sit with the access rules staff already read, so they live in **POL-02 Part C. Staff use rules**, with the customer device and AI tool rules in POL-04:

| Essential rule | Where it lives | Driver |
|---|---|---|
| Use shop systems for shop work; access only what the job needs | POL-02 C.1 | CSF 2.0 PR.AA-05 |
| No customer data in personal email, cloud storage, phones, messaging apps, or personal USB drives | POL-02 C.2 | 15 U.S.C. 45(n) |
| Open only what the repair test needs on a customer device | POL-04 4.4 | Fla. Stat. 501.171(1)(a); 15 U.S.C. 45(n) |
| Card numbers only in the P2PE terminals | POL-04 4.5 | PCI DSS 3.2.1 |
| Lock screens; never share passwords or MFA codes | POL-02 C.3 | CSF 2.0 PR.AA-03 |
| Training at hire and yearly; phishing simulations; terminal tamper training | POL-02 C.4 | PCI DSS 9.5.1.3, 12.6.1 |
| Report suspected incidents at once | POL-02 C.5 and POL-03 4.2 | CSF 2.0 RS.MA-02 |
| Signed acknowledgment at hire and after each annual update | POL-02 C.6 | PCI DSS 12.1.3 |
| Call back on a number on file before changing bank details | POL-02 C.7 | CSF 2.0 PR.AT-01 |
| Approved AI tools only; no customer data in public chatbots | POL-02 C.8 and POL-04 4.8 | 15 U.S.C. 45(a)(1); Fla. Stat. 501.171(2) |

Traceability for these rules is in `policy-control-map.csv` under POL-02 and POL-04.
