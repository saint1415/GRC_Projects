## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how the workforce uses firm systems, devices, and data.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members using firm laptops, smartphones, kiosks, softphones, SaaS accounts, and data, at firm offices, client sites, and remotely.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| CHRO | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and annually; tracks training records |
| Security Manager | Awareness content, phishing simulations, and the approved AI tools list |
| Director of Compliance and Privacy | Role training for onboarding, credentialing, and recruiters |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Firm systems are for firm business. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not involve associate, candidate, or client data or create risk. (PL-4)
4.2 Every workforce member must sign this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4)
4.3 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year, with quarterly phishing simulations that include fake sign-in pages and push-approval relay. (AT-2; AT-2(3); PR.AT-01)
4.4 **Role training each year.** Payroll and billing staff: bank-change fraud, caller impersonation, and invoice redirection. Onboarding and Compliance Specialists: I-9 records handling and the E-Verify rules (plus the E-Verify Tutorial and refreshers). Credentialing staff: medical information rules. Recruiters: FCRA and I-9 record limits and the AI tool rules. MSP staff: supplier invoice fraud. (AT-3; PR.AT-02; 8 CFR 274a.2(g)(1)(iii); E-Verify MOU Art. II.A.5)
4.5 **Payroll and administrator sign-in.** Sign in only from the firm's SSO page or a saved bookmark, never from a link in an email or text. Never approve an MFA prompt you did not start, and report it. (AT-2)
4.6 Never share a password, MFA approval, or E-Verify credential. (IA-5; E-Verify MOU Art. II.A.15)
4.7 Lock your screen when you step away. Do not leave candidate records or credential files open on a lobby kiosk or a screen visible to visitors. (AC-11)
4.8 Do not install software or browser extensions, or connect USB storage, unless IT approves it. (CM-11)
4.9 Report lost devices, suspicious messages, unexpected bank-change requests, and misdirected documents immediately (POL-03 4.2).
4.10 **Generative AI and recruiting AI.** Use only tools on the approved list. Never paste or upload SSNs, I-9 documents, consumer reports, medical information, bank data, or resumes into a tool that is not approved for that data. AI scores and pre-screen answers are a sort aid, not a decision: a person reviews every applicant who meets the job order's minimum requirements, and no applicant is rejected by an AI tool alone (P10). (PL-4)
4.11 **Recorded calls.** Every recorded call with a candidate, associate, or client must begin with a recording announcement. If the platform cannot play one, turn recording off. Never ask a caller to read an SSN on a recorded line; use the IVR keypad. (PT-5; Fla. Stat. 934.03(2)(d))
4.12 **Client sites.** Follow the client's security and privacy rules at its site, use client systems only for assigned work, and return badges and credentials at the end of each assignment. (PE-2)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement (POL-01 4.8). Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), phishing simulation results, and training records.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 4.7. They must be written, risk-rated, approved at the risk acceptance level in POL-01 4.4, and expire within 12 months.
