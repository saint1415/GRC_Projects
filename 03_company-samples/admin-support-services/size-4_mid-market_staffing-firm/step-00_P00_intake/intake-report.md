## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the firm's own records before any assessment work began on 2026-07-13. It covers the organization, the systems that hold candidate, associate, clinician and client data, the suppliers that touch them, and the rules that may bind the firm. At this size the sources are systems of record: the identity provider, the HR roster, the ATS, payroll, credentialing, VMS, timekeeping and contact center admin consoles, the E-Verify user list, the device management and EDR consoles, the SD-WAN and firewall exports, the cloud organization console, the SIEM and the MSSP reports, the accounts payable vendor master, the contract register, the 2025 internal audit report, scan results, the incident log and the payroll fraud case log. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (interviews, samples, tests) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement or the CSF 2.0 benchmark is decided in the gap analysis (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Users and access.** The identity provider holds named accounts for all 600 internal staff on the HR roster, plus the 3 contractor developers, the vCISO and service accounts, and every active staff account has number-matching push MFA registered. E-Verify and the VMS are not federated (EV-001). Conditional access requires push MFA and a managed device for every staff application; no phishing-resistant authenticator is enrolled (EV-002). HR recorded 142 terminations and 38 transfers in the 12 months to 2026-06-30 (EV-003). SSO access reviews run semi-annually, the last in March 2026; ATS roles, E-Verify, VMS and credentialing client users were outside their scope (EV-004). 31 accounts hold privileged roles; cloud administrators use just-in-time elevation, while 3 ATS and 2 payroll administrators hold standing rights on everyday accounts (EV-005). The ATS has 31 custom roles, and 262 users in recruiter, branch manager and account manager roles can open I-9 document images and consumer reports for all branches (EV-006). Associates sign in to the portal and app with SMS one-time codes and can change direct deposit details there (EV-007). Associate-entered bank changes take effect on the next payroll with an email notice, and 2 payroll specialists can both add associates and change pay rates (EV-008). The payroll case log records 41 fraudulent direct deposit changes in 2026 H1, diverting $38,200 (EV-009). E-Verify has 22 named users outside SSO (EV-010), and the VMS has 28 firm users with local accounts and app-based MFA (EV-011). Clinician medical documents sit in the same credential file category as licenses, the Healthcare recruiter role (30 users) can view them, and the client portal releases full physical exam forms (EV-012). The termination checklist does not list E-Verify, VMS or credentialing client accounts (EV-013).

**Devices and network.** Device management lists 640 laptops, 230 smartphones, 42 kiosks and 60 multifunction devices, 972 in total. Laptops have full-disk encryption and remote wipe; the kiosk image allows USB storage; the 12 fingerprint clocks and on-site network devices are not in the inventory (EV-014). EDR runs in blocking mode on all laptops and kiosks, monitored by the MSSP (EV-015). Kiosks have their own segment at Branches 1-5 and share the staff network at Branches 6-14 (EV-016). HQ has dual ISPs, and 5 branches have LTE backup (EV-017). The MSSP scans quarterly: 41 critical findings in 2026 H1, 9 closed after the 30-day target, the longest at 74 days; container images and clocks are outside the scan scope (EV-018). Container base images were last rebuilt in 2025-11 (EV-019). The ATS change log shows about 140 changes in 2026 H1 with no approval field, and the AI auto-advance setting (2025-09-15) and knockout questions (2026-02-02) were switched on with no ticket (EV-020).

**Cloud, data stores and monitoring.** The landing zone has 4 accounts built in 2025, with guardrails, company-managed keys, posture detection in every account and a write-once log bucket kept 3 years; the 2 backup administrators also hold workload administrator rights (EV-021). The data warehouse and integration platform are backed up daily to the backup account in a second region with 30-day write-once retention; the document archive is replicated and not in the backup plan (EV-022). The only restore test recorded is the warehouse, in 2026-03 (EV-023). The warehouse copies full SSNs and bank account numbers for about 168,000 associates nightly; 46 users can query them, query auditing is off, and extracts are written to file shares (EV-024). The archive holds about 74,000 scanned Forms I-9 from 2012-2021, with versioning, object lock and object-level logging off (EV-025). The SIEM receives identity, email, EDR, firewall, SD-WAN and cloud logs; the ATS, payroll, VMS and credentialing platforms are not connected, and no use case covers payroll exports or bank-change volumes (EV-026). The MSSP's 2026 reports describe push-approval relay phishing campaigns (EV-027). The integration platform was built in 2024 by 3 contractor developers under a statement of work with no secure development requirements, and the repository has no secret scanning (EV-028). Outbound recruiter softphone calls are recorded with no announcement, and about 1.1 million recordings since 2022 are kept indefinitely (EV-029). Finger templates for about 1,300 associates are stored on the clocks and in the vendor cloud, with no notice or consent form in the enrollment records and no retention period set (EV-030). The ATS I-9 module locks completed sections and keeps a per-form change history (EV-031).

**Programs and documents.** Five security policies were approved in 2024 with no review record since, and 3 standards date from 2024 (EV-032). The Security Manager was designated Information Security Officer on 2025-02-03, and the vCISO has been engaged since 2024 (EV-033). The enterprise risk register carries one cybersecurity line, and the audit committee calendar schedules a quarterly cyber report from 2026-09 (EV-034). The monthly security report tracks training, phishing and POA&M aging (EV-035). The request for a BIA, contingency plan, manual payroll procedure and exercise records returned a draft branch hurricane plan and the warehouse restore record (EV-036). No retention schedule, no purge job, no written electronic I-9 system description or procedures, and no I-9 quality check records were provided (EV-037). The 2025 internal IT audit left 5 of 12 POA&M items open (EV-038). The incident log holds 46 incidents for 2025 and 2026 H1, with no lessons-learned records and no pay diversion cases (EV-039). Training is 96% complete, the June 2026 phishing click rate was 9.4%, acceptable use acknowledgments are 97% current, and no role-based modules are assigned (EV-040). Payroll is processed Wednesday, funded Thursday and paid Friday (EV-041).

**Suppliers and contracts.** Accounts payable flags 64 vendors that receive personal information, with no tier field; the AI screening and text assistant subscriptions are billed through the ATS marketplace (EV-042). 23 of those 64 contracts contain security terms. The ATS contract has no data return clause, the credentialing contract has no breach notice term, the timekeeping contract has no breach notice or template retention terms, and the AI vendors are on click-through terms (EV-043). SOC 2 reports are on file for 7 vendors with no review record; the payroll vendor states RTO 8 hours, the ATS vendor RTO 12 hours and the VMS vendor RTO 24 hours, and the credentialing and timekeeping vendors state none. The credentialing vendor has no SOC 2 report (EV-044). The cyber policy has a $5 million limit and a $100,000 retention, and the crime policy a $250,000 social engineering sublimit (EV-045). Both MSP client agreements require 48-hour incident notice and a SOC 2 Type 2 report by the renewals (EV-046). Healthcare client agreements require HIPAA training before a clinician's first shift, and the General Counsel declined 2 hospital BAA templates in a memo of 2025-11-14 (EV-047). The AHCA health care services pool registration is current (EV-048). The 2024 screening agreement carries the FCRA certification and security terms; ATS settings let recruiters set Not Eligible without triggering the adverse action workflow (EV-049). 3 kiosks and 2 fingerprint clocks were disposed of in 2025 with no certificate (EV-050). The 2026 Q1 reemployment tax return carries the E-Verify certification (EV-051). The group health plan is fully insured (EV-052).

**Facilities.** Badge access runs at HQ and all branches; 3 Branch Managers report that the network closet is also the supply room; on-site offices rely on client facility security and client guest networks (EV-053).

**Business volume.** Receipts were $100.0 million in FY2025, about $385,000 per business day and about $57,500 per calendar day in Healthcare (EV-054). About 3,600 associates are on assignment in an average week; the firm hires about 13,800 associates and runs about 10,900 background checks a year, and holds about 168,000 associate records and 610,000 candidate profiles (EV-055). The MSP program covers about 1,100 workers from 65 suppliers and about $2.6 million of weekly invoices (EV-056). Payroll is funded by a Thursday credit line draw (EV-057).

**AI tools and email.** Six AI uses were found, including resume ranking with auto-advance for Light Industrial since 2025-09-15 and a text assistant asking knockout questions since 2026-02-02; 85% of Light Industrial placements in 2026 H1 came from auto-advanced applicants; no security review, bias test or validation record was provided (EV-058). The applicant notice does not mention automated tools, and the text assistant script is English only with no accommodation option (EV-059). Email filtering tags external senders, and data loss prevention rules block Restricted data types (EV-060).

**Reconciliation across sources.**
- **Workforce.** The 600 internal staff on the HR roster each have an identity provider account (EV-001, EV-003).
- **Endpoints.** The device management total (972) is the SSP boundary count and the P07 sampling population; the 12 fingerprint clocks are counted separately from the timekeeping console (EV-014, EV-030).
- **Vendors.** 64 vendors flagged in accounts payable receive personal information, and 23 of their contracts contain security terms (EV-042, EV-043). The 17 named vendors and a summary row in [`vendor-register.csv`](vendor-register.csv) add up to the 64.
- **AI tools.** Two marketplace subscriptions in accounts payable, the ATS drafting feature, the productivity suite assistant pilot and two timekeeping features give the 6 rows in [`asset-inventory.csv`](asset-inventory.csv) (EV-042, EV-058).
- **Privileged accounts.** The privileged account list (31) is the P07 population for the MFA test and rights review (EV-005).
