## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce uses group systems and information.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members in every division and in corporate shared services, on any device used for group work. Temporary associates are covered when they use group systems (for example, the self-service app or a kiosk); at client sites they follow the client's rules.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow these rules; complete training; report incidents |
| Managers | Make sure staff complete training and acknowledgments |
| Group HR | Tracks acknowledgments and training completion |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Workforce members must access candidate, worker, patient, and client information only as needed for their job. Looking up records out of curiosity, including coworkers' pay or a family member's patient record, is prohibited. (PL-4; AC-8)

4.2 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy and their division supplement at hire and every year. (PL-4; PR.AT-01)

4.3 Every workforce member must complete security and privacy training at hire and every year. The group sends monthly reminders and runs quarterly phishing exercises. (AT-2; PR.AT-01; 164.308(a)(5)(ii)(A))

4.4 Role-based training is required before access for onboarding staff (Form I-9 and the E-Verify tutorial), payroll and associate service staff (bank-change fraud), credentialing staff (medical file handling), and consultants with client system access (client PHI rules). (AT-3; PR.AT-02; 8 CFR 274a.2(g)(1)(iii); MOU Art. II.A.5)

4.5 Suspected incidents must be reported to the group SOC within 1 hour (POL-03 4.1). (IR-6)

4.6 Restricted information may be used only on group-managed devices and approved services. It must not be sent to personal email, texted from personal phones, or stored on personal devices. Field staff use the approved secure messaging app on their tablets. (AC-19; AC-20; 164.310(b))

4.7 Never share a password or one-time code, including for E-Verify and client systems. Group staff will never ask a worker for a sign-in code by phone. (IA-5; MOU Art. II.A.15)

4.8 **Generative AI.** Only AI tools on the group approved-tools list may be used for group work. Restricted information must never be entered into public AI tools. (PL-4; PL-4(1))

4.9 **Recording conversations** (for example, with an ambient documentation tool in a patient's home) is allowed only after the consent that state law requires is obtained and documented. In states that require all parties to consent (Florida, Fla. Stat. 934.03(2)(d), is the worked example), recording must not start until every party present has consented. (PT-4; PL-4)
