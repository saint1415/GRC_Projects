## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how workforce members use company systems, data, and payment devices, so that everyday work does not expose card data, patron data, or the payment pages.

## 2. Scope
All employees, temporary staff, and contractors (including agency staff and event-day workers who use company devices or payment devices), on any device used for company work.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Director | Owns this policy; collects acknowledgments at hire and annually |
| Managers and event-day leads | Make sure their staff and contractor workers know these rules |
| Security Manager | Keeps the approved software and AI tools lists |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not affect work or security. (PL-4; PCI 12.2.1)
4.2 **Never write down, type, email, message, or photograph a card number or security code.** Take card payments only on a validated P2PE device or in the payment provider's own form while the patron is present or on the phone. (PL-4; PCI 12.2.1; 3.3.1)
4.3 Protect your credentials. Never share passwords or approve an MFA prompt you did not start; report any unexpected prompt at once. (IA-5; PL-4)
4.4 Report suspected phishing, lost devices, and anything unusual (an unknown script or wrong price on an event page, a damaged or unfamiliar payment device) immediately under POL-03. (IR-6)
4.5 Do not install unapproved software or browser extensions, and do not add scripts, tags, or pixels to company web pages except through the change process in POL-01 section 4.12. (CM-11; PL-4)
4.6 Lock your screen when you step away, keep company laptops encrypted, and never disable security tools. (AC-11; SI-3)
4.7 Use patron data only for your job. Never export patron data to personal accounts, personal devices, or unapproved services. (AC-6; PL-4)
4.8 **AI tools.** Use only AI tools on the approved list. Never put Restricted data into any AI tool, and never put Confidential data into a tool that is not approved for it. A person must review any AI-generated content before patrons see it. (PL-4; GV.PO-01)
4.9 **Payment devices on event days.** Bar, stand, and box office leads must inspect their P2PE devices before each event using the checklist and report any sign of tampering or substitution. (AT-3; PCI 9.5.1.3)
4.10 Never post or send a ticket price without the total price, including mandatory fees. (PL-4; 16 CFR 464.2)
4.11 Complete security awareness training at hire and every year, and the role-based module for your job (card handling, website publishing, or administration). (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02; PCI 12.6.3)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.10. Compliance is checked through acknowledgments, training records, card data discovery scans, and the annual independent assessment (P07).

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7.
