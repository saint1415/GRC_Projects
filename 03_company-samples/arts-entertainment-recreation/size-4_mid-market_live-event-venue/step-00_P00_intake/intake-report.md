## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the company's own records before any assessment work began on 2026-07-06. It covers the organization, the systems that sell tickets, take card payments, admit attendees and hold patron data, the suppliers and agencies that touch them, and the rules and contracts that may bind the company. At this size the sources are systems of record: the identity provider, the HR system, the ticketing platform's admin console, the payment partner and POS vendor portals, the tag manager and CMS, the endpoint and EDR consoles, the cloud organization console, the SIEM and MSSP reports, the accounts payable vendor master, the contract register, prior PCI DSS validation and acquirer correspondence, scan reports and the incident queue. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (interviews, samples, captures, tests) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analysis (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Users and access.** The identity provider holds active named accounts for all 600 employees on the HR roster, with MFA registered on every active user account, and 143 accounts were created in the 12 months to 2026-06-30 (EV-001). Conditional access requires MFA for all users; the method is push with number matching, and the password minimum is 14 characters (EV-002). HR recorded 96 terminations and 51 transfers in the 12 months to 2026-06-30 (EV-003). The HR feed disables identity provider accounts on the termination date; on a transfer it changes department and title only, and local ticketing accounts, agency accounts, tag manager users and badges are not connected to it (EV-004). The last user access review was the annual campaign of January 2026 (EV-005). The ticketing tenant has 186 venue users: 175 on SSO and 11 local accounts with passwords and no MFA (4 marketing agency, 3 vendor support, 2 break-glass shared by the IT team, 2 legacy integration); 14 users hold the admin role, and the local password setting allows 8 characters (EV-006). The settings history lists 214 changes from January to June 2026; the venue audit log is kept 90 days; dynamic pricing applies changes automatically, including to accessible seating price levels; and a 2023 setting caps orders with a wheelchair space at 4 seats while the posted limit is 8 (EV-007). The patron data platform's sync key has full administrator scope, sits in plain function settings, and has not been rotated since 2024 (EV-008). There are 38 federated privileged accounts; just-in-time elevation covers cloud administrator roles only (EV-009). 26 staff key card numbers into the payment partner's virtual terminal in a browser on their company laptops, signing in with SMS codes (EV-010). The tag manager has 9 publishers, 4 of them agency staff on local accounts without MFA, and publishing needs no second approval (EV-011). The CMS has 12 named editors on SSO, 3 web agency deploy roles and 23 plug-ins, 5 of them custom (EV-012).

**Devices and network.** The endpoint console lists 520 laptops and PCs and 64 tablets; EDR reports on all 520 and on the CMS servers, and 22 laptops keep local administrator rights by exception (EV-013). 180 handheld scanners are managed through the ticketing platform and can work offline from a manifest downloaded before doors (EV-014). The network diagram dates from 2025. The 4 sites run on SD-WAN with corporate, production, POS, physical security, box office P2PE and guest segments; the Amphitheater and the Music Hall have two internet providers, and the Club has one plus a cellular backup; no card data-flow diagram was provided (EV-015). Production Wi-Fi uses a shared passphrase and bridges to the production segment; rogue access point detection runs at HQ only (EV-016). The integrator's list shows 640 cameras, a VMS at each venue, crowd analytics at 2 venues since 2026-04, 12 integrator-installed servers and controllers, and 4 VMS servers past vendor support; face matching is switched off (EV-017). The IT asset register has no PCI DSS scope or owner field and does not list those 4 VMS servers (EV-018). The POS vendor lists 284 P2PE devices and the payment partner 18 box office devices, both on validated P2PE solutions, with manual card entry disabled on the POS (EV-019). Monthly internal scans cover cloud servers only, with 18 critical findings from January to June 2026 (EV-020). ASV scans of the website addresses passed in all 4 quarters; the 4 site addresses are not in the ASV scope (EV-021). The only penetration test on file is a 2025 external test of the website (EV-022). 61 infrastructure changes went to the weekly change board; ticketing settings, tag manager publishes and CMS plug-in updates did not (EV-023). Baselines exist for laptops and cloud server images, not for CMS servers, SD-WAN edges or VMS servers (EV-024).

**Cloud, backups and monitoring.** The landing zone has 4 accounts. The workloads account holds the website CMS, the patron data platform with a nightly copy of about 1.1 million patron records, and the show settlement application; storage is encrypted with company-managed keys, and 2 users have export rights on the patron data platform (EV-025). Daily backups go to a separate backup account in a second region with 35-day write-once retention and separate credentials. No restore jobs are recorded for the patron data platform or the settlement application (EV-026). The SIEM receives identity provider, cloud, firewall, VPN, web edge and EDR logs; ticketing audit logs, CMS and tag manager change history, patron data platform query logs and payment partner portal activity are not sources, and the detection use cases have not changed since 2024 (EV-027). The MSSP must call the Security Manager within 30 minutes of a high-severity alert (EV-028).

**Programs and documents.** Five security policies were adopted in 2024, with network, endpoint, encryption, authenticator and vulnerability standards; there are no standards for configuration, logging, payment page scripts, card handling or service providers (EV-029). The requests for a PCI DSS scope document, a script inventory, a service provider list with a responsibility matrix, targeted risk analyses, a card data retention procedure, card compromise procedures, restore test records and any prior SOC 2 report returned none (EV-030). The Security Manager is the designated Information Security Officer and PCI DSS program lead (EV-031). The 2024 incident response plan has no card compromise procedures and does not mention the merchant agreement's 24-hour notice; the last exercise was in 2024 (EV-032). The incident queue holds 41 incidents for 2025 and 2026, including a 2025 payment fraud attempt (EV-033). The 2024 IT continuity plan covers the cloud workloads, names 12 contacts and has no event-day functions or test records (EV-034). The Amphitheater has a written manual entry procedure, drilled each March (last 2026-03, passed); the Music Hall and the Club provided none (EV-035). Training completion for 2026 is 96%, and the June 2026 phishing click rate was 6.2% (EV-036). Background checks are required for finance, box office, call center and premium sales roles; group sales roles are not on the list (EV-037). In 2025 the company filed SAQ A for MID-T and SAQ P2PE for MID-F, and a QSA is engaged for the 2026 ROC (EV-038). The acquirer's letter of 2026-03-16 classifies the company as a Visa Level 2 merchant and sets a ROC for MID-T and an SAQ P2PE for MID-F, due 2026-12-15 (EV-039). The merchant agreement requires notice within 24 hours of a suspected compromise (EV-040). The company ran about 2.9 million card transactions in 2025, about 1.6 million of them Visa (EV-041).

**Suppliers and contracts.** Accounts payable shows payments to every technology, agency and staffing supplier in the vendor register (EV-042). The ticketing services agreement has no breach notice time and no recovery commitments; the agency contracts have no security terms; the tag manager vendor is on online terms only; and vendor files record a review at onboarding only (EV-043). The ticketing vendor's AOC is dated 2026-02-20 and its SOC 2 Type 2 report covers the 12 months ending 2026-03-31, with RTO 4 hours and RPO 15 minutes (EV-044). The latest payment partner AOC on file is dated 2024-06-12 (EV-045). The POS vendor's AOC is dated 2026-04-15 (EV-046). The cloud provider's and the productivity suite vendor's SOC 2 reports are on file (EV-047). Each show is settled on the night and approved by the Controller; the settlement procedure has no callback step for bank detail changes, and 3 finance users can both create and approve vendor payments (EV-048). The cyber insurance policy has a $10 million limit, a $250,000 retention and a $1 million card brand sublimit, with notice through the carrier hotline first (EV-049). The County PAC agreement, awarded on 2026-06-30, requires SOC 2 Type 1 and Type 2 reports and 48-hour incident notice (EV-050). 3 staffing contractors supply about 1,400 event-day workers (EV-051).

**Facilities and media.** Walk-throughs on 2026-06-23 and 2026-06-25 found badge readers and CCTV on the HQ server room, box offices and cash rooms; 2 of 9 venue network closets on keys with no key log; no visitor log for box office back rooms; printed premium contracts and notes kept in the premium sales office; and network closets at the Amphitheater not on the generator (EV-052). Badges are issued by HR with issue and removal dates (EV-053). Destruction certificates and laptop wipe records are on file for 2026 (EV-054).

**Business volume.** Revenue was $100.0 million in FY2025, with ticket sales and fees about $54.0 million and food and beverage about $28.0 million; average food and beverage sales per show were about $305,000 at the Amphitheater, $67,000 at the Music Hall and $18,000 at the Club (EV-055). The company ran about 410 shows for about 1.55 million attendees, with about 1,400 online orders on an average day and about 1.1 million patron accounts, about 71% of them with a Florida billing address (EV-056). About 70% of a show's food and beverage sales fall in the 2 hours around doors and the set break (EV-057).

**AI tools, email, website and CRM.** Five AI uses were switched on by departments: dynamic pricing, bot detection and virtual queue, the website chatbot, CCTV crowd analytics and marketing scoring with generative copy. The request for review records returned none (EV-058). Email filtering sandboxes links and attachments; no data loss prevention rules are configured (EV-059). The privacy notice dates from 2023 and does not mention bot detection, the chatbot or scoring; the ticketing FAQ says the order fee is "charged by the ticketing company", while the fee schedule shows the company sets it; 312 calendar listings and 58 email campaigns were published since 2026-01 (EV-060). The premium seating CRM holds 410 accounts, 38 users and 9,840 free-text notes (EV-061). The marketing platform has about 640,000 subscribers (EV-062).

**Reconciliation across sources.**
- **Workforce.** The 600 employees on the HR roster each have an identity provider account (EV-001, EV-003).
- **Ticketing users.** 175 SSO accounts and 11 local accounts make up the 186 venue users (EV-006). The 26 virtual terminal users are separate payment partner accounts (EV-010).
- **Endpoints.** The endpoint console count (520 laptops and PCs, 64 tablets) and the scanner list (180) match the SSP boundary and the P07 sampling populations (EV-013, EV-014).
- **Payment devices.** 284 POS devices and 18 box office devices make the 302 P2PE devices on the two vendor lists (EV-019). Whether each device is physically present was checked in P03 (EV-070).
- **Physical security servers.** The integrator's list includes 4 VMS servers that the IT asset register does not (EV-017, EV-018). That difference stays an open request.
- **AI tools.** The survey and the SaaS settings give the 5 AI uses in [`asset-inventory.csv`](asset-inventory.csv); each tool's vendor appears in accounts payable or is a module of a system already paid for (EV-042, EV-058).
