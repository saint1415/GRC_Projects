## 1. Purpose
Set the rules for using group technology, data, and AI tools.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members, including seasonal staff and contractor workers who use group devices or systems.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow this policy; complete training; report incidents |
| Managers | Make sure their staff, including contractor leads, receive the right briefing |
| Group CISO | Maintains the policy and the approved AI tools list |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Group systems are for business use. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not affect security or work. (PL-4)

4.2 Workforce access to group systems requires a managed device, except the patron-facing apps that everyone may use. (AC-20)

4.3 Only software from approved sources may be installed. (CM-11)

4.4 Never write down, photograph, email, or type into chat a card number or security code. Card payments by phone must use a payment link or a keypad device, never a card number typed by the agent. (PL-4; PCI DSS 12.2)

4.5 Inspect card devices before each shift as trained, and report any device that looks tampered with or is missing. (PL-4; PCI DSS 9.5.1)

4.6 Report suspected phishing with the report button, and suspected incidents to the SOC within 1 hour. (AT-2; PCI DSS 5.4)

4.7 Use only AI tools on the approved tools list for group data. Do not enter Restricted or Confidential data into any other AI tool. (PL-4)

4.8 Complete security awareness training at hire and every year. Contractor leads who supervise card devices complete the card-device module. (AT-2; PCI DSS 12.6)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.7.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.11.
