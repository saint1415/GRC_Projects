At the Micro tier the company keeps three core policies: access control (POL-02), incident response (POL-03), and data classification (POL-04). A separate Information Security Policy would add little for a 7-person club, so its essential rules live in **POL-02 Part A. Program governance**:

| Essential rule | Where it lives | PCI DSS v4.0.1 or law |
|---|---|---|
| Designation of the Security and Privacy Lead (Venue Manager) | POL-02 A.1 | PCI 12.1.4 |
| Annual risk assessment and targeted risk analyses | POL-02 A.2 | PCI 12.3 |
| Who may accept risk | POL-02 A.3 | Program governance |
| Sanctions | POL-02 A.4 | Program governance |
| PCI DSS scope per account and channel; evidence for every SAQ answer | POL-02 A.5 | PCI 12.5 |
| Service provider list, agreements, and yearly AOC checks | POL-02 A.6 | PCI 12.8 |
| Annual independent assessment | POL-02 A.7 | Evidence for the SAQs |
| Total price in every advertised ticket price; privacy notice matches practice | POL-02 A.8 | 16 CFR 464.2, 464.3; 15 U.S.C. 45(a) |
| Policy review, acknowledgment, and 5-year records | POL-02 A.9 | PCI 12.1 |
| Change control for website, ticketing, payment, and firewall settings | POL-02 A.10 | PCI 6.5 |
| Approved script list and weekly checks for pages that host the checkout widget | POL-02 A.11 | PCI 6.4.3, 11.6.1; SAQ A eligibility |
| Exceptions process | POL-02 A.12 | Program governance |

Traceability for these rules is in `policy-control-map.csv` under POL-02. Revisit this choice if the company grows past the Micro tier (10 or more employees) or opens a second room or venue.
