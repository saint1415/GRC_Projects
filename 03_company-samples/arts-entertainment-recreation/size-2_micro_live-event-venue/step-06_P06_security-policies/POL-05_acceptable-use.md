At the Micro tier the company keeps three core policies. The workforce and contractor use rules that an Acceptable Use Policy would hold are short enough to sit with the access rules staff already read, so they live in **POL-02 Part C. Workforce and contractor use rules**, with the AI tool rule in POL-04:

| Essential rule | Where it lives | PCI DSS v4.0.1 or law |
|---|---|---|
| Use company systems for company work; look up only the patrons you serve | POL-02 C.1 | PCI 12.2 |
| Never take a card number by phone, email, text, chat, or on paper; delete any that arrives | POL-02 C.2 and POL-04 4.2 | PCI 3.2, 3.3, 12.2 |
| No patron or card data in personal accounts, messaging apps, or public AI tools | POL-02 C.3 | PCI 12.2; 15 U.S.C. 45(n) |
| Approved AI tools only; new AI features need a P10 review | POL-04 4.7 | 15 U.S.C. 45(a), (n) |
| Lock screens; never share passwords or MFA codes | POL-02 C.4 | PCI 8.3 |
| Training at hire and yearly; season card reader briefing for contractor leads | POL-02 C.5 | PCI 12.6, 9.5 |
| Report suspected incidents at once | POL-02 C.6 and POL-03 4.2 | PCI 12.10 |
| Signed acknowledgment at hire and after each annual update | POL-02 C.7 | PCI 12.1 |
