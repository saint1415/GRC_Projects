## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce uses group systems and the agency, federal, and customer data in them.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members in every division and in corporate shared services, including subcontractor staff, on any device used for group work, and when using agency-owned systems under contract.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow these rules; complete training; report incidents |
| Managers | Make sure staff complete training and acknowledgments before access |
| Group HR | Tracks acknowledgments and training in the screening register |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Workforce members must access agency, federal, and customer records only as their job requires. Looking up a person out of curiosity, including oneself, family, neighbors, or public figures, is prohibited and can carry personal liability (for motor vehicle records, 18 U.S.C. 2724). (PL-4; AC-8)

4.2 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy and their division supplement at hire and every year. Staff with access to CJI, FTI, or CUI must also complete the program-specific training and sign the required agreements before access: the CJIS Security Addendum certification, the FTI penalty notice and certification, and CUI training. (PL-4; AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; CJISSECPOL v6.1 AT-2; Pub. 1075 Exhibit 7 II; 26 CFR 301.6103(n)-1(d))

4.3 Every workforce member must complete security awareness training at hire and every year. The group runs quarterly phishing exercises. (AT-2; PR.AT-01)

4.4 Suspected incidents must be reported to the group SOC within 1 hour (POL-03 4.1). (IR-6)

4.5 Agency and federal data may be used only on group-managed devices, approved services, or devices and systems the agency or federal customer provides. It must not be sent to personal email or stored on personal devices or personal cloud storage. (AC-19; AC-20)

4.6 Remote work is allowed only in the United States for staff with access to CJI, FTI, or CUI, with screens out of view of others and no printing at home. (PE-17; Pub. 1075 sec. 3.3.1)

4.7 **Generative AI.** Only AI tools on the group approved-tools list may be used for group work. Agency, federal, customer, or client data, source code, and credentials must never be entered into public AI tools. (PL-4; PL-4(1))

4.8 **Security claims.** Staff must not describe products or services as "certified," "compliant," or "authorized" under CJIS, FedRAMP, GovRAMP, or CMMC unless the Group General Counsel has confirmed the claim. The CJIS Security Policy sets requirements for agencies and their contractors; it has no product certification. (PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.8.
