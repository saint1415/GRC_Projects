## 1. Purpose
Set up the Cris Santos Company information security program, assign who is accountable, and give every other security policy its authority. The program protects the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the agency data the company holds and of the company's own systems, and keeps the company inside the security terms that allow it to hold criminal justice information (CJI) and federal tax information (FTI).

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (owners, employees, contract developers, and interns), wherever they work. Covers the Agency Case Management Platform (ACMP), every system and service that administers it, and every vendor that stores, processes, or transmits agency data for the company. It applies to all agency data and all company information.

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 The company must maintain an information security program that meets the SP 800-53 Rev. 5 Moderate baseline required by its agency contracts, documented in this policy set and the System Security Plan. Where the CJIS Security Policy or IRS Publication 1075 sets a stricter value, the stricter value applies. (PM-1; PL-2; GV.PO-01)
4.2 The IT Manager is the designated Information Security Officer. The designation must be in writing and named in each agency contract. (PM-2; GV.RR-02)
4.3 A risk assessment must be performed at least annually and after major changes, using NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1. Risks must be tracked in the risk register with an owner and treatment. (RA-3; PM-9; GV.RM-01)
4.4 Risk acceptance authority: the IT Manager may accept Low risks; the Chief Operating Officer, Moderate; the Chief Executive Officer, High and Very High. **No one may accept a risk that breaches a CJIS Security Addendum or Pub. 1075 Exhibit 7 term;** it must be treated or the regulated data removed. (PM-9; GV.RM-01)
4.5 **Designated positions.** HR must keep a list of positions whose duties give access to CJI or FTI. No one in a designated position may receive that access until they have: (a) the screening each agency requires (a national fingerprint-based record check for CJI; the Pub. 1075 background investigation for FTI); (b) the required training; and (c) signed the CJIS Security Addendum certification (CJI) or the FTI confidentiality statement and written penalty notice (FTI). Rescreening follows each agency's schedule, and Pub. 1075 reinvestigation is required within 5 years. (PS-2; PS-3; PS-6; GV.RR-04)
4.6 Security policies must be reviewed at least annually, after major changes or incidents, and within 60 days of each new CJIS Security Policy version. (PL-1; GV.PO-02)
4.7 Exceptions to any security policy must be requested in writing, risk-rated, approved under 4.4, recorded in the risk register, and time-limited to 12 months or less. Exceptions may not waive agency contract terms. (PL-1)
4.8 **Sanctions.** Workforce members who fail to comply with security policies are sanctioned in proportion to intent and harm: retraining, written warning, suspension of access, or termination. Misuse of CJI or FTI is also reported to the affected agency. HR documents each sanction. (PS-8; GV.RR-04)
4.9 **No approval, no agency data.** Before any vendor stores, processes, or transmits agency data, the Contracts and Compliance Manager must complete a security review and put security terms in the contract. A vendor may receive FTI only after the IRS approves it through the agency's notification (Pub. 1075 Exhibit 7 I(8)), and CJI only under terms the CJIS Security Addendum allows. (SA-9; SR-6; GV.SC-05; GV.SC-07)
4.10 Security controls must be assessed at least annually (P07) so that all controls are covered every 3 years, and after major changes. (CA-2)
4.11 Security documentation, assessments, and audit records must be retained for 7 years, which meets the longest agency requirement (Pub. 1075 AU-11). (SI-12; AU-11)
4.12 The Contracts and Compliance Manager must keep a register of every agency security term (notice clocks, screening, locations, encryption, data return) and report open gaps to the Chief Operating Officer quarterly. (GV.OC-03)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under section 4.8. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), the quarterly access reviews (POL-02), and the quarterly contract-terms report (4.12).

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow section 4.7. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the right authority, and expire within 12 months.
