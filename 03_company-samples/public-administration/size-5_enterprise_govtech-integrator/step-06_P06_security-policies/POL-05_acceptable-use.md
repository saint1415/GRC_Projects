## 1. Purpose
Set the rules every workforce member follows when using company systems and handling agency data.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (employees, contractors, and subcontractor staff) in every state and delivery center, including AQ-1 staff from the acquisition date. Covers all company systems, the hosted agency environments (ACMC, IES, legacy hosting, AQ-1), the CUI enclave, and all agency data the company receives, wherever it is stored or processed.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Chief Human Resources Officer | Owns this policy; runs training and attestations |
| Managers | Make sure their staff follow it |
| All workforce | Follow it and attest annually |

## 4. Policy statements
Each statement is testable and tagged with its SP 800-53 control(s) and CSF 2.0 subcategory. `policy-control-map.csv` traces each statement to its regulatory driver and shows whether Internal Audit tested it in 2026 (P07).

4.1 Every workforce member must accept the rules of behavior at hire and annually. (PL-4; PR.AT-01)
4.2 Every workforce member must complete security awareness training at hire and annually; staff with CJI or FTI access must complete role training before access and annually, and access is suspended 7 days after a recertification is overdue. (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02)
4.3 Agency data may be handled only on company-managed devices, or on agency-furnished devices under agency rules. Personal devices must not be used for agency data or administration. (AC-20; AC-19; PR.AA-05)
4.4 Only AI tools on the approved list (STD-05.3) may be used for company work; agency data, source code, and credentials must never be entered in public AI tools. (PL-4; SA-9; PR.DS-01)
4.5 Staff who work remotely with FTI or CJI must use a private workspace, must not print, and must attest annually to the alternate work site rules. (PE-17; PR.AA-06)
4.6 Every workforce member must report a suspected incident within 1 hour (POL-03 4.2). (IR-6; RS.MA-02)
4.7 Agency data must not be attached to tickets or sent to vendors except through the approved secure channel; screenshots containing FTI or CJI must never go to the ticketing system. (AC-21; PR.DS-01)
4.8 Company systems are monitored; users have no expectation of privacy in them, as the sign-in banner states. (AC-8; PL-4; PR.AT-01)

## 5. Standards and procedures under this policy
Standards set measurable requirements (approved by the CISO); procedures give step-by-step instructions (approved by the owning director). Both sit below this policy in the hierarchy and cannot contradict it.
- STD-05.1 Security Awareness and Training Standard
- STD-05.2 Devices and Alternate Work Site Standard
- STD-05.3 Approved AI Tools List

## 6. Compliance and enforcement
Compliance is monitored through continuous control monitoring, the annual Internal Audit assessment (P07), access certifications, and agency audits (CJIS audits, IRS safeguard reviews). Violations are handled under the sanctions procedure (PRC-01.1, POL-01 statement 4.7), from retraining to termination of employment or contract, in proportion to intent and harm.

## 7. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 7 and PRC-01.2: requested in the GRC platform, risk-rated with the P01 method, approved at the authority level for the residual risk, recorded in the exception register with compensating controls, and limited to 12 months. No exception may be granted against a CJIS Security Addendum, Pub. 1075 Exhibit 7, or business associate agreement term, or a statute.
