## 1. Purpose
Set the rules every workforce member follows when using company systems, agency systems, and agency data.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members, on any device, wherever they work, including at agency sites.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Director | Owns this policy; collects acknowledgments at hire and each year |
| Managers | Make sure their staff follow it |
| All workforce | Read, acknowledge, and follow it |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy at hire and each year. Staff in designated positions also sign the CJIS Security Addendum certification or the FTI penalty notice that applies to them. (PL-4; GV.RR-04)
4.2 Agency data and agency systems may be reached only from company-managed devices. Personal devices and personal accounts must never hold agency data. (AC-20)
4.3 Agency data must not be sent by email, chat, or personal storage. Use the secure transfer tool or in-platform secure support. (AC-21)
4.4 Report phishing, lost devices, and anything that looks like an incident within 1 hour (POL-03 4.2). (IR-6)
4.5 **Remote work.** Work from home or another alternate site only on company laptops, in a place where screens cannot be seen by others, with the laptop locked when unattended. Staff with FTI access complete the alternate work site training and annual attestation required by Pub. 1075 section 2.B.7. Regulated data may not be reached from outside the United States. (PE-17; AC-17)
4.6 **Look only when your work needs it.** Opening, searching, or exporting an agency record without a work reason is prohibited, even if your role allows it. All access to regulated records is logged and reviewed. Unauthorized inspection of FTI, misuse of CJI, and obtaining motor vehicle record information for a use not permitted can carry criminal and civil penalties (IRC 7213A; 18 U.S.C. 2722 and 2724). (AU-6; PS-8)
4.7 Do not install software that is not approved; mobile device management blocks it. (CM-11)
4.8 Never share passwords, security keys, or tokens, and never put secrets in code, wikis, tickets, or chat. (IA-5)
4.9 **AI tools.** Use only AI tools on the approved list (P10), and only for the uses approved. Never enter agency data, source code secrets, or credentials into an AI tool that is not approved for them. (PL-4)
4.10 Complete annual security awareness training, and role training when assigned, by the due date; access to regulated data is suspended when required training expires. (AT-2)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 statement 4.8. Compliance is checked through acknowledgments, access analytics, and the annual control assessment (P07).

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 statement 4.7. Statements 4.2, 4.5 (outside the United States), and 4.6 cannot be excepted.
