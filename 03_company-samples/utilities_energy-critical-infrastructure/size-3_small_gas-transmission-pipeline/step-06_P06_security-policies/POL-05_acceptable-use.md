## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how employees and contractors use company systems, devices, and information, with extra rules for the control rooms and field sites.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company employees, contractors, and suppliers with access to company systems, at HQ and the Gas Control Center, Compressor Station 1, the field offices, and all field sites. It covers business IT, OT (SCADA, field devices, telecommunications, and the IT/OT DMZ), the cloud tenant, and SaaS services, including systems that suppliers operate for the company.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Manager | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and annually; tracks training |
| IT Manager | Awareness content, reminders, phishing exercises, and the approved AI tools list |
| Gas Control Manager | Enforces control room rules; adds cyber content to controller training |
| All personnel | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use of business IT is allowed if it creates no risk. **No personal use of any kind is allowed on HMI consoles, SCADA servers, or the engineering workstation.** (PL-4)
4.2 Everyone must sign this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4(1))
4.3 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year. The IT Manager runs at least quarterly phishing exercises. The SCADA Engineer and controllers also receive role-based OT security training each year. (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02)
4.4 **Control room and OT devices:**
- Do not browse the internet, read email, or plug in phones on HMIs or the engineering workstation.
- Do not connect any USB storage device to an OT device unless it has been scanned at the media station that day.
- Supplier laptops must never be connected to the OT network.

(MP-7; CM-11; PR.PS-05)
4.5 Do not install software, browser extensions, or remote access tools unless IT approves them. (CM-11)
4.6 Do not open unexpected attachments or links. Report suspicious messages with the Report Phishing button. (AT-2)
4.7 Report lost or stolen devices, strange system behavior, unexpected SCADA values, and anything suspicious immediately (POL-03 4.2).
4.8 **Generative AI:**
- Use only tools on the approved list.
- Never paste or upload Restricted information into any AI tool, including SCADA configurations, network diagrams, passwords, and security findings.
- The leak-detection model is an advisory tool for controllers only. It is governed by P10.

(PL-4)
4.9 System use notices shown at sign-in on the jump host and business systems apply to everyone. Use of company systems may be monitored. (AC-8)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations may lead to retraining, written warning, loss of access, or termination, depending on intent and impact. For contractors, violations may lead to removal of access and contract action. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07) and training records.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.6. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the majority owner for High risk), and expire within 12 months.
