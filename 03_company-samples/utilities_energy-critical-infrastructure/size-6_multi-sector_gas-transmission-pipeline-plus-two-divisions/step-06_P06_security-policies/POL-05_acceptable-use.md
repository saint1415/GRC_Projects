## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce uses group systems and information, in offices, control rooms, and the field.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members in every division and in corporate shared services, on any device used for group work, including field tablets, assessment toolkits, and personal phones used for authentication.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow these rules; complete training; report incidents |
| Managers and supervisors | Make sure staff complete training and acknowledgments |
| Group HR | Tracks acknowledgments and training completion |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy and their division supplement at hire and every year. (PL-4; PR.AT-01)

4.2 Every workforce member must complete security awareness training at hire and every year, including phishing and insider threat. The group runs quarterly phishing exercises. (AT-2; AT-2(2); PR.AT-01; SD 02G III.D.1.a)

4.3 Staff in OT roles (controllers, SCADA and station engineers, field technicians, OT assessors) must complete role-based OT security training every year. Controllers' training must include cyber-caused abnormal operating conditions. (AT-3; PR.AT-02; 192.631(h)(1))

4.4 Suspected incidents must be reported to the group SOC within 1 hour (POL-03 4.1).

4.5 Only group-managed devices may connect to OT networks. Personal devices, personal storage media, and unscanned removable media must not be connected to SCADA hosts, consoles, station equipment, or field devices. (AC-19; MP-7)

4.6 Restricted information may be used only on group-managed devices and approved services. It must not be sent to personal email or stored on personal devices. (AC-20; PL-4)

4.7 SSI must be handled under POL-04 4.2. Workforce members must not discuss SSI with anyone who does not have a need to know, including in public places and on social media. (PL-4; 1520.9)

4.8 **Assessment toolkits** must be kept with the assessor or locked away, carried with full-disk encryption on, and returned for evidence transfer and wiping at engagement close. (MP-7; PL-4)

4.9 **Generative AI.** Only AI tools on the group approved-tools list may be used for group work. SSI, OT configurations, and client data must never be entered into public AI tools. AI output used in a client deliverable or an operational decision must be reviewed by a qualified person. (PL-4; PL-4(1))

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
