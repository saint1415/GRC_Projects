## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how employees, contractors, and authorized representatives may use company systems, OT, data, devices, removable media, and AI tools.

## 2. Scope
Everyone given access to company systems or data, at all sites and when working remotely, including supplier technicians working at control centers and compressor stations.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Director | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and annually |
| Security Manager | Awareness training, monthly reminders, quarterly phishing simulations, and the approved AI tools list |
| Director of Gas Control | Controller training content under 192.631(h), including cyber-caused abnormal operating conditions |
| Compressor Station Supervisors | Enforce scan-before-connect for all laptops and media at stations |
| All personnel | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use of business IT is allowed if it creates no risk. OT systems are for operations only: no email, web browsing, or personal use on HMIs, engineering workstations, or station computers. (PL-4)
4.2 Every person must acknowledge this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4(1))
4.3 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year, with monthly reminders and quarterly phishing simulations. Role-based training is required for controllers, shift supervisors, SCADA engineers, OT Security Engineers, compressor station technicians (from 2027), finance staff, and anyone with SSI access. Staff responsible for implementing a TSA directive must be briefed on it. (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02; SD 01G III.A.3)
4.4 Lock business IT screens when you step away. HMIs in staffed control rooms are exempt so controllers never lose the alarm display. (AC-11)
4.5 Do not install software or connect USB storage, laptops, or other devices to any OT system unless the media or device has passed the scanning kiosk that day and the owner of the system approves. Supplier laptops connect only through the remote access gateway or, on site, after a scan and with a company escort. (CM-11; MP-7; AC-20; PR.PS-05; SD 02G III.D.1.d)
4.6 Only company-managed devices may access company systems. Field tablets must stay encrypted and enrolled in device management. (AC-19)
4.7 Report lost or stolen devices, suspicious messages, unexpected HMI behavior, unknown devices or cables at a site, and anything unusual immediately (POL-03 4.2).
4.8 **AI tools.** Use only AI tools on the approved list (STD-05). Never paste, upload, or dictate SSI, Restricted information, or employee personal information into a tool that is not approved. Outputs that inform operations, maintenance, patrols, or regulatory filings must be reviewed by a qualified person before use. The leak-detection model is advisory: a controller never ignores a SCADA alarm because the model shows no anomaly. (PL-4)
4.9 Do not photograph control room screens, station panels, or network equipment except for approved work, and never post such images. (PL-4; MP-3)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations may lead to retraining, written warning, loss of access, or termination, depending on intent and impact. For contractors, violations may lead to removal from site and contract action. Compliance is checked through the control assessment (P07), kiosk logs, and phishing results.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7.
