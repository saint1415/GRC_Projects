## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the group's own records before any assessment work began on 2026-05-04. It covers the legal entities, the systems that hold customer, worker, client, cardholder and payroll data in each division and in corporate shared services, the suppliers and affiliates that touch them, and the rules that may bind each entity. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Items are grouped by division: the title of each row starts with Group, Cloud Software, Technology Consulting or Payments and Payroll. Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (interviews, document reviews, scans, tests) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analyses (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Entities and scale.** The holding company is an SEC registrant; the three divisions are separate legal subsidiaries, and corporate shared services sit in the parent. No subsidiary holds a bank charter, an insurance license or a New York license (EV-001). The workforce is 45,000 in 44 states, none outside the United States: Cloud Software about 14,000, Technology Consulting about 16,000 (about 2,600 from the 2025 acquisition), Payments and Payroll about 9,000, corporate about 6,000 (EV-002). FY2025 net revenue was about $18.0 billion: Cloud Software $9.0 billion, Technology Consulting $5.4 billion, Payments and Payroll $3.6 billion (EV-003). The Form 10-K Item 106 disclosure describes board risk committee oversight, and the disclosure committee charter covers Form 8-K Item 1.05 determinations (EV-004, EV-005).

**Group identity and endpoints.** Every SYS-G1 workforce identity uses single sign-on with MFA; administrators use phishing-resistant hardware keys; the about 2,600 acquired-firm consultants sign in through the acquired firm's own identity provider (EV-006). Identity governance runs joiner-mover-leaver workflows from HR events and disables accounts inactive for 35 days; cloud service identities and their keys are outside its scope (EV-007). The Q1 2026 certification covered group and division applications and privileged roles, but not implementation-partner roles in customer tenants, service identities or acquired-firm accounts (EV-008). Privileged access is just in time through PAM with recording; WCP accounts have no standing administrators (EV-009). Group laptops are encrypted and compliance-checked; the acquired firm's are not enrolled (EV-010).

**Group security operations.** EDR covers group endpoints, servers, cloud hosts and container nodes, but not the about 2,600 acquired-firm laptops, which run legacy antivirus (EV-011). The 24x7 SOC correlates cloud, identity, application and CDE logs; behavior analytics cover workforce accounts only; there are no object-level read events for the WCP export bucket and no rule for key use from a new network; the acquired firm's network is not a SIEM source (EV-012). Technical playbooks were exercised each quarter, with no cross-division notification exercise recorded (EV-013). The group notification matrix does not list the FTC Safeguards notice, bank-designated contacts, the 48-hour customer terms, hospital BAA terms, the sponsor banks' 24-hour notice or FAR 52.204-25 reporting (EV-014). Critical vulnerabilities carry a 15-day target, met for 96% in 2026 to date (EV-015); the 2026 penetration test covered tenant isolation, and a public bug bounty runs for Workforce Cloud (EV-016).

**Policies and common controls.** The 2025 group policies are aligned to NIST CSF 2.0; the library has no AI standard; the Technology Consulting supplement is v2023 with no review date, and the acquired firm follows its own 2024 handbook (EV-017). The common control catalog lists 142 controls (110 full, 32 hybrid); inheritance is documented for Cloud Software (SOC 2 description) and Payments and Payroll (PCI DSS responsibility matrix and SOC 1 description), and no Technology Consulting matrix was provided (EV-018).

**Group cloud platform.** Provider A hosts the corporate landing zone, the WCP and its export bucket, and the group data platform; provider B hosts the payroll engine, the CDE in separate accounts and the immutable backup vault (EV-019). 1,140 static cloud access keys exist across group accounts, 212 older than 1 year, with no rotation schedule for service keys; customer-managed keys protect tenant databases, the payroll engine and the CDE, while the export bucket uses provider-managed keys (EV-020). Guardrails enforce encryption, logging and no public storage (EV-021); the hub denies traffic between division accounts by default, but reads through public storage endpoints do not pass the hub (EV-022). Backups are immutable in provider B, and WCP restores were tested twice in 12 months (EV-023). External endpoints accept TLS 1.2 or higher (EV-024). Builds are signed and only the pipeline deploys; secret scanning covers group-hosted repositories only (EV-025). Logs go to a write-once archive kept 1 year searchable and 6 years archived (EV-026).

**Workforce, vendors, AI, risk and legal.** Awareness training and quarterly phishing simulations cover the workforce; secure coding training for about 3,900 Cloud Software engineers shows 97% completion (EV-027). Vendor screening records location but not ownership by covered persons; exit plans exist for the two cloud providers only; affiliates are not in the vendor register (EV-029). AI in use: the workforce assistant (released 2026-03-02), attrition-risk insights, the schedule optimizer, two payments models, the consulting delivery assistant, a coding assistant and an enterprise assistant pilot (3,000 users); a resume screening add-on is in procurement for a 2026-05 pilot (EV-031). The group processed personal information of more than 250,000 California consumers as a business in 2025, and no cybersecurity auditor has been engaged (EV-036). No intercompany agreement covers the payroll handoff or the duties of consultants working in customer tenants; a 2024 agreement covers the embedded checkout (EV-037). Group legal treats Payments and Payroll as a financial institution under 16 CFR 314.2(h) and Cloud Software as a bank service provider; the money transmission review started in 2025 and is open (EV-038).

**Cloud Software.** About 38,000 customers, about 420 of them banks and credit unions. The standard DPA gives 72-hour incident notice (48 hours for about 1,240 customers), 30 days' sub-processor notice, deletion within 90 days, and use only to provide the service; about 3,400 pre-2023 DPAs do not mention aggregated or de-identified data use; the MSA promises 99.95% availability (EV-039). 256 of about 420 bank customers have given a bank-designated contact (EV-040). About 21 million worker profiles, about 1.6 million in Florida and 2.3 million in California; about 14 million monthly active app users; attrition-risk insights is enabled for about 9,800 customers (EV-041). The SOC 2 Type 2 report (period ending 2025-09-30) covers Security, Availability and Confidentiality, and its system description says customer exports are kept 30 days (EV-042). Each night the export service writes signed handoff files (names, Social Security numbers, bank details, gross pay) to the export bucket, which also holds customer exports; the export role and 37 consulting toolkit keys can read every tenant prefix; there is no lifecycle rule and the oldest handoff objects are from 2025-08; data-plane read events are off (EV-044). The export bucket contents are not classified and no owner is recorded for the handoff files; the data use catalog lists aggregated tenant data as a tuning input (EV-045). About 3,800 consultants hold implementation-partner roles with no end date (EV-046). The workforce assistant was released after design review with no privacy impact assessment attached (EV-047); attrition-risk insights has no bias test results on file (EV-048). The model provider's sub-processor notice went out on 2026-01-30 and its assurance review was completed in 2026-04 (EV-049). The region failover is tested yearly and restores twice a year; 12-month availability was 99.97% (EV-050). Customer administrators must use MFA; customer API tokens are long-lived (EV-051). Terminated customers' data is deleted by a manual monthly job (EV-052).

**Technology Consulting.** About 1,900 clients; 14 federal civilian agency contracts contain FAR 52.204-21, none with CUI or DFARS clauses; BAAs with all 31 hospital clients (EV-054). 9 of 22 subcontracts under the federal contracts do not include the 52.204-21(c) flowdown (EV-055). Client data copies in the collaboration workspace have no retention rule, and federal projects share the workspace with commercial ones (EV-056). The migration toolkit uses 37 static keys with read access to the WCP export bucket for every tenant (EV-057). The acquired firm's about 2,600 consultants use their own identity provider, legacy antivirus and a VPN without device checks; leaver notices arrive by email; migration is due 2027-03-31 (EV-058). The client access gateway serves about 160 managed-service clients with MFA and recording (EV-059). For 9 of the 31 hospital clients, patient-level ADT data passes through test environments (EV-060). The delivery assistant opt-in list is kept by delivery leads and not enforced technically (EV-061).

**Payments and Payroll.** Embedded payroll for about 11,500 employers and 2.4 million workers (about 190,000 in Florida) and acceptance for about 26,000 merchants, through sponsor banks A and B, whose agreements require notice within 24 hours (EV-063). The division CISO is the Qualified Individual; the 2025 board report does not mention the WCP handoff (EV-064). Neither the 2025 written risk assessment nor the division data inventory lists the handoff files held in the WCP (EV-065), and the WCP is not listed as a service provider or TPSP (EV-066). CDE scope was confirmed in 2026-01, a network change followed in 2026-03, and the last segmentation test is from 2025 H2; script inventory and tamper detection cover 3 of 5 checkout pages (EV-067). A SOC 1 Type 2 report covers the payroll service (EV-068). MFA is optional for workers viewing pay statements, and some employers receive reports by unencrypted email (EV-069). The payroll engine contingency plan v2025 is on file (EV-070). The 2026 Report on Compliance, validated on 2026-05-29, arrived after the intake window (EV-073).
