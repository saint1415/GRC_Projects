## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce uses group systems and information.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members in every division and in corporate shared services, on any device used for group work.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow these rules; complete training; report incidents |
| Managers | Make sure staff complete training and acknowledgments |
| Group HR | Tracks acknowledgments and training completion |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Workforce members must access customer, worker, client, merchant, and payroll information only as needed for their job. Browsing a tenant or a worker's records out of curiosity is prohibited. (PL-4; AC-8)

4.2 Only group-managed devices may access Restricted or Confidential data. Personal devices may use email and chat through the managed app only. (AC-19; AC-20)

4.3 **Source code and scripts** that touch group or customer systems must be kept only in group-hosted repositories. Personal repositories, public code sites, and paste sites must never hold group code, configuration, or credentials. (PL-4; AC-20)

4.4 Client systems may be used only through the client access gateway or client-provided accounts, and only for the engagement. (AC-20; PL-4(1))

4.5 Workforce members must complete security awareness training at hire and every year, plus role-based training (secure coding, payroll fraud, tenant data handling, incident duties). (AT-2; AT-3; 16 CFR 314.4(e)(1))

4.6 Social media must not be used to discuss customers, clients, incidents, or security controls. (PL-4(1))

4.7 **Generative AI.** Only tools on the group approved list may be used with Internal or higher data. Restricted data (Social Security numbers, bank details, cardholder data, PHI, federal contract information, credentials) must never be entered into any AI tool. Customer or client Confidential data may be entered only into a tool approved for that use and, for consulting, only where the client has opted in. The delivery assistant must not be used on federal engagements unless the contracting officer has approved it in writing. (PL-4; PT-4; 48 CFR 52.204-21(b)(1)(iv))

4.8 AI output used in customer deliverables, product content, or decisions about people must be reviewed by a person who is accountable for it. (PL-4)

4.9 Requests to change payroll, bank, or payment settings that arrive by email, chat, or phone must be verified by calling back a number already on file before acting. (AT-2(3))

4.10 Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the SOC within 1 hour. (IR-6)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.7. Acknowledgments are collected at hire and every year.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.10.
