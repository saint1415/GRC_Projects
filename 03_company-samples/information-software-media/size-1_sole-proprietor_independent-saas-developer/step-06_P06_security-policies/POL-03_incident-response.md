**Where the incident response rules are:**

- POL-01 section 10 (Incident response): runbook, matrix, contact list, and templates kept outside company systems (10.1), same-day incident log with discovery and confirmation times (10.2), subscriber and Florida notice deadlines (10.3), ransom and extortion decisions (10.4), yearly walkthrough and lessons learned (10.5).
- The procedure itself is the P08 runbook (`../step-08_P08_incident-response-runbook/ir-runbook.md`) and its notification matrix.
- SP 800-53 IR-1 is met by POL-01 as a whole.

Traceability for every statement is in `policy-control-map.csv`.
