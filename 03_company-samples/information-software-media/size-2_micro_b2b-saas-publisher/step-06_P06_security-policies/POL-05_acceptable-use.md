At the Micro tier the company keeps three core policies. The workforce use rules that an Acceptable Use Policy would hold are short enough to sit with the access rules everyone already reads, so they live in **POL-02 Part C. Workforce use rules**, with the AI tool rule in POL-04:

| Essential rule | Where it lives | Driver |
|---|---|---|
| Use company systems for company work; look at customer data only for your job | POL-02 C.1 | Customer DPA (use limits) |
| No customer data, credentials, or secrets in personal accounts, public repositories, or unapproved AI tools | POL-02 C.2 | N51-R01; Customer DPA |
| Approved AI tools list | POL-04 4.7 | N51-R01; Customer DPA |
| Never copy production data to a laptop | POL-02 C.3 and POL-04 4.8 | N51-R01 (FTC guidance: don't use personal information when it's not necessary) |
| Company work only on MSP-managed company laptops | POL-02 B.10 | N51-R01 (FTC guidance: ensure endpoint security) |
| Lock laptops; never share passwords, MFA codes, or keys | POL-02 C.4 | SOC 2 CC6.1 |
| Awareness training yearly; secure coding training for developers | POL-02 C.5 | N51-R01 (FTC guidance: train your engineers in secure coding); SOC 2 CC1.4 |
| Report suspected incidents at once | POL-02 C.6 and POL-03 4.2 | Customer DPA (72-hour notice) |
| Signed acknowledgment at hire and after each annual update | POL-02 C.7 | SOC 2 CC1.1 |

Traceability for these rules is in `policy-control-map.csv` under POL-02 and POL-04.
