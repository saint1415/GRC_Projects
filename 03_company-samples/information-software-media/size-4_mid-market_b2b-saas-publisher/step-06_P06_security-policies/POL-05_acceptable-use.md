## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how employees and contractors use company devices, accounts, data, and AI tools.

## 2. Scope
All employees and contractors, on company and personal devices that access company systems.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Director of IT | Maintains this policy and the approved tools list |
| VP People | Collects acknowledgments at hire and each year |
| Managers | Make sure their teams follow it |
| All workforce | Read, acknowledge, and follow it |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems and data are for company business. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not create risk or break any law. (PL-4)
4.2 Use only company-managed laptops for company work. Personal phones may be used only through the managed email and chat apps. Contractors use company virtual desktops. (AC-20; PR.PS-05)
4.3 Never share passwords, security keys, or MFA codes. Use the company password manager. (PL-4; IA-5)
4.4 Do not disable or bypass EDR, device management, encryption, or other security tools. (SI-3)
4.5 Customer data may be accessed only to do your job and only through approved paths (POL-02 4.4 and 4.8). Do not download customer data to laptops, and do not paste it into email, chat, tickets, or documents outside the platform. (PL-4; 164.310(b))
4.6 Do not post company or customer information on social media or public forums, including code snippets that contain secrets or customer data. (PL-4(1))
4.7 Report phishing, lost devices, exposed credentials, or anything suspicious immediately (POL-03 4.2). (AT-2)
4.8 **Approved AI tools.** Use only AI tools on the approved tools list kept by the Director of IT: today the enterprise AI coding assistant (AI-004) and the meeting and call summarizer (AI-005). Do not enter customer data, PHI, credentials, or secrets into any AI tool unless the approved list explicitly allows that data type for that tool. Review AI output before you rely on it or send it. (PL-4; STD-05)
4.9 **Recording calls.** Before a call is recorded or summarized by AI, tell every participant and get their consent at the start of the call; stop recording if anyone objects. Florida law makes interception lawful only when all parties have given prior consent (Fla. Stat. 934.03(2)(d)), and the company applies the same rule to every call. (PL-4)
4.10 Sign the confidentiality agreement at hire, and return all company equipment on your last day. (PS-6)
4.11 Complete security awareness training at hire and each year, and role training if assigned (engineers, support agents, machine learning team). (AT-2; AT-3)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.8. Device compliance is monitored through device management; acknowledgments are tracked by the VP People.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7.
