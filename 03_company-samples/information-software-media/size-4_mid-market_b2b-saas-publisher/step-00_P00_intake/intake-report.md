## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the company's own records before any assessment work began on 2026-07-13. It covers the organization, the Customer Engagement Platform and the services around it, the sub-processors and other suppliers that touch customer data, and the rules that may bind the company. At this size the sources are systems of record: the identity provider and just-in-time access tool, the HR information system, the cloud organization console, source hosting and CI/CD, the device management and EDR consoles, the SIEM and the MDR provider's reports, the scanning service, the bug bounty platform, the GRC tool, the Legal contract database (DPAs, BAAs, sub-processor list), the ERP accounts payable vendor master, the CRM and billing system, the SOC 2 report and the incident queue. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (interviews, samples, tests) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analysis (P03), the control assessment (P07) and the SOC 2 readiness review (P09).

## 3. Observations by area
**Users and access.** The identity provider holds active named accounts for all 600 employees on the HR roster, plus the 18 contractors and service accounts. Every active user account has MFA registered, and the 190 engineers with production or cloud administrator access use security keys (EV-001). Conditional access requires MFA and a device posture check for every sign-in; passwords are 14 characters with a banned list, and accounts are disabled after 45 days of inactivity (EV-002). HR recorded 96 terminations and 58 internal transfers in the 12 months to 2026-06-30 (EV-003). The HR feed disables federated access within 1 hour of the termination date entered in the HR system. A transfer adds new groups without removing prior roles, and local database, search cluster and source hosting accounts are outside the feed (EV-004). Review campaigns ran each quarter, with no record for the data analytics account roles in Q3 2025 (EV-005). There are 210 privileged accounts. Production and healthcare cell access goes through the just-in-time tool, with 312 healthcare cell sessions in Q2 2026, and 14 standing administrator roles remain in the data analytics and legacy accounts (EV-006). Root credentials for the 10 core accounts are sealed; 7 use hardware keys and 3 use software MFA, and the break-glass procedure was last tested in 2025-11 (EV-007). Nine long-lived cloud access keys remain, 4 older than 1 year, and the attachments bucket policy lets the warehouse loader key read every Standard and Enterprise tenant's attachments (EV-008). On the Standard plan, support view opens a tenant with a ticket number. Sessions are logged (about 4,100 in 90 days), and no session review record was provided (EV-009).

**Platform and engineering.** The landing zone has 10 core accounts and 22 sandboxes, with guardrails against public storage, unencrypted volumes, non-U.S. regions and snapshot sharing to staging. Healthcare production has no route to production or development, and egress passes the hub firewall's allow lists, plus the loader's egress to cloud provider B (EV-010). About 90% of production is defined in infrastructure code (EV-011). Pull request review is required on main branches, images are signed and verified, and an emergency path deploys without review (EV-012). The deployment log shows about 7,800 standard and 61 emergency changes from January to June 2026 (EV-013). The 9 CEP data stores holding customer data are encrypted with company-managed keys, with separate keys for the healthcare cell (EV-014). Scanning does not block deployment, and critical image findings took a median 41 days to fix against a 15-day standard (EV-015). The March 2026 penetration test left one High finding, server-side request forgery in the webhook feature, open in the tracker (EV-016). The bug bounty program has received 47 reports since 2024. A 2026-05-19 report showed attachment links valid for 7 days outside the tenant session, and the fix of 2026-06-10 cut expiry to 15 minutes (EV-017). Tenant ID is checked in the application's data access layer. All tenants share database shards and one search cluster credential, and there is no automated cross-tenant test suite (EV-018).

**Data, backups and recovery.** Point-in-time recovery covers 7 days, cross-region replica lag is under 1 minute, and daily snapshots are kept 35 days in the DR account. Those copies are not write-once, and production administrator roles can delete them (EV-019). The February 2026 regional failover took 9 hours against the 4-hour objective. Search indexes are not replicated (about 20 hours to rebuild), and only 3 engineers have run the failover (EV-020). The request for contingency plans returned in-region runbooks and a draft regional failover runbook, with no approved regional DR plan and no emergency mode procedure for the healthcare cell (EV-021). The 2026-03 data map does not show the warehouse copy or personal data in logs (EV-022). Ticket subject lines, tags and requester domains from all tenants, including the healthcare cell, load nightly to the warehouse on cloud provider B, where 140 employees can query them (EV-023). The deletion job removes database rows at 30 days but not attachments or search indexes, and 61 tenants terminated in the last 12 months still have attachments (EV-024). The platform holds about 92 million end-consumer records, about 11 million of them California residents and 6 million Florida residents, and about 1.3 million patients' records in the healthcare cell (EV-025). Usage runs at about 1.9 million tickets and 650,000 chats a day and 140,000 API calls a minute at peak (EV-026).

**Monitoring and endpoints.** Control-plane logs from every account are kept 1 year in a write-once archive. Object-level logging for the attachments bucket and database audit logging are off, and application logs are kept 30 days (EV-027). The SIEM receives cloud, identity provider and EDR events. Its cloud detections are mostly vendor defaults, with no rule for bulk object reads or snapshot sharing (EV-028). The MDR provider monitors endpoints and the identity provider 24x7; cloud workloads are outside its scope (EV-029). All 640 laptops have full-disk encryption and a reporting EDR agent (EV-030).

**Programs and documents.** Five policies first adopted in 2023 and 4 standards are in the GRC tool. There is no configuration, logging, vendor risk, AI, contingency, retention or acquisition due diligence standard (EV-031). No risk analysis specific to ePHI in the healthcare cell is recorded (EV-032). The 2025 incident response plan does not include the bank 4-hour notice, a business associate path or the 24-hour Enterprise terms, and the November 2025 tabletop recorded 5 actions (EV-033). The incident queue holds 44 incidents for 2025 and 2026, with discovery and confirmation times (EV-034). Awareness training completion was 98% for 2025, secure coding 91%, and the June 2026 phishing click rate 6.1% (EV-035). Three sanctions cases are recorded for 2025-2026 (EV-036). The Director of Security was named HIPAA Security Official in 2025-11 (EV-037). The SOC 2 Type 2 report for 2025-04-01 to 2026-03-31 (Security and Availability) has an unmodified opinion and 4 exceptions: late removal for 3 of 40 terminated employees, 2 of 25 emergency changes without approval, one missed quarterly review of the data analytics account, and a DR test that missed the 4-hour objective. A bridging Type 2 covers 2026-04-01 to 2026-12-31 (EV-038). The audit committee received quarterly cyber risk reports in 2025-12, 2026-03 and 2026-06 (EV-039).

**Suppliers and contracts.** Accounts payable shows payments to the 14 sub-processors, the engineering contractor, corporate SaaS vendors and 2 AI tool vendors (EV-040). DPAs are on file for all 14 sub-processors, and 6 of them receive healthcare cell data (EV-041). BAAs are signed with all 46 healthcare customers. Subcontractor BAAs are on file for 4 of the 6, with none located for the SMS provider or the error-tracking SaaS (EV-042). Annual SOC 2 review was done in the last 12 months for 9 of 14 sub-processors (EV-043). The MSA, DPA and BAA set availability credits, incident notice within 48 hours (24 hours for 38 Enterprise customers), 10 business days under the BAAs, 30-day sub-processor notice, deletion within 30 days, no AI training and U.S. hosting. The contracts with the 9 banks mirror the 12 CFR 53.4 notice (EV-044). The company has about 2,400 customers and 88,000 agent users, and a security contact is on file for about 71% of customers (EV-045). The AI model provider's standard endpoint keeps prompts 30 days; the zero-retention endpoint under a BAA serves the healthcare cell (EV-046). The 18 contractors in Poland reach source code through company virtual desktops, with no production access and no DOJ Data Security Program terms (EV-047). The cyber insurance policy has a $15 million limit and a $500,000 retention, with notice through the carrier hotline first (EV-048). Cloud provider A's SOC 2 report covers the 12 months ending 2026-03-31 (EV-049).

**Facilities and media.** Offices use badge access and a visitor log, and no system holding customer data or ePHI is in the offices (EV-050). Thirty-one laptop wipes with certificates are recorded for the 12 months (EV-051).

**Business volume.** Receipts were $100.0 million in 2025 (about $274,000 a calendar day): Enterprise plan about $5.0 million a month, Standard plan about $3.33 million a month and the healthcare cell about $0.5 million a month. The 2026 forecast is about $112 million (EV-052). Availability met the commitment in 11 of the last 12 months (EV-053). Own-purpose data covers about 70,000 California consumers (EV-054).

**AI, product settings and questionnaires.** AI Assist has been generally available since 2026-03 (about 1,100 customers) and Answer Bot in beta since 2026-06 (85 customers). Both launched after a product security review, with no AI risk assessment, hallucination evaluation or privacy review in the records. The triage classifier has run since 2024 (EV-055). The approved tools list names the AI coding assistant and the call summarizer, and the summarizer is set to join calls automatically (EV-056). Questionnaire answers come from a library that sales can edit (EV-057). Card number redaction is on by default since 2025, MFA is enforced for customer administrators and optional for agents, and the product has no voice service (EV-058).

**Reconciliation across sources.**
- **Workforce.** The 600 employees on the HR roster each have an identity provider account; the 18 contractors have virtual desktop accounts (EV-001, EV-003, EV-047).
- **Privileged access.** The 210 privileged accounts include the 190 engineers enrolled with security keys (EV-001, EV-006).
- **Endpoints.** The device management count (640 laptops) matches the SSP and the P07 sampling population (EV-030).
- **Sub-processors.** The 14 on the public list match the DPA register and the 14 sub-processor payees in accounts payable. The 6 that receive healthcare cell data are the egress destinations in the healthcare account, and the warehouse export is an additional flow (EV-010, EV-040, EV-041).
- **AI tools.** The product launch records give AI-001 to AI-003, and the approved tools list, the identity provider app list and accounts payable give AI-004 and AI-005: the 5 tools in [`asset-inventory.csv`](asset-inventory.csv). Use of other AI tools by staff was not established (EV-055, EV-056).
