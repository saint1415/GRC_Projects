## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how workforce members use company systems, devices, and information, in the office, in the communications centers, at stations, and in vehicles.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members and contractors using company systems or devices, including consoles, MDCs, tablets, and station workstations.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Director | Collects acknowledgments at hire and annually; tracks training |
| Security Manager | Security awareness content, reminders, and phishing exercises |
| Director of Communications | Manual dispatch training and drills for telecommunicators |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Consoles, MDCs, and ePCR tablets must not be used for personal browsing, email, or streaming. (PL-4; PR.AT-01; 164.310(b))
4.2 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4; GV.PO-01; 164.310(b))
4.3 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year. Telecommunicators, billing services staff, and administrators also complete role-based training (manual dispatch, payment change fraud, privileged access). (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02; 164.308(a)(5))
4.4 Lock your screen when you step away from an office or station PC. Do not leave a tablet unattended outside the ambulance. Position screens so patients and bystanders cannot read them. (AC-11; PR.AA-06; 164.310(c))
4.5 Do not install software or connect USB storage unless IT approves it. Never plug personal phones into consoles, MDCs, or station alerting equipment. (CM-11; PR.PS-02; 164.310(b))
4.6 Report suspicious messages with the Report Phishing button, and report lost devices, misdirected records, and anything suspicious immediately (POL-03 4.3). (AT-2; IR-6; PR.AT-01; 164.308(a)(5)(ii)(A))
4.7 Look up patient records, call history, and client data only when your job requires it. CAD, ePCR, and billing access is logged and reviewed. (AU-6; DE.CM-03; 164.308(a)(1)(ii)(D))
4.8 **Generative AI:** use only tools on the approved list. Never paste, type, or dictate patient, caller, or client information into a tool that is not approved. AI suggestions in CAD, the ePCR, and billing are advice: you remain responsible for the dispatch priority, the patient care record, and the claim. (PL-4; GV.PO-01; 164.308(a)(5))
4.9 Do not photograph patients, scenes, ECG strips, or screens with personal phones. (PL-4; PR.AT-01; 164.310(b))

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the HIPAA sanctions procedure (POL-01 section 4.8). Compliance is checked through acknowledgment and training reports and the annual assessment (P07).

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7.
