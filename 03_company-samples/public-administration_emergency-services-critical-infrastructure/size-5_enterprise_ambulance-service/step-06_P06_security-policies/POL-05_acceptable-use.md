## 1. Purpose
Set the rules every workforce member follows when using company systems, devices, and information, on the dispatch floor, in the ambulance, at the contact centers, and in the office.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (employees, field clinicians, dispatchers, contractors, students, and volunteers) at all 231 sites and in every ambulance, including acquired operations from their acquisition date. Covers all company systems and devices, including consoles, MDCs, tablets, cardiac monitors, and phones.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Chief Human Resources Officer | Owns this policy, training, and acknowledgments |
| Managers and supervisors | Make sure their staff complete training and follow this policy |
| All workforce | Follow this policy; report incidents within 1 hour (POL-03 4.2) |

## 4. Policy statements
Each statement is testable and tagged with its SP 800-53 control(s) and CSF 2.0 subcategory. `policy-control-map.csv` traces each statement to its regulatory driver and shows whether Internal Audit tested it in 2026 (P07).

4.1 Company systems are for company business; limited personal use is allowed if it involves no patient or member data and creates no risk. (PL-4; GV.PO-01)
4.2 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy before receiving access and every year after. (PL-4(1); PR.AT-01)
4.3 Security and privacy training is required at hire and annually; privileged users, CAD administrators, communications center supervisors, and disclosure committee members must complete role-based training. (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01)
4.4 Workforce must lock screens when stepping away and position screens away from patients and visitors. Dispatch consoles are covered by the documented equivalent measure in exception EXC-2026-008. (AC-11; PR.AA-06)
4.5 Users must not install software or connect storage devices to consoles, MDCs, tablets, or workstations unless IT approves it. (CM-11; PR.PS-05)
4.6 Only AI tools on the approved list (STD-05.3) may be used for company work, and PHI may be entered only into tools approved for PHI. (PL-4; GV.PO-01)
4.7 Calls may be recorded only on approved dispatch and contact center lines that carry the company's recording notice, and recordings must not be copied to personal devices. (PL-4; GV.PO-01)
4.8 Workforce must look up incidents, patient care records, and member records only for their job; lookups are monitored. (AU-6; DE.CM-03)
4.9 Workforce with knowledge of a potential material cybersecurity incident must not trade in company securities and must keep the information confidential until it is public. (PL-4; GV.PO-01)

## 5. Standards and procedures under this policy
Standards set measurable requirements (approved by the CISO); procedures give step-by-step instructions (approved by the owning director). Both sit below this policy in the hierarchy and cannot contradict it.
- STD-05.1 Security Awareness and Training Standard
- STD-05.2 External Systems and Personal Devices Standard
- STD-05.3 Approved AI Tools List

## 6. Compliance and enforcement
Compliance is monitored through continuous control monitoring, the annual Internal Audit assessment (P07), and access certifications. Violations are handled under the HIPAA sanctions procedure (PRC-01.1, POL-01 statement 4.7), from retraining to termination of employment or contract, in proportion to intent and harm.

## 7. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 7 and PRC-01.2: requested in the GRC platform, risk-rated with the P01 method, approved at the authority level for the residual risk, recorded in the exception register with compensating controls, and limited to 12 months.
