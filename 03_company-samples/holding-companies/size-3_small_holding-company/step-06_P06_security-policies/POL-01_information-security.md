## 1. Purpose
Set up one information security program for the whole group, assign who is accountable at the holding company and at each subsidiary, and give every other security policy its authority. The program protects the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of group, employee, and customer information. For CSC Consumer Finance ("Finance"), this policy set, the System Security Plan, and the Finance supplement together form the written information security program required by the FTC Safeguards Rule (16 CFR 314.3(a)).

## 2. Scope
All workforce members (owners, managers, employees, contractors, and temporary staff) of Cris Santos Company, LLC and each subsidiary, at every site and when working remotely. Covers all systems and data the group owns or uses, including the Shared Corporate Services Platform, each subsidiary's own systems, and systems that service providers run for the group. A company the group acquires comes under this policy on the closing date, with a 180-day transition plan.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities

| HR Director | Security steps in hiring, transfer, and termination; records sanctions |
| All workforce | Follow these policies; report suspected incidents immediately |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 The group must maintain one information security program, documented in POL-01 to POL-05 and the System Security Plan. Finance must also maintain a Finance supplement that covers its Safeguards Rule duties. (PM-1; GV.PO-01; 314.3(a))
4.2 The IT Manager is the group security lead and Finance's Qualified Individual. Both designations must be in writing. Finance retains responsibility for its compliance, and the Finance President must direct and oversee the Qualified Individual. (PM-2; GV.RR-02; 314.4(a)(1)-(2))
4.3 The holding company must protect Finance's customer information as the Safeguards Rule requires. This duty must be written into the intercompany services agreement. (SA-9; GV.OC-05; 314.4(a)(3), (f)(2))
4.4 A risk assessment must be performed at least annually (each August) and after an acquisition or major change, using NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1. Risks must be tracked in the risk register with an owner and a treatment. (RA-3; PM-9; 314.4(b))
4.5 **Risk acceptance authority:** the IT Manager may accept Low and Very Low risks; the CFO, Moderate; the CEO, High (reported to the Board); the Board of Managers, Very High. Subsidiary Presidents may not accept risks to the shared platform or to Finance customer information. (PM-9; GV.RM-02)
4.6 Security policies must be reviewed at least annually and updated after major changes, acquisitions, or incidents. (PL-1; GV.PO-02)
4.7 Exceptions to any security policy must be requested in writing, risk-rated, approved per 4.5, recorded in the risk register, and time-limited to 12 months or less. (PL-1)
4.8 **Sanctions.** Workforce members who violate security policies must be sanctioned in proportion to intent and harm: retraining, written warning, suspension, or termination. HR must document each sanction. (PS-8; GV.RR-04)
4.9 **Suppliers.** Before any supplier receives access to group systems, employee data, or Finance customer information, the purchase must include a security review by the IT Manager and a contract with security terms. Critical suppliers must be reviewed at least annually. (SA-4; SA-9; GV.SC-05; GV.SC-06; 314.4(f))
4.10 Security controls must be assessed at least annually (P07) and after major changes. Results must feed the POA&M. (CA-2; 314.4(d)(1), (g))
4.11 The CFO must report on the program to the Board of Managers at least twice a year. The Qualified Individual must report in writing to Finance's Board of Managers at least annually (each September) on the status of Finance's program and material matters. (PM-9; GV.OV-01; 314.4(i))
4.12 **Change triggers.** New systems, new suppliers with data access, new AI features, and acquisitions must be assessed for security risk before go-live. (RA-3; GV.RM-06; 314.4(g))

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement (POL-01 section 4.8). Sanctions range from retraining to termination, depending on intent and harm. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07) and the access reviews in this policy set.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the right level under 4.5, and expire within 12 months.
