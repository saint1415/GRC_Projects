# Scenario facts: Cris Santos Company | Management of Companies and Enterprises | Multi-Sector

All 11 deliverables in this folder use the facts below. The company is fictitious. This scenario is independent of the other sizes. Where a fact comes from a regulation or statute, the citation is given; each was read from the primary source for this sample (eCFR current through 2026-09-23, govinfo.gov for the U.S. Code, flsenate.gov for the 2026 Florida Statutes, and the NAIC model law and state pages).

## 1. The organization
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Legal name | Cris Santos Company Holdings, Inc. (publicly traded SEC registrant and large accelerated filer; parent of an insurance holding company system) |
| Structure | A Florida-headquartered holding company (NAICS 551112) that owns two operating groups through subsidiaries and runs corporate shared services for all of them. 64 legal entities in total |
| Division 1: Holding company and corporate shared services (NAICS 551112), **focus of this scenario** | The parent company: board, executive office, finance, treasury, tax, legal, HR and benefits, IT and security, internal audit, and corporate development. About 5,500 employees. Owns and runs the Shared Corporate Services Platform used by every subsidiary. Sponsors the group's self-insured employee health plan |
| Division 2: Insurance (NAICS 524126, sector 52 Finance and Insurance) | CSC Insurance Group: two Florida-domiciled stock insurers. CSC Property and Casualty Insurance Company writes personal auto and homeowners insurance; CSC Workers' Compensation Insurance Company writes workers' compensation for employers. About 20,500 employees. Licensed in 6 states: Florida, Georgia, Alabama, South Carolina, North Carolina, and Tennessee. Sells through about 2,400 independent agencies and a direct channel. Which state insurance data security laws bind the insurers is decided in the obligations register (N52-R07) |
| Division 3: Health Care Services (NAICS 621111, sector 62 Health Care and Social Assistance) | CSC Health Services: 340 urgent care and occupational medicine clinics in Florida, Georgia, Alabama, and Tennessee. About 19,000 employees. A HIPAA covered entity (health care provider that bills electronically), decided in the intake [obligations register](step-00_P00_intake/obligations-register.csv) (N62-R01-HCS). Accepts Medicare and Medicaid |
| Location | Headquartered in Florida. Operations in 6 southeastern states; employees live in more states. **State law is handled generically** ("each state where affected individuals reside"), with Florida as the worked example |
| Workforce / revenue | 45,000 employees; about $18.0 billion revenue (fictional) |
| Ownership and securities status | Publicly traded. A large accelerated filer (17 CFR 240.12b-2), so the auditor attests to internal control over financial reporting (15 U.S.C. 7262(b)). Files Form 10-K with Reg S-K Item 106 disclosure (17 CFR 229.106) and is subject to Form 8-K Item 1.05. Decided in the obligations register (N55-R01 to N55-R03) |
| Banking status | Not a bank holding company or savings and loan holding company: no subsidiary is a bank or savings association (12 CFR 225.2(b), (c)(1)). Federal Reserve Regulation Y Subpart N and 12 CFR Part 225 Appendix F do not apply. Decided in the obligations register (N55-R04, N55-R05) |
| Insurance holding company status | The holding company is the **ultimate controlling person** of the two insurers. Each insurer registers annually with the Florida Office of Insurance Regulation by April 1 (Fla. Stat. 628.801(1)). As ultimate controlling person, the holding company files the annual enterprise risk report by April 1 with the lead state office of the holding company system, which is Florida because both insurers are domiciled there (628.801(2)). The Office may examine the insurers and their affiliates (628.801(3)). Decided in the obligations register (FL-628.801) |

## 4. Where the evidence is
This file says who the company is. It does not say how well its security works. That is established from evidence:
- **What the records show** is in the [intake report](step-00_P00_intake/intake-report.md) and the [evidence register](step-00_P00_intake/evidence-register.csv). Rows are grouped by owner (Group, Group health plan, Insurance, Health Care Services). Every item has a source system, an owner, and as-of and collected dates.
- **Which rules apply** to each division, to the group health plan and to the group is in the [obligations register](step-00_P00_intake/obligations-register.csv).
- **Gaps** against the CSF 2.0 group profile, the SEC, SOX, HIPAA plan sponsor and Florida obligations, the state insurance data security laws and HIPAA are judged in the gap analyses (P03), which also number the nine group gaps cited across this sample, and **whether controls work** is tested in the control assessment (P07). Both cite evidence IDs.
