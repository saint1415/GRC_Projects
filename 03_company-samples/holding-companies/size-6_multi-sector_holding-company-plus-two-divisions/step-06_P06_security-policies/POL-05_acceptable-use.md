## 1. Purpose
Set the rules every workforce member follows when using group systems, data, and AI tools.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members, contractors, and independent adjusters using group systems or data.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow these rules; complete training; report concerns |
| Managers | Make sure their staff complete training and follow the rules |
| Group CISO | Keeps the approved-tools list |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Workforce members must acknowledge this policy at hire and each year. (PL-4; PS-6)

4.2 Workforce members must complete annual security awareness training. Help desk, call center, treasury, payables, and payroll staff must also complete role training on social engineering and payment-change fraud each year. (AT-2; AT-2(3); PR.AT-02; Model #668 sec. 4D(5))

4.3 **Payment and bank-detail requests.** Never act on a request to change bank details or to send an urgent payment that arrives by email, phone, text, or video call without verifying it through contact information already on file. Urgency or seniority of the requester is not a reason to skip verification. (AC-5; N55-R03)

4.4 Use only group-managed devices for Restricted data. Personal devices may reach email and chat only through the managed app. (AC-20; CM-11)

4.5 Install only approved software; use only approved cloud services for group data. (CM-10; CM-11)

4.6 **Generative AI.** Use only AI tools on the approved-tools list for group data. Do not enter Restricted data into an AI tool except for uses approved under the Group AI Standard. Do not use AI output as the basis for a decision about hiring, discipline, pay, insurance coverage, claims, or patient care. Check every output against its sources before relying on it, and report any output that shows data you should not see. (PL-4; PR.AT-01)

4.7 **Recording.** Do not record or transcribe calls or visits unless every party has been told and has consented where state law requires (Florida worked example: Fla. Stat. 934.03(2)(d)). (PL-4)

4.8 Report lost devices and suspected incidents to the group SOC within 1 hour (POL-03 4.1).

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.7.
