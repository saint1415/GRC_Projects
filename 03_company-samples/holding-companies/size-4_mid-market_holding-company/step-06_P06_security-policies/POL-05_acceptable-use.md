## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how workforce members use group systems, devices, and data, including payment instructions and AI tools.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members of every group company, including staff of acquired companies from closing, on any device used for group work.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow these rules; sign the acknowledgment at hire and each year |
| Managers | Make sure their staff complete training and follow the rules |
| VP of Human Resources | Collects acknowledgments; runs sanctions with POL-01 4.8 |
| Security Manager | Training content; approved-tools list; phishing simulations |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Group systems are for business use. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not create risk or cost. Workforce members must sign this policy at hire and each year. (PL-4; GV.RR-04; 164.310(b))
4.2 Every workforce member must complete security awareness training at hire and each year. Staff without email accounts (warehouse, plant, technicians) complete it on shared kiosks or tablets. Treasury, payables, HR, benefits, Finance, and administrator staff complete role modules each year. (AT-2; AT-2(3); PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02; 314.4(e)(1); 164.308(a)(5))
4.3 Only managed devices may hold Restricted data. Personal phones may reach email and files only through the approved app. (AC-19; AC-20)
4.4 **Payment instructions.** Any request to change a vendor's or employee's bank details, or to send an urgent or unusual payment, must be verified by a callback to a phone number already on file (never one in the request), recorded in the ERP or HRIS before the change, and approved by a second person. Urgency or a request from an executive is never a reason to skip it. (AT-2(3); PR.AT-02)
4.5 Workforce members may not install unapproved software or browser extensions, or connect unapproved devices to group networks. (CM-11; PR.PS-05)
4.6 Workforce members may not forward group email to personal accounts or store group data in personal cloud storage. (AC-20)
4.7 Suspicious email, calls, or texts must be reported with the report button or to the service desk. (AT-2)
4.8 **AI tools.** Only tools on the approved-tools list may be used with group data. Public chatbots may not be used for any group data. Users must check every AI output against its source before relying on it and are responsible for anything they send. (PL-4; PR.AT-01)
4.9 **AI and decisions about people.** AI output may not be the basis for a decision about hiring, discipline, pay, credit, or collections unless that specific use was approved as a High-tier use in P10, with the human review it requires. (PL-4)
4.10 Call and meeting recording or transcription is allowed only with the recording notice the platform gives all parties, and is turned off for calls with outside parties unless every party has consented (Fla. Stat. 934.03). (PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement (POL-01 section 4.8). Compliance is checked through training records, phishing simulations, web proxy reports, and the annual independent assessment (P07).

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7.
