## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the group's own records before any assessment work began on 2026-07-13. It covers the holding company and its four subsidiaries (Supply, Home Services including Home Services North, Fabrication and Finance), the Shared Corporate Services Platform and the subsidiary systems connected to it, the suppliers that touch them, and the rules that may bind the group, Finance and the self-funded group health plan. At this size the sources are systems of record: the identity provider and directory, the HRIS, the endpoint and EDR consoles, the cloud organization console, the SIEM and the MSSP portal, the vendor register, the contract register, the accounts payable and expense records, prior risk assessments and internal audit reports, scan results and the incident log. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (interviews, samples, tests) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analysis (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Users and access.** The identity provider holds accounts for the integrated users of the holding company, Supply, Home Services South and Central, Fabrication and Finance, synchronized from the on-premises directory. Every active account has push MFA with number matching registered, 166 accounts were created in the 12 months to 2026-06-30, 6 global administrators hold standing rights, and the 2 break-glass accounts were last tested in 2026-06. Home Services North users are not in the directory (EV-001). MFA is required for all integrated users; a compliant device is required for administrators only, and no phishing-resistant keys are in use (EV-002). The directory has 14 Domain Admins members (4 of them service accounts), 9 directory administrators and 61 service accounts, 23 with non-expiring passwords, and domain controllers accept a password with no second factor (EV-003). 41 accounts hold privileged roles; the access broker covers cloud console roles only, and directory, identity provider, ERP and treasury administration is standing (EV-004). The HRIS lists 600 employees, with 142 terminations and 58 transfers in the 12 months (EV-005). Subsidiary disable tickets closed on average 2 business days after the last working day, and transfer tickets have no step that removes prior access (EV-006). Review records exist for the ERP only, in January and July 2026 (EV-007). 19 Finance users hold servicing export rights, and servicing logs stay in the vendor platform (EV-008). The HR site with stop-loss and high-cost claimant files is open to 11 HR staff, and none of the 96 collaboration sites has an access review record (EV-009). MFA resets are verified with the employee number and the manager's name (EV-010). Three sponsor users are on the TPA portal (EV-011).

**Devices, servers and network.** The endpoint console lists 430 managed laptops and desktops and 95 technician tablets, with encryption, remote wipe and idle lock. EDR reports on all of them and on the 46 servers, monitored 24x7 by the MSSP since 2025. The 120 warehouse scanners have no encryption policy or EDR agent (EV-012). The HQ file servers and the NAS have no encryption at rest, and documented baselines cover cloud images and the laptop image only (EV-013). The 9 sites run on SD-WAN; HQ and the distribution center have dual ISPs and the plant a single one; the plant floor and plant office are one network; Home Services North is not on SD-WAN (EV-014). Firewall firmware at 2 Home Services shops is one major version behind (EV-015). Two plant machine controllers run an unsupported embedded Windows version, and machine vendors connect through an always-on remote access tool with a shared password (EV-016). Home Services North runs its own email tenant and a field-service product with local accounts; its 38 laptops have no group device management or EDR, and the seller's IT provider holds administrator rights on its server (EV-017).

**Cloud, backups and monitoring.** The landing zone has 5 accounts with guardrails, company-managed keys and a write-once log archive. Warehouse query logs are not forwarded to the SIEM, 4 Finance analysts see loan-level tables, and ACH collection files on the SFTP server have no deletion job (EV-018). Cloud workloads back up daily to a separate account and region with 35-day write-once retention; the suite has a daily third-party backup; HQ servers back up nightly to a NAS in the same server room; the plant server backs up weekly to a removable drive, and 2 of the last 8 were skipped (EV-019). The 2025 cloud recovery plan covers cloud workloads only and the cloud restore test ran in 2026-03; the request for a group contingency plan, a directory forest recovery procedure and restore records for the on-premises servers returned none (EV-020). The SIEM receives identity, suite, EDR, firewall, VPN, directory and cloud logs, and no logs from the ERP, HRIS, subsidiary SaaS, HQ file servers, the plant or Home Services North (EV-021). The MSSP reports monthly and has no egress anomaly alerting for the file servers or the warehouse (EV-022). Quarterly scans cover HQ and the cloud only; 9 of the 50 critical findings in 2026 H1 closed after the 30-day target (EV-023). The external penetration test ran in 2025-11, and the 2026 test is booked for 2026-11 (EV-024).

**Programs and documents.** The 2024 group policy set has no 2025 review record. Three standards date from 2024; the request for an AI standard, an acquisition integration standard, plant procedures, plan security procedures, a configuration standard and a retention schedule returned none (EV-025). Finance's written information security program was updated in 2025, and the Qualified Individual's 2025 report reached Finance's Board of Managers in 2026-02, 5 months after it was due (EV-026). The last group risk assessment is dated 2025-08 and does not include plan PHI in its scope (EV-027). The 2025 risk appetite is one paragraph with no measures, the audit committee calendar schedules one cyber report a year, and the subsidiary Presidents' role descriptions contain no cyber duties (EV-028). The 2025 internal IT general controls audit went to the audit committee (EV-029). The 2024 incident response plan covers the holding company, was exercised once in 2024 with holding company staff only, and has no payment-fraud playbook or plan PHI breach procedure (EV-030). The incident log holds 41 security incidents in the 12 months, including business email compromise attempts in 2026 (EV-031). 96% of staff with email accounts completed the 2025 training; staff without email accounts and Home Services North staff are not assigned it; the June 2026 phishing click rate was 9.4% (EV-032). Background checks, sanctions and code of conduct acknowledgments are recorded (EV-033). The Security Manager has been the written Qualified Individual since 2025 (EV-034). 214 changes to Tier 1 systems were ticketed in 2026 H1, and the change procedure does not name the directory, ERP configuration or the integration service (EV-035). 64 devices were retired in 2026 H1; devices from subsidiary sites are shipped to HQ with no tracking record (EV-036). HQ and the distribution center use badge access; shops and the plant use keys (EV-037).

**Suppliers, contracts and the plan.** The vendor register lists about 160 vendors with access to group systems or data, 22 rated critical, and SOC 2 reports are on file for 8 of the 22 (EV-038, EV-041). The SaaS inventory lists 64 products against 112 in expense reports, including the recruiting software and the AI voice agent (EV-039). The contract template used since 2025 carries security and exit terms; the distribution system contract states a 24-hour recovery time; there are no written terms with the machine vendors or the seller's IT provider (EV-040). Finance has 9 service providers, 3 of them added with no onboarding review record (EV-042). The intercompany services agreement has no security schedule (EV-043). The sponsor's portfolio standard requires an annual independent assessment and 24-hour notice of a severity 1 incident, and the bank partner's draft participation agreement asks for SOC 2 Type 2 and SOC 1 Type 2 reports (EV-044). The cyber policy has a $10 million limit and a $200,000 retention, and the crime policy a $250,000 social engineering sublimit (EV-045). Payables, treasury and positive pay controls are configured, and 310 vendor bank-detail changes were made in the 12 months (EV-046). The self-funded plan covers about 470 participants and about 1,100 covered persons; its 2024 amendment carries the 164.504(f) terms and no 164.314(b) security terms (EV-047). The TPA and PBM BAAs allow 60 days for breach notice, and no BAA was located for the benefits consultant who receives high-cost claimant reports (EV-048). Cards are taken only through the processor's point-to-point encrypted devices and hosted page (EV-049).

**Legal and business volume.** The company is privately held with 24 holders of record, owns no bank, and holds no federal contracts (EV-050). The Home Services North acquisition file holds no cyber due diligence document (EV-051). Consolidated receipts were about $100.0 million in FY2025 over about 250 business days (EV-052). Treasury releases about $3.5 million a business day, and Finance collects about $220,000 (EV-053). Finance holds customer information on about 34,000 consumers and keeps declined application records back to 2019 (EV-054). Servicing reconciliations are signed daily (EV-055).

**AI tools and the suite.** Seven AI uses were found: the AI assistant (180 users since 2026-05-04, with no data-access review record), the credit scorecard (about 38% of applications decided automatically), the applicant ranking in the recruiting software, the ERP invoice coding feature, the after-hours AI voice agent, the distribution system's demand forecasting, and public chatbots. No approved-tools list exists (EV-056). Web proxy logs show 14 users of public chatbots in 30 days, 2 of whom uploaded spreadsheets (EV-057). No suite rule blocks forwarding of labeled files to outside addresses (EV-058).

**Reconciliation across sources.**
- **Workforce.** The HRIS lists 600 employees (EV-005). The identity provider holds the integrated employers' users; the 45 Home Services North employees are outside the directory (EV-001, EV-017).
- **Endpoints and servers.** The endpoint console counts (430 laptops and desktops, 95 tablets) and the scanner management server count (120 scanners) match the SSP boundary; the 38 Home Services North laptops come from the seller's IT provider list and are outside it (EV-012, EV-017). The 46 servers in EV-013 are the P07 configuration scan population.
- **Privileged accounts.** The 41 privileged accounts (EV-004) include the 9 directory administrators (EV-003) and the identity provider and cloud administrators (EV-001).
- **Vendors.** About 160 vendors in the vendor register, 22 critical, 8 with SOC 2 reports on file (EV-038, EV-041). The SaaS inventory and the expense reports differ by 48 products (64 against 112, EV-039). The named vendors and a summary row in [`vendor-register.csv`](vendor-register.csv) cover the register.
- **AI tools.** The discovery (EV-056) and the web proxy review (EV-057) give the 7 uses in [`asset-inventory.csv`](asset-inventory.csv).
