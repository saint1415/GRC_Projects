## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how the workforce uses the group's systems, devices, data, payment processes, and AI tools.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (employees, contractors, and temporary staff) of the holding company, Global Business Services (GBS), and every subsidiary (Building Products, Home Services, Manufacturing, and Finance), at all sites in the six operating states, including acquired businesses from their closing date. Covers all systems and data, including cloud, data centers, SaaS, plant OT, systems that vendors operate for the group, and the services offered to outside customers (SL-1 and SL-2).

## 4. Policy statements
Each statement is testable and tagged with its SP 800-53 control(s) and CSF 2.0 subcategory. `policy-control-map.csv` traces each statement to its regulatory driver and shows whether Internal Audit tested a mapped control in 2026 (P07).

4.1 Company systems must be used for authorized business purposes. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not create risk. (PL-4; GV.PO-01)
4.2 Users must acknowledge this policy before receiving access and annually after that. (PL-4(1); PR.AT-01)
4.3 Users must complete security awareness training at onboarding and annually. Payment, treasury, administrator, and plant OT staff must also complete role-based training each year. (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02)
4.4 Users must not install unapproved software or connect unapproved devices, including to plant networks. (CM-11; PR.PS-05)
4.5 Users must lock unattended devices and report lost or stolen devices immediately. (AC-11; IR-6; PR.AA-05)
4.6 Users may use only AI tools on the approved list (STD-05.3), must check AI output before relying on it, and must not use AI output to make decisions about hiring, discipline, pay, or credit unless the AI governance committee has approved that use. AI must not draft filed or furnished SEC disclosures without disclosure committee review. (PL-4; GV.PO-01)
4.7 No one may change vendor bank details, release a payment, or change direct deposit details on the strength of an email, chat, or phone request alone. The request must be verified by calling back a number already on file (PRC-05.1), even if it appears to come from an executive. (PL-4; AT-3; PR.AT-02)
4.8 Users must report suspected phishing, fraud attempts, and incidents through the report button or the service desk. (IR-6; RS.MA-02)
4.9 External systems and personal devices may be used for company data only as allowed by STD-05.2. (AC-20; GV.PO-01)

## 5. Standards and procedures under this policy
Standards set measurable requirements (approved by the CISO); procedures give step-by-step instructions (approved by the owning director). Both sit below this policy in the hierarchy and cannot contradict it.
- STD-05.1 Security Awareness and Training Standard
- STD-05.2 External Systems and Personal Devices Standard
- STD-05.3 Approved AI Tools List
- PRC-05.1 Payment Instruction Verification (Callback) Procedure

## 6. Compliance and enforcement
Compliance is monitored through continuous control monitoring, quarterly sub-certifications, access certifications, and the annual Internal Audit assessment (P07). Violations are handled under the sanctions procedure (PRC-01.1, POL-01 statement 4.7), from retraining to termination of employment or contract, in proportion to intent and harm.

## 7. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 7 and PRC-01.2: requested in the GRC platform, risk-rated with the P01 method, approved at the authority level for the residual risk, recorded in the exception register with compensating controls, and limited to 12 months.
