At the Sole Proprietorship tier the business keeps **one** consolidated policy for all four entities, because one person writes, follows, and checks it (`01_company-sizes/tier-project-scaling.csv`, t1 P06). This file is kept so the folder has the standard policy set names. It holds no separate rules.

**Where the incident response rules are:**

- POL-01 section 10 (Incident response): runbook and printed contacts (10.1), same-day reporting by anyone (10.2), recording which LLC's data was involved and the agent-to-LLC notice (10.3), notice deadlines per LLC and written no-harm determinations (10.4), ransom and extortion decisions (10.5), annual walkthrough and lessons learned (10.6).
- The procedure itself is the P08 runbook (`../step-08_P08_incident-response-runbook/ir-runbook.md`) and its notification matrix.
- SP 800-53 IR-1 is met by POL-01 as a whole.

Traceability for every statement is in `policy-control-map.csv`.
