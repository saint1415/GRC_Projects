## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how the workforce uses company systems, devices, SCADA equipment, and data.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (owners, employees, contractors, and temporary workers) at headquarters, both field offices, the OCC, and every well site and facility. Covers all business IT and OT systems, including systems that vendors operate for the company.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Manager | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and annually; tracks training |
| IT Manager | Security awareness content, reminders, and phishing exercises |
| SCADA and Automation Supervisor | OT rules at the OCC and field sites; the USB scanning kiosk |
| Field Superintendents | Deliver short security topics at field safety meetings |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use of corporate laptops and phones is allowed if it does not involve Restricted data or create risk. SCADA HMIs and engineering workstations are for operations only: no email, web browsing, or personal use. (PL-4; CM-7)
4.2 Every workforce member must sign this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4(1))
4.3 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year for **everyone**, including lease operators, crews, drivers, and Production Controllers. Field staff may complete it at safety meetings. The IT Manager runs at least quarterly phishing exercises for staff with email. (AT-2; PR.AT-01)
4.4 Lock your screen when you step away from a corporate computer. (AC-11)
4.5 **Removable media and laptops at SCADA equipment.** Do not plug USB drives, phones, or personal devices into HMIs, engineering workstations, controllers, or SCADA network ports. Files for SCADA must go through the scanning kiosk at the OCC. Only company-owned maintenance laptops may connect to field controllers. (MP-7; PR.PS-05)
4.6 Do not install software or browser extensions unless IT (or, for SCADA, the SCADA and Automation Supervisor) approves it. (CM-11)
4.7 Do not open unexpected attachments or links. Report suspicious messages with the Report Phishing button, and report lost tablets or phones, unexpected HMI behavior, or anything suspicious immediately (POL-03 4.2). (IR-6)
4.8 **Generative AI:** use only tools on the approved list. Never paste royalty owner data, employee data, reservoir data, controller programs, or SCADA details into a tool that is not approved. (PL-4)
4.9 Do not share gate combinations, cabinet keys, or passwords with haulers, contractors, or visitors unless your supervisor has approved it and logged it (POL-02 4.11). (PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement (POL-01 section 4.8). Sanctions range from retraining to termination, depending on intent and harm. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07) and the access reviews in this policy set.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the majority owner for High risk), and expire within 12 months.
