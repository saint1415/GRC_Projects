## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the group's own records before any assessment work began on 2026-05-04. It covers the legal entities, the corporate shared services, each division's business and control systems (field SCADA, plant control systems, pipeline SCADA and the business systems around them), the data they hold, the suppliers that touch them, and the rules that may bind each division and the group. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Rows are grouped by division: the title of each row starts with Group, Crude Oil Production, Power Generation or Crude Logistics. Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (interviews, document reviews, tests) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable. The inventories are in [`asset-inventory.csv`](asset-inventory.csv) and [`vendor-register.csv`](vendor-register.csv), and the applicability decisions are in [`obligations-register.csv`](obligations-register.csv).

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analyses (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Entities and scale.** The holding company is an SEC registrant with three operating subsidiaries and corporate shared services; all operations are onshore in Texas, New Mexico, Oklahoma and Florida (EV-001). The workforce is 45,000: Production about 26,000, Power Generation about 2,200, Crude Logistics about 9,800, corporate about 7,000 (EV-002). FY2025 revenue was about $18.0 billion: Production $14.2 billion, Power Generation $2.1 billion, Crude Logistics $1.7 billion from third parties plus $0.9 billion intercompany (EV-003). The contract register shows no federal contracts and no card acceptance; the hydrocarbon accounting and telematics agreements set no breach notice terms, and shipper contracts state no security or availability commitments (EV-004).

**Identity, PAM and the paths into OT.** All 45,000 workforce identities use MFA on SSO applications; IT administrators use phishing-resistant MFA (EV-005). Identity governance runs joiner-mover-leaver workflows from HR events with quarterly certification (EV-006). Privileged access is just in time through PAM with session recording; five OT support jump servers are the PAM landing path for all three divisions (EV-007). Firewall rules let the jump servers reach the OT DMZs of all three divisions and SYS-P5, and let the corporate virtual desktop pool reach the jump servers, although the OT DMZ standard allows access only from privileged access workstations (EV-008). One SYS-G6 service account pulls from the historian brokers in all three OT DMZs with permissions that include write; no interconnection agreement covers the connector or the SYS-P5 links (EV-009).

**Security operations.** The SOC runs 24x7 with an OT desk since 2025. Passive OT sensors feed from the IOC, BCC, PCC and GCC; SYS-P5 and the Florida control room SCADA logs are not on the data source list (EV-010). The case records show quarterly technical exercises per division and no cross-division exercise with the notification matrix (EV-011). EDR covers IT servers and workstations, cloud hosts, the jump servers and the FSPA SCADA servers; no SYS-P5 host appears (EV-012). The KEV tracking sheet lists open known exploited vulnerabilities on OT DMZ and SCADA servers against a 30-day target; OT patches are applied quarterly after testing (EV-013). The 2026-04 penetration test covered business IT and the landing zones, not the IT/OT boundary (EV-015).

**Policies and common controls.** The 2025 group policies are aligned to CSF 2.0, with an OT DMZ standard, an OT reference architecture and a logging standard; the library has no AI standard (EV-016). The Power Generation supplement was aligned on 2026-03-10; the Crude Logistics supplement v2023 was last aligned in 2023-05, names no owner, and differs from group policy on vendor VPNs, severity, termination, change management and notices (EV-017). The common control catalog lists 81 controls; inheritance matrices exist for Production and Power Generation, and no Crude Logistics matrix was provided (EV-018).

**Cloud, WAN and backups.** Provider A hosts the landing zone, SYS-G6, SYS-P4, SYS-P6, SYS-M3 and the SYS-E3 servers in one primary region; provider B hosts DR replicas and the backup vault; the control systems are on premises (EV-019). Guardrails apply to every division account and the hub denies traffic between accounts by default (EV-020). Storage is encrypted with customer-managed keys (EV-021); WAN links use IPsec and control traffic stays on OT networks (EV-022). Cloud workloads and SCADA server images are backed up daily to the immutable vault, with quarterly test restores of SCADA images (EV-023).

**Incident, disclosure and risk.** One notification matrix covers NERC, PHMSA, EPA, SEC, state and shipper and market notices; no exercise of it is recorded (EV-025). The disclosure committee charter has no worksheet of OT-specific materiality factors, and the FY2025 annual report carries the Item 106 disclosure (EV-026). The board risk committee receives quarterly cyber reports (EV-027). The risk strategy sets acceptance authority by level and requires High safety and environmental risks to be treated (EV-028). Internal audit is independent and planned the common control assessment for July and August 2026 (EV-029).

**Workforce, vendors, AI and data.** Awareness training completion is 97% for office staff and 88% for field staff against a 95% target (EV-030). The OT security contract schedule has applied to new contracts since 2025; the Mid-Continent legacy integrator and 3 controller vendors are on older contracts without it, and no assessment of the integrator is recorded (EV-032). Vendor SOC 2 reports are on file for the cloud providers, identity, SIEM, hydrocarbon accounting, bidding and telematics vendors (EV-033). AI in use or in pilot: the predictive maintenance model, production optimization, seismic interpretation, turbine anomaly detection, leak detection, driver camera scoring, route optimization and a generative AI pilot (EV-034). Monthly SYS-P3 exports of about 190,000 owners' data land on a finance file share that the data inventory does not list (EV-036).

**Crude Oil Production.** About 16,800 operated wells, 410 batteries and about 520,000 barrels of oil per day: Permian about 74%, Mid-Continent about 22%, Florida about 4% (EV-040). About 250 batteries meet the SPCC overfill rule through high-level alarms sent to SCADA; the plans do not describe actions when that alarm path fails (EV-041). Hardwired shutdowns act independent of SCADA (EV-042). About 140 controllers hold named SCADA accounts; the Florida control room also has 2 shared operator logins (EV-043). About 85% of about 38,000 field devices have model, firmware and address recorded (EV-044). Licensed radio links at about 30% of Permian sites carry legacy protocols without encryption or device authentication (EV-045). The program repository covers about 60% of controller models (EV-046). The BCC failover test was last run on 2025-10-14 (EV-047). SYS-P5 runs on a flat network with an always-on integrator connection on a shared account, unsupported HMI operating systems, and local backups with no restore jobs logged (EV-049). The 22 Florida HMIs run an unsupported operating system without allowlisting, and their logs are reviewed on paper (EV-050). SYS-P3 holds about 190,000 royalty owners, about 21,000 of them in Florida; owner bank changes are accepted by mail and email without a callback (EV-051).

**Power Generation.** Registered GO and GOP; P1, P2, P3 (1,180 MW) and the GCC hold low impact BES Cyber Systems only, with categorization approved 2026-03-10 (EV-055). The CIP-003-9 policy and plans are on file, and the 2024 Regional Entity audit recorded no findings (EV-056). The Plant P3 turbine OEM has a standing remote access path outside group PAM and the OT sensors, with no recorded method to detect malicious communications (EV-057). No reviews of OEM laptops are recorded since 2026-04-01 (EV-058). The EOP-004-4 Operating Plan relies on corporate email and phones (EV-059).

**Crude Logistics.** The 140-mile trunk line is a regulated pipeline; 120 miles of gathering lines are regulated rural and about 2,180 miles are reporting-regulated-only (EV-061). The last backup SCADA test at the backup PCC is recorded on 2025-04-22, and the control room procedures do not mention loss or manipulation of SCADA (EV-062); controller training does not list it as an abnormal operating condition (EV-063). Point-to-point verification sheets for the 2026-03 SCADA expansion are incomplete (EV-064). The hazmat security plan risk assessment lists no threats to dispatch, run tickets or telematics, and in-depth training covers physical threats only (EV-066). Driver camera AI scores flow into discipline with no review step (EV-067). The shipper platform requires MFA for shipper users; every dispatch user can see every loaded truck (EV-068). The three largest shippers asked for a SOC 2 Type 2 report by the end of 2027 (EV-070). No TSA notice has been received (EV-071).
