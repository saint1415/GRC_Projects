## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the company's own records before any assessment work began on 2026-07-06. It covers the organization, the Field SCADA and Production Accounting System and the business systems around it, the suppliers that touch them, and the rules that may bind the company. At this size the sources are systems of record: the identity provider, the HR system, the OT directory and SCADA configuration, the endpoint, EDR and OT sensor consoles, the cloud organization console, the SIEM and the MDR provider's reports, the ERP vendor list and contract files, the pipeline records tool, prior risk assessments and internal audit reports, scan results and the incident log. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (interviews, walkthroughs, samples, tests) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analysis (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Users and access.** The identity provider holds named accounts for employees and contractors, created by the HR feed and disabled on the termination date, plus about 20 shipper guest accounts. Every active account has MFA, and IT and cloud administrators use hardware keys (EV-001). MFA is required for email, SaaS, the corporate VPN, the cloud consoles and the OT jump host, with lockout after 10 failed attempts and a 45-day inactivity disable (EV-002). HR recorded 850 employees and, in the 12 months to 2026-06-30, 96 terminations, 38 transfers and 110 hires (EV-003). SCADA local accounts, controller passwords and gate combinations are left to area staff, who learn of field leavers by email from HR (EV-004). OCC HMIs use named accounts from the OT directory; the South Florida and BCC HMIs share one engineering account with administrator rights, and local accounts are reviewed once a year, last in 2025-11 (EV-005). 38 privileged accounts were listed; the vault covers cloud and directory administrators, not SCADA server administrator accounts or the OCC engineering workstations (EV-006). Controller passwords are set per area and changed yearly (EV-007). Payment runs need two approvers and bank changes need call-back verification (EV-008). A full owner deck copy with Social Security numbers sits in the data platform, readable by all 34 engineering users, and the 2025 data inventory does not list it (EV-009). Owner tax files go to the outside tax vendor by email as password-protected attachments (EV-010). SaaS and cloud roles were reviewed quarterly in 2026; shipper portal accounts were not (EV-011).

**Endpoints, OT and networks.** The endpoint console lists 520 laptops and desktops, 240 rugged tablets and 300 smartphones, with encryption and remote wipe (EV-012). EDR reports on all corporate laptops, desktops and servers under 24x7 MDR monitoring; OCC HMIs and SCADA servers have allowlisting and USB blocking, while the South Florida and BCC HMIs run antivirus only and have no USB block (EV-013). The SCADA configuration covers about 610 RTUs and PLCs, 130 ESP drives, 260 flow meters, 6 LACT flow computers, licensed radio, 180 cellular modems and 2 microwave links. About 140 older RTUs and the radio protocol do not support authenticated commands. Safety shutdowns and the pump station high-pressure switches are hardwired. Field devices are not time-synchronized, and field office HMI logs roll over after 30 days (EV-014). The OCC was upgraded to a supported version in 2025; the BCC standby server and the 3 South Florida HMIs run an operating system past vendor support (EV-015). The OT sensors inventory the OCC, the Panhandle and the gathering system; South Florida and Alabama devices are only in area spreadsheets, and the SCADA and Automation Manager estimates about 70% coverage (EV-016). The OT DMZ at the OCC carries only 3 flows through 22 reviewed rules; the South Florida HMIs and the BCC reach the OCC through site firewalls that allow traffic from corporate subnets, with no DMZ (EV-017). The jump host has named accounts, MFA, per-session approval and recording; the packager's 4 cellular gateways and the flow computer vendor's modem connect outside it with no MFA (EV-018). The 2025 project left architecture, hardening and capacity records; none exists for the 2026 compression station additions (EV-019). The CAB covers SCADA servers and HMIs; pump station setpoint changes go by work order (EV-020). The controller repository holds about 60% of programs, and South Florida and Alabama controller changes appear only as work orders (EV-021).

**Monitoring and scanning.** The SIEM receives identity, cloud, firewall, EDR and jump host logs with 1-year retention; SCADA logs outside the OCC and flow computer changes are not sources, and the MDR contract excludes OT (EV-022). Monthly MDR and metrics reports run for the 12 months (EV-023). OT sensors sit at the OCC and the Central Facility only, and their alerts are reviewed in business hours (EV-024). Quarterly authenticated scans of corporate systems found 46 critical findings in Q1 and Q2 2026, each ticketed; ICS advisories are matched by hand for OCC equipment (EV-025).

**Cloud, measurement and backups.** The landing zone has 5 accounts, guardrails, federated sign-in, sealed root credentials, a write-once log archive and deny-by-default rules that let the OT data account accept only the push from the OT DMZ (EV-026). LACT tickets are hashed and compared nightly with flow computer records; the shipper portal has one administrator, and daily statements are published without a reconciliation to LACT tickets (EV-027). Flow computer configuration changes are recorded on paper at the Central Facility (EV-028). Cloud workloads back up daily to a second region with 35-day write-once retention, and restores have been tested quarterly since 2026 Q1 (EV-029). Weekly offline SCADA images are stored at the BCC; the historian was restored in 2025, no SCADA server restore is recorded, and the last BCC failover test was partial, in 2023 (EV-030). Some productivity suite sites keep broad company-wide sharing, and the AI assistant answers from any file a user can open (EV-031).

**Program documents and records.** Five policies date from 2024 with annual reviews, and 5 standards date from 2024; there is no standard for OT change control, vendor risk, remote access, AI use or backup and recovery (EV-032). The Security Manager was designated in 2024 (EV-033). Risk assessments exist for 2024 and 2025, with a 2024 benchmark review (EV-034). The audit committee met quarterly (EV-035), and the 2025 internal IT audit focused on financial reporting controls (EV-036). The 2026 business plan includes bolt-on acquisitions (EV-037). Monthly IT/OT security meetings were minuted from 2026-01 to 2026-06 (EV-038). The 2024 contingency plan has no SCADA rebuild or BCC failover steps (EV-039). The emergency response plan and pipeline emergency procedures, including the 8-hour trunk line rule and the release estimate procedure, do not refer to cyber events (EV-040). The 2024 incident response plan has no OT annex, and only an IT tabletop was held in 2025 (EV-041). 31 incidents were logged from 2025-01-01 to 2026-06-30 (EV-042). Office staff train yearly, the June 2026 phishing click rate was 6.1%, and a field security briefing started in 2026 (EV-043). Communication templates exist for owners, shippers and partners (EV-044).

**Pipeline records.** The segment identification record shows 14 miles of 8-inch trunk line meeting the regulated rural gathering line criteria and 78 miles of other gathering lines; the MOP record and setpoint sheet are on file (EV-045). The OQ program holds about 120 qualification records, and the annual report was filed on 2026-06-10 (EV-046).

**Facilities and media.** Badge access covers the OCC, the BCC and the field offices; RTU cabinets at pads are padlocked with no door alarms, and gate combinations change once a year (EV-047). Corporate drives have destruction certificates; retired RTUs, HMIs and flow computers have no wipe records (EV-048).

**Suppliers and contracts.** 64 vendors have system or data access; SaaS vendors are tiered and OT vendors are not (EV-049). About half of the 64 agreements carry security terms; the packager and flow computer vendor agreements and the 5 gathering agreements have none for data handling or incident notice (EV-050). SOC 2 reports were reviewed in 2025 for 4 SaaS vendors, and no OT vendor has been assessed (EV-051). Field devices come from authorized distributors with no firmware authenticity check (EV-052). The largest shipper asked for a SOC 2 Type 2 report on gathering and measurement services (EV-053). The cyber insurance policy has a $15 million limit and requires MFA on all remote access (EV-054).

**Business volume.** FY2025 revenue was about $265 million, including about $660,000 of crude sales per day (EV-055). The company operates 640 wells and produces about 9,800 barrels of oil per day, and the gathering system carries about 1,400 barrels per day for 5 shippers (EV-056). About 14,000 royalty owners in more than 40 states are paid monthly, and 22 partners are billed (EV-057).

**AI tools.** Discovery found 5 AI tools in use or licensed, adopted before any AI inventory existed. No completed bias test record was provided for the predictive maintenance model, and the HR vendor supplied no impact data for the screening feature (EV-058).
