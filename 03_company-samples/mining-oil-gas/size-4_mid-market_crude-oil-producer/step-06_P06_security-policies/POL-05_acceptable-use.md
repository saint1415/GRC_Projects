## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce and contractors may use company systems, SCADA equipment, data, devices, and AI tools.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members and anyone else given access to company systems or data, including SCADA integrator, vendor, and contractor staff at field sites, at every location and when working remotely.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Director | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and annually; keeps training records |
| Security Manager | Training content, monthly reminders, quarterly phishing simulations |
| SCADA and Automation Manager | OT role-based training for Production Controllers, automation technicians, and integrator staff |
| Field Superintendents | Deliver the security briefing in the annual field safety meetings |
| Production Engineering Manager | AI tool rules with the Security Manager (STD-05) |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use of corporate devices is allowed if it creates no risk. SCADA equipment, HMIs, and engineering workstations are for operations only, with no personal use. (PL-4)
4.2 Every workforce member and contractor must acknowledge this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4(1))
4.3 **Training.** Security awareness training is required at hire and every year for all workforce members. Office staff receive quarterly phishing simulations. Field crews and drivers receive the security briefing in the annual field safety meeting, and all of them must have attended by 2026-12-31. Production Controllers, automation technicians, and integrator staff must complete OT security training at hire and a refresher every year. Accounting, treasury, and land staff must complete payment fraud training. (AT-2; AT-2(3); AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02)
4.4 Lock your screen when you step away. HMI screen lock follows POL-02 4.8.
4.5 **Removable media and laptops in OT.** Do not connect USB storage, phones, or personal devices to SCADA servers, HMIs, engineering workstations, or field controllers. Files for OT must pass the scanning kiosk (at the OCC today; at the South Florida office and the BCC by 2026-12-31). Vendor and integrator laptops must be checked by the OT Security Engineer or an automation supervisor before they connect to any field network. (MP-7; AC-20; PR.PS-05)
4.6 Do not install software or browser extensions unless IT (or, for OT, the change advisory board) approves it. (CM-11)
4.7 Only company-managed devices, including the rugged tablets and managed smartphones, may access company systems. Report a lost or stolen device immediately. (AC-19)
4.8 Report suspected incidents, suspicious messages, and anything unusual on an HMI or at a field site immediately (POL-03 4.2).
4.9 **Payment changes.** Changes to royalty owner, partner, or vendor bank details must be verified by a call-back to a number already on file, never to a number in the request. (AT-2(3); PL-4)
4.10 **AI tools.** Use only AI tools on the approved list (STD-05). Never enter Restricted or Confidential data into a tool that is not approved for that data. Outputs from approved tools must be reviewed by a qualified person before they are used in a decision, a regulatory filing, or a message to an owner, shipper, or partner. AI outputs must never be used to change SCADA settings, and must not be used to evaluate or rank employees without HR review. (PL-4)
4.11 Company systems, including vehicle telematics, are monitored for security, safety, and dispatch. Users should have no expectation of privacy in company systems beyond what law provides. Telematics data may be used for safety, dispatch, and hours-of-service purposes, not for unrelated tracking. (PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement (POL-01 section 4.8). Compliance is checked through the annual independent control assessment (P07), training records, phishing results, and kiosk logs.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.10.
