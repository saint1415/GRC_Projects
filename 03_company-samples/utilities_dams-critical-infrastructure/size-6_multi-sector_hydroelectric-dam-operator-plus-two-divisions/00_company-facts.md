## 4. Current security posture: a defined program with gaps at scale and between divisions

**In place today:**
- One group security program aligned to NIST CSF 2.0, with NIST SP 800-82 Rev. 3 as the OT benchmark; group policies since 2024 with division supplements
- A common control catalog for identity, security operations, cloud, HR, and governance controls provided by corporate
- 24x7 group SOC for IT, with OT sensor alerts from both HOCs and 14 plants
- Medium and low impact CIP programs since 2016; CIP Senior Manager designated with documented delegations; last SERC compliance audit 2025-04 (1 finding, mitigated by 2025-08)
- FERC Security Program documents for all 29 Group 1 and 2 dams: Vulnerability Assessments for the 6 Group 1 dams, Security Assessments for Group 2, Security Plans with a fleet Cyber/SCADA Security Plan for the HOCs, and Annual Security Compliance Certification Letters every year
- An Owner's Dam Safety Program with a Chief Dam Safety Engineer; EAPs reviewed and tested annually; Part 12D independent consultant inspections on schedule; independent external audit of the ODSP in 2024 by an unaffiliated firm (18 CFR 12.65)
- Privileged access management for IT; OT PAM and Intermediate Systems with MFA for all Interactive Remote Access into the HOC ESPs
- Quarterly CIP access verification (CIP-004-7 R4.2) and quarterly access certification for other critical systems
- Immutable backups for cloud workloads; offline SCADA backups at both HOCs; annual HOC failover exercise
- A Federal Projects Enclave in a government-community cloud region for CUI; SPRS self-assessment posted
- A tiered third-party risk program and a CIP-013 supply chain cyber security risk management plan
- Reg S-K Item 106 disclosure in the annual report on Form 10-K; a disclosure committee charter that covers cybersecurity incidents

**Gaps found in the 2026 assessments:**
1. **Construction connectivity into Hydro OT.** Constructors' commissioning kits (laptop plus cellular router) are connected to plant and gate control networks during rehabilitation projects, bypassing the Intermediate Systems. They are treated as internal because Constructors is an affiliate. At DEV-05 a kit stayed connected to the gate control network for 41 days. Kits are not in EDR, and the CIP-003-9 Attachment 1 Section 5 (Transient Cyber Assets) and Section 6 (vendor electronic remote access) controls were not applied to them: a potential noncompliance at BES plants.
2. **Section 9 enhanced measures below the HOC.** Gate control at Group 1 and 2 dams is Critical, but OT network monitoring covers only 14 of 41 HOC-operated plants, and 15 of 41 have not had an OT vulnerability assessment in the last 12 months.
3. **The DSMS data path.** 17 HOC-operated plants replicate historian data to the DSMS over a two-way connection instead of a one-way diode, so an Engineering cloud service has a network path back toward Hydro OT DMZs.
4. **Legacy OT.** 58 plant HMIs and 11 gate control workstations run an unsupported operating system; PLC logic backups at 12 plants are more than 12 months old.
5. **CUI outside the enclave.** Constructors' Level 2 self-assessment score is 74 of 110, below the 88 needed for a Conditional status. CUI-marked USACE drawings were found on the commercial project platform (SYS-C1) and in productivity suite shares. CMMC Phase 2 (planned for 2026-11-10) is suspended, but SP 800-171 Rev. 2 under DFARS 252.204-7012 still applies.
6. **Client CEII and DSMS assurance.** Engineering holds client CEII in general engineering shares for some projects. The DSMS has no SOC 2 report, and alert thresholds and model changes lack formal change control.
7. **Division supplements and inheritance.** The Constructors supplement (2023) has not been re-aligned with the 2026 group policies and treats Hydro plants as ordinary jobsites. Common control inheritance is documented for Hydro but not for Constructors' and Engineering's division systems.
8. **Multi-regulator notification.** One incident can trigger FERC, NERC, DOE, DoD (DFARS), client contract, state breach, and SEC duties at once across three divisions. The single notification matrix has never been exercised, and the disclosure committee has never run an OT or dam safety scenario.
9. **Third-party concentration.** One OEM services governors and exciters on 55% of Hydro units; its 2025 renewal skipped the CIP-013 procurement terms.
10. **AI governance.** 10 AI use cases across the group; 6 have completed Group AI council review. AI-001 alerts are already used to set manual instrument review priorities at Hydro dams.
