## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce uses group systems and information, in offices, control rooms, and jobsites.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members in every division and in corporate shared services, including craft workers who use group devices, on any device used for group work.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow these rules; complete training; report incidents |
| Managers and superintendents | Make sure staff complete training and acknowledgments |
| Group HR | Tracks acknowledgments and training completion |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Workforce members must use group systems and information only for their job. (PL-4; AC-8)

4.2 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy and their division supplement at hire and every year. (PL-4; PR.AT-01)

4.3 Every workforce member with a group account must complete security awareness training at hire and every year; people with access to CIP-scope systems also receive quarterly security awareness. (AT-2; PR.AT-01; CIP-004-7 R1)

4.4 Anyone who works on OT, including affiliates' staff, must complete OT role-based training before access and at least every 15 calendar months. (AT-3; PR.AT-02; CIP-004-7 R2; Table 9.3a training)

4.5 Suspected incidents must be reported to the group SOC within 1 hour, and OT events to the HOC shift supervisor at once (POL-03 4.1). (IR-6)

4.6 Control system computers may be used only for approved control system tasks: no email, web browsing, or personal use. (PL-4; CM-7; Table 9.3a approved use)

4.7 **Removable media** may be used on OT only after scanning at a kiosk, and on FPE systems not at all unless approved. Unknown media must be handed to security. (MP-7; CIP-003-9 Att. 1 Sec. 5.3; Form 3 Q17; SP 800-171 Rev. 2 3.8.7)

4.8 Restricted information may be used only on group-managed devices and approved services. It must not be sent to personal email or stored on personal devices. CUI may be printed only at approved locations. (AC-19; AC-20)

4.9 **Generative AI.** Only AI tools on the group approved-tools list may be used for group work. CUI, BCSI, and client CEII must never be entered into external AI tools. Engineers remain responsible for any AI-assisted content in sealed work. (PL-4; PL-4(1))
