## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for using company systems, with specific rules for control systems and field work at client sites.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members and contractors using company devices, accounts, OT systems, or data.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow these rules and acknowledge them yearly |
| Managers | Make sure their teams complete training and acknowledgments |
| IT Director and OT Security Manager | Maintain technical enforcement |

## 4. Policy statements
Each statement ends with the SP 800-53 controls, CSF 2.0 subcategory, and regulatory driver it implements.

4.1 Control system consoles, HMIs, and engineering workstations are used only for approved control system activities: no email, web browsing, or personal use. (CM-7; PL-4; PR.PS-05; C-DAMS-R01 (Table 9.3a access control (approved control system activities)))
4.2 Removable media may be connected to OT only after it is scanned at a media kiosk; personal USB devices are prohibited in OT. (MP-7; PR.PS-05; C-DAMS-R01 (Form 3 Q17); C-DAMS-R03 (CIP-003-9 Att. 1 Sec. 5.3))
4.3 Laptops connected to OT, including vendor laptops, must be checked under the transient cyber asset procedure before connection. (MA-3; SI-3; PR.PS-01; C-DAMS-R03 (CIP-003-9 Att. 1 Sec. 5.1-5.2))
4.4 Field services crews connect company laptops to client equipment only with the client's supervision, using the hardened field engineering build, and never connect client equipment to company networks. (AC-20; MA-3; GV.SC-05; Field services contracts)
4.5 Staff complete security awareness training each year and before receiving control system access; staff with access to BES assets also receive the quarterly reinforcement. (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; C-DAMS-R01 (Table 9.3a training); C-DAMS-R03 (CIP-003-9 Att. 1 Sec. 1))
4.6 Only approved AI tools may be used for company work; public AI tools may not receive Restricted or Confidential information. (PL-4; PR.AT-01; CEII, 18 CFR 388.113)
4.7 Users must report lost devices, suspicious messages, and unusual control system behavior immediately to the ROC or the IT service desk. (IR-6; AT-2; RS.MA-02; C-DAMS-R01 (Form 3 Q25a))
4.8 Company systems may be monitored for security purposes; users have no expectation of privacy in company systems, subject to law. (AU-6; PL-4; DE.CM-03; Company policy)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Compliance is checked through the annual independent assessment (P07), quarterly OT access reviews, NERC compliance evidence reviews by the NERC Compliance Manager, and metrics reported to the audit committee. Violations are handled under POL-01 4.14.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions must be requested in writing, risk-rated, approved under POL-01 4.4, recorded in the risk register, and limited to 12 months. No exception may waive a FERC or NERC requirement.
