## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how the workforce uses company systems, the control system, devices, and information.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (employees, seasonal staff, contractors, and vendors with access). Covers company devices, the control room and OT systems, SaaS and cloud services, and personal devices used for company work.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Manager | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and each year; tracks training |
| IT Manager | Awareness content, reminders, and phishing exercises |
| Plant Manager | Role-based OT security training for operators and I&C staff |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use of corporate devices is allowed if it creates no risk. **Control system computers (HMIs, SCADA servers, the engineering workstation) must be used only for approved control system work**: no email, web browsing, or personal use. (PL-4; CM-7; Rev. 3A Table 9.3a)
4.2 Every workforce member must sign this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4(1))
4.3 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year for all staff, including seasonal staff. Operators, I&C technicians, and anyone with OT access must also complete role-based control system security training **before receiving OT access** and every year after. (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02; Rev. 3A Table 9.3a training)
4.4 **Removable media.** USB drives and other removable media must not be connected to any OT device unless the media is company-issued, scanned at the scanning kiosk immediately before use, and logged. Personal USB devices, including phones for charging, must never be connected to OT equipment. (MP-7; Rev. 3A Form 3 Q17)
4.5 Do not install software, browser extensions, or remote access tools on any company device unless IT (corporate) or the Controls Engineer (OT) approves it. (CM-11)
4.6 Do not open unexpected attachments or links. Report suspicious messages with the Report Phishing button. Never act on a call or email asking you to change gate settings, unit settings, or passwords; verify through the Operations Supervisor using a known number. (AT-2)
4.7 Report lost or stolen devices, badges, and keys, suspicious people or drones, and anything unusual in the control system **immediately** (POL-03 4.3).
4.8 **Generative AI:** use only tools on the approved list. Never enter CEII, security details, control system information, or employee personal information into a tool that is not approved for that level (see P10 and POL-04 4.10). (PL-4)
4.9 Do not photograph or post images of the spillway, gate controls, control room screens, or security equipment, and do not discuss security measures outside the company. Recreation staff answer public questions about the dam using the approved public information sheet only. (PL-4; Rev. 3A 3.2 OPSEC)
4.10 Control room and HMI sign-ins are monitored and reviewed. (AC-8)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the company's disciplinary procedure (POL-01 section 5). Connecting unapproved media to OT equipment is a serious violation. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07) and training records.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.8. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the President for High risk), and expire within 12 months.
