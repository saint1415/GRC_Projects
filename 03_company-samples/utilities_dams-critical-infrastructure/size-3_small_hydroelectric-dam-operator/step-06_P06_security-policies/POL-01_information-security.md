## 1. Purpose
Set up the Cris Santos Company security program for both physical and cyber security, assign who is accountable, and give every other security policy its authority. The program protects the people downstream of the dam, the workforce, the project works, and company information.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (employees, seasonal staff, contractors, and vendors with access) at the Cypress Fork Hydroelectric Project and anywhere they work for the company. Covers the Plant Control and Dam Monitoring System (PCDMS, the operational technology or OT), the corporate network, SaaS and cloud services, and all company information, including critical energy infrastructure information (CEII) and security-sensitive documents.

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 The company must maintain a security program that meets the FERC Security Program for Hydropower Projects for a Security Group 2 dam, including Section 9 (Computer Security and SCADA), and is documented in this policy set, the FERC Security Plan, and the PCDMS System Security Plan. (PM-1; PL-2; GV.PO-01)
4.2 The Compliance and Security Coordinator is the FERC primary security contact; the Operations Supervisors are alternates. The Controls Engineer and IT Manager are the designated cyber leads for OT and IT. These designations must be in writing and confirmed in each annual certification letter. (PM-2; GV.RR-02)
4.3 The Security Assessment and the Security Plan, including its Cyber/SCADA annex and Internal Emergency Response sub-element, must be updated at least once a year and whenever the site, systems, or procedures change. The Security Assessment must be reprinted at least every 10 years. (PL-2; GV.PO-02; Rev. 3A 6.4, 7.5)
4.4 **Accurate certification.** Before the Annual Security Compliance Certification Letter is signed, the Compliance and Security Coordinator and the Controls Engineer must each confirm every statement against dated evidence. A statement that cannot be supported must be reported as a gap with a plan and schedule, not certified. (CA-5; GV.OV-01; Rev. 3A 8.0)
4.5 A risk assessment must be performed at least annually and after major changes using NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1, including the threat scenarios in Rev. 3A section 5.3 (insider, vandalism, armed intruders, vehicle and boat attack, and SCADA controls attack). The Section 9 cyber asset criticality must be re-evaluated at the same time and whenever a cyber asset is added. (RA-3; PM-9; ID.RA-04)
4.6 Risk acceptance authority: the IT Manager or Controls Engineer may accept Low risks; the Vice President of Operations, Moderate; the President, High and Very High. A risk that could cause an uncontrolled release of the reservoir must not be accepted at High; it must be reduced. (PM-9; GV.RM-02)
4.7 Security policies must be reviewed at least annually and after major changes or incidents. (PL-1; GV.PO-02)
4.8 Exceptions to any security policy must be requested in writing, risk-rated, approved per 4.6, recorded in the risk register, and time-limited to 12 months or less. (PL-1)
4.9 **Change control for OT.** No change to gate, unit, instrumentation, or OT network systems (including new remote access) may be made without a written security impact review by the Controls Engineer and approval by the Plant Manager. Changes that affect security must be added to the Security Plan. (CM-3; ID.RA-07; Rev. 3A Table 9.3a system lifecycle)
4.10 **Vendors.** OT vendors must accept written security terms (named technicians, approved remote sessions only, notice of incidents, verified software and firmware) before receiving access. OT vendors must be reviewed each year. (SA-4; SA-9; SR-6; GV.SC-05)
4.11 Security controls must be assessed at least annually (P07), and every weakness must be tracked in the POA&M, which also serves as the Section 9 plan and schedule for FERC. (CA-2; CA-5)
4.12 An OT/IT security meeting (Plant Manager, Controls Engineer, IT Manager, Compliance and Security Coordinator, Chief Dam Safety Engineer) must be held monthly. (PM-2; GV.RR-02; Rev. 3A Table 9.3a coordination)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the company's disciplinary procedure, in proportion to intent and harm: retraining, written warning, suspension, or termination. Vendors who violate these policies lose access. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), FERC inspections, and the reviews in this policy set.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.8. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the President for High risk), and expire within 12 months.
