## 1. Purpose
Sort company information by the harm its loss, change, or disclosure could cause, set simple handling rules for each level, and keep the copies the company needs to rebuild its control system.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members and outside parties who handle company information, in any form: in the suite, the monitoring service, the HMI and PLCs, on laptops and USB drives, on paper, and in vendors' systems.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Office and Compliance Administrator | Owns this policy; keeps the restricted CEII folder and its guest list; keeps the office and SaaS inventory; runs the new-tool questions (4.5) |
| Controls and Electrical Technician | Keeps the OT inventory, the OT USB drive, and the OT copies; runs restore tests with the integrator |
| Plant Superintendent | Approves who outside the company may see Restricted documents; approves new OT tools |
| MSP | Encrypts office laptops, runs the suite backup, and wipes retired office devices as directed |
| All workforce | Handle information according to its level |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company information has three levels:

| Level | Examples | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| **Restricted** | CEII-type information: design drawings, EAP inundation maps, the control network drawing, PLC logic, the HMI project, governor and exciter settings, firewall rules, the SSP, risk register, Security Assessment, and site security plan; passwords and MFA codes; employee Social Security numbers and bank details | Restricted folder or approved OT location only (4.3); named people only; encrypted at rest and in transit; never in personal accounts or public AI tools; label "CEII - Restricted" |
| **Internal** | Instrument readings and trends, operations logs, gate and unit records, contracts, financial records, FERC correspondence that is not security-related | Workforce and approved vendors only; encrypted in transit |
| **Public** | Recreation information, public license documents, the company website | No restriction |

When unsure, treat information as Restricted. (RA-2; ID.AM-07)

4.2 **CEII handling.** The 18 CFR 388.113 designation procedures apply to information submitted to or generated by FERC. The company applies the same care internally to the Restricted examples above. Security information given to FERC must be marked "Privileged - Security Sensitive Material" (Rev. 3A 3.4.3.4). Restricted documents may be shared outside the company only with named people approved by the Plant Superintendent (the integrator, the consulting dam safety engineer, the assessor, emergency agencies for the EAP), never by "anyone with the link" sharing. Printed copies stay in the office safe or the control room. (AC-3; MP-3; PR.DS-01; Form 1 Q22; Rev. 3A 3.2 OPSEC)

4.3 Restricted information may be kept only in: the restricted CEII folder in the suite (named members and named external guests only), the HMI PC and engineering laptop, the encrypted OT copy drives (4.8), the fire-resistant records cabinet in the office, and, for employee information, the HR folder and the payroll service. Laptops must use full-disk encryption. (AC-3; SC-28; SC-8; PR.DS-01; PR.DS-02)

4.4 **Inventory.** The Controls and Electrical Technician keeps a one-page OT inventory (device, location, firmware or software version, owner, and every network connection), and the Office and Compliance Administrator keeps the office device and SaaS list. Both are updated with every change form (POL-02 A.10) and reviewed every July. (CM-8; ID.AM-01; ID.AM-02)

4.5 **New tools and features.** Before buying or switching on any OT device, remote tool, SaaS service, or SaaS feature, including free trials and AI add-ons, the requester must answer five questions on the change form: what data it receives; whether it connects to the control network or could send commands; whether it supports named accounts and MFA; what the vendor's incident notice and data-use terms are; and who supports it. The Office and Compliance Administrator records the answers. The Plant Superintendent approves, and the Owner also approves anything that touches the HPCDMS or receives Restricted information. (SA-4; SA-9; GV.SC-05; Table 9.3a acquisition)

4.6 **AI tools.** Company information may be entered only into AI tools on the approved list kept by the Office and Compliance Administrator. Today the list has one entry: the monitoring service's anomaly detection add-on (P10 AI-001), approved for Internal instrument data, advisory use only, under the P10 conditions. Public or personal AI chatbots may be used only for general drafting with Public information; Internal and Restricted information must never be entered into them. No AI output may be sent back to the control system. (SA-9; PL-4)

4.7 **Removable media.** Only the company's encrypted OT USB drive may be used to move files to or from the HMI PC or the engineering laptop, including by the integrator. It must be scanned on an MSP-protected office PC before each use and kept in the control room when not in use. (MP-7; PR.PS-05)

4.8 **OT copies and backups.** The Controls and Electrical Technician must copy the gate and unit PLC logic, the HMI project, the governor and exciter settings, and the firewall configuration after every change and at least every quarter, onto 2 encrypted drives: one in the office safe and one in the fire-resistant records cabinet. Copies must be checked against the running version, and a restore must be tested at least once a year with the integrator. The MSP backs up the suite nightly with at least 30 days of versions and restores a sample each year. (CP-9; CP-4; PR.DS-11; Table 9.3a restoration and recovery)

4.9 **Disposal.** Devices and media that held Restricted information, including retired HMI PCs, laptops, and USB drives, must be wiped or destroyed by a vendor that gives a certificate of destruction. Paper with Restricted information is shredded. The Office and Compliance Administrator keeps each record. (MP-6; ID.AM-08; Table 9.3a disposal)

4.10 **Retention.** Permanent project records, including design data, instrumentation data, and reports of conditions affecting safety, are kept as 18 CFR 12.12 requires. Security records are kept as POL-02 A.7 requires. (SI-12)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Breaking this policy is handled under POL-02 A.4. Compliance is checked through a quarterly suite sharing report, the quarterly OT copy record, the monthly MSP report, and the annual assessment (P07).

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-02 A.9.
