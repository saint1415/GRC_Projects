## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the company's own records before the BIA, the enterprise risk analysis and the gap analysis began on 2026-06-01, and before Internal Audit's control assessment fieldwork began on 2026-07-13. It covers the organization (8 plants, including the acquired plant PLT-08, 4 DCs, headquarters and 2 colocation sites), the plant OT, cloud and business systems that make, monitor or record food, the vendors that touch them, and the rules that may bind the company and its two external service lines (SL-1 and SL-2). Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (BIA interviews, risk workshops, gap analysis samples and walk-throughs, Internal Audit tests) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

At this size the sources are enterprise systems of record across business units and plants: the identity platform, the plant OT directories and the HR system; the OT asset inventory, OT monitoring and the OT remote access gateway; the cloud consoles, backup accounts and SIEM; the central MES, the plant historians and the food safety records platform; the third-party risk register, the accounts payable vendor master and the contract repository; FSQA document control (HACCP plans, the PLT-07 food defense plan, FDA registration); board and committee records; prior Internal Audit and SOX workpapers; the SEC filings; and regulator correspondence.

Each register row names the owning business unit's system of record and owner. The [asset inventory](asset-inventory.csv) reconciles the OT asset inventory and CMDB with the cloud account inventory, the EDR and endpoint consoles and the refrigeration system register; the [vendor register](vendor-register.csv) reconciles the third-party risk register with the accounts payable vendor master and the contract repository.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analysis (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Identity and access.** Workforce IT accounts at headquarters, the 4 DCs and 7 plants are federated through SSO with MFA registered; PLT-08 users sign in through a legacy directory that is not federated (EV-001). MFA is required for every SSO application, FIDO2 keys for privileged roles and MES approvers, and lockout follows 10 failed sign-ins (EV-002). The 46 privileged MES, cloud and OT engineering accounts are vaulted in PAM, with just-in-time elevation and session recording (EV-003). Joiner, mover and leaver events flow from the HR system, accounts inactive 60 days are disabled, and 4 quarterly certification campaigns for IT systems closed in the last 12 months; the plant-local OT directories at PLT-05 and PLT-08 are not connected to these workflows (EV-004, EV-006). HR holds about 12,000 employees by site; agency temporary workers are listed by agency (EV-005). Each plant has its own OT directory, federated for named engineering access at 6 plants, with about 210 OT accounts holding write access (EV-006). The central MES has about 610 users in 5 roles and about 4,100 formulations; releases need a recipe author and a different FSQA approver and are signed (EV-007). At the HMI, the supervisor role can change cure and brine setpoints within the pushed range with no second-approval step, and override events stay on the HMI with no alert rule (EV-008).

**Endpoints, OT assets and network.** About 6,800 IT endpoints and the OT Windows servers at 6 plants report EDR; the PLT-05 historian and PLT-08 OT hosts have no agent (EV-009). The OT asset inventory holds about 3,300 records, including 118 HMIs and 9 engineering workstations on unsupported operating systems; passive discovery runs at 6 plants, and the OT Security team estimates the inventory at about 91% complete (EV-011). OT monitoring runs at 6 plants; PLT-08 and the PLT-05 engine room segment have no sensors and send no OT logs to the SIEM (EV-012). The OT DMZ and zone firewalls are in place at 6 plants; PLT-05 has no OT DMZ and allows corporate-to-SCADA traffic, and PLT-08 is one flat IT and OT network on a legacy site VPN (EV-013). The OT remote access gateway serves 6 plants; 51 of 65 OT vendors use it and 14 use legacy paths at PLT-05 and PLT-08, where the controls integrator's VPN account has no MFA (EV-014). One refrigeration contractor keeps an always-on cellular modem on the PLT-05 refrigeration controller (EV-015). OT vulnerabilities are identified passively at 6 plants and patched in sanitation windows; PLT-08 OT is not covered (EV-016). OT baselines are documented for 7 plants and allowlisting runs at 6; PLT-08 has no documented OT baseline, and USB storage is not blocked at PLT-05 and PLT-08 (EV-018).

**Cloud, logging, backup and recovery.** Both clouds use account vending, guardrails as code and daily posture scans (EV-019). Cloud backups go to separate accounts with write-once retention and a weekly copy to COLO-2; plant OT backups have weekly offline copies at 7 plants, while PLT-08 backs up to a domain-joined device with no offline copy (EV-020). In the 2026-05-16 tier-1 test the central MES recovered in 7.5 hours against a 4-hour RTO; plant OT restores are recorded at PLT-01, PLT-03, PLT-04 and PLT-06 only (EV-021). HMI override events are not a SIEM source (EV-023). OT cases in SOC case management carry an optional FSQA product-impact field, and PLT-08 events reach the SOC only when escalated by its local IT firm (EV-024). The contingency plan records the manual temperature log procedure as exercised at 3 of 12 sites and manual labeling at 5 of 8 plants (EV-025). Historian audit trails are off at PLT-02, PLT-05 and PLT-08 (EV-026). The records platform is live at 7 plants with named e-signatures; the PLT-08 legacy eHACCP application uses shared accounts and typed initials (EV-027).

**Governance, prior assurance and disclosure.** The policy set in force (POL-01 to POL-05, approved 2025-09) sits in the policy portal with standards, procedures and an exception register (EV-028). The 2025 risk analysis rolled into the ERM register, and the board approved the risk appetite in 2026-02 (EV-029, EV-030). Internal Audit's 2025 assessment and the FY2025 SOX ITGC testing are on file (EV-031). The materiality playbook lists quantitative factors with no method for product loss, recall costs or chargebacks, and the 2025-11 tabletop did not include a production halt with product holds and recalls (EV-032). The FY2025 10-K Item 1C states that vendor remote access to plants goes through the OT gateway (EV-033). The 2026-03-19 technical OT tabletop ran with PLT-01 and PLT-04 without the disclosure committee, and the runbook has no step linking a refrigeration upset to release reporting (EV-034). A cyber insurance policy is in force (EV-037).

**Workforce.** Plant workforce security awareness completion is 78%, with multilingual modules launched in 2026-03 (EV-039). At PLT-07, agency temporary workers are trained by their agencies, which send the training records to the plant (EV-040).

**Third parties, contracts and service lines.** The vendor register lists about 1,400 vendors, 65 with OT remote access; 27 of 38 tier-1 OT and cold-chain vendors (71%) have a review in the last 12 months (EV-042). Two refrigeration contractor contracts contain no security terms, the cold-chain monitoring contract states no recovery time, and the e-commerce contract has no breach notice term (EV-044). The cold-chain vendor's SOC 2 Type 2 report lists an exception on alert delivery (EV-045). SL-1 holds a SOC 2 Type 1 report (Security, Availability) as of 2025-12-31 (EV-046). Two national retail customers require SL-2 to provide a SOC 2 Type 2 report including Confidentiality and Processing Integrity by the end of 2027 (EV-047).

**Facilities and disposal.** Badge access, quarterly restricted room reviews and CCTV on the PLT-07 actionable process steps are recorded (EV-049). Generators hold about 72 hours of fuel, with priority fuel contracts at 9 of 12 sites (EV-050). Certificates of destruction are on file (EV-051).

**Business volume and structure.** FY2025 revenue was about $4.8 billion over 255 production days, about $18.8 million per production day (EV-052). About $240 million of refrigerated and frozen inventory sits at the 12 sites on a typical day (EV-053). PLT-08 was acquired in 2025-10 and remains on its legacy directory, network, recipe system and eHACCP application until integration, due 2027-03-31 (EV-055).

**Food safety and regulatory records.** All 8 plants hold federal grants of inspection with HACCP plans, SSOPs and recall procedures; 2025-2026 mock recalls traced lots within 4 hours at 7 plants and in 9 hours at PLT-08 (EV-056). PLT-07 has been FDA-registered since 2022-03 (EV-057). Its food defense plan was reanalyzed in 2025-03; the vulnerability assessment covers physical and local HMI access and has no step for recipe release or setpoint override (EV-058). The central MES migration (2025-06 to 2026-02) has no food defense reanalysis screening entry in its change records (EV-059). Counsel's applicability memo is in EV-061, and the results are in [`obligations-register.csv`](obligations-register.csv).

**Other operating records and AI.** About 12,000 employee records and about 140,000 consumer accounts are held in the ERP and payroll SaaS and the online store (EV-065). The cold-chain service covers about 3,400 sensors at 12 sites and 380 trailers through one vendor (EV-067). The AI council register lists 10 use cases, 6 with completed council review; testing records for AI-001 at 4 of its 5 plants, AI-002 and AI-007 cite vendor data (EV-068). Unapproved public generative AI domains are blocked at the web gateway from 2026-05 (EV-069).
