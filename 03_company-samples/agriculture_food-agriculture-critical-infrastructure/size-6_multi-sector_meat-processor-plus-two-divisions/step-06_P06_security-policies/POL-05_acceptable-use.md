## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce uses group systems, OT equipment, and information.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members in every division and in corporate shared services, including agency temporary workers, on any device used for group work.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow these rules; complete training; report incidents |
| Managers and supervisors | Make sure staff, including agency workers, complete training before they work |
| Group HR | Tracks acknowledgments and training completion |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Workforce members may use group systems and information only for their job. (PL-4; AC-8)

4.2 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy and their division supplement at hire and every year. (PL-4; PR.AT-01; PCI DSS Req. 12.2)

4.3 Every workforce member must complete security awareness training at hire and every year. Workers at actionable process steps at Plant 6, including agency workers, must complete food defense awareness training before their first shift. Cashiers must complete card skimming awareness. (AT-2; PR.AT-01; 21 CFR 121.4(b)(2); PCI DSS Req. 12.6)

4.4 Controls engineers, FSQA staff, and administrators must complete role-based training before receiving their access. (AT-3; PR.AT-02)

4.5 Suspected incidents, including unexplained setpoint or formulation values, must be reported to the group SOC within 1 hour (POL-03 4.1). (IR-6)

4.6 Personal devices and removable media must never be connected to HMIs, PLCs, SCADA, MES, or payment terminals. Only group-managed engineering laptops and scanned media may be used in OT. (AC-19; AC-20; MP-7)

4.7 **Generative AI.** Only AI tools on the group approved-tools list may be used for group work. Restricted information (formulations, food defense plans, personal and cardholder data) must never be entered into public AI tools. (PL-4; PL-4(1))

4.8 **Cameras and images.** Images from AI vision inspection and store camera analytics may be used only for the approved purpose in the AI inventory (P10). They must not be used to monitor or evaluate individual workers or to identify shoppers. (PL-4; PT-3)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.7.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.10.
