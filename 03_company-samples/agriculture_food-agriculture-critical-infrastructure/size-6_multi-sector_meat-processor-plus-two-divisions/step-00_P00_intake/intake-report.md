## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the group's own records before any assessment work began on 2026-05-04. It covers the legal entities, the IT and OT systems in each division and in corporate shared services (plant control systems, the cold-chain platform, DC automation, store and payment systems), the data they hold (food safety and food defense records, formulations, 3PL customer records, cardholder data, loyalty, employee and driver data), the suppliers that touch them, and the rules that may bind each division and the group. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Items are grouped by division: the title of each row starts with Group, Meat Processing, Food Distribution or Grocery Retail. Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (interviews, record samples, tests) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement or the CSF 2.0 benchmark is decided in the P03 gap analyses and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Entities, scale and applicability facts.** The holding company is an SEC registrant with three wholly owned operating subsidiaries; corporate shared services sit in the parent, and group internal audit reports to the board audit committee (EV-001). The workforce is 45,000: Meat Processing about 14,000, Food Distribution about 7,000, Grocery Retail about 21,000 and corporate about 3,000 (EV-002). FY2025 revenue was about $18.0 billion: Meat Processing about $7.0 billion in external sales over about 250 production days, Food Distribution about $5.0 billion and Grocery Retail about $6.0 billion, of which online orders are about 9% (EV-003). Group legal reports no federal contracts, no MTSA-regulated facility, no business in California, no in-store pharmacies, no child-directed services and no online marketplace; the co-branded card is issued by a partner bank (EV-004). The Form 10-K Item 106 text describes group policies, the SOC and board oversight, and does not describe plant OT or division programs (EV-005).

**Policies and common controls.** The 2025 group policies are aligned to CSF 2.0. The Meat Processing supplement was aligned in 2025-10; the Food Distribution standards are dated 2023-04 with no named owner or review date (EV-006, EV-063). The common control catalog lists 82 controls; inheritance is recorded for Meat Processing and for Grocery Retail (through the ROC responsibility matrix), and no Food Distribution matrix was provided (EV-007).

**Group identity and directory.** MFA is required for all workforce access to IT systems, with phishing-resistant authenticators for administrators and OT engineers (EV-008). Joiner, mover and leaver workflows run from HR events, quarterly certification covers IT applications, and privileged access is just in time through PAM; 34 privileged directory service accounts are not vaulted in PAM or certified (EV-009). OT servers at Plants 2 and 5, the DC automation servers and the store back-office servers are joined to the corporate domain; Plants 1, 3, 4 and 6 run a separate OT domain; the last directory restore test is from 2025 (EV-010).

**Group security operations.** The SOC runs 24x7. EDR covers IT and OT Windows hosts at four plants; OT monitoring feeds come from Plants 1, 3, 4 and 6 and DC-1 and DC-3 only; no cold-chain, WMS or DC automation source is listed; data loss prevention covers email only (EV-011). Food Distribution cases use the division's own severity scale (EV-012). Incident response plan v4 is dated 2025-11-30; the OT ransomware playbook has no product hold step, and no exercise has included a product hold or an FSIS notice decision (EV-013). The notification matrix is a draft with no recorded exercise and no 3PL or independent grocer notice terms (EV-014). OT at Plants 2 and 5 has no scan or passive matching results (EV-015).

**Cloud and colocation.** Provider A hosts the landing zone, the data platform, SYS-M5, the recipe library and SYS-D3; provider B hosts SYS-R2, the DR replicas and the immutable backup vault; guardrails apply to every division account (EV-016). Firewalls deny by default between division accounts; private links are encrypted; Plants 3 and 4 each have one WAN carrier (EV-017). Endpoints accept TLS 1.2 or higher only (EV-018); data stores use customer-managed keys (EV-019). Cloud backups are immutable and restore-tested each quarter; the last SYS-M5 failover exercise is from 2025 (EV-020). Logs are archived write-once for 6 years (EV-021). The standards library holds the logging standard with an OT addendum, the OT reference architecture and the OT remote access standard (EV-022).

**OT security services and the cold-chain platform.** SYS-G5 is deployed for Plants 1, 3, 4 and 6 and DC-1 and DC-3, not for Plants 2 and 5 or DC-2, DC-4 and DC-5 (EV-023, EV-024). About 21,000 cold-chain sensors send alerts through one alert integration server in the colocation data center; gateways at DCs and stores sit on general networks; no alert feed reaches the SIEM (EV-025). The server's DR runbook is a draft and no failover test is recorded (EV-026). The vendor contract gained group security terms in 2025; the last recorded review of the vendor's SOC 2 report is from 2024 (EV-027).

**Vendors, ERP, workforce, board and records.** No review is recorded for the telematics vendor, the DC automation vendor, the Plants 2 and 5 integrator, the refrigeration contractors or the camera analytics vendor (EV-028); SOC 2 reports are on file for the cloud providers, the identity, ERP and WMS vendors (EV-029). The ERP serves all divisions with a contractual 1-hour RPO (EV-030). Training covers all workforce; incident response training is not recorded for plant FSQA managers (EV-031). Terminations disable accounts within 4 hours (EV-032). The board risk committee receives quarterly cyber and food safety reports (EV-033); the 2025 registers and risk strategy are on file (EV-034); cyber insurance is in force (EV-035); food defense records are kept 3 years (EV-036). HR file shares and payroll exports hold Social Security numbers and bank details (EV-038).

**AI tools.** AI vision inspection runs on 7 lines at Plants 1, 3 and 4 since 2025; a predictive maintenance model, WMS and ERP forecasting, TMS route optimization, loyalty offers, a scheduling engine and a generative AI assistant pilot (1,500 users) are in use; camera analytics run at 12 stores since 2026-04-06, with no privacy review on file; the forecasting and routing tools are in no AI register (EV-037, EV-078).

**Meat Processing.** Six further-processing plants hold federal grants of inspection; Plants 2 and 5 were acquired in 2024; Plant 6 added a plant-based line in 2024 (EV-042) and is the only FDA-registered plant (EV-043). Plants 2 and 5 have flat IT and OT networks with no zone drawings; the other four plants have an OT DMZ and a separate OT domain (EV-044). The six plants have about 2,100 OT devices and 310 HMIs; 24 HMIs at Plants 2 and 5 run an unsupported operating system, and those plants' inventories are 2024 spreadsheets (EV-045). HMIs at every plant use shared operator logins, and MES at Plants 2 and 5 uses shared logins; the other four plants use named MES accounts with two-person formulation approval; recipe library administrators are cloud administrators (EV-046). Historian audit trails are disabled at Plants 2 and 5 (EV-047). Plants 2 and 5 back up OT to domain-joined storage with no restore test (EV-048). The 2025 Plant 6 MES upgrade has no food defense reanalysis record, and the integrator runs changes at Plants 2 and 5 in its own system (EV-049). Plants 2 and 5 have no OT patch, allowlisting or removable media records (EV-050). The integrator uses a shared always-on VPN account without MFA, a refrigeration contractor keeps an always-on modem on the Plant 5 controller, and the AI vision vendor's remote tool is always on at Plants 1, 3 and 4 (EV-051). The integrator and refrigeration contracts have no security terms (EV-052). Five plants hold anhydrous ammonia above the threshold (EV-053). HACCP plans, recall procedures and mock recall reports are on file; the unforeseen-deviation procedure and the contingency plans describe a single-plant outage (EV-054, EV-055). The Plant 6 food defense plan (2024) evaluates brine dosing, CIP valves and recipe setpoints for physical access only, lists records review as its only verification, and has not been revised since the 2025 MES upgrade (EV-056). Agency workers are trained in their first week (EV-057). Edge servers for AI vision sit on line control networks (EV-058). Integrator staff work unescorted after hours at Plants 2 and 5 (EV-059). Formulation sheets are readable by all plant office staff (EV-060). The recipe library logs every change and sends no change alert to FSQA (EV-061).

**Food Distribution.** Five DCs are FDA-registered and hold only packaged food; the FSIS registration file lists DC-5's previous address (EV-062). The 2023 standards set 30-day log retention, next-business-day termination and monthly temperature record review (EV-063). DC automation servers are on the corporate domain, back up to domain-joined storage with no restore test, and keep logs 30 days; the automation vendor uses its own remote tool at DC-2, DC-4 and DC-5 (EV-064). Dispatchers share telematics portal logins, and the portal keeps temperature history 90 days (EV-065). DC-2, DC-4 and DC-5 review temperature records monthly, and the DC rooms have no alarm path independent of SYS-G6 (EV-066). The 2021 sanitary transportation procedures do not address telematics outages (EV-067). About 60 3PL agreements sit on four contract versions, and 14 brands asked for a SOC 2 report by the end of 2027 (EV-068). The 3PL portal separates customers by role and row-level security; authorization tests run at major releases; EDI credentials are rarely rotated (EV-069).

**Grocery Retail.** The 2025 ROC is compliant with two compensating controls (EV-070). The 2026 segmentation test found a path from the store Wi-Fi management network to POS lanes in one store design used at 12 stores (EV-071, EV-072). P2PE is live in 90 stores and 30 stores run older terminals outside it (EV-073). The payment page script inventory is a draft with about 70% of scripts justified (EV-074). The program documents, including the 2026 scope document, do not mention the Wi-Fi management path (EV-075). Operating records show no stored PAN and 11 store-server service accounts with static passwords (EV-076). Customer MFA on the loyalty platform is optional, and reporting exports to analysts are untokenized (EV-077). The privacy notice does not mention camera analytics (EV-078). 40 stores keep grinding logs on scales with no central copy (EV-079).
