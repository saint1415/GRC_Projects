At the Sole Proprietorship tier the shop keeps **one** consolidated policy, because one person writes, follows, and checks it (`01_company-sizes/tier-project-scaling.csv`, t1 P06). This file is kept so the folder has the standard policy set names. It holds no separate rules.

**Where the incident response rules are:**

- POL-01 section 10 (Incident response): runbook and printed contacts (10.1), same-day incident log (10.2), product held first when temperatures, programs, or cure may be affected (10.3), notices per the P08 matrix (10.4), no ransom without counsel and an OFAC check (10.5), annual walkthrough before the busy season (10.6).
- POL-01 section 11: contingency, hourly manual temperature readings when alerts are down, and the storm procedure.
- The procedure itself is the P08 runbook (`../step-08_P08_incident-response-runbook/ir-runbook.md`).
- SP 800-53 IR-1 is met by POL-01 as a whole.

Traceability for every statement is in `policy-control-map.csv`.
