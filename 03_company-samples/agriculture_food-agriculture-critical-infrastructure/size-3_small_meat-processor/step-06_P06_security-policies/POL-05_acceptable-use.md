## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules (provided in English and Spanish) for how the workforce uses company systems, plant equipment controls, and data.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (owners, employees, temporary and agency workers, and contractors) at the Florida plant and outlet store. Covers all systems and data, including process control systems, cloud and SaaS services, and systems that vendors operate or maintain for the company.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Manager | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and annually, including from agency workers; keeps training records |
| IT Manager | Security awareness content, reminders, and phishing exercises |
| FSQA Manager | Food defense awareness content for workers at actionable process steps |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use of office computers is allowed if it does not involve Restricted data or create risk. HMIs, SCADA, and plant terminals are for production use only. (PL-4)
4.2 Every workforce member, including temporary and agency workers, must sign this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4(1))
4.3 Security and food defense awareness training is required at hire and every year for **everyone**, including production, sanitation, maintenance, and temporary workers. Workers at actionable process steps must also receive step-specific instruction. Training must be recorded with date, type, and names. (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; 21 CFR 121.4(b)(2), (e))
4.4 Do not use another person's HMI, recipe, or records login, and do not share yours. Sign out of shared terminals when you leave. (AC-11; PL-4)
4.5 Do not connect USB drives, phones, laptops, or any other device to an HMI, PLC, or plant network unless the Controls Engineer has approved it and the media has been scanned at the kiosk. (MP-7; CM-11)
4.6 Do not open unexpected attachments or links. Report suspicious messages with the Report Phishing button. (AT-2)
4.7 Report immediately anything unusual at a process step: an unknown person, a broken seal or lock, an HMI screen or setpoint you did not expect, or a monitoring alarm that stopped (POL-03 4.2).
4.8 **Generative AI:** use only tools on the approved list. Never paste or upload formulations, the food defense plan, PLC programs, employee data, or customer data into a tool that is not approved. The AI vision inspection system may be used only as described in its approval (see P10). (PL-4)
4.9 Photos and video inside the plant require supervisor approval. Never photograph restricted areas, HMI screens, or formulation sheets. (PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement (POL-01 section 4.9). Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07) and training records.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.8. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the majority owner for High risk), and expire within 12 months.
