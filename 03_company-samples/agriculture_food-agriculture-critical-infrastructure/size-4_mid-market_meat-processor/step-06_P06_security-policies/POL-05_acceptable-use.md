## 1. Purpose
Set out what every worker, including agency temporaries and contractors, must and must not do with company systems, control systems, and information.

## 2. Scope
Everyone who uses a company account, a shared production-floor terminal or tablet, an HMI, or a company device, at both plants and the corporate offices.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Director | Rules of behavior at onboarding (including staffing agencies); acknowledgment records |
| Security Manager | Training content, phishing simulations, monitoring |
| Supervisors | Make sure floor workers and temporaries know the reporting rules |
| All workforce | Follow this policy |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Every worker must acknowledge these rules at hire and each year. Floor workers and agency temporaries acknowledge a one-page plant version at orientation. (PL-4; GV.PO-01)
4.2 **Security awareness for everyone.** Workers with accounts complete annual training and phishing simulations. Production, sanitation, and maintenance workers and temporaries receive a short security module at orientation and in the annual food defense refresher: report tampering signs, unknown devices, and odd HMI behavior; never share passwords; no USB drives on plant equipment. (AT-2; PR.AT-01; C-FOOD-AG-R01 (benchmark: 21 CFR 121.4(b)(2)))
4.3 Controls engineers, MES approvers, and refrigeration operators receive role-based security training each year. (AT-3; PR.AT-02)
4.4 Do not share passwords, badges, or MFA devices, or sign in for someone else on an HMI or terminal. (PL-4; PR.AA-01)
4.5 Do not connect USB drives, phones, laptops, or other devices to HMIs, PLCs, engineering workstations, or OT network ports unless the Controls Engineering Manager has approved the device for that task. (MP-7)
4.6 Use only company-approved software and services. Do not disable EDR or other security tools. (SI-3)
4.7 **AI tools.** Use only AI tools on the approved list (STD-09). Never enter Restricted data (formulations, food defense plans, employee personal information, credentials) into an AI tool that is not approved for it. AI output that affects food safety, process control, or decisions about people must be reviewed by a person who owns the decision. (PL-4; GV.PO-01)
4.8 Mobile devices that access company email or apps must be enrolled in mobile device management. (AC-19)
4.9 Report lost devices, phishing, and anything suspicious immediately (POL-03 4.2).

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 5. Staffing agencies must apply these rules to their workers by contract.

## 6. Exceptions
Follow POL-01 section 4.8.
