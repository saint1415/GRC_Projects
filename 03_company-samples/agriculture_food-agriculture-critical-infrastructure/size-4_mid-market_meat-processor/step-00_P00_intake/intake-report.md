## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the company's own records before any assessment work began on 2026-07-06. It covers the organization, both plants and the corporate functions, the IT and OT systems that run and record production and cold storage, the suppliers that touch them, and the rules that may bind the company. At this size the sources are systems of record: the identity provider, the HR system, the endpoint and EDR consoles, the Plant 1 passive OT sensor, the cloud organization console, the SIEM, the ERP accounts payable vendor master, the contract register, the FSQA and engineering libraries, prior risk assessments and internal audit reports, scan results and the incident log. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (interviews, samples, tests, walkthroughs) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analysis (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Users and access.** The identity provider holds about 560 workforce accounts (office, supervisors, maintenance, FSQA, IT), each with MFA registered. Email, the ERP, the WMS, the cloud console, the records application and VPN sign in through it, and no OT system does (EV-001). MFA is required for all workforce accounts; non-privileged users use push with number matching, and cloud administrators use security keys (EV-002). HR lists about 850 employees and recorded 74 terminations and 31 transfers of identity provider account holders in the 12 months to 2026-06-30 (EV-003). The HR feed creates, disables and regroups identity provider accounts; OT accounts are outside it (EV-004). The last access review was the annual campaign in January 2026, and it did not include OT accounts (EV-005). 22 privileged identity provider and cloud accounts are named; just-in-time elevation covers cloud and domain administrators only (EV-006). HMIs at both plants use shared operator logins, and 47 OT privileged or engineering accounts (Plant 1 31, Plant 2 16) use passwords only (EV-007). The prior owner's directory still runs Plant 2 OT logins and file shares and holds 9 accounts with no identified owner (EV-008). The records application keeps an audit trail, signs entries with typed initials and has one shared administrator account (EV-009).

**Devices and network.** The endpoint console lists about 520 laptops and desktops and 160 shared floor terminals and tablets, with EDR on all managed endpoints (Plant 2 since 2025-11) and full-disk encryption on laptops. OT Windows hosts have no EDR agent (EV-010). The Plant 1 passive sensor (installed 2026-01) lists about 180 PLCs and 64 HMIs, about 75% of Plant 1 OT devices by comparison with maintenance records, and its alerts stay on a local console reviewed weekly (EV-011). Plant 2 has no company OT inventory; integrator and maintenance records list about 60 PLCs and 22 HMIs (EV-012). 9 HMIs (6 Plant 1, 3 Plant 2) and 2 engineering workstations run operating systems past vendor support, and OT hosts are patched at most once a year (EV-013). Plant 1 has an OT DMZ (2024) with deny-by-default rules. The Plant 2 office, OT and cold-chain gateways share one network with allow-all internal routing (EV-014). Plant 2 cold-chain gateways and scanners use the office Wi-Fi, and Plant 2 has one ISP (EV-015). Plant 1 vendors connect through a remote access gateway with MFA and recording, and 2 vendor accounts stay enabled between sessions (EV-016). The Plant 2 integrator uses a shared, non-expiring VPN account without MFA, and the Plant 2 refrigeration contractor keeps an always-on cellular modem on the controller (EV-017). Scans in 2026 Q1-Q2 recorded 64 Critical and High findings; OT hosts are outside the scan scope (EV-018).

**Cloud, backups, monitoring and OT records.** The landing zone has 4 accounts with organization guardrails, company-managed keys and a 2-year write-once log bucket (EV-020). Cloud workloads back up daily to a second-region backup account with 35-day write-once retention (EV-021). Plant 1 SCADA and MES backups go nightly to an online OT backup server with no write-once setting; the PLC repository covers about 75% of Plant 1 PLCs; Plant 2 PLC programs and blend recipes sit on the integrator's laptops and in the HMIs, and the Plant 2 label server has no backup job (EV-022). Restore tests are on record for the Plant 1 SCADA server (2025) and the records application (2026-04) only (EV-023). The SIEM receives identity provider, cloud, firewall, email and EDR logs and no OT logs; the MSSP contract requires a call within 30 minutes of a high-severity alert (EV-024). The Plant 2 historian audit trail is off (EV-025). Plant 1 MES formulation releases need an author and a separate FSQA approver (EV-026). The change system holds 186 Plant 1 and 41 Plant 2 OT change records for 2026 Q1-Q2, and the ticket template has no food safety or PSM review field (EV-027). The cold-chain service has about 260 sensors; Plant 1 alerts escalate through three roles, while Plant 2 alerts go by SMS to one supervisor (EV-028).

**Programs and documents.** A program charter (2024) is led by the vCISO, with quarterly reports to the audit committee since 2025 and a 2025 internal IT audit (EV-029). Five security policies date from 2024; no OT standard, configuration standard or AI policy was returned (EV-030). The 2024 incident response plan covers IT incidents, with no OT playbook and no product hold, FSIS notice or PSM emergency step; the 2024 IT disaster recovery plan covers the cloud workloads (EV-031). The last enterprise risk assessment is dated July 2025 (EV-032). The incident log holds 17 security incidents, all IT events (EV-033). Account holders take annual awareness training and phishing simulations (June 2026 click rate 9.4%); floor workers and temporaries receive food defense awareness only (EV-034). The cyber insurance policy has a $15 million limit and a $500,000 retention, with notice through the carrier hotline first (EV-035). HR files hold the code of conduct, job descriptions, checklists and staffing agency contracts (EV-036).

**Food safety and process safety.** Both plants hold federal grants of inspection, make only FSIS-inspected meat products and have no FDA registration (EV-037). HACCP plans, hazard analyses (reassessed 2026-02 and 2026-04) and validation files are current; no reassessment record exists for the 2025-09 AI-001 deployment (EV-038). Written SSOPs, recall procedures and the Plant 1 Listeria program are in place; mock recalls in 2026-02 and 2026-06 traced lots in 2.5 and 3 hours; 7 hold-and-test events occurred in 2026 H1 (EV-039 to EV-041). The Plant 1 food defense plan (2022) assesses physical access, and Plant 2 uses the prior owner's 2019 plan (EV-042). Plant 1 holds about 42,000 lb and Plant 2 about 18,000 lb of ammonia under PSM and RMP Program 3; the last PHAs are 2022 and 2021, and the Plant 2 compliance audit by the prior owner has 3 findings open (EV-043). 24 management of change records were logged in 2025-2026 (EV-044). The Plant 2 ammonia detection panel and cold-chain gateways are not on generator circuits (EV-045). Injector 1 has a hardwired CIP interlock and Injector 2 does not (EV-046).

**Suppliers and contracts.** The accounts payable vendor master lists the cloud, SaaS, OT, AI and service vendors named in the vendor register (EV-047). The contract register flags 26 vendors with OT, cloud or data access; the contracts for the 2 controls integrators, 2 refrigeration contractors, the cold-chain vendor and the AI vision vendor have no security or incident notice terms (EV-048). SOC 2 reports are on file for the cloud provider, identity provider, ERP, WMS, MSSP and HR SaaS vendors, and none from the cold-chain or AI vision vendors (EV-049, EV-050). The largest customer's 2026 supply agreement requires a food defense plan, 24-hour event notice and a SOC 2 Type 2 report on the portal and EDI services by 2027-12-31 (EV-051). The portal serves 38 customer organizations with MFA enforced since 2026-06, and about 1,900 EDI documents pass the gateway each week (EV-052).

**Facilities, media and file shares.** Badge access controls plant entrances and the Plant 1 dosing skid and OT server rooms; Plant 2 refrigeration contractors hold badge access to the engine room (EV-053). Cameras cover docks, entrances and the Plant 1 dosing skid room, and none covers Plant 2 production (EV-054). Destruction certificates are on file (EV-055). The Plant 2 general share, open to all Plant 2 office staff, holds the Plant 2 food defense plan, formulation copies and HR files (EV-056).

**Business volume.** Sales were about $480 million in the 12 months to 2026-06-30: Plant 1 about $330 million and Plant 2 about $150 million over about 250 production days. Cooler and freezer inventory is about $9 million at Plant 1 and $3 million at Plant 2 on a typical day (EV-057).

**AI tools.** Four AI systems are in use and one is proposed: AI vision inspection on Plant 1 Lines 5-7 (since 2025-09), ERP demand forecasting, refrigeration predictive maintenance (since 2026-03), the HR SaaS applicant ranking feature (turned on by the vendor in 2026-04), and a proposed enterprise generative AI assistant. Staff report using public generative AI tools. No AI policy or approved-tools list was found (EV-058). The AI vision vendor's remote tool is always on, and 2 model updates in 2026 arrived without notice (EV-059).

**Reconciliation across sources.**
- **Workforce.** HR lists about 850 employees (EV-003); about 560 hold identity provider accounts (EV-001). The rest are production, sanitation and warehouse workers who use shared floor terminals and HMIs. The 74 terminations and 31 transfers are the HR records matched to identity provider account holders, and they are the P07 sampling populations.
- **Endpoints.** The endpoint console (about 520 laptops and desktops, about 400 of them laptops, and 160 floor terminals and tablets) matches the counts in the SSP and the P07 laptop population (EV-010).
- **OT devices.** Plant 1: about 180 PLCs and 64 HMIs from the sensor, about 75% of the devices in maintenance records (EV-011). Plant 2: about 60 PLCs and 22 HMIs from integrator and maintenance records only (EV-012). Together, 86 HMIs. The unsupported list (EV-013) adds 6 Plant 1 and 3 Plant 2 HMIs to 9, which matches both sources.
- **Vendors.** The contract register flags 26 vendors with OT, cloud or data access (EV-048). [`vendor-register.csv`](vendor-register.csv) names 18 of them and adds a summary row for the other 8, so it adds up to 26. Other paid suppliers from accounts payable (EV-047) are listed after them.
- **AI tools.** Two AI vendors appear in accounts payable (AI vision and refrigeration analytics). Forecasting is a module of the ERP subscription, the applicant ranking feature came from the HR SaaS release notes, and the assistant is a proposal. Public tool use comes from the survey only (EV-047, EV-058). [`asset-inventory.csv`](asset-inventory.csv) lists all six entries.
