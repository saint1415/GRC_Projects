At the Micro tier the shop keeps three core policies. The workforce use rules that an Acceptable Use Policy would hold are short enough to sit with the access rules everyone already reads, so they live in **POL-02 Part C. Workforce use rules**, with the AI tool and USB stick rules in POL-04:

| Essential rule | Where it lives | Duty or benchmark it supports |
|---|---|---|
| Use shop systems for shop work; look only at what your job needs | POL-02 C.1 | CSF 2.0 PR.AT-01 |
| No company information in personal accounts; no personal USB sticks or phones on shop equipment | POL-02 C.2 | FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(iii) |
| Approved AI tools only; never FCI, the rewind library, or employee data in any AI tool | POL-04 4.7 | FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(iii); customer confidentiality terms |
| Shop-owned, scanned USB sticks only on the test PC, oven HMI, and winding machine | POL-04 4.9 | FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(xv) |
| Lock screens; never share passwords or MFA codes | POL-02 C.3 | CSF 2.0 PR.AA-01 |
| Training at hire and yearly; phishing simulations | POL-02 C.4 | CSF 2.0 PR.AT-01 |
| Report suspected incidents at once | POL-02 C.5 and POL-03 4.2 | CSF 2.0 RS.MA-02 |
| Signed acknowledgment at hire and after each annual update | POL-02 C.6 | CSF 2.0 GV.PO-01 |
| Call back before any bank detail change | POL-02 C.7 | CSF 2.0 PR.AT-01 |

Traceability for these rules is in `policy-control-map.csv` under POL-02 and POL-04.
