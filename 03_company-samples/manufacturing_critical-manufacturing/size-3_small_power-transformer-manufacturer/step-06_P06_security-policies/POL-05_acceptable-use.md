## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how the workforce and contractors use company systems, plant equipment, devices, and data.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (owners, employees, temporary workers, and contractors) on the Florida campus and in the field. Covers office IT, plant control systems (OT), the high-voltage test bay, cloud and SaaS services, and systems that service providers operate for the company. It applies to all company information, customer information shared under NDA, and federal contract information (FCI).

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Manager | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and annually; tracks training for office, production, and field staff |
| IT Manager | Awareness content, reminders, phishing exercises, approved AI tools list, web filtering |
| Shift supervisors | Deliver short security modules in shift meetings; enforce plant-floor media rules |
| Maintenance Manager | Makes sure OEM technicians follow the media rules during service visits |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use of office devices is allowed if it does not involve company data or create risk. Plant-floor equipment (HMIs, kiosks, test PCs) is for production use only. (PL-4)

4.2 Every workforce member, including production workers and field technicians, must acknowledge this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4)

4.3 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year for everyone. Production workers receive short modules in shift meetings. Email users also receive monthly reminders and at least quarterly phishing exercises. (AT-2; PR.AT-01)

4.4 Lock your screen when you step away. Never share your kiosk PIN or badge. (AC-11)

4.5 Do not install software or browser extensions unless IT approves it. Do not connect USB drives, phones, or laptops to HMIs, kiosks, test PCs, or controllers unless the media was first scanned at the plant scanning station. OEM technicians follow the same rule. (CM-11; MP-7; MA-3(2); PR.PS-05)

4.6 Do not open unexpected attachments or links. Report suspicious messages with the Report Phishing button. Always confirm supplier bank-detail changes by phone using a number already on file. (AT-2)

4.7 Report lost or stolen devices, misdirected drawings, and anything suspicious on a computer or machine immediately (POL-03 4.2).

4.8 **Generative AI.** Use only tools on the approved list (P10). Never paste Restricted data, customer drawings, or FCI into a public generative AI tool. A person must review AI output before it is used in a design, a quote, or a customer document. (PL-4; AC-20; FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(iii))

4.9 Do not post photos of HMIs, drawings, or plant layouts, or any information about the federal project, on public sites or social media without approval from the Contracts and Compliance Manager. (AC-22; FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(iv))

4.10 Field laptops are assigned to named technicians. Do not save utility or company passwords in browsers; use the company password manager. (IA-5; AC-17)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 statement 4.12. Consequences range from retraining to termination of employment or contract, depending on intent and harm. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), access reviews, and the quarterly obligations register review.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 statement 4.11. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the President for High risk), and expire within 12 months. Exceptions for plant equipment that cannot technically meet a statement (for example, an HMI without individual accounts) must name the compensating controls.
