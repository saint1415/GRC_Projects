## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how workforce members use company systems, plant equipment, and AI tools, and what training they receive.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members, including production workers, field technicians, contractors, and temporary staff, on any company device or account.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Director | Owns this policy with the Security Manager; acknowledgments and training records |
| Security Manager | Training content, phishing exercises, approved-tools list |
| Plant Managers and shift supervisors | Shift-briefing modules and plant-floor rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use of office devices is allowed if it creates no risk and involves no company data. Plant equipment (HMIs, kiosks, test PCs, engineering workstations) is for production use only. (PL-4; GV.PO-01)

4.2 Every workforce member, including production workers and field technicians, must acknowledge this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4; PR.AT-01)

4.3 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year for everyone. Production workers receive short modules in shift briefings. Email users also receive phishing exercises at least quarterly. (AT-2; PR.AT-01)

4.4 Role-based security training is required each year for administrators, controls staff, test engineers, field technicians (including utility access rules), and software developers. (AT-3; PR.AT-02)

4.5 Users must not install software on company devices; software comes from the service desk or the approved catalog. (CM-11; PR.PS-05)

4.6 USB storage and personal devices must not be connected to plant equipment. OEM media must be scanned at the plant scanning kiosk before use. (MP-7; PR.PS-01)

4.7 Company data must be used only on company-managed devices or approved external systems; FCI may not be processed on personal devices or personal cloud accounts. (AC-20; PR.DS-10)

4.8 **AI tools.** Only AI tools on the approved list may be used for company work. Restricted data and FCI may be entered only into tools approved for them (today, the enterprise assistant AI-004). Personal accounts on public AI services must not be used for company work. A qualified person must review any AI output used in a design, quote, customer document, or decision about a person. (PL-4; AC-20; GV.PO-01)

4.9 Users must report lost devices, suspicious messages, and anything unusual on plant equipment immediately (POL-03 4.2). (IR-6; RS.MA-02)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.13. Compliance is verified through acknowledgment and training records, phishing results, web filtering reports, and the annual control assessment (P07: AT-2).

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.12.
