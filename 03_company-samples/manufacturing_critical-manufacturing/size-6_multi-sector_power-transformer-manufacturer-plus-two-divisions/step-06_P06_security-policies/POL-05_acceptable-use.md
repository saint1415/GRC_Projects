## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce uses group systems, plant and utility equipment, and customer systems.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members in every division and in corporate shared services, on any device used for group work, and when working at customer and client sites.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow these rules; complete training; report incidents |
| Managers | Make sure staff complete training and acknowledgments |
| Group HR | Tracks acknowledgments and training completion |
| Division security and compliance leads | Set role-based training content for their divisions |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Workforce members must use group systems and information only as their job requires. (PL-4; AC-8)

4.2 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy and their division supplement at hire and every year. (PL-4; PR.AT-01)

4.3 Every workforce member must complete security awareness training at hire and every year. The group sends monthly reminders and runs quarterly phishing exercises. (AT-2; PR.AT-01)

4.4 **Role-based training.** Before receiving access, these roles must complete role-based training: plant controls staff and OT engineers (the group OT security course), integration hub and GEPS administrators, the PSIRT and firmware engineers (secure development and signing), and staff with CIP access (the Electric Utility's or the client's CIP-004-7 training, renewed within 15 calendar months). (AT-3; PR.AT-02)

4.5 Suspected incidents must be reported to the group SOC within 1 hour (POL-03 4.1). (IR-6)

4.6 Restricted and Confidential information may be used only on group-managed devices and approved services. It must not be sent to personal email or stored in personal cloud storage. (AC-19; AC-20)

4.7 **Removable media.** Only company-issued media that has been scanned at a scanning kiosk may be connected to plant OT, to control center systems, or to commissioning laptops. Personal USB devices are prohibited on all group devices. (MP-7)

4.8 **Generative AI.** Only AI tools on the group approved-tools list may be used for group work. Designs, customer drawings, BCSI, CEII, and other Restricted or Confidential information must never be entered into public AI tools. (PL-4; PL-4(1))

4.9 **Laptops at plants, substations, and client sites.** Only commissioning or service laptops built from the hardened group image may be connected to plant control systems or to customer or client substation equipment, and only with the site's or customer's approval. (CM-6; MP-7; PR.PS-01)
