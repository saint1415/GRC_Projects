## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the group's own records before any assessment work began on 2026-05-04. It covers the legal entities, the systems that schedule and run production, control the grid, hold designs, firmware, BCSI, CEII, FCI and personal information in each division and in corporate shared services, the suppliers that touch them, and the rules that may bind each entity. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Items are grouped by division: the title of each row starts with Group, GEPS, Transformer Manufacturing, Electric Utility or Grid Engineering. Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (interviews, walkthroughs, document reviews, samples, tests) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analyses (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Entities and scale.** The holding company is an SEC registrant with three subsidiaries; corporate shared services sit in the parent. The P8 organization chart shows no OT security role (EV-001). The workforce is 45,000: Transformer Manufacturing about 24,000, Electric Utility about 9,000, Grid Engineering about 7,000, corporate about 5,000; the OT security team has 8 staff and the NERC compliance team 6 (EV-002). FY2025 revenue was about $18.0 billion: Transformer Manufacturing $9.6 billion, Electric Utility $6.0 billion, Grid Engineering $2.4 billion from external clients; exports are about 8% of Manufacturing revenue (EV-003). The FY2025 annual report carries the Item 106 disclosure; the disclosure committee has no pre-agreed materiality factors for a production-stopping incident (EV-004). The board risk committee receives quarterly cyber reports and approved the 2026 risk strategy in 2026-03 (EV-005). Risk registers use one SP 800-30 scale and roll up per NIST IR 8286 Rev. 1 (EV-006). Internal audit reports to the board audit committee (EV-007).

**Policies, standards and common controls.** Group policies v2026 were approved 2026-03-24 and are aligned to CSF 2.0; the Group AI Standard was adopted in 2026-03 (EV-008). Group standards include the group OT security standard based on SP 800-82 Rev. 3 (EV-009). The Electric Utility supplement was aligned 2026-02-27. The Transformer Manufacturing supplement (2025 edition) covers P1 to P7 and has no section on P8 or AI. Grid Engineering has no supplement; its 2023 standards set 90-day platform log retention, allow personal USB drives and shared settings-tool accounts, use a 3-level severity scale, and leave client notices to project managers (EV-010). The common control catalog lists 104 controls; inheritance matrices exist for Transformer Manufacturing and the Electric Utility, and none was provided for Grid Engineering (EV-011).

**Group identity.** All 45,000 workforce identities use MFA with number matching; administrators use phishing-resistant hardware keys; SYS-G1 is not used inside the Electric Utility's ESPs (EV-012). Identity governance disables terminated users within 4 hours and inactive accounts after 60 days, and the Q1 2026 certification was completed; the 20 GEPS service accounts are outside identity governance and certification (EV-013). Privileged access is just in time through PAM with recording, including ERP vendor support (EV-014). A two-way trust links the corporate domain to the P8 legacy domain (EV-015).

**Group security operations.** The SOC runs 24x7 with OT sensors at P1 to P7 and at the TCC and backup TCC. GEPS APS and hub job logs, GEPS service account sign-ins, FMS cross-tenant query logs, P8 and the DCC are not in the SIEM source list, and hub-to-plant traffic has no baseline (EV-016). Cases are triaged within 30 minutes on the group scale; Grid Engineering cases carry the division's own 3-level scale (EV-017). EDR covers IT, cloud and the OT DMZ servers at P1 to P7, and not P8 (EV-018). Scans run weekly and critical findings are fixed within 15 days; P8 is outside the scanning scope and has no patch records (EV-019). CISA ICS advisories are reviewed daily (EV-020). IR plan v5 and the P1 to P7 plant playbooks are on file; the plan has no cross-division notification steps, no P8 content and no link to GEPS scheduling recovery, and no cross-division exercise is recorded (EV-021). The cyber insurance tower includes an OT-capable forensic firm on retainer (EV-022). The 2026-03 penetration test excluded the integration hub and plant interfaces (EV-023).

**Group cloud platform.** Provider A hosts the corporate hub, the GEPS, the data platform and the EAM; provider B hosts the FMS, the client project platform, GEPS disaster recovery and the immutable vault (EV-024). Guardrails apply to every division account (EV-025). Hub firewalls deny by default; SD-WAN reaches plants over two carriers with cellular backup (EV-026). Data is encrypted at rest with group keys and endpoints accept TLS 1.2 or higher (EV-027). Backups are immutable with a separate backup identity; no P8 system is backed up to the vault (EV-028). Logs go to a write-once archive (EV-029). Both providers' SOC 2 reports are on file (EV-030).

**Workforce, suppliers, AI and records.** 2025 awareness training completion was 96% overall and 97% for office staff; no role-based OT training is recorded at P8 or for integration hub engineers (EV-031). Group HR's screening attestation for engineers with TCC access does not list the checks performed (EV-032). About 2,400 suppliers are tiered and 46 tier 1 suppliers are reviewed each year; OT and product component suppliers are reviewed only at onboarding; the files for the TMU contract manufacturer and the P8 OEMs name no security contact (EV-033). ERP and EDI contracts carry 24-hour notice; AMI and CIS contracts carry 72-hour notice (EV-034). AI found: AI-001 to AI-009, with AI-001 and AI-002 in production before the Group AI Standard and no validation record (EV-036). Users on plant office networks reach public generative AI sites (EV-037). Legacy file servers hold HR exports with Social Security numbers and about 1,400 Grid Engineering closed project folders (EV-038). The 2019 supply and 2020 services intercompany agreements have no security schedule (EV-039).

**GEPS.** About 16,500 named users and 1,900 supplier portal users; the 6 integration hub service accounts can write work orders and bills of materials to all 8 plant MES with static keys older than 1 year (EV-040). Flows to P1 to P7 terminate in the OT DMZs under interface agreements; the P8 legacy MES link and 2 direct supplier EDI links have no agreement (EV-041). Audit generation is on for all modules (EV-042). Interface changes that affect plant MES are not reviewed by plant OT engineers (EV-043). The contingency plan covers finance recovery; the 2026-02 DR test restored the finance modules only, and APS and the integration hub have not been restored in a test (EV-044). Export orders are screened before release and records kept 7 years; the manual fallback has no drill on record (EV-045).

**Transformer Manufacturing.** About 3,850 OT assets at P1 to P7 are inventoried with owner and criticality; no P8 asset is listed (EV-046). P1 to P7 have OT DMZs, deny-by-default IT/OT firewalls, gateway-only OEM access with MFA and recording, and automated controller backups (EV-047). P8 was acquired 2025-09; due diligence did not check covered telecommunications equipment; the P8 MES is backed up to local disk and controller programs are copied ad hoc (EV-048). The firmware signing key is in a software keystore on a build server in the corporate domain; SBOMs exist for 3 of 7 firmware lines; incoming boards are not checked for firmware integrity (EV-049). The addenda register lists 140 utilities (95 with 48-hour and 45 with 24-hour incident notice); the incident notice step has not been exercised (EV-050). The departure checklist gained a utility notice step in 2026-04 (EV-051). The FMS serves about 70 subscribers; isolation tests are run by hand, AI-003 changes are released outside the pipeline gate, the standby region has not been failed over, and no subscriber commitments or system description exist (EV-052). 14 federal contracts carry FAR 52.204-21, -23 and -25 (9 also -30); FCI labels are on 9 of 14 contract folders (EV-053). Spare yards have intrusion detection at 2 of 3 (EV-054). Products are EAR99; the bid review gate declined 1 bid in 2026-04 (EV-055). AI-001 sizes the storm reserve with no written allocation rule; each plant MES holds about 48 hours of released work orders (EV-056). TDMS integrity checks are limited to database backups; PLM bulk exports raise no alert (EV-057). Supplier bank changes are approved by email, with call-back above $250,000 (EV-058).

**Electric Utility.** CIP-002 categorization (2025-11-18): medium impact at the TCC and backup TCC, low impact at 58 transmission substations (EV-059). The CIP Senior Manager is designated; CIP-003-9 policies are approved; the Transient Cyber Asset plan accepts Grid Engineering and 3 relay vendors without a documented review (EV-060). Detection sensors on the vendor remote access path are at 22 of 58 substations (EV-061). TCC EAPs, the Intermediate System, physical security, patching, antimalware and logging records are on file; the DCC has no OT sensors (EV-062). Recovery plans were tested 2025-10 (EV-063). About 140 Grid Engineering engineers hold TCC access; their PRAs rest on an attestation that lists no checks (EV-064). The CIP-008-6 plan has no criteria for incidents that start in group IT; DCC DOE-417 procedures lack the cyber criteria (EV-065). The CIP-012 plan (2023 version) does not document availability or recovery (EV-066). No CIP-013-2 risk assessment is recorded for either affiliate (EV-067). Both 2024 SERC audit items were closed in 2025 (EV-068). The CIS holds Social Security and bank account numbers for about 1.4 million meters (EV-069). All large power transformer spares come from the affiliate (EV-070).

**Grid Engineering.** 41 client contracts flow down CIP terms with 24- to 72-hour notice; no register of them exists and no procedure routes SOC findings to client notices (EV-071). Project workspaces inherit project-wide permissions, BCSI folders are not separately restricted, logs are kept 90 days, and the platform holds the Electric Utility's TCC design documents (EV-072). The 2025 SOC 2 Type 2 report covers Security and Confidentiality (EV-073). About 700 commissioning laptops: hardened image at 31 of 40 offices; personal USB drives allowed; personal cloud storage not blocked (EV-074). Settings files are released after peer review with no hash; engineers use only clients' remote access paths (EV-075). The design assistant pilot was used with client documents before approval (EV-076).
