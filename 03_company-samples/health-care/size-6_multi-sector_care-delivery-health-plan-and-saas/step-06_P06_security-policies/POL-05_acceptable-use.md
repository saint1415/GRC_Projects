## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce uses group systems and information.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members in every division and in corporate shared services, on any device used for group work.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow these rules; complete training; report incidents |
| Managers | Make sure staff complete training and acknowledgments |
| Group HR | Tracks acknowledgments and training completion |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Workforce members must access patient, member, and customer information only as needed for their job. Looking up records out of curiosity, including one's own family's, is prohibited. (PL-4; AC-8)

4.2 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy and their division supplement at hire and every year. Group Data Platform users must also acknowledge the permitted-purpose addendum. (PL-4; PR.AT-01)

4.3 Every workforce member must complete security and privacy training at hire and every year. The group sends monthly reminders and runs quarterly phishing exercises. (AT-2; PR.AT-01; 164.308(a)(5)(ii)(A))

4.4 Data engineers, analysts, and data scientists must complete role-based training on purpose tags and minimum necessary before receiving platform access. (AT-3; PR.AT-02)

4.5 Suspected incidents must be reported to the group SOC within 1 hour (POL-03 4.1). (IR-6)

4.6 Restricted information may be used only on group-managed devices and approved services. It must not be sent to personal email or stored on personal devices. (AC-19; AC-20; 164.310(b))

4.7 **Generative AI.** Only AI tools on the group approved-tools list may be used for group work. Restricted information must never be entered into public AI tools. (PL-4; PL-4(1))

4.8 **Recording patient conversations** (for example, with an AI scribe) is allowed only after the consent that state law requires is obtained and documented. In states that require all parties to consent (Florida, Fla. Stat. 934.03(2)(d), is the worked example), recording must not start until every party has consented. (PT-4; PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
