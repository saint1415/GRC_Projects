## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how the workforce uses company systems, devices, and data, in the office and in the field.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (owners, employees, temporary staff, and interns) at the main office, the equipment yard, and every jobsite. Covers all company systems and data, including systems that vendors operate for the company, and company-issued devices wherever they are used.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Payroll and HR Specialist | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and annually; tracks training |
| IT Manager | Security awareness content, reminders, and phishing exercises |
| Superintendents | Brief craft workers and enforce trailer rules on their jobsite |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not involve company data or create risk. (PL-4)
4.2 Every workforce member with a company account must sign this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4)
4.3 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year. Project Managers, estimators, and accounting staff must also complete payment-fraud training every year. The IT Manager sends monthly reminders and runs at least quarterly phishing exercises. (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02)
4.4 **Never act on a bank change received by email, text, or phone.** Send it to the Accounting Manager, who follows the call-back rule in POL-01 4.7. This applies even when the request comes from the President, an owner, or a long-time subcontractor. (AT-2)
4.5 Do not forward company email to personal accounts. Do not store drawings, pay apps, or other FCI on personal phones, personal computers, or personal cloud storage. (AC-20)
4.6 Lock your screen when you step away. Lock the jobsite trailer when no company employee is inside. (AC-11; PE-3)
4.7 Do not install software or browser extensions, or use USB storage, unless IT approves it. (CM-11)
4.8 Report suspicious messages with the Report Phishing button. Report unexpected MFA prompts, lost devices, and anything else suspicious to the IT incident line immediately (POL-03 4.2).
4.9 **Generative AI:** use only tools on the approved list. Never paste or upload federal drawings, bid documents, pricing, client security details, or personal information into a tool that is not approved (see P10). (PL-4)
4.10 Do not post photos or videos of federal facility interiors, client security systems, or drawings on social media or personal accounts. (AC-22)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.14. Sanctions range from retraining to termination, depending on intent and harm. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), the CMMC self-assessment, and the access reviews in this policy set.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.6. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the President for High risk), and expire within 12 months.
