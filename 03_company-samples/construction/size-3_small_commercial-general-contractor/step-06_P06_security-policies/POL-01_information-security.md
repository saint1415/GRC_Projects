## 1. Purpose
Set up the Cris Santos Company information security program, assign who is accountable, and give every other security policy its authority. The program protects:
- Federal Contract Information (FCI)
- payment instructions and bank details
- employee and subcontractor personal information
- client facility security details
- the company's bid and pricing data

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (owners, employees, temporary staff, and interns) at the main office, the equipment yard, and every jobsite. Covers all company systems and data, including systems that vendors operate for the company, and company-issued devices wherever they are used. Subcontractors are bound through their subcontracts (statement 4.13).

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 The company must maintain an information security program that meets FAR 52.204-21 for every system that processes, stores, or transmits FCI, and is documented in this policy set and the System Security Plan. (PM-1; GV.PO-01)
4.2 The IT Manager is the designated security lead. The President is the designated CMMC Affirming Official under 32 CFR 170.22(a)(1). Both designations must be in writing. (PM-2; GV.RR-02)
4.3 A risk assessment must be performed at least annually and after major changes, using NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1. Risks must be tracked in the risk register with an owner and treatment. (RA-3; PM-9)
4.4 Risk acceptance authority: the IT Manager may accept Low risks; the CFO, Moderate; the President, High and Very High. A risk that would make a federal representation or affirmation inaccurate may not be accepted; it must be fixed. (PM-9; GV.RM-01)
4.5 Security policies must be reviewed at least annually and updated after major changes or incidents. (PL-1; GV.PO-02)
4.6 Exceptions to any security policy must be requested in writing, risk-rated, approved per 4.4, recorded in the risk register, and time-limited to 12 months or less. An exception may not be granted against a FAR 52.204-21 requirement while the company holds or seeks a CMMC status. (PL-1)
4.7 **Payment instruction changes.** Any new or changed bank details must be verified before use. This covers a subcontractor, supplier, owner, or employee, and the company's own EFT information in SAM. Verification means all three of these steps:
- a phone call to a number already on file (never a number or link in the requesting message);
- approval by a second authorized person;
- a record in the vendor master change log.

Urgency or seniority of the requester is never a reason to skip a step. (AC-5; PR.AA-05)
4.8 Before any vendor or external system holds FCI or Restricted data, the external system must be on the approved external systems list. It must also have passed a security review and have contract terms covering confidentiality, incident notice, and no use of company data to train models. (SA-9; GV.SC-05)
4.9 **Section 889.** The company must not provide, install, or use any equipment or service from an entity named in FAR 52.204-25, or its subsidiaries or affiliates, on any project or in company operations. Every submittal and purchase of video surveillance, telecommunications, or networking equipment must confirm the actual manufacturer. Identified covered equipment must be reported under POL-03 4.7. (SR-5; GV.SC-05)
4.10 CMMC affirmations in SPRS and Section 889 representations in SAM may be made only by the President. The IT Manager and Contracts Administrator must first complete a documented evidence review, and outside counsel must review it. (CA-2; GV.OV-01)
4.11 Security controls must be assessed at least annually (P07). The CMMC Level 1 self-assessment must be repeated at least annually and its results entered in SPRS (32 CFR 170.15(a)(1)). (CA-2)
4.12 Security records must be retained for 6 years; this includes CMMC self-assessment artifacts, counted from the CMMC status date (32 CFR 170.15(c)(2)). Certified payroll records must be retained for 3 years after the work is completed (FAR 52.222-8(a)). (SI-12; GV.PO-02)
4.13 Subcontracts under federal prime contracts must include the substance of FAR 52.204-21 (when the subcontractor may hold FCI) and FAR 52.204-25. Once a prime contract includes DFARS 252.204-7021, subcontracts must flow down the CMMC level required by 32 CFR 170.23. The Contracts Administrator must also confirm the subcontractor's current status before award. (SA-4; GV.SC-05)
4.14 **Sanctions.** Workforce members who break security policies must be sanctioned in proportion to intent and harm: retraining, written warning, suspension, or termination. HR must document each sanction. (PS-8; GV.RR-04)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.14. Sanctions range from retraining to termination, depending on intent and harm. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), the CMMC self-assessment, and the access reviews in this policy set.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.6. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the President for High risk), and expire within 12 months.
