## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce uses group systems and information.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members in every division and in corporate shared services, including craft workers who use jobsite tablets, on any device used for group work.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow these rules; complete training; report incidents |
| Managers and superintendents | Make sure their staff complete training and acknowledgments |
| Group HR | Tracks acknowledgments and training completion |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Workforce members must use group information only for their job. (PL-4; AC-8)

4.2 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy and their division supplement at hire and every year. (PL-4; PR.AT-01)

4.3 Every workforce member with a group account must complete security awareness training at hire and every year, including how to recognize business email compromise and false payment instructions. The group runs quarterly phishing exercises. (AT-2; AT-2(3); PR.AT-01)

4.4 **CUI training.** Anyone who will create, receive, or handle CUI, including superintendents, field engineers, and project accountants on DoD projects, must complete CUI handling training (marking, where CUI may live, and how to report a spill) before receiving enclave access or CUI drawings, and every year after. (AT-3; PR.AT-02; SP 800-171 R2 3.2.2)

4.5 **Never act on an emailed bank change.** No one may change, or ask anyone else to change, bank or remittance details based on an email, text, or call that starts the request. Follow POL-01 4.15 and report the request to the SOC. (AT-2(3); IR-6)

4.6 Suspected incidents must be reported to the group SOC within 1 hour (POL-03 4.1). (IR-6)

4.7 FCI, CUI, and Restricted information may be used only on group-managed devices and approved services. They must not be sent to personal email or stored on personal devices. Jobsite tablets must stay enrolled in device management and must not be shared between crews without signing out. (AC-19; AC-20)

4.8 **Generative AI.** Only AI tools on the group approved-tools list may be used for group work, and only for the information classes each tool is approved for (POL-04 4.13). FCI, CUI, bid pricing, and personal information must never be entered into public AI tools. (PL-4; PL-4(1))

4.9 **Photos and drones.** Photos, video, and drone footage of federal sites, client security systems, or tenant security areas must not be posted or shared outside approved project systems without approval (POL-04 4.14). (PL-4; AC-22)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.7.

Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.10.
