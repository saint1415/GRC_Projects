## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the group's own records before any assessment work began on 2026-05-04. It covers the legal entities and their federal contracts, the systems that process FCI, CUI, payment instructions, personal information, client facility security details and building controls in each division and in corporate shared services, the suppliers that touch them, and the rules that may bind each entity. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Items are grouped by division: the title of each row starts with Group, PDPP, CUI enclave, Federal contracts, Construction, Property or A&E. Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (interviews, document reviews, samples, scans, tests) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analyses (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Entities and scale.** The holding company is an SEC registrant with three subsidiaries; corporate shared services sit in the parent. Construction and Design each hold their own SAM registration and CAGE codes. The group operates in 14 states, none of them California, Colorado, Illinois, Connecticut or New York (EV-001). The workforce is 45,000: Construction about 31,000 (about 19,000 craft), A&E about 7,100, Property about 2,400, corporate about 4,500 (EV-002). FY2025 revenue was about $18.0 billion: Construction $14.2 billion, Property $2.1 billion, A&E $1.7 billion; about 28% of Construction revenue is federal (EV-004). The 10-K carries the Item 106 disclosure (EV-005). The disclosure committee charter covers cyber incidents; its minutes record no cyber scenario exercise (EV-006). The board risk committee receives a quarterly cyber report (EV-007). Internal audit reports to the board audit committee (EV-008). The 2025 risk registers and risk strategy set acceptance levels from the division lead to the board (EV-009).

**Policies and common controls.** The 2025 group policies are aligned to CSF 2.0. Construction and A&E supplements are on file; the Property supplement is v2024, last aligned 2024-04. The telework standard covers homes, not jobsite trailers. There is no group approved-manufacturer list for Section 889 (EV-010). The common control catalog lists 142 controls; inheritance matrices exist for Construction (2025) and A&E (2026); none was provided for Property (EV-011).

**Identity and security operations.** About 45,000 workforce identities use single sign-on. Administrators, finance and treasury staff and project executives use phishing-resistant authenticators; everyone else, including project managers, uses number-matching MFA (EV-012). Identity governance runs from HR events with quarterly certification; PDPP-local external accounts are outside it (EV-013). Privileged access is just in time with recording (EV-014). The SIEM source list does not include Property BAS servers, building or garage networks, or PDPP remittance edits and bulk downloads (EV-015). The SOC case list holds no building-system or parking incidents (EV-016). EDR covers managed endpoints, servers and cloud workloads, and about 6,000 jobsite tablets are in MDM (EV-017). DMARC is at reject on group domains and at monitor-only on 23 property website domains (EV-018). Scans run weekly, and the 2025 penetration test covered the PDPP portals (EV-019).

**Cloud and network.** Provider A hosts the commercial landing zone and division accounts; provider B holds DR and the immutable backup vault (EV-020, EV-023). Guardrails and a deny-by-default hub firewall apply to every division account (EV-021). Keys are customer-managed per division account, and endpoints accept TLS 1.2 or higher (EV-022). The log archive is write-once, 13 months hot and 7 years archived (EV-024).

**Payments, people, vendors and records.** The payment factory disburses about $900 million a month to about 9,000 subcontractors and suppliers; bank changes need a call-back and a second approver (EV-025). Seven staff hold SAM Entity Administrator rights, and the reasonable inquiry for Section 889 is filed by TSSI only (EV-026). Awareness training completion was 97% in 2025; the CUI handling course is assigned only to enclave users (EV-028). 9 smaller vendors holding personal information have no notice terms (EV-029). The AI estimating vendor provides a SOC 2 Type 1 report only (EV-030). The draft notification matrix has no owner, tenant or surety terms and no intercompany reporting path, and no exercise has covered an incident across divisions (EV-032). The records schedule has no closeout step for federal project records held outside the enclave (EV-033).

**AI tools.** The AI estimating and bid assistant (SYS-D6) has about 725 licensed users, up from a 4-person pilot, including on federal bids. Discovery also found jobsite safety computer vision at 40 jobsites, an AI resume screening pilot, generative design and code checking assistants in A&E, lease abstraction and BAS energy optimization in Property, AP anomaly detection, and an enterprise assistant pilot with 3,000 users (EV-035). The SYS-D6 tenant has the vendor's pooled pricing feature enabled (EV-059).

**PDPP.** About 21,000 workforce and 46,000 external users work on about 1,350 projects. No setting blocks uploads of CUI-marked files, and the pay application remittance block is free text that project accountants can edit (EV-036). External accounts carry no expiry and no inactivity rule; accounts linked only to closed projects remain active; 61% of external users have not enrolled in MFA (EV-037). Remittance edits and bulk downloads are logged but not forwarded to the SIEM (EV-038). The data inventory draft does not record which projects hold FCI, CUI or client facility security details, and the information location record says design-build drawings live only in the enclave (EV-039). The nightly export was restore-tested once by IT (EV-040). One SaaS vendor holds all project records; its SOC 2 report lists complementary user entity controls for external user removal, external MFA and audit log review (EV-041).

**CUI enclave and CMMC.** The enclave SSP is v2.3, dated 2025-03 (EV-042). Final Level 1 (Self) for the PDPP (2026-01-20) and Final Level 2 (Self) for the enclave (2026-03-16) are in SPRS; the March workbook scores every requirement MET or not applicable (EV-043). About 3,400 users, about 900 of them added in 2026, sign in with phishing-resistant MFA; a 'field release' role exists (EV-044). The desktop policy lets the field release role export drawings to commercial systems; 31 engineering plug-ins sit under one blanket allowlist exception (EV-045). Logging failure alerts go to an operations queue that is not staffed after hours (EV-046). One CUI file-transfer gateway component is configured in non-FIPS mode (EV-047). The provider's offering is FedRAMP Moderate authorized (EV-048). Plan rooms at 2 A&E offices are locked and badge-controlled (EV-050).

**Construction.** 212 federal awards (61 DoD, CUI on 23; 13 with DFARS 252.204-7021) (EV-051). The 2025 subcontract rider flows down the federal clauses; prequalification has no CMMC status step (EV-052). TSSI screens submittals for federal sites; private-label products are checked by distributor declaration (EV-053). The TSSI vault holds administrator credentials for 140 managed-service clients, whose 140 agreements carry varying security terms; no recovery test of the remote access platform is on record (EV-054). Subcontractor certified payrolls arrive by email, many with full Social Security numbers, and no standing letter tells owners how remittance details change (EV-057). Some jobsite networks carry subcontractor devices (EV-060).

**Property.** 64 properties in 9 states (19 in Florida), 18 garages, about 1,600 business tenants, 9 federally leased properties (EV-061). Security commitments are abstracted for 6 of 9 GSA leases (EV-062). One approver can change a vendor or tenant-refund bank account in SYS-D3; the tenant portal does not require MFA; tenants have no standing remittance letter (EV-063). Building systems share flat networks at 41 properties and with pay stations at 6 garages; integrators hold the controller backups; inventories do not record component manufacturers (EV-064). 11 integrators keep persistent remote access outside group PAM (EV-065). The parking provider handles card data under an annual attestation; the responsibility matrix is a draft; managers share logins without MFA at 4 garages; inspection logs came from 7 of 18 garages; no segmentation test report was provided (EV-066).

**A&E.** 88 federal awards (47 DoD with CUI); a subcontractor to Construction on 6 design-build projects; about 400 subconsultants with no status step before award; the intercompany agreement does not say how A&E passes a DoD incident report number to Construction (EV-067). The digital twin service hosts 52 owner clients in a single region and promises 99.5% availability (EV-068). The design collaboration service allows anyone-with-link sharing (EV-069). Master specifications list acceptable video and network products with no manufacturer screening step (EV-070). Invoices are emailed as PDFs with remittance details (EV-071).
