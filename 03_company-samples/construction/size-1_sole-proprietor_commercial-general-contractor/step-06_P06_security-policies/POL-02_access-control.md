At the Sole Proprietorship tier the company keeps **one** consolidated policy, because one person writes, follows, and checks it (`01_company-sizes/tier-project-scaling.csv`, t1 P06). This file is kept so the folder has the standard policy set names. It holds no separate rules.

**Where the access control rules are:**

- POL-01 section 7 (Accounts, passwords, and payments): unique accounts and no shared credentials (7.1), MFA (7.2), passphrases and default passwords (7.3), user removal and quarterly review (7.4), emergency access (7.5), bank-detail change verification (7.6), remittance notice to clients (7.7), monthly sign-in and mailbox rule check (7.8).
- POL-01 section 9.2 and 9.8: physical access to the home office, keys and codes, visitor log, truck box.
- POL-01 sections 6.5 and 6.6: subcontractor access to FCI.
- SP 800-53 AC-1, IA-1, and PE-1 are met by POL-01 as a whole. These rules answer FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(i), (ii), (v), (vi), (viii), and (ix).

Traceability for every statement is in `policy-control-map.csv`.
