## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how the workforce uses company systems, devices, and data, in the office, in the field, and in client buildings.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members with a company account or company device, at every location, including craft workers who use shared time-clock kiosks and jobsite tablets.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Director | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and annually; tracks training completion |
| Security Manager | Awareness content, CUI training content, and phishing exercises |
| Superintendents | Brief craft workers and trade foremen and enforce trailer rules on their jobsite |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not involve company data or create risk. (PL-4)
4.2 Every workforce member with a company account must sign this policy before receiving access and again each year. CPE users must also sign the CUI handling acknowledgment. (PL-4)
4.3 **Training.** Security awareness training is required at hire and every year, with at least quarterly phishing exercises. Project Managers, estimators, billing and accounts payable staff, and executives must also complete payment-fraud training every year. CPE users and FC-4 field staff must complete CUI awareness and role-based CUI training, including insider threat indicators, before access and every year. (AT-2; AT-2(2); AT-2(3); AT-3; SP 800-171 Rev. 2 3.2.1 to 3.2.3)
4.4 **Never act on a bank change received by email, text, or phone.** Send it to the Controller, who follows the call-back rule in POL-01 4.8. This applies even when the request seems to come from the CEO, an owner, or a long-time subcontractor.
4.5 **CUI stays in the enclave.** Do not download, photograph, print outside the approved printers, or upload CUI anywhere else. If you see CUI anywhere outside the CPE, report it (POL-03 4.2). (AT-2)
4.6 Do not forward company email to personal accounts. Do not store drawings, pay apps, or other company data on personal phones, personal computers, or personal cloud storage. (AC-20)
4.7 Lock your screen when you step away. Lock the jobsite trailer when no company employee is inside, and keep the CUI room closed. (AC-11; PE-3)
4.8 Do not install software or browser extensions, or use USB storage, unless IT approves it. (CM-11)
4.9 Report suspicious messages with the Report Phishing button. Report unexpected MFA prompts, lost devices or security keys, and anything else suspicious to the security incident line immediately (POL-03 4.2).
4.10 **AI tools:** use only tools on the approved list (P10). Never put CUI into any AI tool. Never paste or upload federal drawings, bid documents, pricing, client security details, or personal information into a tool that is not approved for that data. (PL-4)
4.11 Do not post photos or videos of federal facility interiors, jobsite trailers, client security systems, or drawings on social media or personal accounts. (AC-22)
4.12 **Client buildings.** Technicians must use only company-approved commissioning laptops and the company gateway on client networks, must not browse or read email from those laptops, and must never copy client data to personal devices. (PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.18. Compliance is checked through training records, phishing exercise results, and the annual control assessment (P07).

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7.
