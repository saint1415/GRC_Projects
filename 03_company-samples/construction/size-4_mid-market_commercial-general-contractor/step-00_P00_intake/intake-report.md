## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the company's own records before any assessment work began on 2026-07-06. It covers the organization, the systems that hold FCI, CUI, payment data, employee personal information and client facility security details, the suppliers and subcontractors that touch them, and the rules that may bind the company. At this size the sources are systems of record: the identity provider, the HR and payroll system, the endpoint and EDR consoles, the cloud organization console, the CPE admin consoles, the SIEM and MSSP portal, the ERP vendor master and change log, the bank portal, the contract register and subcontract files, SPRS and SAM, prior assessments and internal audit reports, scan results and the incident log. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (interviews, samples, tests) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analysis (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Users and access.** The identity provider holds about 610 active workforce accounts, one for each of the 600 employees on the HR roster plus contractor accounts. Every active account has MFA registered. Non-administrator users, including Project Managers, accounting staff and payroll self-service, use push with number matching; 22 administrators use FIDO2 security keys (EV-001). Conditional access requires MFA for all users and checks device compliance and sign-in risk; accounts are disabled after 45 days of inactivity (EV-002). HR recorded 286 terminations and 38 project transfers involving FC-4 in the 12 months to 2026-06-30 (EV-003). The HR feed disables accounts when HR records a termination, and superintendents send field terminations to HR; a project transfer changes department and title only, and CPE accounts are removed by hand (EV-004). Access reviews run twice a year; the last campaign was January 2026 (EV-005). SYS-01 has about 2,600 external users, 31 external accounts are active on projects closed more than 90 days earlier, 9 Project Managers hold company-administrator rights, and external users sign in with the vendor's sign-in, where MFA is optional (EV-006). The privileged access broker covers cloud accounts and servers; SaaS administrator roles are standing (EV-007). The help desk made 64 MFA resets in the year, verifying callers with employee ID and manager name (EV-008).

**Devices and network.** The endpoint console lists 420 laptops and desktops, 380 smartphones, 110 rugged tablets and 12 commissioning laptops, with encryption, passcode and remote wipe profiles (EV-009). The tablet and router register lists 150 rugged tablets, 40 of them not in device management, and 22 jobsite cellular routers with no firmware version or configuration baseline recorded (EV-010). The carrier portal shows the routers configured one at a time, with remote administration enabled on the internet-facing interface of 7 of the 22 (EV-011). EDR reports on all managed laptops, desktops, commissioning laptops and cloud servers, with alerts routed to the MSSP; enclave laptops use the provider's endpoint protection (EV-012). SD-WAN links the offices and yard; jobsite trailers connect only through cellular routers; there is no network path between corporate systems and the CPE (EV-013). Authenticated scans run quarterly on cloud workloads, office networks and the MBSS account and monthly on the CPE desktop image; enclave laptops and jobsite routers are outside the scan scope; Q1 and Q2 2026 had 140 critical and high findings (EV-014). Baselines exist for laptops, cloud servers, enclave laptops and the desktop image, and not for routers, rugged tablets or trailer Wi-Fi (EV-015). In SYS-01, 26 FC-4 tablets hold offline drawing sets that include CUI-marked sheets, and the mobile app signs in without a device compliance check (EV-016).

**Cloud, backups and monitoring.** The landing zone has 5 accounts with guardrails, company-managed keys and federated sign-in; the estimating database is a single instance, the file-transfer portal is internet-facing in a public subnet, and the MBSS servers run in one region (EV-017). Daily backups go to a separate backup account in a second region with 35-day write-once retention; the CPE and exports of SYS-01 and SYS-02 records are not in the backup scope (EV-018). The 2026-03 restore test recovered the estimating database in 2 hours and the BIM/CAD servers in 10 hours; no other recovery test ran in the 12 months (EV-019). The SIEM receives identity, email, cloud, EDR and office firewall logs; SYS-01 audit logs, SYS-02 vendor-master changes, the CPE and jobsite routers are not sources, and the MSSP contract excludes the CPE (EV-020, EV-021). CPE logs are kept 180 days and reviewed by hand once a week by the CPE administrators (EV-022).

**CUI and federal contracts.** The CPE has 52 named accounts (41 staff, 8 A&E guests, 3 administrators), FIDO2 for all, sharing limited to 9 allow-listed domains, and no backup beyond provider versioning (EV-023). Its services are FedRAMP authorized at Moderate or higher, and the provider's CRM (version 2025-02) is on file (EV-024). The SPRS Basic Assessment score of 71 was posted 2025-02-14 for the CPE scope; the self-assessment assumes CUI stays in the enclave and does not cover the jobsite, tablets, SYS-01 or printed CUI (EV-025, EV-026). FC-1 to FC-4 carry FAR 52.204-21 and 52.204-25; FC-3 and FC-4 carry DFARS 252.204-7012, 7019 and 7020; none carries 252.204-7021 (EV-027). FC-4 has about 6,800 CUI-marked sheets and documents, and the distribution list names the A&E firm and 14 trades as recipients (EV-028). CUI-marked FC-4 sheets appear in SYS-01 RFI and submittal folders, and the FC-4 folder template grants read to all 230 external project members from 14 firms (EV-029). Only the A&E subcontract includes DFARS 252.204-7012, and SPRS verification records exist for 3 of the 15 FC-4 subcontractors (EV-030). The only medium assurance certificate expired 2026-05-20 (EV-031). SAM has one Entity Administrator (EV-032). Section 889 screening covers installed equipment by brand; private-label products are not traced to the original manufacturer, and rented equipment is outside the procedure (EV-033). At the 2026-06 industry day the Army Corps of Engineers said the follow-on MATOC will require CMMC Level 2 (C3PAO) (EV-034).

**Programs and documents.** Five security policies date from 2024, with 2024 standards for cloud configuration and endpoints, logging, authentication, encryption and facility security; there is no standard for vendors and subcontractors, payment verification, AI use, contingency or CUI handling, and the 2024 contingency plan does not cover the CPE or MBSS (EV-035). The 2024 incident response plan and the 2025 ransomware tabletop cover corporate systems; 2 of the 5 tabletop actions were open in June 2026, and no CUI incident or DIBNet exercise is recorded (EV-036). The incident log holds 46 tickets since 2025-01-01, 17 of them in the last 12 months (EV-037). Awareness training is 96% complete for 2026, the June 2026 phishing click rate was 6.4%, accounting staff and Project Managers take a payment-fraud module, and the only CUI training was a 2025-03 briefing for the original FC-4 team (EV-038). The 2025 internal IT audit was reported to the audit committee (EV-039). The company is privately held, with a board audit committee that receives a quarterly cyber risk report (EV-040).

**Payments and email.** Every ACH batch and wire needs two approvers, and positive pay is on; about 900 payments and about $5.2 million a month go to about 450 active subcontractors and suppliers (EV-041). The ERP recorded 212 bank-detail changes in the year; each needs a second approver and a call-back field, and the workflow lets the second approver accept an 'urgent' override without call-back evidence; the change log is not sent to the SIEM (EV-042). Owner pay app templates say nothing about how the company changes remittance details, and no notice to private owners was found (EV-043). DMARC is at quarantine, and no lookalike-domain monitoring is in place (EV-044).

**Suppliers and contracts.** Accounts payable lists about 450 active subcontractors and suppliers and about 210 other vendors, including 38 IT and SaaS contracts; 31 design firms use the file-transfer portal under project agreements without security terms (EV-045). SOC 2 reports are reviewed each year for 9 SaaS vendors, and vendors are not tiered (EV-046). The project platform vendor's SOC 2 states RTO 4 hours and RPO 1 hour, and the ERP vendor's states RTO 24 hours and RPO 4 hours; the project platform vendor has no FedRAMP authorization (EV-047). The cyber policy has a $10 million limit, a $1 million social engineering sublimit and a $250,000 retention, with notice through the carrier hotline first (EV-048). The employee health plan is fully insured, and no card payments are accepted (EV-049).

**MBSS.** 38 client sites and about 9,400 devices are monitored; 27 sites are reached through the company gateway with named accounts and session recording, and 11 through the client's own tool with shared technician accounts; two clients require a SOC 2 Type 2 report by 2027-12-31 (EV-050). The MBSS platform runs in one region, and no full rebuild test is recorded (EV-051).

**Facilities and media.** Walk-throughs on 2026-06-24 to 2026-06-26 found badge readers at headquarters, the FC-4 project office, the MBSS monitoring room and the yard. In the FC-4 trailer, printed CUI drawing sets lay on an open plan table, paper waste went to an ordinary recycling bin, trades and inspectors entered during working hours without a check, and visitors signed a paper binder (EV-052, EV-053). Destruction certificates cover retired laptops and drives (EV-054). A file share scan found HR exports with Social Security numbers and commissioning spreadsheets with client credentials in 2023 and 2024 project folders (EV-055).

**Business volume.** Revenue was about $100 million in FY2025: about $94 million construction and about $6 million MBSS, over about 250 business days; the follow-on MATOC is about 15% of planned revenue for 2027 to 2031 (EV-056). Pay apps total about $7.8 million a month on 22 projects, 15 of them due in the last 5 business days; about 1,800 people work on jobsites each day; about 6 major bids go out a month (EV-057).

**AI tools and jobsite technology.** Six AI uses were adopted by departments (AI-001 to AI-006), with no security, privacy or bias review record for any of them; the productivity suite assistant was enabled for all users in 2026-04, and no written notice to workers about jobsite video analytics was found (EV-058). Jobsite technology (telematics, rented cameras, time clocks, drone photos) is vendor-operated under 6 rental agreements and other service agreements (EV-059).

**Reconciliation across sources.**
- **Workforce.** The 600 employees on the HR roster each have an identity provider account; the about 610 accounts include contractor accounts (EV-001, EV-003).
- **Endpoints.** The console lists 922 managed devices. The tablet register adds the 40 unmanaged tablets, giving 150 tablets and 962 devices in total (EV-009, EV-010). The 26 FC-4 tablets with offline sets come from SYS-01's device report (EV-016); which of them are unmanaged was not established at intake.
- **Routers.** The register and the carrier portal both list 22 routers, one per jobsite trailer (EV-010, EV-011).
- **CPE accounts.** 41 staff, 8 A&E guests and 3 administrators make the 52 accounts in the CPE export (EV-023).
- **CUI recipients.** The distribution list names 15 subcontractors (EV-028). The subcontract files show the DFARS clause in 1 of them and SPRS verification for 3 (EV-030).
- **Vendors.** About 450 subcontractors and suppliers and about 210 other vendors in accounts payable (EV-041, EV-045). The named vendors and two summary rows in [`vendor-register.csv`](vendor-register.csv) cover both groups.
- **AI tools.** Two AI vendors are paid directly in accounts payable (AI-001, AI-004); the other four uses are features of products bought for other purposes, found through the admin settings and the survey. Together they give the 6 AI uses in [`asset-inventory.csv`](asset-inventory.csv) (EV-045, EV-058).
