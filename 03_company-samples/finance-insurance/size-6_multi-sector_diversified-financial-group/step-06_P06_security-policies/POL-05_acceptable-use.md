## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce uses group systems and information.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members in every division and in corporate shared services, on any device used for group work.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow these rules; complete training; report incidents |
| Managers | Make sure staff complete training and acknowledgments |
| Group HR | Tracks acknowledgments and training completion |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Workforce members must access customer, client, and guarantor information only as needed for their job. (PL-4; AC-8)

4.2 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy and their division supplement at hire and every year. (PL-4; PR.AT-01)

4.3 Every workforce member must complete security training at hire and every year. The group runs quarterly phishing and payment-change simulations. (AT-2; AT-2(3); PR.AT-01; App. B III.C.2)

4.4 **Payment-fraud role training.** Everyone who handles payment instructions, in any division, must complete the payment-fraud module before handling payments and every year: bank wire, treasury, and contact center staff; CRE closers and funding staff; Financial Software client support and billing staff; and group accounts payable. (AT-3; PR.AT-02; App. B III.C.2; App. F III.C.2)

4.5 Suspected incidents must be reported to the group SOC under POL-03 4.1 (15 minutes for suspected payment fraud; 1 hour otherwise). (IR-6)

4.6 Restricted information may be used only on group-managed devices and approved services. It must not be sent to personal email or stored on personal devices. (AC-19; AC-20)

4.7 **Generative AI.** Only AI tools on the group approved-tools list may be used for group work. Restricted information must never be entered into public AI tools. (PL-4; PL-4(1))

4.8 Workforce members must never act on a change of payment or contact details that arrives only by email, even from a known sender, until it has been verified under POL-02 4.13. (PL-4; AT-2)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.15.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.11.
