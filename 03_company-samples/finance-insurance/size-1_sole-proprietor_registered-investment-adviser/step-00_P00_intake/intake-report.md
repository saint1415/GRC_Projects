## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the adviser's own records before the self-assessment began on 2026-07-13. A one-person adviser has no HR system, asset database or internal audit, so the systems of record are the custodian's advisor portal, the SaaS vendors' admin settings and reports, the device and router settings screens, the inbox and file storage, the signed agreements folder, the compliance files, bank and card statements, the tax files, and the insurance agent's portal. Intake covers the organization, the systems that hold customer information, the vendors that touch them, and the rules that may bind the adviser. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (self-reviews, walk-throughs, tests) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analysis (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Custodian and money movement.** The custodian portal has one adviser login, the owner's, with trading, fee, account opening and money movement permissions. The custodian enforces an authenticator app, and the owner's phone is the only registered second factor (EV-001). The custodian holds about $19 million for about 70 households, about 120 individual clients and about 140 accounts; 5 households live outside Florida (EV-002). The adviser never holds client funds. Third-party wires need a client-signed letter of authorization, and the custodian reviews them, calling the client on some. A SOC 1 report is available on request and has not been requested (EV-003). Money movement requests carry the client's e-signed form, sent to the client's email on file, and the records have no field for a call to the client (EV-004). On 2026-06-18 a request from a client's real email address asked for $36,000 to go to a new bank account. The owner sent the wire form to that address, and the custodian's own call to the client stopped it. No record shows a call or a report by the owner (EV-005).

**Portfolio platform.** The owner's administrator account signs in with a password; MFA is available and off (EV-006). Fees of about $45,000 a quarter are billed in arrears and deducted by the custodian (EV-007). The vendor's SOC 2 Type 2 report covers Security and Availability for the 12 months ending 2026-03-31, with one remediated exception, an RTO of 24 hours and an RPO of 4 hours (EV-008).

**CRM, email and files.** The CRM holds SSNs, dates of birth, ID images, contact details and notes on about 160 consumers, about 40 of them former clients back to 2015. MFA is available and off (EV-009). The email and file suite is a business plan with a data protection addendum (EV-011). Its one user is also the tenant administrator, MFA is available and off, sign-in history and file versions are kept 30 days, and no backup service is connected (EV-010). Client folders go back to 2015, the mailbox holds statements, tax returns and onboarding forms, client files go to the compliance consultant and some clients as ordinary attachments, and withdrawal and transfer requests arrive by email (EV-012).

**Other SaaS and passwords.** The planning software vendor enforces MFA (EV-013). The e-signature service and the accounting SaaS sign in with a password only, with MFA available and off; e-signature links go to the client's email on file (EV-014, EV-015). The browser's password checkup flags the email password as reused on two other sites, and no password manager is installed (EV-016).

**Devices, network and home office.** The laptop has full-disk encryption, automatic updates, built-in antivirus and a host firewall on, and locks after 5 minutes. It has one user account, which a family member sometimes uses, and its sync and downloads folders hold client documents (EV-017). The phone is encrypted, locks after 1 minute, holds the authenticator app, and has no remote wipe set up (EV-018). The USB backup drive is not encrypted and holds one full copy of the client folders, refreshed weekly (EV-019). The router still has its default admin password, and family and smart-home devices share the one network (EV-020). The home office door locks, paper files are in a locked cabinet, the USB drive is in an unlocked drawer, paper is cross-cut shredded, and a 2021 laptop sits in a closet, never wiped (EV-021).

**AI tools.** The consumer AI assistant has been used about 3 times a week since 2026-05-04 to summarize statements and draft review letters. Statements for about 25 households went in, some with full account numbers, and a search found no SSNs. The model-training setting is on (EV-022). The consumer terms let the vendor use chats to improve its models (EV-023). The video meeting service's AI summary feature is off and shows no use (EV-024).

**Agreements and compliance files.** The IT consultant signed a services agreement with security terms on 2026-07-10, the first one. The compliance consultant's engagement letter has no security or confidentiality terms. No vendor list, vendor review, CRM or e-signature assurance, or prompt incident notice clause was located (EV-025). Form ADV shows Florida registration, about $19 million under management and fee-only advice. The compliance manual has one generic security paragraph, and the privacy notice promises "physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards" (EV-026). No written security program, risk assessment, Qualified Individual designation, data inventory, retention schedule, incident plan, contact list, successor arrangement or review record was found (EV-027). Continuing education covers compliance topics only (EV-028).

**Business volume and insurance.** Statements show the SaaS subscriptions, the IT consultant's hourly invoices, the compliance consultant's and CPA's fees, an errors and omissions premium, and no cyber insurance premium (EV-029). Gross receipts were about $180,000 in 2025, with no wages paid (EV-030). The errors and omissions declarations page does not show whether a social engineering or cyber endorsement applies (EV-031).
