## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how the workforce uses bank systems, devices, and data, and how staff spot and stop payment fraud and identity theft.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members, contractors, and temporary staff, on any device used for bank work.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Director | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and each year; tracks training |
| Information Security Officer | Awareness content, monthly phishing simulations, reminders |
| Director of Payments Operations | Wire-fraud and callback training content |
| Retail Banking Director and Contact Center Director | Contact-change verification and caller verification training content |
| Model Risk Manager | Keeps the approved AI tools list with the ISO |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Bank systems are for bank business. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not involve customer data or create risk. (PL-4)
4.2 Every workforce member must sign this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4(1))
4.3 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year. The ISO runs monthly phishing simulations and sends monthly reminders. (AT-2; PR.AT-01; III.C.2)
4.4 **Role-based fraud training.** Branch staff, relationship managers, contact center staff, wire room staff, and correspondent operations staff must complete training at hire and every year on business email compromise, contact-change fraud, voice impersonation (including synthetic voices), the callback standard, and how to escalate. (AT-3; AT-2(3); PR.AT-02; III.C.1.a; 12 CFR 41.90(e)(3))
4.5 **Never trust contact details in a request.** Verify every emailed or phoned payment request, change of payment instructions, or change of contact information by using contact details already on file (POL-02 4.6 and 4.11). If a customer, colleague, or caller who sounds like an executive pressures you to skip verification, stop and escalate to your manager.
4.6 Lock your screen when you step away. Position teller screens so customers cannot read other customers' information. (AC-11)
4.7 Do not install software or browser extensions, or connect USB storage, unless IT approves it. (CM-11)
4.8 Do not open unexpected attachments or links. Report suspicious messages with the Report Phishing button.
4.9 Report suspected fraud or an unexpected contact change within 15 minutes, and anything else suspicious immediately (POL-03 4.2).
4.10 **AI tools.** Use only AI tools on the approved list (P10). Never enter customer information, SAR information, loan files, or respondent data into a tool that is not approved (POL-04 4.9). Check AI outputs before using them; you are responsible for anything you send or decide. (PL-4)
4.11 Look up customer accounts only when your job requires it. Access to the core is logged and reviewed. (AU-6)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions procedure (POL-01 section 4.8). Compliance is checked through the annual independent assessment (P07), phishing simulation results, and monthly callback and contact-change sampling.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7. No exception may waive the verification in 4.5.
