At the Micro tier the credit union keeps three core policies. The workforce use rules that an Acceptable Use Policy would hold are short enough to sit with the access rules staff already read, so they live in **POL-02 Part C. Workforce use rules**, with the AI tool rule in POL-04:

| Essential rule | Where it lives | NCUA citation |
|---|---|---|
| Use systems for credit union work; look only at the member information your job needs | POL-02 C.1 | 748.0(b)(2) |
| No member information in personal email, personal cloud storage, messaging apps, or public AI chatbots | POL-02 C.2 | App. A II.B |
| Approved AI tools only; the AI credit scoring add-on only under the P10 conditions | POL-04 4.6 | App. A III.D.1 |
| Lock screens; never share passwords, MFA codes, or wire tokens | POL-02 C.3 | App. A III.C.1.a |
| Security training at hire and yearly, including BEC and wire fraud; phishing exercises | POL-02 C.4 | App. A III.C.2 |
| Report suspected incidents at once (15 minutes for a suspected fraudulent wire) | POL-02 C.5 and POL-03 4.2 | App. A III.C.1.g |
| Signed acknowledgment at hire and after each annual update | POL-02 C.6 | App. A III.C.2 |

Traceability for these rules is in `policy-control-map.csv` under POL-02 and POL-04.
