## 1. Purpose
Set the rules every workforce member follows when using group systems, devices, OT, and AI tools.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members and all group devices, accounts, OT, and approved AI tools.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow this policy; acknowledge it every year |
| Managers | Make sure their staff complete training and acknowledgment |
| Group HR | Tracks acknowledgments and training |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Group systems are for business use. Limited personal use of email and the internet is allowed if it does not interfere with work or security. (PL-4)

4.2 Personal devices must never connect to OT networks or to the CUI enclave. (AC-19; AC-20)

4.3 USB and other removable media may be used on OT only after scanning at a kiosk, and never on systems holding covered defense information unless issued for that purpose by the enclave team. (MP-7; NIST SP 800-171 3.8.7)

4.4 Report suspected phishing with the report button, and any unexplained HMI behavior to the control room supervisor at once. (AT-2)

4.5 Never share credentials or approve an MFA prompt you did not start. (PL-4)

4.6 Use only group-approved AI tools. Never enter covered defense information, FCI, RRA or ERP content, SCADA diagrams, credentials, or client data into an AI tool unless the Group AI Standard approves that tool for that class of data. (PL-4; P10)

4.7 Commissioning laptops and technician laptops that connect to client or Water Utility OT must stay under group management and must not hold covered defense information. (AC-20; NIST SP 800-171 3.1.20)

4.8 The group monitors use of its systems for security. Where video analytics or telematics are used to monitor workers, the division must give notice and follow its supplement. (PL-4; PT-2)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Annual acknowledgment and training (AT-2, P07). Violations are handled under POL-01 4.7.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 4.10.
