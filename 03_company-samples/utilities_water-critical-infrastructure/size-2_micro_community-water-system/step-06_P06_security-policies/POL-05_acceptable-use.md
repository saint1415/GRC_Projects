At the Micro tier the company keeps three core policies. The staff use rules that an Acceptable Use Policy would hold are short enough to sit with the access rules staff already read, so they live in **POL-02 Part C. Workforce use rules**, with the AI tool rule in POL-04:

| Essential rule | Where it lives | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| Company systems for company work; look up customer information only as the job needs | POL-02 C.1 | CSF PR.AT-01 |
| No Restricted information in personal email, personal cloud storage, messaging apps, or public AI chatbots | POL-02 C.2 | CSF PR.AT-01 |
| Approved AI tools only; the anomaly detection feature only as advisory under the P10 conditions | POL-04 4.8 | CSF GV.SC-05 |
| No personal devices or USB drives on the HMI computer or plant network; no web or email on the HMI computer | POL-02 C.3 | CSF PR.PS-01; SP 800-82 Rev. 3 sec. 6.2.4 |
| Security training at hire and yearly; phishing simulations; OT training for operators | POL-02 C.4 | CSF PR.AT-01, PR.AT-02 |
| Report suspected incidents at once | POL-02 C.5 and POL-03 4.2 | CSF RS.MA-02 |
| Signed acknowledgment at hire and after each annual update | POL-02 C.6 | CSF PR.AT-01 |

Traceability for these rules is in `policy-control-map.csv` under POL-02 and POL-04.
