## 1. Purpose
Set the rules every person must follow when using company systems, devices, and OT equipment.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members, contractors, integrators, and vendors who use company systems or connect equipment to company or OT networks.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Director | Annual acknowledgment and training records |
| IT Director | Approved device and tool lists |
| Plant Managers | Enforce OT rules at their plants, including for contractors |
| All users | Follow the rules and report problems |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Every user must read and acknowledge this policy at hire and every year. (PL-4; GV.RR-04)
4.2 Personal devices and unapproved USB media must never be connected to OT networks or OT equipment. Contractor and integrator laptops may connect to OT only after the Plant Manager approves them and a malware scan is recorded, or must use a company-provided laptop. (AC-19; AC-20; MP-7)
4.3 HMIs and engineering workstations may be used only for operating and maintaining the water system. Email, web browsing, and personal use on them are prohibited. (CM-7; PL-4; PR.PS-01)
4.4 Only AI tools on the approved list may be used for company work. Users must review AI output before relying on it, and must never enter Restricted or Confidential data in an unapproved tool (POL-04 statement 4.7). (PL-4)
4.5 Users must report suspicious activity immediately, including an HMI that changes by itself, an unexpected remote session, a suspicious call or text, or a lost device. (IR-6; AT-2)
4.6 All workforce members, including field and plant staff without company email, must complete security awareness training at hire and every year. Operators, SCADA staff, and ROC staff must also complete OT role-based training before receiving OT access and every year after. (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02)
4.7 Users must not post or share details of plants, control systems, security arrangements, or emergency plans on social media or with anyone without a need to know. (PL-4)
4.8 Company systems are monitored. A system use notification is shown at sign-in where the system supports it. (AC-8)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Checked through training records, acknowledgments, plant walkthroughs, and the annual assessment (P07). Violations are handled under POL-01 statement 4.8.

## 6. Exceptions
Under POL-01 statement 4.7. No exception is allowed for statement 4.2.
