## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how employees and contractors may use company systems, building systems, data, devices, and AI tools.

## 2. Scope
All employees and contractors, including integrators and guard contractor staff, given access to company systems, building systems, or data, at all properties and when working remotely.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Director | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and annually; tracks training completion |
| Security Manager | Training content, monthly reminders, quarterly phishing simulations, OT training with engineering |
| Director of Security Operations | Rules for video and access data use; training for officers and SCC operators |
| Vice President of Engineering | Rules for engineering workstations and integrator media |
| Everyone | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems and building systems are for company business. Limited personal use of corporate devices is allowed if it involves no company data and creates no risk. Building systems and security consoles are for work use only. (PL-4)
4.2 Every employee and contractor must acknowledge this policy before receiving access and again each year. Integrators must also sign the rules of behavior for the remote access gateway. (PL-4)
4.3 Security training is required at hire and every year, with quarterly phishing simulations. Engineering staff, SCC operators, and lobby officers must also complete role-based OT security training each year. Staff who take card payments must complete card handling and terminal tampering training. (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02; CPG 3.J; PCI DSS 12.6.1)
4.4 Lock your screen when you step away. (AC-11)
4.5 Do not install software or connect USB storage to corporate endpoints or console PCs. Integrator media must be scanned at the engineering scanning kiosk before it is used on any building system. (CM-11; MP-7; CPG 3.R)
4.6 Only company-managed devices may connect to building system networks. Personal hotspots and unmanaged laptops are not allowed in engineering rooms or the SCC. (AC-19; AC-20)
4.7 Report lost or stolen devices, suspicious messages, unexpected changes in building systems, and anything unusual immediately (POL-03 4.2).
4.8 **AI tools.** Use only AI tools and features on the approved list (STD-06). Never enter Restricted or Confidential information into a tool that is not approved. A qualified person must review AI outputs before they are used for a lease, a tenant notice, or any decision about a person. (PL-4)
4.9 **Video and access data.** Use video, access history, and visitor records only for security and building operations. Do not view, copy, or share them for personal reasons. Requests from tenants, police, or anyone else go to the Director of Security Operations. (PL-4)
4.10 **Audio.** Do not enable audio recording on cameras or any other building device without written approval from the General Counsel. Florida law restricts intercepting oral communications without the consent of all parties (Fla. Stat. 934.03). (PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.15. Compliance is checked through the annual independent assessment (P07), training records, and quarterly video access reviews.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7.
