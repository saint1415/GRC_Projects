## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce uses group systems, building systems, and information.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members in every division and in corporate shared services, on any device used for group work, including engineering workstations and security consoles.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow these rules; complete training; report incidents |
| Managers | Make sure staff complete training and acknowledgments |
| Group HR | Tracks acknowledgments and training completion |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Workforce members must access tenant, guest, badge, and video information only as needed for their job. Viewing video or badge records out of curiosity, or to follow a person for any reason other than a security task, is prohibited. Video may be exported only for an incident or a legal request. (PL-4; AC-8)

4.2 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy and their division supplement at hire and every year. (PL-4; PR.AT-01)

4.3 Every workforce member must complete security awareness training at hire and every year. The group runs quarterly phishing exercises. (AT-2; PR.AT-01)

4.4 Role-based training is required before access: OT security and manual operation for engineers, RBOC operators, and BTI technicians; card handling and terminal tamper checks for front desk, outlet, and event staff; CUI handling for federal project teams and BTI technicians. (AT-3; PR.AT-02)

4.5 Suspected incidents must be reported to the group SOC within 1 hour (POL-03 4.1). (IR-6)

4.6 Group information may be used only on group-managed devices and approved services. USB storage must not be connected to engineering workstations, site supervisors, or security consoles except through an approved transfer kiosk. BTI technicians must use group-managed laptops at group sites. (AC-19; AC-20; MP-7)

4.7 **Generative AI.** Only AI tools on the group approved-tools list may be used for group work. Restricted information, FCI, and CUI must never be entered into an AI tool outside its approved scope (POL-04 4.9). (PL-4; PL-4(1))

4.8 **Audio.** Audio recording must not be enabled on building or hotel cameras. In states that require all parties to consent to recording a conversation (Florida, Fla. Stat. 934.03(2)(d), is the worked example), recording conversations without that consent is unlawful. (PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.7.
