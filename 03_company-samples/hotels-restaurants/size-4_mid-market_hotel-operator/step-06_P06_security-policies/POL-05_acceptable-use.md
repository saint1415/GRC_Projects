## 1. Purpose
Tell every employee and contracted worker what they may and may not do with company systems, guest data, and payment devices.

## 2. Scope
Everyone who uses company systems or handles guest or card data, including contracted workers and contractors.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Director | Owns this policy; collects acknowledgments at hire and each year |
| Department heads | Make sure their staff follow it |
| GRC Analyst | Training content and records |
| All users | Follow it and report problems |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Every user must read and acknowledge this policy at hire and each year, and complete security awareness training. (PL-4; AT-2; PCI DSS 12.2.1, 12.6.1)
4.2 Only company-managed devices may be used to handle card data or to sign in to the PMS, POS, or lock systems. Personal devices may be used only for email and chat through the approved apps. (AC-19; AC-20)
4.3 Never ask for, accept, or send card numbers by email, chat, text message, or on paper. Send the guest a payment link or take the card on a payment device. (PL-4; PCI DSS 3.2)
4.4 Never give guest information (room numbers, stay dates, folio details) to a caller or visitor without verifying their identity under the front office procedure. Treat urgent requests for guest data, refunds, or payment changes as possible social engineering. (AT-2(3); PCI DSS 12.6.3.1)
4.5 Report suspicious emails with the report button. Do not install software; request it through the service desk. (SI-8; CM-11; PCI DSS 5.4.1)
4.6 Lock your screen when you step away; front desk PCs lock automatically. (AC-11; PCI DSS 8.2.8)
4.7 Check payment devices at the start of each shift for signs of tampering (broken seals, extra parts, changed cables) and report any concern to your manager at once. Never let anyone service or swap a device without the manager's confirmation. (SR-10; PCI DSS 9.5.1.3)
4.8 Connect remotely only through the company VPN or approved access tools. Never install or approve a remote access tool for a vendor. (AC-17)
4.9 Never share your password or MFA approval. Never approve an MFA prompt you did not start. (IA-2; PCI DSS 8.3)
4.10 Use only AI tools on the approved-tools list. Never enter Restricted or Confidential data in any other AI tool. Review AI output before using it with guests. (PL-4; P10)
4.11 CRO and front desk staff must use the recording announcement on every recorded call, including transferred and outbound calls, and must pause recording before a guest gives card details. (PL-4; Fla. Stat. 934.03(2)(d); PCI DSS 3.3.1)
4.12 Company systems are monitored for security. Users have no expectation of privacy in their use of company systems, within the limits of law. (PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Acknowledgment rates, training completion, and phishing results are reported quarterly. Violations are handled under POL-01 4.15.

## 6. Exceptions
Under POL-01 4.7. No exception may allow card numbers in email, chat, text, or paper.
