At the Micro tier the motel keeps three core policies. The workforce use rules that an Acceptable Use Policy would hold are short enough to sit with the access rules staff already read, so they live in **POL-02 Part C. Workforce use rules**, with the card data and AI tool rules in POL-04:

| Essential rule | Where it lives | PCI DSS v4.0.1 requirement group (N72-R01) |
|---|---|---|
| Motel systems for motel work; front desk PC for the PMS, email, and approved sites only | POL-02 C.1 | 12.2 |
| No guest or card data in personal accounts, messaging apps, or public AI chatbots | POL-02 C.2 | 12.2 |
| Card numbers only on the P2PE terminals, pay-by-link, or the booking engine; never typed into a PC or written down | POL-04 4.2 | 3.2, 4.2 |
| Approved AI tools only | POL-04 4.7 | 12.2 |
| Lock or switch accounts; never share passwords or MFA codes | POL-02 C.3 | 8.2 |
| Caller and visitor verification; no room numbers or guest details by phone | POL-02 C.4 | 12.6 |
| Training at hire and yearly | POL-02 C.5 | 12.6 |
| Report suspected incidents within 1 hour | POL-02 C.6 and POL-03 4.2 | 12.10 |
| Signed acknowledgment at hire and after each yearly update | POL-02 C.7 | 12.2 |
