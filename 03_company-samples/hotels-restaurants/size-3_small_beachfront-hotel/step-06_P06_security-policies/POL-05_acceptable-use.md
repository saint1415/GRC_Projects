## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how the workforce uses hotel systems, devices, and data, including payment devices and AI tools.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (owners, employees, contractors, and staff supplied by the staffing company) at the hotel, its restaurant, and its bars. Covers all systems and data, including services that vendors operate for the hotel (PMS, payment services, booking engine, channel manager, cloud tenant, chatbot, and pricing system). It applies to payment card data, guest personal information, and all other hotel information.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |

| IT Manager | Security awareness content, reminders, and phishing exercises |
| Front Office Manager, Food and Beverage Director | Device inspections and caller verification in their areas |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Hotel systems are for hotel business. Limited personal use (for example, checking personal email on a break on the staff Wi-Fi) is allowed if it does not involve guest or card data or create risk. Personal devices must never connect to the front desk network. (PL-4)
4.2 Every workforce member must sign this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4(1); PCI 12.2)
4.3 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year. It covers phishing, card data handling, device tampering, and social engineering by phone. The IT Manager sends monthly reminders and runs phishing exercises at least quarterly. (AT-2; PR.AT-01; PCI 12.6)
4.4 **Callers.** Never give out a guest's room number, folio details, or any card digits by phone. Verify callers who claim to be guests with the verification script, and transfer anyone asking for card details to the Front Office Manager. (AT-2; PR.AT-02)
4.5 **Payment devices.** Inspect your terminals and pay-at-table handhelds at the start of each shift for overlays, loose parts, or changed serial labels, and record the check. Never let anyone service or replace a device without a work order confirmed by your manager. (PCI 9.5.1)
4.6 Lock your screen when you step away. Position front desk screens so guests cannot read them. (AC-11)
4.7 Do not install software, browser extensions, or connect USB storage unless IT approves it. (CM-11)
4.8 Do not open unexpected attachments or links, especially "group booking" or "invoice" emails. Report suspicious messages with the Report Phishing button. (AT-2; PCI 5.4)
4.9 Report lost key encoders, suspicious devices, card numbers found in email or chat, and anything unusual to IT immediately (POL-03 4.2).
4.10 **Generative AI:** use only tools on the approved list (P10). Never paste guest or card data into a tool that is not approved. Only the Revenue Manager may change pricing-system settings, and only the Director of Sales and Marketing may change chatbot answers. (PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions rule in POL-01 section 4.8. Sanctions range from retraining to termination, depending on intent and harm. For contracted staff, the staffing company is asked to remove the person from the hotel assignment. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), the PCI DSS self-assessment, and the access reviews in this policy set.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.6. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the majority owner for High risk), and expire within 12 months. No exception may allow storage of card security codes after authorization.
