## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the group's own records before any assessment work began on 2026-05-04. It covers the legal entities; the systems that store, process, or transmit card data or guest, visitor, owner, children's, or loan customer information in each division and in corporate shared services; the suppliers that touch them; and the rules that may bind each division and the group. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Items are grouped by division: the title of each row starts with Group, Hotels, Attractions, or Vacation Ownership. The 2026 penetration and segmentation test ran after the intake window; its report was requested at intake and is recorded as an intake follow-up (EV-069). Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (interviews, scans, document reviews, tests) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable. The [asset inventory](asset-inventory.csv), [vendor register](vendor-register.csv), and [obligations register](obligations-register.csv) were built from the same records.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the regulatory analyses (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Entities and scale.** The holding company is an SEC registrant with three subsidiaries and corporate shared services in the parent; the finance subsidiary joined with Vacation Ownership on 2024-07-01. No entity holds a gaming license or a bank charter, the group has no federal contracts and no Illinois operations, and it does not franchise (EV-001). The workforce is 45,000: Hotels about 25,000, Attractions about 11,500 at peak, Vacation Ownership about 6,500, and corporate about 2,000 (EV-002). FY2025 revenue was about $18.0 billion: Hotels $7.45 billion, Attractions $5.60 billion, and Vacation Ownership $4.95 billion (EV-003).

**Card payments and PCI DSS validation.** About 57 million card transactions were processed in 2025. The three merchant agreements set a QSA ROC for Hotels (due 2026-11-30) and Attractions (due 2026-12-15), an SAQ D for Vacation Ownership (due 2026-12-31), and acquirer notice within 24 hours of a suspected compromise (EV-004). The 2025 Hotels merchant ROC (2025-11-14) records 4 compensating controls, all on the legacy POS; the Hotels service provider ROC (2026-03-20) covers the systems the division runs for the 58 managed hotels and SYS-G4 in full; the Attractions ROC is dated 2025-12-05 (EV-005). Scope documents were confirmed in 2025-12, quarterly reviews ran, and the targeted risk analyses set weekly POI inspections (EV-006).

**Group identity and access.** About 38,500 workforce and 6,200 contractor and vendor identities sign in with MFA across 52 federated applications, administrators with phishing-resistant authenticators; Vacation Ownership's 6,500 users stay on the division directory until 2027-03-31 (EV-007). Identity governance runs on HR events, temporary accounts expire, and inactive accounts are disabled after 90 days; legacy POS and kiosk local accounts are outside it (EV-008). PAM covers 11 of 13 property-system vendors; the legacy POS vendor and one door lock vendor use their own always-on remote tools (EV-009). The Q1 2026 certification was completed for SYS-G1-connected systems (EV-010).

**Group security operations and testing.** EDR covers managed endpoints, cloud POS stations, and cloud workloads, but not the legacy POS and kiosk servers or the Vacation Ownership legacy data center (EV-011). The 24x7 SOC receives no logs from legacy POS servers or the Vacation Ownership legacy data center and directory, and the CRS integration service account is outside behavior analytics (EV-012). Cases follow one severity scale, except in Vacation Ownership, which uses its 2023 procedure (EV-013). The 2025 incident response plan and playbooks are tested technically each quarter; no exercise has covered a cross-division incident, and the draft notification matrix has not been exercised (EV-014). Scans run quarterly with passing ASV scans, except in the Vacation Ownership legacy data center, which was scanned once in 12 months (EV-015). The 2026 penetration test statement of work excludes the legacy data center (EV-016).

**Policies and common controls.** The 2025 group policies are aligned to CSF 2.0 and PCI DSS v4.0.1, and the Group AI Standard was adopted in 2026-03 (EV-017). The Attractions supplement does not address ride control or children's information, and Vacation Ownership follows its 2023 standards (EV-018). The common control catalog lists 91 controls, with inheritance matrices for Hotels and Attractions and none for Vacation Ownership (EV-019).

**Cloud, network, and payment services.** Each provider has a landing zone with division accounts, a dedicated CDE account for SYS-G4, and guardrails that deny public storage and unencrypted volumes (EV-020). Payment VLANs at 88 hotels deny by default; at 9 managed hotels the owner's corporate network connects to the property edge under rule sets approved before 2020 (EV-021). SYS-G4 holds about 11.8 million tokens, with vault keys under dual control and an active standby region (EV-022). Backups are immutable with quarterly restore tests, and the 2026 failover tests of SYS-G1 and SYS-G4 each recorded recovery within 1 hour (EV-023). Logs are kept 12 months under object lock (EV-024).

**Guest profile hub and privacy.** SYS-G5 holds about 52 million profiles in one store, about 7.9 million with identity document numbers, with no retention rule; a payment preferences table synced in 2025 holds full bank account and routing numbers for about 212,000 autopay owners (EV-025). Member MFA is optional, with 27% adoption (EV-026). Hotel and park notices do not name vacation ownership sales tours as a use of guest data (EV-027).

**Disclosure, governance, risk, and audit.** The FY2025 Form 10-K includes the Item 106 description. The materiality playbook has not been exercised with a cross-division incident, and the escalation procedure has no trigger for incidents that start at a managed hotel owner, the legacy POS vendor, or a Vacation Ownership service provider (EV-028). The board sets risk acceptance levels (EV-029), the risk strategy and 2025 registers are on file (EV-030), and internal audit has mapped a CCPA cybersecurity audit with no auditor engaged (EV-031).

**Workforce, vendors, AI, records, and continuity.** 2026 training completion is 96% in Hotels, 98% in Attractions, and 91% in Vacation Ownership, which uses 2023 content; managers at the 23 legacy POS hotels have no device inspection module (EV-032). Background checks cover CDE and front office roles (EV-033). There are about 1,400 vendors, 140 of them high risk; Vacation Ownership vendors have no review recorded since the acquisition (EV-034). The legacy POS vendor's AOC covers its software and not its remote support service (EV-035). 11 AI use cases were found, most of them live before the AI council formed in 2026-03 (EV-036). The retention schedule has no entry for SYS-G5 guest profiles (EV-037). Facility, media disposal, change, and continuity records are in EV-038 to EV-040.

**Hotels.** 88 hotels: 30 owned or leased and 58 managed for 41 ownership groups; 31 management agreements signed before 2020 assign no security responsibilities or incident notice duties (EV-041). The responsibility matrix has reached 23 of the 41 ownership groups, and owners and lenders asked for a SOC 2 report (EV-042). Validated P2PE runs at 71 front desks and 142 outlets; 72 outlets at 23 hotels run the legacy POS; 17 managed hotels keep device lists by hand; the replacement program is 61% complete (EV-043). The support contract sets the legacy POS vendor's always-on remote tool and its anti-malware-only terms, and the POS operating system leaves vendor support on 2027-01-31 (EV-044). Card data is in clear text in legacy POS memory, local accounts use 6-digit PINs, and the CRS integration credential is stored on each server (EV-045). The PMS has about 14,800 users, and 42 accounts can display full card numbers (EV-046). The CRS integration service account key was last rotated in 2024 (EV-047). One door lock vendor uses its own always-on remote tool (EV-048). Revenue management publishes rates automatically at 30 owned and 49 managed hotels, with an emergency freeze at the owned hotels only, and the vendor product includes pooled benchmarking (EV-049). Total price has been shown on brand channels since 2025-05; the chatbot's fee table does not include the 6 resort hotels (EV-050). Tone-masking keeps card numbers off contact center desktops (EV-051), and payment links replaced emailed card forms in 2025 (EV-052).

**Attractions.** 7 parks with about 24 million visits and about 820,000 passholders; 96 kiosks and carts at the 2 Florida theme parks run the legacy POS (EV-053). Finger-scan templates for about 640,000 people are held by the gate vendor, with no deletion after a pass expires (EV-054). The kids' club has about 96,000 child profiles; one parent email consent covers both collection and sharing with a marketing analytics vendor, and no deletion rule is set (EV-055). The online notice has no retention section, and the vendor file has no written assurances from the analytics vendor (EV-056). 3 agency-built ticket microsites are outside the script inventory and tamper monitoring (EV-057). Ride control networks are isolated by design, with a vendor maintenance jump host (EV-058). The 2026-02 park tabletop did not cover the shared vendor or the cross-division matrix (EV-059).

**Vacation Ownership.** 24 resorts, about 410,000 owner families, about 126,000 loans, and about 212,000 owners on autopay; about 46% of tours come from hotel and park leads (EV-060). The finance subsidiary board designated the Qualified Individual in 2024-09; the Identity Theft Prevention Program was last updated in 2021 (EV-061). 340 users reach loan origination without MFA, and no written approval of an equivalent control was provided (EV-062). The loan document archive of about 3.1 million documents has no encryption at rest, and SYS-V3 restore was tested once, in 2025 (EV-063). The 2023 incident response plan has no FTC notification step (EV-064). 12 of 31 service providers with customer information have no assessment since the acquisition (EV-065). The credit model maps reason codes to generic text, and the inventory model keeps no record of each reservation decision (EV-066). In 9 of 24 associations the same team prepares and reviews billing reconciliations (EV-067). Owner portal MFA is optional, and gallery terminals connect to division workstations (EV-068).

**After the window.** The 2026 penetration test found the door lock vendor's default administrator password on a lock server and a path through a vendor jump host into ride control at 2 of 7 parks (EV-069).
