## 1. Purpose
Set the rules every workforce member follows when using group systems and handling guest, visitor, owner, and card information.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members, including seasonal staff and staff at managed hotels, and all group-owned or group-managed devices and accounts.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Read, acknowledge, and follow this policy |
| Managers | Make sure their staff complete training before receiving access |
| Group HR director | Runs training and acknowledgment tracking |
| Group CISO | Maintains the approved-tools list with the Group AI council |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy at hire and every year. (PL-4; Req 12.2)

4.2 Use group systems only for authorized business. Do not install software, connect personal devices to staff or payment networks, or disable security tools. (PL-4; AC-20)

4.3 Complete security awareness training at hire, before access to card data or customer information, and every year; take part in phishing simulations. Report suspected phishing with the report button. (AT-2; PR.AT-01; Req 5.4.1, 12.6; 314.4(e)(1))

4.4 Role-based training is required for front desk, outlet, contact center, gate, sales gallery, and loan processing staff, including terminal inspection, guest identity verification, card handling, and Red Flags procedures. (AT-3; PR.AT-02; 16 CFR 681.1(e)(3))

4.5 **Card data handling.** Never write down, email, message, photograph, or store card numbers. Take card payments only on approved terminals, payment links, or the tone-masking phone system. Inspect terminals as the supplement requires and report any sign of tampering. (PL-4; Req 3.2, 9.5)

4.6 **Prices.** When quoting a room rate, event ticket, or vacation rental, quote the total price including mandatory fees first, by phone, chat, or in person. (PL-4; 16 CFR 464.2)

4.7 **Generative AI.** Use only approved AI tools. Do not enter Restricted or Confidential information into any AI tool unless the approved-tools list allows that class. AI output that will be sent to a guest, owner, borrower, or regulator must be reviewed by a person. (PL-4(1); GV.PO-01)

4.8 **Removable media and printing.** Do not copy Restricted information to removable media. Collect printed guest and owner documents immediately and shred them. (MP-7; PL-4)

4.9 **Social engineering.** Do not issue room keys, change reservations, reset accounts, or change payment details for callers or visitors without the verification steps in the division supplement. (PL-4; AT-2)

4.10 Systems display a use notice at sign-in, and use may be monitored. (AC-8)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.7. Compliance is checked through training records, phishing results, card data discovery scans, and P07.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.11.
