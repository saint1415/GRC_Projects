**Where the acceptable use rules are:**

- POL-01 section 9 (Acceptable use and training): devices for inn work and the owner only (9.1), physical care of devices (9.2), updates and antivirus (9.3), guest and owner networks (9.4), AI tools (9.5), annual training (9.6), accurate prices and privacy statements (9.7).
- POL-01 section 5: sanctions and exceptions. POL-01 Appendix B: device settings checklist.
- SP 800-53 PL-4 and AT-1, and PCI DSS Requirement 12.2 (acceptable use), are met by POL-01 as a whole.
