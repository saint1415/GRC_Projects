At the Sole Proprietorship tier the inn keeps **one** consolidated policy, because one person writes, follows, and checks it (`01_company-sizes/tier-project-scaling.csv`, t1 P06). This file is kept so the folder has the standard policy set names. It holds no separate rules.

**Where the incident response rules are:**

- POL-01 section 10 (Incident response): runbook and printed contacts (10.1), same-day incident log (10.2), 24-hour notice to the payment facilitator (10.3), notification deadlines (10.4), ransom decisions (10.5), annual walkthrough and lessons learned (10.6).
- The procedure itself is the P08 runbook (`../step-08_P08_incident-response-runbook/ir-runbook.md`) and its notification matrix.
- SP 800-53 IR-1 and PCI DSS Requirement 12.10 (incident response plan) are met by POL-01 section 10 with the P08 runbook.

Traceability for every statement is in `policy-control-map.csv`.
