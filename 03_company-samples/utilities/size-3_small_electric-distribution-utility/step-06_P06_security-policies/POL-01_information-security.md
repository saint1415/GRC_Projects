## 1. Purpose
Set up the Cris Santos Company security program for both information technology (IT) and operational technology (OT). Assign who is accountable, and give every other security policy its authority. The program protects the safe and reliable delivery of electricity, the Bulk Electric System equipment the company owns, and customer information.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (employees, contractors, and temporary staff) at every company site, including substations and crew yards. Covers all company systems and data: corporate IT, the Distribution Operations Platform (SCADA, OMS, substation and field networks), the low impact BES Cyber Systems at Substation N and Substation E, the cloud tenant, and systems that vendors operate for the company.

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 The company must maintain one security program for IT and OT, documented in this policy set, the System Security Plan, and the CIP low impact cyber security plan. (PM-1; GV.PO-01)

4.2 The IT Manager is the Information Security Lead. The CIP Senior Manager must be identified by name in a dated record approved by the President and CEO, and any change must be documented within 30 calendar days. Delegations of CIP Senior Manager authority must name the delegate or title, the actions delegated, and the date, be approved by the CIP Senior Manager, and be updated within 30 days of a change. (PM-2; GV.RR-02; CIP-003-9 R3, R4)

4.3 A risk assessment must be performed at least annually and after major changes, using NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1. Risks must be tracked in the risk register with an owner and treatment. (RA-3; PM-9)

4.4 Risk acceptance authority: the IT Manager may accept Low risks; the President and CEO, Moderate; the majority owner, High and Very High. Risks to public or worker safety rated High may not be accepted without a dated treatment plan. A known noncompliance with a NERC Reliability Standard may never be accepted as a risk. (PM-9; GV.RM-01)

4.5 Security policies must be reviewed at least annually. The policy statements that cover the CIP-003-9 low impact topics (table below) must also be reviewed and approved by the CIP Senior Manager at least once every 15 calendar months. (PL-1; GV.PO-02; CIP-003-9 R1)

4.6 The CIP-002 identifications must be reviewed at least once every 15 calendar months and approved by the CIP Senior Manager or delegate, even if nothing changed. Any new substation, 115 kV Protection System, UFLS scheme change, or change in NERC registration must trigger a review before it goes into service. The record must state why excluded systems (UFLS relays, the DCC, the distribution SCADA) are out of scope. (RA-2; ID.AM-05; CIP-002-5.1a R1, R2)

4.7 Exceptions to any security policy must be requested in writing, risk-rated, approved per 4.4, recorded in the risk register, and time-limited to 12 months or less. No exception may be granted to a requirement of a NERC Reliability Standard. (PL-1)

4.8 **Sanctions.** Workforce members who break security policies must be sanctioned in proportion to intent and harm: retraining, written warning, suspension, or termination. HR must document each sanction. (PS-8; GV.RR-04)

4.9 Before a vendor gets remote access to OT, handles customer personal information, or hosts a company system, its contract must include security terms: incident notice within 72 hours at most, remote access rules, and the right to request its assurance reports. Key vendors must be reviewed every year (P09). (SA-9; GV.SC-05)

4.10 Anyone who suspects the company is not meeting a NERC Reliability Standard must tell the NERC Compliance Coordinator within 5 business days. The CIP Senior Manager decides on a Self-Report to SERC, and mitigation must be tracked in the POA&M. (CA-5; GV.OC-03)

4.11 Security controls must be assessed at least annually (P07) and after major changes. CIP evidence must be kept for at least three calendar years, and longer if SERC requires it or a noncompliance is still being mitigated. Other security records must be kept for at least three years. (CA-2; SI-12; CIP-003-9 Compliance 1.2)

4.12 **CIP Exceptional Circumstances** (for example, a hurricane, a risk of injury, or a declared emergency) may be declared only by the CIP Senior Manager or the Manager of System Operations on duty. The declaration, its start and end, and the requirements affected must be recorded. Normal controls must be restored as soon as the circumstance ends. (IR-4; CP-2; CIP-003-9 R1 Part 1.2.7)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement (POL-01 4.8). Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), the access reviews in POL-02, and the NERC Compliance Coordinator's calendar.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 4.7. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the majority owner for High risk), and expire within 12 months. NERC requirements cannot be excepted.
