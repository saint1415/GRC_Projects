## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how the workforce uses company systems, devices, and data, in the office, in the DCC, and in the field.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (employees, contractors, and temporary staff) at every company site, including substations and crew yards. Covers all company systems and data: corporate IT, the Distribution Operations Platform, the low impact BES Cyber Systems at Substation N and Substation E, the cloud tenant, and systems that vendors operate for the company.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Manager | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and annually; tracks training |
| IT Manager | Awareness content, reminders, and phishing exercises |
| Manager of Engineering and Protection | Transient Cyber Asset and removable media rules at substations |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use of corporate email and internet is allowed if it creates no risk. OT systems (SCADA HMIs, engineering workstations, relays) must never be used for anything else. (PL-4)

4.2 Every workforce member must sign this policy before getting access and again each year. (PL-4)

4.3 **Awareness.** All staff, including field and DCC staff, must complete security awareness training at hire and every year. This always happens within 15 calendar months, which meets CIP-003-9. The IT Manager must send monthly reminders and run phishing exercises for all staff with company email at least quarterly. (AT-2; PR.AT-01; CIP-003-9 Attachment 1 Section 1)

4.4 DCC operators and protection staff must complete role-based training on recognizing and reporting OT cyber events every year. (AT-3; PR.AT-02)

4.5 Lock your screen when you step away from a corporate computer. Do not install software or browser extensions unless IT approves it. (AC-11; CM-11)

4.6 **Transient Cyber Assets.** Only company-managed laptops may connect to relays, gateways, or SCADA equipment. The one exception is a contractor laptop that protection staff have reviewed before connecting. The review must cover antivirus level, allowlisting, or hardening, and a decision on any extra step, such as a scan at the company kiosk. The review must be recorded. (SI-3; PR.PS-01; CIP-003-9 Attachment 1 Sections 5.1, 5.2)

4.7 **Removable media.** USB drives and other removable media must be scanned at a company scanning kiosk before they are connected to any relay, gateway, or SCADA computer. Media with detected malware must not be used and must be reported to the IT Manager. Personal USB drives are never allowed on OT. (MP-7; SI-3; CIP-003-9 Attachment 1 Section 5.3)

4.8 Do not open unexpected attachments or links. Report suspicious messages with the Report Phishing button. Report lost or stolen devices, keys, and badges, and anything suspicious at a substation, to the DCC immediately (POL-03 4.2). (AT-2)

4.9 **Generative AI:** use only tools on the approved list (P10). Never enter customer personal information, relay settings, network diagrams, or other Restricted data into a tool that is not approved. (PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement (POL-01 4.8). Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), training records, and the Transient Cyber Asset review records.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 4.7. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the majority owner for High risk), and expire within 12 months. Statements 4.3, 4.6, and 4.7 carry NERC requirements and cannot be excepted.
