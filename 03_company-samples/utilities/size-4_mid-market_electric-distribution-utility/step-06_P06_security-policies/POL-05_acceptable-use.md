## 1. Purpose
Set the rules every workforce member follows when using company systems, devices, data, and AI tools.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members, contractors, and vendors who use company systems or devices, including truck tablets, DCC consoles, laptops, phones, and SaaS accounts.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Read and accept this policy at hire and each year; follow it |
| Managers | Make sure their staff complete training and follow this policy |
| HR Director | Collects acceptances; applies sanctions with the manager |
| Information Security Manager | Training content, phishing simulations, web filtering |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Every workforce member must accept this policy at hire and each year. (PL-4; PR.AT-01)

4.2 **Training.** Every workforce member, including field and DCC staff, must complete the annual security and CIP awareness module and take part in phishing simulations; office staff monthly and field and DCC staff each quarter on their tablets and phones. Role-based training is required for OT engineers, protection staff, dispatchers, and staff handling customer identity data (Red Flags). (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02; CIP-003-9 Attachment 1 Section 1)

4.3 Company systems are for business use. Limited personal use of email and the internet is allowed on IT devices. **No personal use is allowed on DCC consoles, engineering workstations, OT laptops, or truck tablets.** (PL-4)

4.4 **Removable media.** USB drives and other removable media may be connected to OT systems or relays only after a scan at a company kiosk the same day. Personal media may never be connected to company OT. (MP-7; DE.CM-09; CIP-003-9 Attachment 1 Section 5.3)

4.5 **Transient devices.** Only company-managed laptops may be connected to relays and substation devices, unless a contractor laptop passes the pre-connection checklist signed by protection staff. (AC-20; CIP-003-9 Attachment 1 Section 5.2)

4.6 **Remote access equipment.** No one may install a modem, cellular router, wireless access point, or remote access tool at any substation or on the OT network without an approved change and a CIP-002 and access path review. (SC-7; CM-3)

4.7 Personal devices may reach email and chat only through the company app protection, and never the OT network, the CIS, or the AMI head-end. (AC-19; AC-20)

4.8 **Generative AI.** Only AI tools approved through P10 may be used for company work. Customer data, client data, Restricted data, CEII, and credentials must never be entered into unapproved AI tools. Output from any AI tool must be checked by the user before it is used in a decision, a customer communication, or an operating plan. (PL-4; PR.DS-01)

4.9 Users must lock their screens when leaving a workstation (DCC operator consoles are exempt, POL-02 4.14), report lost devices within 1 hour, and report suspicious email with the report button. (AC-11; IR-6)

4.10 Users must not try to bypass security controls, test security without written authorization, or access data they do not need for their job. (PL-4)

4.11 The company monitors use of its systems for security, as allowed by law. Users have no expectation of privacy on company systems. (PL-4; AU-6)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 4.10. Compliance is checked through training records, phishing results, web filtering reports, and P07.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 4.9. Statements 4.4 to 4.6 carry NERC requirements or protect OT and cannot be excepted.
