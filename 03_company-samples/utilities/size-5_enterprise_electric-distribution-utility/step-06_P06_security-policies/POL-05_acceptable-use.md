## 1. Purpose
Set the rules every workforce member follows when using company devices, networks, data, and AI tools, with extra rules for the control rooms, substations, and other OT locations.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members and vendor personnel who use company systems or enter company control centers and substations.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Chief Human Resources Officer | Owns this policy, attestations, and training records |
| Managers | Make sure their staff complete training and follow these rules |
| Director, OT Security | Transient Cyber Asset and Removable Media rules for OT |
| Chief Data and Analytics Officer | Approved AI tools list (STD-05.2) |
| All workforce | Follow these rules and report violations |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy and the code of conduct at hire and every year. (PL-4; GV.RR-04)
4.2 Every workforce member must complete security awareness training at hire and every year; staff with CIP access must also complete CIP role-based training before access and at least once every 15 calendar months and receive security awareness reinforcement every calendar quarter. (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02)
4.3 Phishing simulations must include control room and field staff, with vishing scenarios for the TCC and DCC. (AT-2(3); PR.AT-01)
4.4 Only company-managed laptops, tablets, and phones may connect to company networks or access Restricted or Confidential data; personal devices may use only approved web email and collaboration with conditional access. (AC-19; AC-20; PR.PS-01)
4.5 Only company-issued, scanned removable media may connect to OT systems; every device (laptop, test set, or media) must be checked under STD-05.3 before it connects to a BES Cyber System or the ADMS, including vendor and contractor devices. (MP-7; SI-3; PR.PS-01)
4.6 Workforce members must not install software or change security settings on company devices unless their role permits it. (CM-11; PR.PS-01)
4.7 Only AI tools on the approved AI tools list may be used for company work, within the data classes approved for each tool; any new AI use, including AI features switched on in existing products, must be registered with the AI council before use. (AC-20; SA-9; GV.RM-06)
4.8 Workforce members must not post photographs or descriptions of control rooms, substations, OT screens, or security measures on social media or public sites. (PL-4(1); PR.AT-01)
4.9 In the TCC and DCC, personal phones must stay outside operator positions, and only approved devices may be connected to consoles (PRC-05.1). (AC-19; PE-5; PR.AA-06)
4.10 Workforce members must report lost or stolen devices, suspected phishing, and suspicious activity at facilities immediately. (IR-6; RS.MA-02)
4.11 Workforce members must not share passwords or authenticators or let others use their badges. (IA-5; PE-3; PR.AA-01)
4.12 Use of company systems may be monitored; there is no expectation of privacy in company systems, except as the law provides. (PL-4; AC-8; GV.PO-01)

## 5. Standards and procedures under this policy
- STD-05.1 Security Awareness and Training Standard (including CIP-004 training)
- STD-05.2 Approved AI Tools List
- STD-05.3 Transient Cyber Asset and Removable Media Standard
- PRC-05.1 Control Room Rules Procedure (TCC and DCC)

## 6. Compliance and enforcement
Compliance is monitored through attestations, training reports, phishing results, endpoint and DLP alerts, and the annual Internal Audit assessment (P07). Violations are handled under PRC-01.1.

## 7. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 7. CIP-004 training and CIP-010 Transient Cyber Asset requirements cannot be excepted.
