## 1. Purpose
Set the rules every workforce member follows when using group systems, OT equipment, and client information.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members, contractors, and vendor personnel who use group systems or connect to group or client OT.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
Every user acknowledges this policy at hire and every year. Managers enforce it. The Group CISO owns the approved-tools list.

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Use group systems for business purposes. Limited personal use of IT systems is allowed; **no personal use of OT systems**. (PL-4)

4.2 Only company-managed devices may connect to OT networks or client substations. Commissioning and relay laptops must meet the Transient Cyber Asset rules in the division supplement before each connection. (AC-20; CIP-010-4 R4; CIP-003-9 Attachment 1 Section 5)

4.3 Removable media must be scanned at an approved kiosk before use in any OT environment. (MP-7)

4.4 Do not store company or client information in personal cloud storage, personal email, or personal devices. (AC-20; PL-4(1))

4.5 **AI tools.** Use only AI tools on the approved-tools list, and only with the data classes approved for that tool. Never enter Restricted data, CEII, BCSI, or client documents into an AI tool unless the tool is approved for that data and, for client data, the client has agreed. Engineers remain responsible for every design, settings file, or calculation an AI tool helps produce, and must review it before release. (PL-4; CM-10)

4.6 Install software only through approved processes. (CM-11)

4.7 Report phishing and suspected incidents at once (POL-03 4.1). (AT-2)

4.8 Do not post grid, field, or client infrastructure details on social media or public sites. (PL-4(1))

4.9 Wear badges in group facilities, escort visitors, and never allow tailgating into control centers, substations, or the POC. (PE-3)

4.10 Group systems are monitored. Users have no expectation of privacy on them, within the limits of applicable law. (PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.7.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.10.
