## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how workforce members use hospital systems, devices, and information, so that everyday work does not create avoidable risk to patients or their data.

## 2. Scope
Everyone with access to hospital systems, including affiliated practice users and agency staff, who must acknowledge this policy before their account is activated and each year after.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Director | Owns this policy; acknowledgment records for employees |
| Information Security Manager | Technical monitoring; approved AI tools list (with the AI governance process) |
| Medical Staff Office Manager | Acknowledgments by physicians |
| Department leaders and sponsors | Make sure their staff and non-employees know and follow these rules |
| All users | Follow these rules and report violations |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Use hospital systems for authorized work. Limited personal use of the internet is allowed on non-clinical workstations if it does not affect work or security. (PL-4)

4.2 Look up a patient's record only when you have a work reason for that patient. Looking up your own record, your family's, a coworker's, or a public figure's out of curiosity is a violation, and access is monitored. (PL-4; AU-6)

4.3 Never share passwords or badges, and never sign in for someone else. Lock or tap out when you step away from a workstation. (PL-4; IA-2)

4.4 Report suspected phishing with the report button, and report lost devices, misdirected PHI, or anything unusual to the service desk or privacy hotline right away. (IR-6; AT-2)

4.5 Complete security and privacy training at hire (before account activation for non-employees) and each year. (AT-2; 164.308(a)(5))

4.6 Do not install software, connect personal devices to clinical networks, or plug unknown USB media into hospital equipment. Personal phones used for secure messaging must use the hospital's app container. (CM-11; AC-19; AC-20)

4.7 Send PHI outside the hospital only through approved channels (secure messaging, encrypted email, saved fax numbers, the HIE). Do not use personal email or personal cloud storage for hospital work. (SC-8; PL-4)

4.8 **Generative AI.** Do not enter PHI, patient identifiers, or Confidential information into public AI chatbots or AI tools that are not on the approved-tools list. Approved tools are listed on the intranet with their allowed uses (STD-05). You are responsible for checking any AI output you use. (PL-4; SA-9)

4.9 **Recording.** Do not record patients, visitors, or coworkers (audio or video) without their consent. Ambient AI scribes may be used only by approved clinicians, after every person in the room has been told and has agreed, as Florida law requires all-party consent (Fla. Stat. 934.03); stop recording if anyone objects. (PL-4)

4.10 **Clinical decision support settings.** Do not change, disable, or ask a vendor to change alert thresholds, sepsis model settings, or other decision support rules outside the Clinical Decision Support Committee's change process. (CM-3; PL-4)

4.11 Keep screens that show PHI out of public view, and use privacy filters at registration and nursing stations near public areas. (164.310(c))

4.12 Do not try to bypass security controls, test systems for weaknesses, or access systems you are not authorized to use. (PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Use is monitored as described in POL-02. Violations are handled under POL-01 4.8 and, for physicians, the medical staff bylaws.

## 6. Acknowledgment
Each user acknowledges this policy electronically. Records are kept by HR (employees), the Medical Staff Office (physicians), and IT (affiliated practice users, agency staff, and vendors).
