## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how the workforce uses hospital systems, devices, and data.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members of Cris Santos Company, LLC: employees, contracted clinicians, agency staff, students, and volunteers. Covers all hospital systems and devices and any personal device used to reach hospital systems.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Manager | Collects signed acknowledgments from employees and agency staff at start and annually; tracks training |
| Medical staff office | Collects acknowledgments from contracted clinicians at appointment and reappointment |
| IT Manager | Security awareness content, reminders, and phishing exercises |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Hospital systems are for hospital business. Limited personal use (for example, checking personal email on a break) is allowed if it does not involve patient data or create risk. (PL-4; 164.310(b))
4.2 Every workforce member, including contracted clinicians and agency staff, must sign this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4)
4.3 Security awareness training is required before access and every year. The IT Manager sends monthly security reminders and runs at least quarterly phishing exercises. (AT-2; PR.AT-01; 164.308(a)(5)(ii)(A))
4.4 Lock or badge out of your workstation when you step away. Position screens so patients and visitors cannot read them. (AC-11; 164.310(c))
4.5 Do not install software, browser extensions, or connect USB storage unless IT approves it. (CM-11)
4.6 Do not open unexpected attachments or links. Report suspicious messages with the Report Phishing button. (AT-2)
4.7 Report lost or stolen devices, misdirected PHI, unusual device behavior, and anything suspicious to the 24x7 incident line immediately (POL-03 4.2).
4.8 **Generative AI:** use only tools on the approved list. Never paste or dictate patient information into a tool that is not approved. (PL-4)
4.9 Look up patient records only when your job requires it, including records of neighbors, coworkers, and well-known people. Access is logged and reviewed monthly. (AU-6)
4.10 Do not change settings on medical devices, dispensing cabinets, or clinical decision support tools (such as the sepsis alert) outside the approved change process. (CM-3)
4.11 Use the EHR mobile app on a personal phone only after the phone is enrolled in the hospital's device management. (AC-19)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement (POL-01 4.8). Sanctions range from retraining to termination or removal from the schedule, depending on intent and harm. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07) and the access reviews in this policy set.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.6. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the governing body for High and Very High risk), and expire within 12 months.
