## 1. Purpose
Set up the hospital's information security program, assign who is accountable, and give every other security policy its authority. The program protects the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of patient and hospital information, and keeps the hospital able to care for patients when technology fails or is attacked.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members of Cris Santos Company, LLC: employees, contracted clinicians (emergency physicians, telehospitalists, telepharmacists, teleradiologists, and medical staff with privileges), agency staff, students, and volunteers. Covers all systems and data, including medical devices, building and clinical OT on the hospital network, and systems that business associates operate for the hospital. It applies to electronic protected health information (ePHI) and all other hospital information.

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 The hospital must maintain an information security program that meets the HIPAA Security Rule and is documented in this policy set and the System Security Plan. (PM-1; GV.PO-01)
4.2 The IT Manager is the designated Security Officer. The designation must be in writing. (PM-2; GV.RR-02; 164.308(a)(2))
4.3 A risk analysis must be performed at least annually and after major changes (for example a new clinical system, a new AI tool, or a network redesign), using NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1. It must cover medical devices, building OT, and cloud services. Risks must be tracked in the risk register with an owner and treatment. (RA-3; PM-9; 164.308(a)(1)(ii)(A)-(B))
4.4 Risk acceptance authority: the IT Manager may accept Low risks; the CEO, Moderate; the governing body, High and Very High. Patient-safety risks rated High or above may not be accepted without a dated treatment plan. (PM-9; GV.RM-01)
4.5 Security policies must be reviewed at least annually and updated after major changes or incidents. (PL-1; GV.PO-02)
4.6 Exceptions to any security policy must be requested in writing, risk-rated, approved per 4.4, recorded in the risk register, and time-limited to 12 months or less. (PL-1)
4.7 **Emergency preparedness.** Cyberattacks and prolonged loss of the EHR, network, or internet must be scored in the all-hazards risk assessment of the emergency preparedness program, and the emergency plan must include an IT outage annex that points to the incident response runbook and downtime procedures. (CP-2; ID.IM-04; 42 CFR 485.625(a)(1)-(2))
4.8 **Sanctions.** Workforce members who fail to comply with security or privacy policies must be sanctioned in proportion to intent and harm: retraining, written warning, suspension, or termination. For contracted clinicians and agency staff, sanctions run through the medical staff rules or the contract, up to removal from the schedule. The Privacy Officer must document each sanction. (PS-8; GV.RR-04; 164.308(a)(1)(ii)(C))
4.9 **Vendors.** Before any vendor creates, receives, maintains, or transmits ePHI for the hospital, or services a device that stores ePHI, it must sign a business associate agreement and complete a security review. No BAA, no ePHI. Key vendors (EHR, MSP, teleradiology, telepharmacy, cloud) must be reviewed every year. (SA-9; GV.SC-05; 164.308(b)(1))
4.10 **Medical devices and OT.** Purchases of networked medical devices and building systems must require the manufacturer's security documentation (MDS2 or equivalent), a software bill of materials where available, and a supported patch path. Every networked device must be in the inventory. (SA-4; CM-8; ID.AM-01)
4.11 Security policies, procedures, assessments, and required actions must be retained for 6 years from creation or last effective date, whichever is later. (SI-12; 164.316(b)(2)(i))
4.12 Security controls must be evaluated at least annually (P07) and after major changes. (CA-2; 164.308(a)(8))

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement (POL-01 4.8). Sanctions range from retraining to termination or removal from the schedule, depending on intent and harm. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07) and the access reviews in this policy set.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.6. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the governing body for High and Very High risk), and expire within 12 months.
