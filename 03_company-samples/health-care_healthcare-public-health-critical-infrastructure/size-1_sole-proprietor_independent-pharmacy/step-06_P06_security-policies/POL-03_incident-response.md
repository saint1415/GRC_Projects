At the Sole Proprietorship tier the pharmacy keeps **one** consolidated policy, because one person writes, follows, and checks it (`01_company-sizes/tier-project-scaling.csv`, t1 P06). This file is kept so the folder has the standard policy set names. It holds no separate rules.

**Where the incident response rules are:**

- POL-01 section 10 (Incident response): runbook and printed contacts (10.1), same-day incident log (10.2), four-factor breach risk assessment (10.3), DEA one-business-day EPCS report (10.4), CSOS 24-hour revocation (10.5), notification deadlines (10.6), ransom decisions and annual walkthrough (10.7).
- POL-01 section 11.4: dispensing and PDMP reporting during a PMS outage.
- The procedure itself is the P08 runbook (`../step-08_P08_incident-response-runbook/ir-runbook.md`) and its notification matrix.
- SP 800-53 IR-1 is met by POL-01 as a whole.

Traceability for every statement is in `policy-control-map.csv`.
