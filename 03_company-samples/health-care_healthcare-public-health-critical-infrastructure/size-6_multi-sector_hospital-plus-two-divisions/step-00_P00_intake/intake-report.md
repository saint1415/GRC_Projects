## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the group's own records before any assessment work began on 2026-05-04. It covers the legal entities, the systems that create, receive, maintain or transmit PHI, member nonpublic personal information or student customer information in each division and in corporate shared services, the suppliers that touch them, and the rules that may bind each entity. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Items are grouped by division: the title of each row starts with Group, Hospital System, Health Plan or College. Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (interviews, samples, tests) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analyses (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Entities and scale.** The holding company is an SEC registrant with three subsidiaries; no affiliated covered entity designation is recorded for the Hospital System and the Health Plan; corporate shared services sit in the parent (EV-001). The workforce is 45,000: Hospital System 29,000, Health Plan 8,000, College 2,000, corporate 6,000 (EV-002). FY2025 revenue was about $18.0 billion: Hospital System $10.5 billion, Health Plan $7.1 billion, College $0.4 billion (EV-003). The 2021 Hospital System intercompany BAA does not mention the community-connect practices' PHI; the 2022 Health Plan BAA covers the ASO plans; the 2025 College services agreement sets same-day phone notice and omits the FERPA redisclosure limits; the 2023 data sharing addendum for the ADT feed has no minimum-necessary protocol attached (EV-004).

**Group identity and access.** About 43,000 workforce users sign in through SYS-G1 with MFA push and number matching; administrators use phishing-resistant authenticators; the College's 2,000 staff are not on SYS-G1 (EV-005). Identity governance runs joiner-mover-leaver workflows from HR events, and student and trainee EHR accounts are outside it (EV-006). The Q1 2026 certification was completed; Health Plan reviews were late twice in 2025 (EV-007). Privileged and vendor access to data center, cloud and application systems runs through PAM with session recording; 103 of 140 device vendor connections use PAM (EV-008). One directory forest serves DC1, DC2 and every division except the College, and its highest-privilege tier is not isolated (EV-009).

**Group security operations.** The SOC runs 24x7. Device networks at 4 hospitals, the 37 device vendor connections outside PAM, College campus networks, the College directory and College SaaS logs are not in the SIEM data source list (EV-010). Technical playbooks are exercised quarterly; no exercise covered cross-division notification or ransomware downtime and diversion (EV-011). IR plan v5 (2025) does not cover the College or link to the hospitals' emergency plan, and the draft notification matrix lacks insurance commissioner, Federal Student Aid and FTC contacts and has not been exercised (EV-012). The disclosure committee charter covers cyber incidents (EV-013). EDR covers servers and workstations outside the College, and College servers since 2025 (EV-014). Device networks at 4 hospitals are excluded from scanning (EV-015). The 2026-03 penetration test covered the data centers and EHR web services (EV-016).

**Data centers, network, cloud and backup.** Inside each data center, HCIS, Health Plan claims core and College file share zones are not separated from each other; hospitals have diverse carriers and analog lines (EV-017). DC2 is a colocation facility about 300 miles from DC1 (EV-018). Cloud guardrails hold, with 2% of resources deviating without an approved exception (EV-019). Storage, databases and workstations are encrypted with group-managed keys (EV-020). Daily immutable copies are kept 35 days at provider B under a separate backup identity; the restore log lists no full EHR restore from the vault (EV-021). Endpoints accept TLS 1.2 or higher only (EV-022).

**Policies, controls, risk and workforce.** The pre-2026 group policies are aligned to CSF 2.0; there is no College supplement, and the College's 2022 IT standards remain in use (EV-023, EV-078). Inheritance of the 99 common controls is documented for the Hospital System (2024) and the Health Plan (2025); for the College it is partial (EV-024). Board risk committee minutes record no written report on the College's program (EV-025). Internal audit adds the College to its 2026 plan (EV-026). Employee training completion is 98% with monthly phishing exercises; for students and trainees the hospitals hold only the schools' attestations (EV-027). Terminations disable SYS-G1 access (EV-028), and sanctions are logged (EV-029).

**Vendors, AI and shared services.** The vendor program reviews tier 1 clinical vendors each year; templates do not require signed updates or SBOMs; College vendors are outside the program (EV-030). Vendor SOC 2 reports are on file for the cloud providers, the DC2 colocation operator, the EHR vendor and the telehealth vendor (EV-031). AI in use includes the sepsis model at 9 hospitals since 2025, a deterioration index, a CT triage tool, an ED AI scribe pilot, a UM triage model in shadow mode, a fraud model, College proctoring and tutoring, and an enterprise assistant pilot (EV-032). College shares on the group file service kept their old permission groups; Health Plan reporting extracts have no purge schedule (EV-036).

**Hospital System.** 9 hospitals with 3,620 beds (all 100 or more beds), 9 EDs with about 610,000 visits a year, and no transplant or Part 2 program (EV-037). About 3.4 million patients in 3 states; about 140,000 Health Plan members were hospital patients in the last 12 months (EV-038). Claims go through a clearinghouse as standard transactions (EV-039). The HCIS inventory lists 11 ancillary servers on an unsupported operating system, and interfaced devices are not linked to their interfaces (EV-040). About 34,000 users hold unique accounts (EV-041); about 4,500 students and trainees a year get local accounts from emailed rosters, with no end date and a password only (EV-042), and the 22 outside schools' agreements do not require withdrawal notices (EV-043). Privacy monitoring alerts are reviewed weekly (EV-044). The contingency plan v4 covers site loss and assumes DC2 is clean; the vault rebuild estimate is 5 to 7 days and untested (EV-046). The 2026-04 failover test brought the EHR back at DC2 in 1 hour 40 minutes (EV-047). Downtime workstations at 3 community hospitals had no test in the last 12 months (EV-048). The median time to apply critical patches on ancillary servers is 47 days (EV-050). The ADT feed to the Health Plan sends full segments (EV-051). 63 interface and service accounts have static passwords older than 1 year (EV-052). About 52,000 devices: inventory about 81% complete, about 6,900 on unsupported operating systems (EV-054); segmentation at 5 of 9 hospitals (EV-055); 37 of 140 vendor connections bypass PAM (EV-056). The unified emergency plan and hazard analyses have no system-wide IT outage scenario or diversion criteria, and transfer agreements point mostly to group hospitals (EV-057, EV-058). The walk-through of 3 hospitals saw badge-tap workstations, privacy screens and locked downtime workstations (EV-060). Community-connect practices' MFA is attested, not verified (EV-062). Patient portal MFA is optional (EV-063). The sepsis model runs at the vendor default threshold at all 9 hospitals, with local validation records for the flagship only (EV-064).

**Health Plan.** Licensed in 5 states, none in New York; about 620,000 members, including 85,000 in 40 ASO plans (EV-065). Commissioner contacts and 72-hour clocks sit in the compliance officer's list, outside the group matrix (EV-066). The claims core restore is tested once a year (last 2026-02), and its runbooks are only on the claims file share (EV-067). UM committee minutes to 2026-03 do not mention the AI triage pilot, which runs in shadow mode (EV-068, EV-069). Brokers may still use SMS codes (EV-070). Delegated vendors are reviewed once a year (EV-071). Purposes for hospital data received for shared members are not documented (EV-072). Bank changes are verified by call-back; callers by date of birth and member ID (EV-073). The SOC 1 Type 2 report covers ASO claims administration (EV-074).

**College.** About 9,600 enrolled students and records for about 31,000 former students; about 80% receive Title IV aid; no clinic (EV-075). The 2025 compliance audit reported a finding on the outdated risk assessment (EV-076). No written Qualified Individual report to the board since 2023 (EV-077). The written risk assessment and standards date from 2022 (EV-078). MFA is enforced for email only, not for the SIS and financial aid system (EV-079). SIS logs are not reviewed or sent to the SIEM (EV-080). The last penetration test was in 2023 (EV-081). The 2022 IR plan is outside the group plan; the 2025 phishing incident compromised 3 mailboxes (EV-082). Staff training has no phishing exercises (EV-083). Vendor contracts are reviewed at signing only, and the proctoring contract is silent on face data retention (EV-084). Former students' records have no disposal schedule (EV-085). Clinical students sign a FERPA consent (EV-086). Rosters and some financial aid documents are emailed without encryption (EV-087).
