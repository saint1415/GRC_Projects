## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce uses group systems and information.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members in every division and in corporate shared services, including students and trainees on rotation in the hospitals, on any device used for group work.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow these rules; complete training; report incidents |
| Managers, preceptors, and faculty | Make sure staff and students complete training and acknowledgments |
| Group HR | Tracks acknowledgments and training completion, including for students and trainees from 2026-12 |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Workforce members must access patient, member, and student information only as needed for their job or rotation. Looking up records out of curiosity, including one's own family's or a coworker's, is prohibited. (PL-4; AC-8)

4.2 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy and their division supplement at hire and every year. Students and trainees must acknowledge it at the start of each rotation. (PL-4; PR.AT-01)

4.3 Every workforce member must complete security and privacy training at hire and every year. The group runs monthly phishing exercises for all divisions; the College joins the group program by 2026-12-31. Students and trainees must complete the hospital's training module before their EHR account is activated. (AT-2; PR.AT-01; 164.308(a)(5)(ii)(A); 16 CFR 314.4(e)(1))

4.4 Clinical, EHR security, downtime super user, and financial aid staff must complete role-based training for their duties. (AT-3; PR.AT-02)

4.5 Suspected incidents must be reported to the group SOC within 1 hour (POL-03 4.1). (IR-6)

4.6 Restricted information may be used only on group-managed devices and approved services. It must not be sent to personal email or stored on personal devices. (AC-19; AC-20; 164.310(b))

4.7 **Generative AI.** Only AI tools on the group approved-tools list may be used for group work. Restricted information must never be entered into public AI tools. (PL-4; PL-4(1))

4.8 **Clinical information in teaching.** Students and faculty must not copy patient information from the hospitals into course work, the College's learning management system, or personal devices. Case material used in teaching must be de-identified. (PL-4; 164.514(b))

4.9 **Clinical workstations and decision support.** Users must badge out of shared workstations when they leave. No one may change clinical decision support settings (for example, sepsis alert thresholds) outside the EHR change control process. (CM-3; PL-4; 164.310(c))

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
