## 1. Purpose
Set up the Cris Santos Company information security and CPNI protection program, assign who is accountable, and give every other security policy its authority. The program protects customer information, the network that carries voice and 911 calls, and the company's lawful-intercept obligations.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (employees, contractors, and temporary staff) at CO-1, CO-2, the warehouse and fleet yard, remote cabinets, and remote work locations, and the agents of vendors who access company systems, including the overflow call center. Covers all systems and data, including systems that vendors operate for the company. It applies to customer proprietary network information (CPNI), lawful-intercept information, subscriber personal information, network configuration and outage information, and all other company information.

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 The company must maintain an information security program documented in this policy set, the System Security Plan (P02), and the CPNI operating procedures. The program must take reasonable measures to discover and protect against attempts to gain unauthorized access to CPNI (47 CFR 64.2010(a)). The company uses NIST CSF 2.0 and the CISA Cross-Sector Cybersecurity Performance Goals as its benchmark for reasonable measures. (PM-1; GV.PO-01)
4.2 The IT Manager is the designated security and compliance lead, the COO is the CPNI compliance officer, and the Vice President of Network Operations is the CALEA senior officer. Designations must be in writing, and the CALEA designation and 24x7 contact appendix must be refiled with the FCC within 30 days of any change. (PM-2; GV.RR-02; 1.20003(a), (b)(4))
4.3 A security risk assessment must be performed at least annually and after major changes, using NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1. Risks must be tracked in the risk register with an owner and a treatment. (RA-3; PM-9)
4.4 Risk acceptance authority: the IT Manager may accept Low risks; the COO, Moderate; the Chief Executive Officer, High and Very High. Risks to 911 call completion or to lawful-intercept confidentiality rated High may not be accepted without a dated treatment plan. (PM-9; GV.RM-01)
4.5 Security controls must be assessed at least annually by someone who does not operate them (P07). The results must support the statement accompanying the annual CPNI certification. (CA-2; 64.2009(e))
4.6 **Annual CPNI certification.** By March 1 each year, the COO must sign and file the compliance certificate for the prior calendar year. The accompanying statement must be based on evidence: the P07 results, the POA&M, training records, complaint logs, and breach records. It must say plainly where the company is not yet compliant. Counsel reviews the draft by December 15. (PM-1; GV.OV-01; 64.2009(e))
4.7 Exceptions to any security policy must be requested in writing, risk-rated, approved under 4.4, recorded in the risk register, and time-limited to 12 months or less. (PL-1)
4.8 **Sanctions.** Workforce members who misuse CPNI or violate security policies must be sanctioned in proportion to intent and harm: retraining, written warning, suspension, or termination. Accessing or disclosing CPNI without a business need is a serious violation. HR and the Regulatory Affairs Manager must document each CPNI-related sanction. Vendor contracts must require equivalent discipline for vendor agents. (PS-8; GV.RR-04; 64.2009(b))
4.9 **Vendors.** No vendor may access CPNI or subscriber personal information until the contract includes CPNI confidentiality, security, training, and 24-hour security incident notice terms and the vendor has passed a security review (assurance report or questionnaire). Vendors are re-reviewed each year. (SA-9; GV.SC-05)
4.10 Retention: CPNI approval and notification records and marketing campaign records at least 1 year (64.2007(a)(3); 64.2008(a)(2); 64.2009(c), (d)); CPNI breach records at least 2 years (64.2011(d)); security policies, assessments, and POA&Ms at least 3 years. (SI-12; GV.PO-02)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions procedure (POL-01 section 4.8), which expressly covers misuse of CPNI (47 CFR 64.2009(b)). Sanctions range from retraining to termination, and for vendor agents, removal from company work. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), the access reviews in POL-02, and the evidence gathered for the annual CPNI certification.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the Chief Executive Officer for High risk), and expire within 12 months. No exception may permit a practice the CPNI, CALEA, or outage rules prohibit.
