## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how the workforce and vendor agents use company systems, devices, network elements, and customer information.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (employees, contractors, and temporary staff) at CO-1, CO-2, the warehouse and fleet yard, remote cabinets, and remote work locations, and the agents of vendors who access company systems, including the overflow call center. Covers all systems and data, including systems that vendors operate for the company. It applies to customer proprietary network information (CPNI), lawful-intercept information, subscriber personal information, network configuration and outage information, and all other company information.

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not involve customer data or create risk. (PL-4)
4.2 Every workforce member and vendor agent must sign this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4(1))
4.3 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year for all staff, including field technicians. The IT Manager sends monthly reminders and runs quarterly phishing exercises. (AT-2; PR.AT-01)
4.4 **CPNI training.** Everyone who can see CPNI, including overflow call center agents, must complete CPNI training before access and every year. It covers when CPNI may and may not be used, customer authentication, pretexting, and how to report a suspected breach. (AT-3; 64.2009(b))
4.5 Look up a customer account only when your job requires it. Never discuss call detail on a customer-initiated call without the account password (POL-02 4.10). BSS views of call detail are logged and reviewed monthly. (AU-6; 64.2010(b))
4.6 Lock your screen when you step away. (AC-11)
4.7 Do not install software, browser extensions, or connect USB storage unless IT approves it. Do not connect any device to a network element console port or the management network unless you are authorized network staff. (CM-11)
4.8 Do not open unexpected attachments or links. Report suspicious messages, calls asking for customer or network information, and anything unusual to the NOC or IT immediately (POL-03 4.2). (AT-2)
4.9 **Generative AI:** use only tools on the approved list. Never paste customer information, call detail, network configurations, or lawful-intercept information into a tool that is not approved. The customer chatbot is operated only under the P10 conditions. (PL-4)
4.10 Calls may be recorded only with the notice played by the contact center platform. Florida requires the consent of all parties to record a call (Fla. Stat. 934.03(2)(d)). (PL-4)
4.11 Field technicians must keep cabinet keys and tablets secured, and report a lost key or tablet to the NOC the same day. (PE-3; MP-6)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions procedure (POL-01 section 4.8), which expressly covers misuse of CPNI (47 CFR 64.2009(b)). Sanctions range from retraining to termination, and for vendor agents, removal from company work. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), the access reviews in POL-02, and the evidence gathered for the annual CPNI certification.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the Chief Executive Officer for High risk), and expire within 12 months. No exception may permit a practice the CPNI, CALEA, or outage rules prohibit.
