## 1. Purpose
Set the rules every workforce member and vendor agent follows when using company systems, customer information, and the network.

## 2. Scope
All employees, contractors, temporary staff, and vendor agents (including the overflow call center) who use company systems, devices, cabinet keys, or customer information.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Security Manager | Owns this policy; runs awareness training and phishing exercises; keeps the approved AI tools list |
| HR Director | Collects signed acknowledgments; tracks training completion |
| Director of Customer Operations | Ensures vendor agents sign and are trained before access |
| All users | Follow this policy |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not involve customer data or create risk. (PL-4)
4.2 Every workforce member and vendor agent must sign this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4)
4.3 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year for all staff, including field technicians and vendor agents. Quarterly phishing exercises are run for employees. (AT-2; PR.AT-01)
4.4 **CPNI training.** Everyone who can see CPNI, including overflow call center agents and vendor staff who support the chatbot, must complete CPNI training before access and every year. It covers when CPNI may and may not be used, customer authentication, pretexting, and how to report a suspected breach. (AT-3; 64.2009(b))
4.5 **Role-based security training.** Network engineers and NOC staff must complete annual training on secure administration of network elements, the management plane rules in POL-02, and the outage notification steps in POL-03. (AT-3; PR.AT-02)
4.6 Look up a customer account only when your job requires it. Never discuss call detail on a customer-initiated call without the account password. BSS views of call detail are logged and reviewed monthly. (AU-6; 64.2010(b))
4.7 Lock your screen when you step away. (AC-11)
4.8 Do not install software, browser extensions, or connect USB storage unless IT approves it. Do not connect any device to a network element console port or the management network unless you are authorized network staff. (CM-11)
4.9 Report suspicious messages, calls asking for customer or network information, and anything unusual to the NOC or the security team immediately (POL-03 4.2). (AT-2)
4.10 **AI tools.** Use only tools on the approved AI tools list, under the conditions listed for each. Never paste customer information, call detail, network configurations, or lawful-intercept information into a tool that is not approved for that data. Review AI outputs before relying on them; you remain responsible for what you send or do. (PL-4; AC-20)
4.11 **Call recording and transcription.** Calls may be recorded or transcribed (including by the agent assist tool) only when the contact center platform plays the approved notice. Florida requires the consent of all parties to record a call (Fla. Stat. 934.03(2)(d)); if a caller objects, stop recording and transcription. (PL-4)
4.12 Field technicians must keep cabinet keys and tablets secured and report a lost key or tablet to the NOC the same day. (PE-3; MP-6)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.8. Compliance is checked through training records, monthly call sampling, the annual assessment (P07), and the acknowledgment report.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 6.
