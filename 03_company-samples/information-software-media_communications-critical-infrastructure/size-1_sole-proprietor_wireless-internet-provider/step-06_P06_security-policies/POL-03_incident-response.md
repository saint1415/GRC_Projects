At the Sole Proprietorship tier the company keeps **one** consolidated policy, because one person writes, follows, and checks it (`01_company-sizes/tier-project-scaling.csv`, t1 P06). This file is kept so the folder has the standard policy set names. It holds no separate rules.

**Where the incident response rules are:**

- POL-01 section 10 (Incident response): runbook and printed contacts (10.1), same-day incident log (10.2), CPNI breach notice to the USSS and FBI through the FCC reporting facility within 7 business days and the 7-business-day hold on customer notice (10.3), 2-year breach record (10.4), CALEA compromise report (10.5), 911 center outage contact and thresholds (10.6), no ransom without counsel and an OFAC check (10.7), annual walkthrough and lessons learned (10.8).
- The procedure itself is the P08 runbook and notification matrix (`../step-08_P08_incident-response-runbook/`).
- SP 800-53 IR-1 is met by POL-01 as a whole.

Traceability for every statement is in `policy-control-map.csv`.
