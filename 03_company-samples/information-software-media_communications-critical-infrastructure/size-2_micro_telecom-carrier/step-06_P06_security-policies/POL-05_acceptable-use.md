| Essential rule | Where it lives | Citation |
|---|---|---|
| Use systems for company work; look up only the accounts you are serving, never your own, family, or neighbors' call detail | POL-02 C.1 | 47 CFR 64.2009(b) |
| No CPNI, customer PII, network configurations, or intercept information in personal email, personal cloud storage, messaging apps, or public AI chatbots | POL-02 C.2 | 47 CFR 64.2010(a) |
| Approved AI tools only; the portal assistant only under the P10 conditions | POL-04 4.7 | 47 CFR 64.2010(c) |
| Lock screens; never share passwords or MFA codes, including with callers claiming to be vendors | POL-02 C.3 | Internal |
| Security training at hire and yearly; CPNI training before handling accounts and yearly; phishing simulations | POL-02 C.4 | 47 CFR 64.2009(b) |
| Report suspected incidents at once, including pressure for call detail without a PIN | POL-02 C.5 and POL-03 4.2 | 47 CFR 64.2011 |
| Signed acknowledgment at hire and after each annual update | POL-02 C.6 | Internal |
