## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce, including outsourced agents and contractors, uses group systems, networks, and information.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members in every division and in corporate shared services, and outsourced agents, on any device used for group work.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow these rules; complete training; report incidents |
| Managers and vendor managers | Make sure staff and vendor agents complete training and acknowledgments |
| Group HR | Tracks acknowledgments and training completion |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Workforce members must access customer, landowner, and network information only as needed for their job. Looking up accounts or call detail out of curiosity, including for family, celebrities, or public figures, is prohibited. (PL-4; AC-8)

4.2 Every workforce member and outsourced agent must acknowledge this policy and their division supplement at hire and every year. (PL-4; PL-4(1); PR.AT-01)

4.3 Every workforce member must complete security awareness training at hire and every year, including pretexting by callers who pose as customers, data brokers, or law enforcement. The group runs phishing exercises each quarter. (AT-2; AT-2(3); PR.AT-01)

4.4 Anyone who can view CPNI, including outsourced agents and affiliate staff, must complete role-based CPNI training on when CPNI may and may not be used before receiving access, and every year after. (AT-3; PR.AT-02; 47 CFR 64.2009(b))

4.5 Suspected incidents must be reported to the group SOC within 1 hour (POL-03 4.1). (IR-6)

4.6 Restricted information may be used only on group-managed devices and approved services. It must not be sent to personal email or stored on personal devices. (AC-19; AC-20)

4.7 **Generative AI.** Only AI tools on the group approved-tools list may be used for group work. Restricted information must never be entered into public AI tools. (PL-4)

4.8 **Recording calls.** Customer calls may be recorded only with the notice or consent that state law requires. In states that require all parties to consent (Florida, Fla. Stat. 934.03(2)(d), is the worked example), the IVR consent announcement must play before recording starts. (PT-4; PL-4)

4.9 Agents must never discuss call detail with a caller who has not given the account password, unless the caller provides the call detail without help. Otherwise, call detail goes only to the address of record or by a call to the telephone number of record. (AT-3; PL-4; 47 CFR 64.2010(b))
