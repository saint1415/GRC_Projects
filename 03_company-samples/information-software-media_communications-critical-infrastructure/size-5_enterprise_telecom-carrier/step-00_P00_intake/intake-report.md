## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the company's own records before the BIA, the enterprise risk analysis and the gap analysis began on 2026-06-01, and before Internal Audit's control assessment began on 2026-07-13. It covers the organization (410 central offices, two NOCs, two data centers, and the carrier subsidiaries including the acquired carriers AQ-01 to AQ-03, of which AQ-03 closed on 2026-05-15 during intake), the systems that hold CPNI or customer personal information or support tier-1 processes, the vendors that touch them, and the rules that may bind the company and its two business service lines (SL-1 and SL-2). Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (BIA interviews and exercises, risk workshops, gap analysis samples, Internal Audit tests, the AI portfolio review) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

At this size the sources are enterprise systems of record across the business: the identity platform and HR system, TACACS+ and the element managers, the OSS network inventory, the cloud consoles and SIEM, the SOC and NOC case records, the third-party risk register and accounts payable vendor master, the contract repository, FCC and SEC filing records, the lawful intercept compliance files, board and committee records, and the SOX program's workpapers. Records for AQ-03 were collected after its closing.

Each register row names the owning business unit's system of record and owner. The [asset inventory](asset-inventory.csv) reconciles the OSS network inventory with the cloud account inventory, the endpoint consoles, the Integration Management Office's records for AQ-02 and AQ-03, and the AI council register; the [vendor register](vendor-register.csv) reconciles the third-party risk register with the accounts payable vendor master and the contract repository.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analysis (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Identity and access.** About 12,400 workforce and care vendor agent accounts are federated through SSO with MFA registered; about 2,600 of them are care vendor agents. The AQ-02 and AQ-03 workforce signs in through legacy directories that are not federated (EV-001). MFA is required for every SSO application and for remote, cloud and administrative access, with FIDO2 keys for privileged roles (EV-002). The 64 privileged OSS/BSS accounts are vaulted, with just-in-time elevation and session recording (EV-003). Joiner, mover and leaver events flow from the HR system, quarterly certifications run for IT systems and for vendor agents by vendor, and an inactivity job disables accounts after 60 days (EV-004). HR holds 12,000 employees, about 1,050 of them at AQ-02 and AQ-03, whose terminations reach the AQ service desks through a daily HR report (EV-005). The vendor rosters record each agent's BSS access date and training completion date (EV-006). The two AQ legacy directories hold about 1,050 accounts with disable dates entered by hand, and the AQ-03 transition services provider holds administrator accounts (EV-007). The BSS has about 9,800 workforce users and about 2,600 vendor agent accounts; 46 users hold rating change or bill-run release roles, and bulk export entitlements are listed by user (EV-008).

**Network, management plane and voice core.** Of about 41,000 managed network elements, 82% authenticate through TACACS+ with named accounts and MFA. About 7,400 legacy access elements, about 2,900 of them at AQ-02 and AQ-03, use shared local administrator accounts whose passwords rotate quarterly by script (EV-009). The AQ-02 and AQ-03 management networks are flat segments that reach the enterprise management network over 9 site VPN tunnels, 4 of which carry access lists; the AQ-03 transition services provider has a standing VPN; the lawful-intercept enclave sits on its own segment (EV-010). Network element logs reach the SIEM from 58% of elements, with no flow telemetry from the AQ networks and no AQ legacy billing log sources (EV-011). The SOC runs 24x7; AQ-site cases were opened after local IT had first handled the event (EV-012). The two NOCs hand suspected security events to the SOC by bridge call, logged by hand, and AQ alarms reach the NOC through a manual bridge (EV-013). Scan results are tracked against remediation SLAs, including a 14-day SLA for edge devices (EV-014). The 2026-03 penetration tests covered the portal, API gateway, OSS adapters and the SL-2 platform (EV-015). 61 TDM switches and two AQ-02 SBC clusters run past vendor support (EV-016). AQ-03 elements are not yet loaded into the OSS inventory (EV-017). CDR feeds from 41 TDM switches use clear-text file transfer, and switches buffer CDRs for 72 hours (EV-018). STP gateway screening rules were last reviewed in 2024 (EV-019). The only joint NG911 failover test on record is with the Florida provider (EV-020). Route origin validation runs on 3 of 4 transit sessions, and some Carolinas aggregation routes have no diverse path (EV-021).

**Cloud, logging, change, backup and recovery.** Both clouds use account vending with a CPNI data-class tag, guardrails as code and daily posture scans, and public access is blocked on storage tagged CPNI (EV-022). Keys are customer-managed for the BSS database, the CDR store, backups and the SL-2 platform (EV-023). Backups sit in separate accounts with write-once retention, a second region and a weekly offline copy to DC-2 (EV-024). In the DR test of 2026-05-09 the BSS recovered in 7.5 hours against its 4-hour RTO, with manual database failover steps (EV-025). Contingency plan v3 sets the 4-hour RTO and the offline account report covers about one shift of care (EV-026). The BSS logs CPNI views, call detail displays, exports and account changes, and the log archive is write-once (EV-027). BSS changes go through the change advisory board, with Regulatory Affairs review where CPNI logic changes; rating table changes have no reconciliation record after release (EV-028). Baselines cover the 12 BSS servers and 9 container images (EV-029). EDR reports on 98% of laptops and servers; AQ-03 servers have no EDR agent (EV-030). One CCaaS tenant serves in-house and vendor agents (EV-031).

**Governance, prior assurance and disclosure.** POL-01 to POL-05 are in force in their 2025 versions, with standards, procedures, an exception register and attestation reports (EV-032). The 2025 risk analysis rolled into 8 enterprise risks, with CSF 2.0 profiles and network work prioritized using the CISA Cross-Sector Cybersecurity Performance Goals (EV-033). The risk and technology committee received quarterly cyber reporting and approved the risk appetite in 2026-02 (EV-034). FY2025 SOX IT general control results are on file (EV-035). The disclosure committee has 8 members, 3 of whom joined in 2026; the materiality playbook v2 does not mention 47 CFR 64.2011 or Item 1.05(d) correspondence, and the minutes record no exercise with the new members (EV-036). The FY2025 Form 10-K includes Item 1C (EV-037). Incident response plan v5 covers CPNI, PSAP and CALEA reporting; the enterprise tabletop ran on 2026-03-24 (EV-038). The Chief Compliance Officer is the CPNI compliance officer, and the main operating company and AQ-02 have designated CALEA senior officers (EV-039).

**Workforce, privacy and CPNI records.** Annual security and CPNI training is recorded for the workforce, and 91% of care vendor agents have completed CPNI training (EV-040). One CPNI breach was determined in 2025-2026: a 2025 pretexting breach affecting 14 customers, reported on business day 3, with the 7-full-business-day hold observed (EV-041). CY2025 CPNI certifications were filed on 2026-02-27 for the main operating company, AQ-01 and AQ-02, with no evidence package kept with each statement; AQ-03's first certification is due by 2027-03-01 (EV-042). Main company approvals and 2025-09 notices are on file; AQ-02 and AQ-03 customers last received notices from their former owners in 2023 and 2022 (EV-043). The main portal resets by a one-time code to the number or email of record and sends immediate change notices; the agent desktop prompts for the account password before call detail (EV-044). The AQ-02 portal resets passwords and backup authentication with date of birth and SSN4, and AQ systems notify password changes only (EV-045). Three care vendors handle about 35% of care calls, CV-2 about 17% (EV-046).

**Third parties, contracts and service lines.** The vendor register lists about 1,400 vendors, 230 with CPNI or customer personal information; the CALEA trusted third party was last reviewed in 2023 (EV-047, EV-048). The vendor template requires 24-hour incident notice and CPNI terms; the CV-2, CV-3 and chatbot contracts allow 72 hours, and contracts inherited from AQ-02 and AQ-03 have no CPNI terms (EV-049). 1,900 enterprise and government contracts carry CPNI terms, and about 140 federal contracts include the FAR reporting clauses (EV-050). Vendor SOC 2 reports are on file for both clouds, the CCaaS vendor, the SD-WAN and firewall controllers and others (EV-051). SL-1 has issued a SOC 2 Type 2 report each year since 2024; the 2025 report had one exception (EV-052). SL-2 serves about 1,250 customers and has no SOC 2 report (EV-053); 22% of its customer administrator accounts have no MFA enforced, and 11 terminated customers have no recording deletion verification on file (EV-054). Threat signature subscriptions lapsed on about 3% of SL-1 managed firewalls (EV-055).

**Facilities and records.** Data centers have quarterly badge reviews, cabinets are alarmed, and some rural central offices have no cameras (EV-056). Most central offices have 72 hours of generator fuel; 19 small offices rely on portable generators (EV-057). Destruction certificates and a records retention schedule are on file (EV-058).

**Business volume and structure.** FY2025 revenue was about $4.8 billion, about $13.2 million per calendar day (EV-059). The company serves about 3.0 million accounts, about 2.6 million broadband subscribers and about 1.15 million voice lines, with about 3.1 million care contacts a month (EV-060). It operates 410 central offices in four states through four carrier subsidiaries (EV-061). AQ-01 is integrated; AQ-02 and AQ-03 remain on legacy billing, directories and flat management networks until their 2027 migrations (EV-062). Both AQ billing systems back up nightly to local storage with no restore test record and send no logs to the SIEM, and AQ-02 and AQ-03 intercepts run on legacy platforms (EV-063).

**AI.** The AI council register lists 8 use cases, 7 with completed review, and a procurement block on unregistered AI features has applied since 2026-03 (EV-064). Feature discovery found 3 AI uses not in the register: AI-005, AI-008 and AI-011 (EV-065). The customer-service chatbot (AI-001) reads account data through the BSS API after sign-in, and its monthly accuracy samples report results by chat language (EV-066).

**Obligations.** The carrier subsidiaries provide common carrier and interconnected VoIP services, no CMRS, no video or broadcast service, and no submarine cable (EV-067). The AQ-03 SSI policies on file are the seller's, and their refiling date is 2026-08-13 (EV-068). NORS, DIRS and PSAP contact records are kept by the NOC (EV-069). The company is an SEC registrant (EV-037), holds about 140 federal contracts (EV-050, EV-072), and serves customers in four states (EV-061, EV-073). Counsel's view on each candidate rule is in EV-070; the results are in [`obligations-register.csv`](obligations-register.csv).
