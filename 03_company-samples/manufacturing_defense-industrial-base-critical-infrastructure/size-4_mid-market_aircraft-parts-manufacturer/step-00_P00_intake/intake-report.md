## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the company's own records before any assessment work began on 2026-07-06. It covers the organization, the CUI Engineering Enclave in the government-community cloud, the shop-floor systems and operational technology at both plants, the corporate systems outside the enclave, the suppliers that receive CUI or touch company systems, and the rules that may bind the company. At this size the sources are systems of record: the enclave and corporate identity providers, the HR system, the privileged access broker, the endpoint and EDR consoles, the asset inventory, the cloud organization console, the SIEM and the MSSP contract, the scanner, the change system, the ERP accounts payable vendor master, the contract register and trade compliance files, the SSP v3.0 and the 2025 SPRS record, internal audit reports and the incident log. Plant 2 devices are listed only in a 2024 spreadsheet, so that spreadsheet is the source for them. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (interviews, samples, scans, walkthroughs, tests) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analysis (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Users and access.** The enclave identity provider holds 318 accounts: 290 named users, 12 administrators, 4 break-glass and 12 service accounts. Every user and administrator account has MFA registered; administrators and break-glass accounts use hardware security keys, and named users an app-based second factor. Accounts inactive for 45 days are disabled automatically (EV-001). Conditional access requires MFA and a compliant enrolled device for every enclave service; passwords are 14 characters minimum with a banned list, and sessions end after 30 minutes idle (EV-002). The about 610 corporate accounts all have MFA registered (EV-003). The HR roster lists 850 employees; HR recorded 96 terminations, 41 transfers and 71 new hires with enclave accounts in the 12 months to 2026-06-30 (EV-004). Each enclave account request carries manager approval and a U.S.-person verification, and the 2 foreign-national engineers hold no enclave accounts (EV-005). Enclave access reviews run every 6 months; the last was completed 2026-02-27 (EV-006). The 12 administrators use just-in-time activation through the privileged access broker. Machine tool vendors connect through the broker; the additive printer vendor is not on its vendor list, and local administrator accounts on the MES and DNC servers sit outside it (EV-007). At Plant 1, MES terminals sign in by name with badge and PIN. At Plant 2, the 20 legacy MES terminals use 3 shared terminal logins, and those logins hold the program edit permission (EV-008).

**Devices, networks and plant systems.** The EDR console lists 120 CAD workstations and 110 enclave laptops with EDR and full-disk encryption, and about 410 corporate endpoints with EDR. Allowlisting runs on CAD workstations and Plant 1 terminals, and device control blocks removable media except listed encrypted drive models (EV-009). The asset inventory carries CMMC asset categories for Plant 1 and the cloud, recorded in 2025; Plant 2 devices are in a 2024 spreadsheet. 82 of the 90 CNC machines receive programs from DNC, and 8 legacy Plant 2 machines are loaded by USB drive (EV-010). The Plant 2 MES and DNC servers run an operating system past end of vendor support, and the Plant 1 MES servers are 2 quarters behind on security updates (EV-011). At Plant 1 an enclave firewall separates the engineering and shop-floor VLANs. At Plant 2 the shop floor and the corporate segment are one network. The site-to-cloud VPN runs in FIPS mode; the export does not show the FIPS mode status of the Plant 2 SD-WAN appliance, which carries the tunnel between the plants. The Plant 2 handheld scanner network uses a shared key. Plant 1 has two internet circuits and Plant 2 one (EV-012). Authenticated scans run quarterly on cloud workloads and enclave endpoints; the scope does not include the MES and DNC servers at either plant or any Plant 2 system. Q1 and Q2 2026 produced 74 Critical and High findings (EV-013). Baselines exist for cloud workloads and endpoints and not for MES, DNC or OT servers. The change system holds 164 tickets for 2026 Q2, all for cloud and endpoint systems (EV-014). The Plant 2 MES and DNC back up weekly, without encryption, to a storage device in the same room as the servers (EV-015).

**Cloud, backups and monitoring.** The backup account sits in a second region, with 35-day write-once retention and separate credentials for 2 backup administrators. Daily cloud backups and nightly Plant 1 MES and DNC backups completed from April to June 2026; no Plant 2 system is in the vault (EV-016). The 2024 disaster recovery procedure covers the cloud landing zone. The only restore on record is a PLM restore in 2025-04, which took 9 hours; there is no MES restore record at either plant (EV-017). The landing zone has 4 accounts built in 2025 under organization guardrails, with 38 cloud servers, a 120-session virtual desktop pool, and an MFT gateway in FIPS mode serving 27 partner organizations. Production workloads run in a single region (EV-018). The SIEM receives the identity provider, suite, cloud, EDR and Plant 1 firewall logs, and no MES, DNC, OT or Plant 2 network source. The MSSP must call within 30 minutes of a high-severity alert, and logs are kept 1 year searchable and 6 years archived. No customer responsibility matrix came with the MSSP contract (EV-019). The cloud offering is FedRAMP authorized at Moderate or higher, and its CRM is on file (EV-020).

**Programs and documents.** Five policies were adopted in 2024. 2024 standards cover configuration (cloud and endpoints), identity, cryptography, vulnerability management and Plant 1 physical security; no logging, supplier, OT, AI, contingency or CUI marking standard was provided (EV-021). SSP v3.0 (2025-05-30) covers the landing zone and Plant 1. It does not include Plant 2 or list the MSSP as an External Service Provider, and its module table has no entry for the Plant 2 SD-WAN appliance. The data flow register lists no AI tool flows (EV-022). The SPRS Basic Assessment score of 74 was posted 2025-06-12 for the Plant 1 and cloud scope, with no later entry. For several technical requirements the workbook cites policy documents only (EV-023). Internal IT audits were reported to the audit committee in 2024 and 2025 (EV-024). The 2024 incident response plan covers DoD reporting roles and malware submission. The preservation procedure covers cloud systems only, and the 2025-09-24 tabletop exercised ransomware (EV-025). The incident log holds 14 incidents in 12 months (EV-026). Two medium assurance certificates are held, valid to 2027-05; the last DIBNet drill on record is from 2024 (EV-027). Training is 97% complete and has included CUI handling since 2025; its outline has no insider threat topic, and the June 2026 phishing click rate was 6.1% (EV-028). A COO memo designates the Security Manager as program lead (EV-029).

**Contracts, obligations and suppliers.** The Prime A, Prime B and Prime C subcontracts carry DFARS 252.204-7012, 252.204-7019 and 252.204-7020, FAR 52.204-21 and FAR 52.204-25. All were awarded before 2025-11-10, none carries DFARS 252.204-7021, and no contract involves classified work (EV-030). All three primes told suppliers that solicitations from 2026-11-10 would flow down CMMC Level 2 (C3PAO); Prime A's next lot is expected to award in 2027-06 (EV-031). The 3 services agreements require incident notice within 72 hours and a SOC 2 Type 2 report by 2027-12-31, and state no recovery objectives (EV-032). The company is registered with DDTC, and a technology control plan limits ITAR technical data to U.S. persons (EV-033). Enclave suite sharing is limited to 31 allow-listed domains (EV-034). ERP attachments are disabled and routings cite drawing numbers only (EV-035). About 60 IT, OT and service vendors have access to company systems or data, and 22 suppliers and outside processors receive CUI drawings (EV-036). Standard purchase order terms include DFARS 252.204-7012 and 252.204-7020 since 2025; earlier purchase orders use the previous template, and no template includes DFARS 252.204-7021 (EV-037). The additive printer vendor supports its printers through its own remote tool, and the predictive maintenance subscription is on the vendor's standard commercial terms (EV-038). The cyber insurance policy has a $15 million limit and a $500,000 retention (EV-039).

**Facilities and media.** Badge access controls the engineering center, server rooms and shop floors at both plants and is reviewed quarterly. Plant 1 uses an electronic visitor system; Plant 2 visitors sign a paper log at the gate (EV-040). The document control procedure's storage and destruction steps name Plant 1 cells, and destruction certificates cover Plant 1 bins only (EV-041).

**Business volume.** FY2025 revenue was about $240 million: about $528,000 of Plant 1 shipments, $392,000 of Plant 2 shipments and $40,000 of services revenue per production day over about 250 days (EV-042). The plants produce about 260 shipments and 400 inspection reports a day, and the services line handles about 30 jobs a month (EV-043).

**AI tools, corporate records and maintenance.** Five AI tools are in use, and none has a security or legal review record. AI-001 is the enclave suite assistant, enabled 2026-05-11 for 20 users. AI-002 is machine-vision inspection, which the vendor updated twice without notice. AI-003 is a predictive maintenance gateway on the Plant 1 shop-floor VLAN that streams CNC telemetry to the vendor's commercial cloud. AI-004 is the corporate suite assistant, with about 400 users. AI-005 is applicant ranking, turned on by the HR vendor's default settings in 2026-04. The web filter blocks public AI sites on company networks (EV-044). The company is privately held and holds no facility clearance (EV-045). Maintenance is scheduled and recorded, and tool inspection is logged at Plant 1 only (EV-046).
