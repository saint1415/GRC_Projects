## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how workforce members use company systems, CUI, AI tools, and the plants, so that everyday work does not put CUI, production, or contracts at risk.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members and contractors who use company systems or enter either plant.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Director | Owns this policy; tracks training and annual acknowledgments |
| Managers | Make sure their staff complete training and follow this policy |
| Security Manager | Keeps the approved-tools list with the AI review group |
| Facilities and Security Manager | Visitor management at both plants |
| All workforce | Follow this policy and acknowledge it each year |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for business use. Activity on company systems is monitored, and users see a notice at sign-in. (PL-4; AC-8; 3.1.9)
4.2 CUI must never be sent to, stored on, or opened from personal devices, personal email or cloud accounts, corporate (non-enclave) systems, or commercial email. (AC-20; 3.1.20)
4.3 **AI tools.** Only tools on the approved-tools list may be used, and only for the uses listed. CUI, export-controlled data, part numbers with drawing notes, and services customer data must never be entered into public AI services or the corporate AI assistant. AI output is never the source for a dimension, tolerance, NC program, build parameter, test value, or inspection decision; numbers must be copied from the source document. (PL-4; STD-05)
4.4 **Training.** Every workforce member must complete security awareness, CUI handling, and insider threat training at hire and every year. Administrators, engineers who mark CUI, quality staff, and manufacturing systems staff must also complete role-based training. (AT-2; AT-2(2); AT-3; 3.2.1 to 3.2.3)
4.5 Report suspected phishing, lost devices, unusual shop-floor behavior, unescorted visitors, and suspected misuse of CUI to the security team at once. Reports made in good faith are never punished. (IR-6)
4.6 **Visitors.** Every visitor must sign in through the visitor system, wear a badge, and be escorted by an employee at all times in production, engineering, and test areas. No photography on shop floors without written approval. Foreign-national visitors are announced to the Director of Trade Compliance and Contracts in advance. (PE-3; 3.10.3; 22 CFR 120.56)
4.7 Remote work with CUI is allowed only on enclave laptops or virtual desktops, in a private space, with no printing at home. (PE-17; AC-17; 3.10.6)
4.8 Users must not install software on company devices; software is installed through device management. (CM-11; 3.4.9)
4.9 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy at hire and each year. (PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement in POL-01 section 4.8. Use of an unapproved AI tool with CUI is also handled as a possible incident under POL-03.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7.
