## 1. Purpose
Set the rules of behavior for using company systems and information, including training, visitors, AI tools, and personal devices.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (employees, contractors, temporary workers, and interns) at all 8 sites in Florida, Georgia, Alabama, Texas, Kansas, and Arizona and at the 2 data centers, including acquired operations from their acquisition date. Covers all company systems and data, including the government-community and commercial clouds, SaaS, plant OT, and systems that suppliers and service providers operate for the company, with added rules for the CUI Engineering Enclave (CEE) and the Manufacturing Operations Zone (MOZ). The classified information system at FL-1 also follows NISPOM and DCSA requirements, which prevail where stricter.

## 4. Policy statements
Each statement is testable and tagged with its SP 800-53 control(s) and CSF 2.0 subcategory. `policy-control-map.csv` traces each statement to its regulatory driver and shows whether Internal Audit tested it in 2026 (P07).

4.1 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy at hire and annually. (PL-4; GV.RR-04)
4.2 Security awareness training covering CUI handling, export control, insider threat, and social engineering must be completed at hire, annually, and after significant cyber events. (AT-2; AT-2(2); AT-2(3); PR.AT-01)
4.3 Role-based training with practical exercises must be completed annually by administrators, PLM administrators, SOC analysts, OT engineers, and other specialized roles, with results shared with supervisors. (AT-3; AT-2(1); PR.AT-02)
4.4 Visitors in CUI areas must be escorted at all times by their host, signed in and out in the visitor management system, and kept away from unattended CUI. (PE-3; PE-8; PR.AA-06)
4.5 Only tools on the approved AI tools list (STD-05.3) may be used for company work. CUI, export-controlled, or classified information must never be entered into public generative AI services. (AC-20; PR.DS-01)
4.6 AI output must never be the source of a dimension, tolerance, NC program, build parameter, inspection criterion, or export classification; numbers must be copied from the controlled source document. (SI-7; PR.DS-01)
4.7 Personal devices may not connect to the CEE or the MOZ, and company CUI may not be stored on them. (AC-20; PR.AA-05)
4.8 Workforce members must report suspected incidents, suspicious contacts, and lost devices immediately; cleared employees must also follow NISPOM reporting duties. (IR-6; RS.MA-01)
4.9 Cleared employees must complete NISPOM refresher training every 12 months. (AT-2; PR.AT-01)

## 5. Standards and procedures under this policy
Standards set measurable requirements (approved by the CISO); procedures give step-by-step instructions (approved by the owning director). Both sit below this policy in the hierarchy and cannot contradict it.
- STD-05.1 Security Awareness and Training Standard
- STD-05.2 External Systems and Personal Devices Standard
- STD-05.3 Approved AI Tools List
- PRC-05.1 Visitor Escort Procedure

## 6. Compliance and enforcement
Compliance is monitored through continuous control monitoring, the annual Internal Audit assessment (P07), access certifications, and the CMMC readiness checks before each affirmation. Violations are handled under the sanctions procedure (PRC-01.1, POL-01 statement 4.7), from retraining to termination of employment or contract, in proportion to intent and harm. A suspected unauthorized release of ITAR or EAR technical data is also referred to the Vice President, Trade Compliance for a voluntary disclosure decision.

## 7. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 7 and PRC-01.2: requested in the GRC platform, risk-rated with the P01 method, approved at the authority level for the residual risk, recorded in the exception register with compensating controls, and limited to 12 months.
