# Scenario facts: Cris Santos Company | Defense Industrial Base | Enterprise

All 11 deliverables in this folder use the facts below. The company is fictitious. This scenario is independent of the other sizes. Where a fact comes from a regulation or contract clause, the citation is given. Regulatory text was checked on eCFR (version date 2026-09-23) and the Federal Register.

## 4. Where the evidence is

This file says who the company is. It does not say how well its security works. That is established from evidence:
- **What the records show** is in the [intake report](step-00_P00_intake/intake-report.md) and the [evidence register](step-00_P00_intake/evidence-register.csv). Every item has a source system, an owner, and as-of and collected dates. At this size the sources are enterprise systems of record across IT, OT, the plants, KS-1 and AZ-1, the CMMC Program Office, Trade Compliance and the FSO's files, prior Internal Audit and SOX workpapers, and board and committee records.
- **Which rules apply** is in the [obligations register](step-00_P00_intake/obligations-register.csv), reviewed by the General Counsel's office.
- **Gaps against NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2, CMMC Level 3 and the other applicable rules** are judged in the gap analysis (P03), and **whether controls work** is tested by Internal Audit in the control assessment (P07). Both cite evidence IDs.
