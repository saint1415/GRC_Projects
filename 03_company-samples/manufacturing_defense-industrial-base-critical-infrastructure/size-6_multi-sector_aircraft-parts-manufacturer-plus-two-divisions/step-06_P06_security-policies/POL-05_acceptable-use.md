## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce uses group systems and data, especially CUI, export-controlled data, and AI tools.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members and all group systems, devices, and accounts, and any customer system or GFE used for group work.

## 3. Rules of behavior
Every workforce member must sign these rules at onboarding and each year. (PL-4; PS-6; 3.1.9)

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Use group systems for authorized business. Limited personal use is allowed on corporate systems only, never on CUI systems. (PL-4)

4.2 Complete security awareness and insider threat training on hire and every year, and role-based training when assigned. (AT-2; AT-2(2); PR.AT-01; 3.2.1; 3.2.3)

4.3 Never share credentials or hardware keys. Report lost keys and devices to the SOC at once. (IA-5; PL-4)

4.4 Install software only from the group catalog. CAD plug-ins, scripts, and developer packages must be on the approved list. (CM-10; CM-11; 3.4.9)

4.5 Do not move CUI to personal devices, personal email, personal cloud storage, or any system not in the CUI location register. On customer sites, keep customer CUI on the customer's systems unless the site agreement says otherwise. (AC-20; PR.AA-05; 3.1.20)

4.6 Do not discuss or show export-controlled technical data to anyone whose U.S.-person status or export authorization you have not confirmed. Cover drawings during visits. (AC-21; PL-4; 22 CFR 120.56)

4.7 **Approved AI tools only.** Use only AI tools on the group approved-tools list, and only for the data classes the list allows. (PL-4(1); AC-20)

4.8 **Never put CUI, export-controlled data, Government data, or export-controlled source code into a public AI service.** AI output is never the source for a dimension, tolerance, NC program, inspection criterion, export classification, or maintenance decision; copy numbers from the source document. (PL-4(1); AC-20; 3.1.20)

4.9 Report suspected phishing, lost media, unescorted visitors, and suspected misuse to the SOC within 1 hour (POL-03 4.1). (IR-6; AT-2)

4.10 Expect monitoring. Use of group systems is logged and reviewed, and the CUI notice banner applies. (AC-8; 3.1.9)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.7.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.12. No exception may permit CUI in a public AI service.
