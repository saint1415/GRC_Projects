## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how workforce members use group systems, data, and AI tools.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members (employees, faculty, adjuncts, contractors, student workers, volunteers) in every division. Students follow the separate student acceptable use terms, which carry the same rules for lab computers and campus networks.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow these rules and acknowledge them each year |
| Managers | Make sure their staff complete the acknowledgment |
| Group CISO | Maintains the approved AI tools list with the group AI council |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Use group systems and data only for authorized work. Look up a student, patient, or customer record only when your job requires it; curiosity is not a legitimate educational interest or a permitted purpose. (PL-4; PR.AT-01; 34 CFR 99.31(a)(1)(i)(A); 164.502(b))

4.2 Never share passwords or MFA codes. Report any MFA prompt you did not start, and any message asking you to "verify" an ISIR correction, bank change, or account, to the SOC immediately. (IA-2; IR-6)

4.3 Restricted data may be accessed only on group-managed devices or through the approved virtual desktop. (AC-19; PR.AA-05)

4.4 Do not send Restricted data by unencrypted email or to personal accounts or storage. Use the approved secure transfer service. (SC-8; PR.DS-02)

4.5 **Generative AI.** Use only tools on the approved list. Do not enter Restricted or Confidential data into any tool that is not approved for that class. (PL-4; SA-9)

4.6 **AI outputs about people.** AI scores and summaries about applicants, students, or patients are prompts for human judgment, not decisions. A person must review the underlying record before any action. Never use an early-alert score, applicant score, or proctoring flag to discourage enrollment, limit a service, or start an academic integrity case without your own review of the evidence. (PL-4; GV.RM-01)

4.7 **Recording.** Record a clinic visit with the AI scribe only after every party has consented and the consent is captured in the EHR field; follow the strictest rule where participants are in different states (Fla. Stat. 934.03(2)(d) is the Florida all-party consent example). Lecture capture follows the college's recording notice. (PT-4)

4.8 Group systems are monitored for security. Users have no expectation of privacy in group systems beyond what law provides. (AC-8)

4.9 Do not install unapproved software, disable security tools, or connect unapproved devices to staff networks. (CM-7; CM-11)

4.10 Report lost devices, suspected incidents, and mistaken disclosures to the SOC immediately. (IR-6; RS.MA-01)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.7. Acknowledgment is tracked in the HR system each year.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.10.
