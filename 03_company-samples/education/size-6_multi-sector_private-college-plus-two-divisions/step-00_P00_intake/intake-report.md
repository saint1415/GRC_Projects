## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the group's own records before any assessment work began on 2026-05-04. It covers the legal entities, the systems that hold education records, customer information (16 CFR 314.2), children's personal information or PHI in each division and in corporate shared services, the suppliers that touch them, and the rules that may bind each entity. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Items are grouped by division: the title of each row starts with Group, Higher Education, SRLP (the college's Student Records and Learning Platform, the P02 system), Education Software or Student Health. Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (interviews, document reviews, tests) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analyses (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Entities, workforce and scale.** The holding company is an SEC registrant with three subsidiaries; Student Health was acquired in 2024; corporate shared services sit in the parent (EV-001). The workforce is 45,000: Higher Education about 25,000, Education Software about 8,500, Student Health about 6,500, corporate about 5,000 (EV-002). FY2025 revenue was about $18.0 billion: Higher Education $9.6 billion, Education Software $5.8 billion, Student Health $2.6 billion (EV-003). The 2021 intercompany service agreement for the college's Campus Platform tenant has no safeguards schedule, incident notice term, right-to-assess clause, or FERPA direct-control terms (EV-004). Corporate and Student Health signed an intercompany BAA in 2024, and the college and Student Health a clinic services agreement in 2024; other data requests between them are handled by ticket with no written data-sharing standard (EV-005).

**Group identity and access.** About 41,000 federated workforce users have MFA, by push approval for workforce users and hardware keys for administrators; Higher Education and Education Software are fully federated and about 60% of Student Health users are (EV-006). Identity governance runs joiner-mover-leaver workflows, quarterly certification, 60-day inactivity disabling and contractor end dates; the Student Health legacy domain is outside it (EV-007). Privileged access is just in time through PAM with session recording; 9 named college administrators hold the SIS tenant administrator role (EV-008). The Q1 2026 certification was completed, including warehouse roles and separation-of-duties checks (EV-009).

**Group security operations.** Managed endpoints lock after 10 minutes idle, laptops and tablets have disk encryption, and EDR covers endpoints, servers, cloud hosts and the 12 SAIG workstations (EV-010). The SOC runs 24x7 with identity, cloud, platform, hub, warehouse and SIS audit logs; no rule covers integration hub service accounts or support console sessions, and egress rules do not include warehouse storage exports (EV-011). Logs are kept write-once for 1 year online and 6 years archived (EV-012). Incidents are tracked in one case system with a liaison for each division (EV-013). Hub and warehouse scans run monthly; legacy campus patch reports are kept by campus IT (EV-014). The 2026-03 penetration test covered the college tenant and student portals; its scope letter does not list the hub, the warehouse or the 6 legacy campuses (EV-015). The notification matrix lists FSA, the FTC, HIPAA, customer contracts, state law and the SEC; exercises have covered technical playbooks only (EV-016).

**Policies, risk and governance.** The 2025 group policies and standards are aligned to CSF 2.0; the Education Software supplement was aligned in 2025-10 and the Student Health standards date from 2023 (EV-017). The common control catalog lists 98 controls; inheritance is documented for Higher Education and Education Software, and no Student Health matrix was provided (EV-018). The risk strategy sets acceptance authority by level (EV-019). The board risk committee receives quarterly reports (EV-020), the FY2025 Form 10-K carries the Item 106 disclosure (EV-021), and the 2026 internal audit plan assesses common controls once and samples divisions (EV-022).

**Group cloud platform.** Provider A hosts the landing zone, the integration hub and the warehouse; provider B hosts both platforms, the support console, the backup vault and the DR replica; the hub runs in one region with no standby (EV-023). Guardrails, deny-by-default hub rules and private endpoints apply to every division account (EV-024). Keys are customer-managed per division account (EV-025). Backups of provider A workloads go daily to an immutable vault in provider B, with quarterly sample restores (EV-026). SRLP endpoints accept TLS 1.2 or higher only (EV-027). Provider SOC 2 reports are on file (EV-028).

**Workforce, vendors, AI and records.** Awareness and FERPA training completion is 97%, with quarterly phishing simulations; no role-based module is assigned to warehouse analysts or integration engineers (EV-029). Screening, 4-hour termination disabling and contractor FERPA terms are recorded (EV-030). The vendor master lists tier 1 vendors with yearly assessments; the Campus Platform is billed as an intercompany charge and is not in it (EV-031). AI in use spans admissions scoring, student-success scoring, proctoring flags, the K-12 AI tutor pilot, two analytics products, the AI scribe pilot, EHR decision support, a coding assistant and an enterprise assistant pilot; a counseling chatbot is proposed (EV-032). The warehouse keeps SIS and aid extracts with no expiry set (EV-033). No division has CUI, E-Rate funds or a Part 2 program, and card payments stay on hosted or point-to-point encrypted services (EV-034).

**Higher Education.** The college holds one PPA, which includes the Safeguards Rule, and a SAIG Enrollment Agreement (EV-036). It holds customer information on about 1.4 million consumers and serves about 290,000 current aid recipients (EV-037). About 410,000 students enrolled in the year, about 295,000 of them fully online in all 50 states; no student is under 13; the tenant holds about 2.3 million student records (EV-038). The FY2025 compliance audit reported no GLBA finding (EV-039). The Qualified Individual was designated in writing in 2023, and written board reports went out in 2024 and 2025; the 2025 report does not discuss arrangements with Education Software or Student Health (EV-040). FAMS, CRM and proctoring vendors are reviewed each year; no assessment or SOC 2 review exists for the Campus Platform, used since 2021 (EV-041). About 1,900 admissions and enrollment staff see every student's full record (EV-042). MFA applies to all students since 2025, and bank detail changes need a fresh MFA challenge and out-of-band confirmation (EV-043). Changes go through the college change board (EV-044). Monthly privacy reviews do not include support console access to the college tenant (EV-045). FAMS vendor support accounts are local, and no college review of FAMS logs was provided (EV-046). SAIG workstations sit in a badge-controlled room (EV-047). The 6 legacy campuses run flat networks with local administrator accounts, about 340 legacy SIS users without MFA, and file servers holding scanned aid documents without encryption at rest (EV-048). The CRM scoring feature has run since 2025 and scored about 380,000 applications; no bias test results, applicant notice or Colorado review were found (EV-049). Proctoring flags go to faculty with no written review rule (EV-050). The FERPA annual notice does not name contractors as school officials, and automated disclosures are not recorded per student (EV-051). Campus safety relies on one mass notification vendor (EV-052).

**SRLP warehouse and integration hub.** Warehouse analyst roles can query all student schemas; 64% of tables carry owner and data type tags; no purpose field is populated; FAFSA-derived fields sit in tables open to analysts (EV-053). The hub's defined interfaces include the two-way clinic enrollment and immunization exchange; 14 service accounts run them (EV-054); their secrets are static, and 5 are older than 1 year (EV-055). The SRLP contingency plan has manual workarounds, and no test has included the hub, FAMS interfaces or refund reconciliation (EV-056).

**Education Software.** About 900 colleges and 5,400 districts are customers; about 4,100 districts have users under 13; standard contracts set 72-hour notice; about 1,300 district contracts carry state-specific terms with no register (EV-057). The SOC 2 Type 2 report for the period ending 2025-06-30 is on file (EV-058). 61 support accounts can read every customer tenant, including the college's, with no ticket link or time limit (EV-059). The trust page says staff access customer data only with permission; the subprocessor list does not name the model provider (EV-060). The children's information security program was adopted in 2026-03 with a coordinator designated in 2026-02 (EV-061). Data of 212 former districts is held after contract end against a 90-day deletion policy (EV-062, EV-061). The AI tutor pilot started in 2026-01 in 140 districts with a security review and no children's privacy review, district contract update or subprocessor notice (EV-063). Tenants are isolated by keys and row-level policies, with failover tested twice a year (EV-064). 23 of about 140 integration partners have no written security assurances (EV-065). Releases follow change approval and automated tests (EV-066). Analytics product documentation is silent on prohibited uses (EV-067).

**Student Health.** 96 clinic sites serve about 310,000 student and 190,000 nonstudent patients a year; urgent care sites accept Medicaid (EV-068). About 40% of users sign in through the legacy domain with passwords only on clinical workstations (EV-069). The 2023 standards predate the acquisition and allow front desk logins and exports approved by clinic managers (EV-070). File servers have been backed up since 2026-03 with no restore test, and 9 of 16 legacy sites have volume encryption (EV-071). The EHR has no field showing whether a record is PHI or a FERPA record (EV-072). BAAs and the 58 clinic management contracts are on file, with notice terms that vary (EV-073). HIPAA officials were designated in 2024 (EV-074). The AI scribe pilot has no required consent field, and EHR decision support tools are not inventoried (EV-075). The EHR vendor's SOC 2 report is on file (EV-076).
