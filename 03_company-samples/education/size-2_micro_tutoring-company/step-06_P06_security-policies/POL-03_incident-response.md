## 1. Purpose
Make sure the company spots, contains, reports, and recovers from security incidents quickly and lawfully, and meets every notice deadline, including the district's 48-hour term.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members of Cris Santos Company, including contractor tutors, and every system and copy of company information, including systems the MSP and SaaS vendors run for the company and contractor tutors' own computers when used for company work. A "security incident" includes any attempted or successful unauthorized access, use, disclosure, change, or destruction of information, or interference with a system, as well as lost or stolen devices, a recording or report sent to the wrong parent, and any unknown adult gaining contact with a student through company systems.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Center Director | Incident lead; decides with breach counsel whether an incident is a breach under Florida law and the district contract; keeps the incident log |
| Owner | Backup incident lead; calls the cyber insurer; approves outside communications and any spending |
| Director of Tutoring | Platform actions (account lockouts, log exports); contact for the district |
| MSP | Technical response: isolate, investigate, rebuild, restore; preserves logs |
| Cyber insurer and its panel vendors | Breach counsel and forensic firm, engaged through the insurer's hotline |
| All workforce | Report suspected incidents at once |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 The company must keep an incident response runbook for its most likely serious incident, ransomware with theft of student records (P08), with a printed copy and contact card at the front desk and at the Center Director's and Owner's homes. (IR-8; RS.MA-01)

4.2 Workforce members must report any suspected incident to the Center Director **at once, and within 1 hour at most**, in person or by phone. If the Center Director cannot be reached, report to the Owner. Examples: clicking a suspicious link, locked or renamed files, a lost laptop or phone, a recording or report sent to the wrong parent, a student's account used by someone else, or anyone asking for student information without a reason. Child-safety concerns follow the company's child-safety reporting procedure as well as this policy. (IR-6; RS.MA-02)

4.3 The Center Director must log every incident, including those that turn out to be harmless, with the date found, what happened, what was done, and the outcome. (IR-5)

4.4 For any incident that may involve students' or families' information, the Center Director must, with breach counsel: record the date the company determined a breach occurred or had reason to believe one occurred (this starts the Florida 30-day clocks); record when the company discovered unauthorized access to district data (this starts the district's 48-hour clock); and decide whether the data involved is "personal information" under Fla. Stat. 501.171(1)(g). (IR-6; RS.AN-03)

4.5 Notifications to the district, affected families, the Florida Department of Legal Affairs, and consumer reporting agencies must meet the deadlines in the P08 notification matrix. Breach counsel must review each notice before it is sent. A decision not to notify individuals under Fla. Stat. 501.171(4)(c) must be in writing, kept for at least 5 years, and sent to the Department within 30 days. (IR-6; RS.CO-02; RS.CO-03)

4.6 For any suspected ransomware, data theft, or account takeover, the Owner must call the cyber insurer's breach hotline before hiring any outside firm, and the MSP must be engaged under its contract. (IR-4; RS.MA-02)

4.7 No ransom may be paid without the Owner's approval, advice from breach counsel and the insurer, and an OFAC sanctions check. (IR-4)

4.8 Vendors and contractor tutors must report incidents to the Center Director as their contracts require. As third-party agents under Fla. Stat. 501.171(6), vendors must notify the company within 10 days of determining a breach; the company's own contracts ask for faster notice. The Center Director must log each report and handle it under this policy. (IR-6; SA-9; GV.SC-08)

4.9 The runbook must be tested every year with a tabletop exercise that includes the MSP, and after any real incident that used it. (IR-3; ID.IM-02)

4.10 Lessons learned must be written up within 30 days of closing any incident that required notification or outside help, and fed into the risk register and training. (IR-4; ID.IM-02)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Breaking this policy leads to consequences under POL-02 A.5. Failing to report is a violation; reporting a mistake in good faith is never sanctioned. Compliance is checked in the annual assessment (P07) and the annual tabletop.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-02 A.9. No exception may delay a legally or contractually required notification.
