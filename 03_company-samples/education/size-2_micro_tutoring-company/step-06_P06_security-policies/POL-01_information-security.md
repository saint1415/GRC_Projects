At the Micro tier the company keeps three core policies: access control (POL-02), incident response (POL-03), and data classification, handling, and retention (POL-04). A separate Information Security Policy would add little for a 7-person company, so its essential rules live in **POL-02 Part A. Program governance**. POL-02, POL-03, POL-04, and the SSP (P02) together form the written information security program that the COPPA Rule requires (16 CFR 312.8(b)).
