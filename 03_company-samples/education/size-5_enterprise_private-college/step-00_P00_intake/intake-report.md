## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the company's own records before the enterprise BIA, risk assessment and gap analysis began on 2026-06-01, and before Internal Audit's control assessment fieldwork began on 2026-07-13. It covers the organization (the College's 23 campuses, the online division and the two service lines SL-1 and SL-2), the systems that create, receive, maintain or transmit customer information under the Safeguards Rule or education records under FERPA, or that support tier-1 processes, the vendors that touch them, and the rules that may bind the company. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (BIA interviews, risk workshops, gap analysis samples, Internal Audit tests, the AI portfolio review) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

At this size the sources are enterprise systems of record across administrative units: the identity platform and HR system, the SIS security console, the CMDB and endpoint consoles, the cloud consoles and SIEM, the third-party risk register and accounts payable vendor master, the contract repository, the financial aid regulatory files, board and committee records, prior Internal Audit, SOX and Title IV compliance audit workpapers, and regulator correspondence.

Each register row names the owning unit's system of record and owner. The [asset inventory](asset-inventory.csv) reconciles the CMDB with the cloud account inventory, the endpoint consoles, the integration route inventory and the AI governance committee register; the [vendor register](vendor-register.csv) reconciles the third-party risk register with the accounts payable vendor master and the contract repository.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analysis (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Identity and access.** Workforce accounts for employees and adjunct faculty are federated through SSO with MFA registered, and about 9,400 hold SRLP roles (EV-001). MFA is required for every workforce SSO application, FIDO2 keys for privileged roles, and lockout follows 10 failed attempts in 15 minutes (EV-002). Privileged access is just in time through PAM with session recording; there are 34 privileged SRLP accounts, 11 of them SIS security administrators (EV-003). Joiner, mover and leaver events flow from the HR system; workforce accounts inactive for 60 days are disabled, with adjunct accounts exempt between terms, and adjunct accounts are outside the quarterly certification scope between terms (EV-004). The help desk resets MFA after knowledge-based questions (EV-005). HR records 3,800 adjunct faculty on term contracts, and a contract that ends without a renewal is not recorded as a termination event (EV-006). Students sign in with a password, bot detection and breached-password checks; MFA is optional except for viewing aid offers (EV-007). Refund bank details can be changed in the portal after an email confirmation, without MFA re-authentication; changes over $5,000 go to manual review (EV-008), and bank changes and disbursements are reconciled daily (EV-009). All 2,100 enrollment advisors hold an SIS inquiry role that shows the academic and account records of every student (EV-010). Awarding and disbursing roles are defined as conflicting (EV-011). The 14 SL-2 partner tenants have faculty accounts administered by the partners, with no attestation or inactivity rule configured (EV-012).

**Endpoints, network and testing.** EDR reports on 98% of about 22,300 endpoints and on every SRLP server; workforce laptops have full-disk encryption and remote wipe (EV-013). The CMDB lists about 1,950 SRLP-related components. The legacy document imaging system in the colocation data center holds about 14 million scanned verification and tax documents and runs an unsupported operating system with local accounts, under an extended support contract (EV-014). NAC is enforced at 18 of 28 sites, 6 campuses still use legacy VPN concentrators, and the 2 SAIG transmission servers sit in the integration subnet (EV-015). Authenticated scans run weekly on SRLP hosts and quarterly across the estate, with a 15-day SLA for critical findings (EV-016). An independent firm ran the annual external penetration test on 2026-03-09 (EV-017).

**Cloud, logging, backup and recovery.** Both clouds use account vending, guardrails as code and daily posture scans; the SRLP has a warm standby in a second Cloud provider A region (EV-018). Backups sit in separate accounts with write-once retention and a weekly offline copy in the colocation data center; the imaging system is not in that design (EV-019). Keys are customer-managed (EV-020). The SIEM does not list the SAIG transmission software or the imaging system as sources, and no use case covers bulk SIS report exports or mass record views (EV-021). The SOC runs 24x7 with managed security service provider overflow (EV-022). In the 2026-04-25 DR test the SIS met its 15-minute RPO and recovered in 11.5 hours against the plan's 8-hour RTO, with integration services and the SAIG servers rebuilt by hand (EV-023). The contingency plan v3 has no revision after that test (EV-024). The LMS contract sets an RTO of 24 hours and an RPO of 1 hour, and the vendor does not support a customer-observed failover test (EV-025). The colocation data center also holds the telephony core, whose cloud contact-center failover has no test record (EV-026).

**Governance, prior assurance and disclosure.** The 2025 policy set (POL-01 to POL-05 with standards, procedures and an exception register) is in force, and the program, aligned to CSF 2.0, maps each 314.4 element to a policy or standard (EV-027). The board risk committee designated the CISO in writing as Qualified Individual in February 2024 and received written reports in 2024 and 2025 (EV-028); the board approved the risk appetite in 2026-02 (EV-029). The 2025 risk assessment rolled into the ERM register (EV-030). FY2025 SOX IT general control results show no open items (EV-031), and the 2025 Title IV compliance audit report has no GLBA findings (EV-032). The materiality playbook does not mention the FSA breach report, a corrective action plan or administrative capability, and the disclosure committee minutes record no exercise of the playbook since the General Counsel and CFO changed in 2026 (EV-033). The FY2025 Form 10-K includes Item 1C (EV-034). The 2026-03-26 tabletop covered ransomware with technical and academic continuity; the disclosure committee did not take part and the FSA and FTC steps were not exercised (EV-035). Security staffing and the common control catalog are in EV-036 and EV-037.

**Workforce and privacy records.** Annual security and FERPA training (97% completion for 2025-26), monthly phishing simulations with aid-themed lures, and role-based modules are recorded (EV-038). Sensitive positions are designated and screened, and sanctions are logged (EV-039). The FERPA annual notice is in the 2026 catalog and the portal; its school-official definition covers contractors generally and does not name SL-2 partner staff or AI vendors. Requests, disclosures and SL-1 consents are recorded (EV-040).

**Third parties and service lines.** The vendor register lists about 1,100 vendors, 260 of them handling student records or customer information; 37 of the 260 have no current assurance review (EV-041). Accounts payable shows payments to the SIS, LMS and CRM vendors, the servicers and the AI tool vendors (EV-042). The contract template has included Safeguards Rule, FERPA school-official and direct-control, no-model-training and 72-hour notice terms since 2024; 19 legacy contracts do not have them, and the Title IV third-party servicer contract allows 10 days for incident notice (EV-043). SOC reports are on file for both clouds, the colocation provider, the LMS, CRM and ERP vendors, the productivity suite and the proctoring vendor, whose report has 2 exceptions (EV-044). SL-1 issued its 2025 SOC 2 Type 2 report (EV-045). SL-1 serves about 310 employers and about 52,000 sponsored students; SL-2 serves 14 partners and about 41,000 partner students under an 8-hour LMS recovery commitment (EV-046). The SL-1 portal shows employers only students with a current consent flag (EV-047).

**Facilities, campus safety and disposal.** Badge access, CCTV and alarms protect the processing areas (EV-048). The emergency notification service was tested at all 23 campuses on 2026-03-17 with no failed tests; contact lists sync nightly from the SIS, and the procedure does not describe sending notifications when the SIS and email are unavailable (EV-049). Certificates of destruction are on file for media and paper (EV-050).

**Business volume and structure.** Receipts were about $4.8 billion in FY2025, about $13.2 million per calendar day (EV-051). About $3.2 billion of Title IV funds are disbursed a year, and about 410,000 credit balance refunds are paid (EV-052). About 285,000 students are enrolled, about 238,000 of them online, and about 3.4 million former students' records are kept permanently (EV-053). The College has 23 campuses in six states under one institution (EV-054). The CRM receives about 1.6 million inquiries a year (EV-055).

**Obligations and regulatory status.** The College has one PPA and a SAIG Enrollment Agreement, and about 640 financial aid staff hold Department system accounts (EV-056). Customer information is held on about 2.3 million consumers (EV-057). The minimum age at enrollment is 17 (EV-058). The College bills no health care electronically, and counseling is provided by a contracted telehealth vendor (EV-059). It receives no E-Rate discounts and holds no sponsored research awards or federal contracts (EV-060). The employee health plan is a separate covered entity (EV-061). Counsel's view on each candidate rule is in EV-062; the results are in [`obligations-register.csv`](obligations-register.csv).

**Data, records and AI.** The nightly SIS extract to the data platform includes ISIR-derived fields (Pell eligibility, aid index band, dependency status, first-generation status) (EV-065), and those fields appear in data sets used for AI-003 model features and marketing audience modeling (EV-066). The records schedule is a draft that has not been approved, and no disposal run of electronic customer information is recorded (EV-067). The AI governance committee register records reviews for 8 use cases, with AI-005 and AI-006 awaiting review; the AI-003 entry shows a 2026-01 model refresh that added ISIR-derived features with no re-review recorded (EV-068). The CRM scoring feature (AI-002) was switched on in the CRM tenant on 2026-03-02 with no committee review record, its fairness documentation covers the vendor's pooled client data, and inquiry and application pages carry no notice about scoring (EV-069).

**Late answer (intake follow-up).** After the 2025-26 award year closed, student finance reported 212 confirmed refund redirection cases (about $0.9 million) for the year, and 1.8% of 2026 online applications flagged by the CRM fraud rules (EV-077).
