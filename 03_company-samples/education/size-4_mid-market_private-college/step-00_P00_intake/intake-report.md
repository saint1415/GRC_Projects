## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the college's own records before any assessment work began on 2026-07-06. It covers the organization, the systems that hold customer information (16 CFR 314.2(d)) and education records, the suppliers that touch them, and the rules that may bind the college. At this size the sources are systems of record: the identity provider, the ERP HR and accounts payable modules, the SIS, LMS and FAMS admin consoles, the endpoint and EDR consoles, the cloud organization console, the SIEM and MSSP portal, the contract register, prior risk assessments, the Title IV compliance audit, scan and penetration test reports, the incident queue, and the Title IV program agreements. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (interviews, samples, tests) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analysis (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Users and access.** The identity provider holds named accounts for all 600 employees and about 7,800 students. Every staff and faculty account has MFA registered, and 54% of student accounts do (EV-001). Conditional access requires MFA for staff and faculty on SSO applications, the VPN and the cloud console; MFA is optional for students (EV-002). HR recorded 142 terminations and 58 internal transfers in the 12 months to 2026-06-30, and about 310 adjunct course assignments a term (EV-003). The ERP feed creates and disables staff accounts; a transfer opens a role change ticket; 186 staff and faculty accounts were created in the 12 months; the third-party servicer creates its own FAMS accounts (EV-004). The last access review was the annual campaign in February 2026, and local LMS and third-party accounts were outside its scope (EV-005). 37 administrator accounts exist: 11 cloud identities use just-in-time elevation through the access broker, and SaaS administrator roles are standing (EV-006). The SIS has 64 roles, and admissions and all 45 advisors can view full student records, including aid holds (EV-007). No user holds both aid-awarding and disbursement roles (EV-008). About 140 local LMS accounts sit outside the identity provider (EV-009). The servicer's 8 FAMS accounts sign in with a password only (EV-010). Refund bank details, recovery contacts and MFA methods change in the portal with no re-authentication step (EV-011). The service desk resets student passwords after security questions (EV-012). The SAIG mailbox runs on 3 workstations, and 28 staff hold Department system accounts (EV-013).

**Devices and network.** The 780 staff and faculty endpoints all report EDR and full-disk encryption (EV-014). The 950 lab and library computers reset to their image at reboot, and the 410 lab computers at Campuses 2 and 3 share one local administrator password set in the image (EV-015). Campuses 1 and 2 have separate VLANs for staff, labs, campus safety devices and Wi-Fi. At Campus 3 these devices share one wired network. Campus 1 has dual internet with cellular failover, and Campuses 2 and 3 have one carrier each (EV-016). The 64 door controllers and 11 video recorders run firmware the vendors list as past end of support (EV-017). The emergency notification console is reached only through SSO, with no break-glass account, and the last annual test was on 2026-02-11 (EV-018). Quarterly authenticated scans found 40 Critical findings in Q1 and Q2 2026; the lab and campus safety VLANs are outside the scan scope (EV-019). The November 2025 penetration test covered internet-facing hosts (EV-020). SIS configuration and integration job changes (112 from 2026-01 to 2026-06) have no ticket category; cloud infrastructure changes are ticketed (EV-021). The SaaS register was last reconciled in 2024 (EV-022).

**Cloud, data, backups and monitoring.** The landing zone has 4 accounts with guardrails, posture management in every account, write-once log retention for 3 years, and college-managed keys. There is no egress alert on the data warehouse, and query logging by user is off. Partner portal users sign in with a password only (EV-023). The integration platform runs 37 interfaces; 6 have no written interconnection terms, and 6 service account passwords were last changed in 2023 (EV-024). ISIR-derived fields are copied nightly into the data warehouse, where 46 users can query all student-level data, and they appear in the AI-002 feature list and marketing analytics queries (EV-025). Daily backups go to a separate backup account in a second region with 30-day write-once retention and separate credentials. No restore job is recorded for any workload (EV-026). The SIEM receives identity provider, email, firewall, cloud and EDR logs; SIS, FAMS and LMS logs are not connected, and no use case covers portal account takeover or refund bank changes (EV-027). The use case list is unchanged since 2023 (EV-028). SIS and FAMS audit logs are kept for 180 days, and no record of a user activity review was provided (EV-029).

**Programs and documents.** The WISP dates from 2023 and was updated in 2025. The 5 policies date from 2023, and 4 standards exist (EV-030). The request for an IT disaster recovery plan, restore test records, a retention and disposal schedule, and an OIG referral procedure returned none; financial aid records are kept from 2009 onward (EV-031). The last written risk assessment is dated July 2025 (EV-032). The vCISO was designated Qualified Individual in January 2025, with the CIO as senior overseer (EV-033). The first written Qualified Individual report went to the board on 2025-10-23 (EV-034). The FY2025 Title IV compliance audit was performed by an independent auditor (EV-035). The 2023 incident response plan has no section on the FTC notice, the FSA notice, multi-state notification or fraud referrals, and the last tabletop was in 2024 (EV-036). The incident queue holds 44 incidents for 2025 and 2026 (EV-037). In the 2025-26 award year, 23 student portal accounts were taken over and $61,400 of refunds diverted; $38,000 was recovered (EV-038). In fall 2025, admissions flagged 310 online applications as likely fraudulent; 41 reached enrollment, 6 received a Pell disbursement, and none was referred to the OIG (EV-039). Training completion is 94%, and the June 2026 phishing click rate was 9.4% (EV-040). 3 sanctions were recorded in fiscal 2026 (EV-041).

**Suppliers and contracts.** Accounts payable lists the SaaS, cloud, security, servicer, bank, campus safety and AI vendors (EV-042). About 90 vendors receive student data; 68 contracts have FERPA school-official and security terms and 22 do not, and 31 have no incident notice term. The servicer contract has no MFA or incident notice term (EV-043). Vendors are reviewed only at onboarding (EV-044). The SIS vendor's SOC 2 report states RTO 24 hours and RPO 4 hours; the LMS vendor's states RTO 6 hours and RPO 1 hour; the FAMS vendor's Security-only report states none (EV-045). 14 SaaS tools, including the AI-003 assistant and the AI-004 tutor, were bought on purchasing cards and are not on the SaaS register (EV-046). The cyber policy has a $5 million limit and a $150,000 retention (EV-047). The PPA commits the college to the Safeguards Rule, and the SAIG Enrollment Agreement has a breach notice term (EV-048). 9 employer partners sponsor about 640 students; 2 hospital-system partners require a SOC 2 Type 2 report before their 2027-12-31 renewals (EV-049).

**Facilities and media.** Walk-throughs at Campus 1 and Campus 3 on 2026-06-23 and 2026-06-24 found badge readers on Campus 1 offices and closets. The 3 Campus 3 network closets use keyed locks, and no key log was presented. Campus 3 door controllers and video recorders share switches with staff and lab computers (EV-050). Badge records cover Campuses 1 and 2 (EV-051). Drive destruction certificates are on file; the 4 copier returns in 2025-26 have no sanitization certificate (EV-052).

**Business volume.** FY2025 receipts were about $100 million over about 250 business days: online about $38 million, Campus 1 about $30 million, Campus 2 about $19 million and Campus 3 about $13 million (EV-053). About $64 million of Title IV funds and about 9,000 credit balance refunds were paid in 2025-26 (EV-054). About 7,800 students are enrolled, 3,500 of them fully online and living in 41 states (EV-055). The college holds customer information on about 41,000 consumers (EV-056).

**AI tools and applicability.** Five AI tools are in use, all adopted by departments, with no security, privacy or bias review record (EV-057). The counseling center does not bill for services (EV-058). The college receives no E-Rate discounts, has no sponsored research or federal contracts, and has no on-campus housing (EV-059). The fall 2025 FERPA notice and the request and consent records are kept by the Registrar (EV-060).

**Reconciliation across sources.**
- **Workforce.** The 600 employees on the HR roster each have an identity provider account (EV-001, EV-003).
- **Endpoints.** The endpoint console count (780) is the SSP boundary count (EV-014).
- **Administrators.** 11 cloud identities and 26 SaaS administrator roles give the 37 administrator accounts (EV-006).
- **Vendors.** The 17 named student-data vendors and a summary row in [`vendor-register.csv`](vendor-register.csv) add up to the about 90 in the contract register (EV-043).
- **AI tools.** Two AI tools are features of vendors in accounts payable (AI-001 in the CRM, AI-005 with the proctoring vendor), two were found on purchasing cards (AI-003, AI-004), and one was built in house (AI-002), giving the 5 tools in [`asset-inventory.csv`](asset-inventory.csv) (EV-042, EV-046, EV-057).
