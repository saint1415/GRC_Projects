## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the company's own records before any assessment work began on 2026-07-06. It covers the organization, the systems that store, process or transmit account data or can affect the cardholder data environments (CDEs), the suppliers that touch them, and the rules that may bind the company. At this size the sources are systems of record: the identity provider and PAM vault, the HR system, the endpoint and EDR consoles, the Cloud A organization console and the Cloud B console, the SIEM and the MSSP portal, the scanner and patch reports, the accounts payable vendor master, the contract register and third-party risk files, the QSA's reports, the sponsor agreements and bank correspondence, board materials and the incident log. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (interviews, samples, tests) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analysis (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Users and access.** The identity provider holds active named accounts for all 600 employees on the HR roster, plus contractor and service accounts; every active user account has MFA registered, with push and number matching for standard users and security keys for the 110 core administrators. Cloud A, PAM and the SaaS tools sign in through the identity provider; Cloud B consoles do not (EV-001). Conditional access requires MFA and device compliance, passwords are 14 characters with a banned list, and accounts lock after 6 failures (EV-002). HR recorded 142 terminations and 88 internal transfers in the 12 months to 2026-06-30 (EV-003). The HR feed disables identity provider accounts on the termination date; Cloud B IAM users and SaaS local accounts are not on the feed (EV-004). Two six-month access review campaigns cover the core platform, Cloud A and the cages; none is recorded for Cloud B users or service accounts (EV-005). PAM gives just-in-time, recorded sessions to the 110 core administrators for Cloud A and both cages and does not cover Cloud B (EV-006). In Cloud B, 61 IAM users hold access keys up to about 3 years old, and 26 Integrated Payments engineers hold standing administrator roles, signing in with push MFA directly from the internet (EV-007). There are 212 service accounts, with no review schedule (EV-008). The core merchant portal (about 64,000 users) requires MFA for administrator, refund, funding account and virtual terminal roles; the Integrated Payments portal (about 15,000 users) signs users in with a password only, and funding account changes need only the password (EV-009). The Qualified Individual approved dynamic risk analysis for core portal users in 2025-04 (EV-010).

**Endpoints, network and platforms.** The endpoint console lists about 720 laptops and desktops with disk encryption and EDR, including 136 administrator endpoints that reach the CDE only through PAM (EV-011). EDR covers all managed endpoints and servers; Cloud B containers run the acquired company's agent, which alerts the gateway team's email (EV-012). The Cloud A organization has 8 accounts with guardrails, posture management, a write-once log archive and a warm standby promoted by hand (EV-013). Network procedures and data-flow diagrams (v2026-03) do not show the gateway-to-switch link or the routes added on 2026-04-18, and there is no review record for Cloud B security groups (EV-014). The 2026-04-18 interconnect change has no segmentation retest recorded; the last core segmentation test was 2026-01 (EV-015). The cages hold 58 servers, including 12 settlement servers; 4 settlement batch servers run an operating system out of vendor support since 2025-10, with no compensating controls recorded (EV-016). ASV scans passed in 4 of 4 quarters; Cloud B internal scans run without credentials; 188 critical findings were opened on cage servers in Q1 and Q2 2026 (EV-017), with a median of 41 days to patch against a 30-day target (EV-018). The core platform was penetration-tested in 2026-01; Cloud B was last tested in 2025-07 by the acquired company's tester (EV-019). Cloud B images and the 4 unsupported servers have no approved baseline (EV-020). The Integrated Payments repository allows self-approved merges and has no dependency scanning (EV-021). The core hosted payment page has script inventory, integrity checks and tamper-detection since 2025-03; the Integrated Payments hosted payment fields have none (EV-022). The DR cage HSMs were last loaded in 2025-08, and the cryptographic architecture document does not cover Cloud B (EV-023). The settlement archive keeps full PAN for 7 years with no business justification on file (EV-024). External endpoints accept TLS 1.2 or higher (EV-025).

**Backups, recovery and monitoring.** Cloud A backups go to a separate vault account in a second region with 35-day write-once retention; Cloud B has same-region snapshots only, with no restore record (EV-026). The 2025-11 authorization failover took 2 hours 40 minutes against a 1-hour target, and the 2026-02-21 settlement DR test took 11 hours against 8; neither sponsor bank took part, and there is no recovery plan for the gateway (EV-027). The SIEM receives the core platform, the cages, the identity provider, PAM and Cloud A; Cloud B is not connected; the MSSP contract requires escalation within 30 minutes (EV-028). MSSP reports and daily review records cover core sources only (EV-029). Cloud B keeps its audit logs 30 days inside Cloud B, and any Cloud B administrator can delete them (EV-030).

**Programs and governance.** Five policies were approved in 2024, with standards for configuration, cryptography, secure development, authenticators and third parties; the logging standard is a draft, and there is no service account standard. Cloud B follows the acquired company's wiki pages (EV-031). The last written risk assessment is dated July 2025; targeted risk analyses cover 9 core requirements and none for Integrated Payments; no risk appetite statements were found (EV-032). Quarterly security task reviews are signed for the core platform in 4 of 4 quarters, with no Q1 2026 gateway review; the PCI DSS scope document (2026-03) lists no Cloud B components (EV-033). The 2025 core AOC is dated 2025-12-15 and the acquired gateway's AOC 2025-09-30; both banks accepted one combined 2026 AOC on 2026-06-12 (EV-034). Both sponsor agreements name clearing, settlement, reconciliation and funding file services as subject to 12 U.S.C. 1867(c) (EV-035). Bank A's designated contacts were confirmed on 2026-07-01; no Bank B contacts are recorded (EV-036). The Qualified Individual was designated in writing in 2025-01 (EV-037). The first Qualified Individual report to the board (2025-12) does not mention the acquisition (EV-038). The 2025 internal IT audit was issued to the audit committee (EV-039). The 2025 incident response plan covers the core platform and the cages, and the last tabletop was in 2025-06 (EV-040). The incident log holds 46 incidents for 2025 and 2026 (EV-041). The June 2026 phishing click rate was 4.9%, and Integrated Payments developers have no secure coding training record (EV-042). Background checks precede hire, and the acquired company's contractors are not on the contractor list (EV-043). The cyber insurance policy has a $20 million limit and a $500,000 retention (EV-044).

**Suppliers and contracts.** Accounts payable paid 182 vendors in 12 months, 41 of them carried over from the acquisition (EV-045). Of the 34 PCI DSS service providers (Tier 1), 7 have no current AOC or responsibility matrix located, among them the contact center SaaS; the 41 acquired vendors are not tiered (EV-046). The ISV partner agreements have click-through security terms and no security schedule for the 20 largest partners; a referral agreement covers PIN debit (EV-047). AOCs are on file for the cloud, colocation and content delivery providers (EV-048).

**Facilities and media.** Cage access lists have 4 of 4 quarterly reviews; 410 cage visits were logged from 2026-01 to 2026-06 (EV-049). Destruction certificates and a 2026-06 tape inventory are on file (EV-050).

**Business volume and settlement.** Receipts were $100.0 million for the 12 months to 2026-06-30 (EV-051). The company processes about 2.25 million transactions and about $104 million of merchant sales a day for about 31,000 merchants (EV-052). Funding files go to Bank A by 03:00 and Bank B by 04:00 Eastern; they are encrypted in transit and not digitally signed (EV-053).

**AI, support, onboarding and other.** Five AI tools and models are in use (AI-001 to AI-005). AI-001 and AI-002 have no independent validation or bias test record, the generative AI tools have no security or model risk review record, and no AI inventory or approved-tools list exists (EV-054). Call recordings depend on agents to pause and resume, and about 310,000 tickets from before the 2025 masking project are retained (EV-055). Onboarding files hold merchant owner SSNs and bank accounts with no disposal schedule (EV-056). The data platform receives tokens only, with a PAN block on load (EV-057). Inbound email filtering is in place (EV-058). The company holds no New York license and is not an SEC registrant (EV-059).

**Reconciliation across sources.**
- **Workforce.** The 600 employees on the HR roster each have an identity provider account (EV-001, EV-003).
- **Privileged users.** The 110 core administrators in PAM and the 26 Cloud B administrators make up the 136 privileged users and 136 administrator endpoints used in the SSP boundary and the P07 population (EV-006, EV-007, EV-011).
- **Vendors.** 182 vendors in accounts payable, 34 of them PCI DSS service providers and 41 carried over from the acquisition (EV-045, EV-046). The named vendors and the summary rows in [`vendor-register.csv`](vendor-register.csv) add up to the 34 service providers and the 182 vendors.
- **AI tools.** The accounts payable vendor master, the model registry and the department survey give the 5 tools in [`asset-inventory.csv`](asset-inventory.csv) (EV-045, EV-054).
