At the Sole Proprietorship tier the agency keeps **one** consolidated policy, because one person writes, follows, and checks it (`01_company-sizes/tier-project-scaling.csv`, t1 P06). This file is kept so the folder has the standard policy set names. It holds no separate rules.

**Where the incident response rules are:**

- POL-01 section 11 (Incident response): runbook and printed contacts (11.1), same-day incident log (11.2), breach determination with counsel and the 5-year record (11.3), notice deadlines (11.4), ransom decisions (11.5), annual walkthrough and lessons learned (11.6).
- POL-01 section 8 (Money movement and invoices): the callback rule and transfer alerts that stop payment redirection.
- The procedure itself is the P08 runbook (`../step-08_P08_incident-response-runbook/ir-runbook.md`) and its notification matrix.
- SP 800-53 IR-1 is met by POL-01 as a whole.

Traceability for every statement is in `policy-control-map.csv`.
