At the Sole Proprietorship tier the agency keeps **one** consolidated policy, because one person writes, follows, and checks it (`01_company-sizes/tier-project-scaling.csv`, t1 P06). This file is kept so the folder has the standard policy set names. It holds no separate rules.

**Where the data classification and handling rules are:**

- POL-01 section 9 (Data handling): three classification levels (9.1), approved storage locations (9.2), document exchange through the AMS portal or secure links (9.3), encryption (9.4), backup and AMS export (9.5), retention tied to Fla. Stat. 626.748 and 626.561(2) (9.6), disposal (9.7).
- POL-01 section 6 (Vendors and insurers): no client data to a vendor without written terms.
- POL-01 Appendix A: devices and data locations.
- SP 800-53 MP-1 and SC-1 are met by POL-01 as a whole.

Traceability for every statement is in `policy-control-map.csv`.
