## 1. Purpose
Set the rules every user follows on company systems, including the portable media rules that protect CDAs and the rules for AI tools.

## 2. Scope
All employees, long-term and outage contractors, and vendors who use company systems or data. Acknowledged at hire, at contractor in-processing, and each year.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| IT Security Manager | Owns this policy and the training content |
| Managers and outage supervisors | Make sure their staff and contractors know the rules |
| All users | Follow the rules and report anything suspicious |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for authorized business use; users have no expectation of privacy on them, and activity is monitored. (PL-4; AC-8; PR.AT-01; C-NUCLEAR-S06)
4.2 Never connect a personal or business device, cable, or USB drive to any plant equipment, control panel, or CDA. Only CST-approved media that passed the PMMD kiosk may be used there. (PL-4; MP-7; PR.AT-01; PR.PS-01; C-NUCLEAR-R01 (RG 5.71 B.1.19))
4.3 Never install software or remote access tools, or connect unapproved devices or wireless access points to the business network. (CM-11; PL-4; PR.PS-05; C-NUCLEAR-S06)
4.4 Never place SGI or Restricted information on general shares, personal storage, personal email, or external sites. (PL-4; AC-3; PR.DS-01; C-NUCLEAR-S01)
4.5 Use only approved AI tools (the approved-tools list kept by the vCISO). Never enter Restricted information, plant configuration details, or personal information of others into any AI tool unless the approved-tools list says the tool is cleared for that data. (PL-4; PR.AT-01; C-NUCLEAR-S01; C-NUCLEAR-S06)
4.6 Complete security awareness training: annually for employees and long-term contractors, and the outage contractor module (including PMMD and SRI content) before receiving an account. (AT-2; PR.AT-01; C-NUCLEAR-R01 (73.54(d)(1)); C-NUCLEAR-R04 (CIP-003-9 Att. 1 Sec. 1))
4.7 Report suspected phishing, lost devices, or unusual system behavior immediately (POL-03 4.2). (IR-6; RS.MA-02; C-NUCLEAR-S06)
4.8 Lock screens when away and protect badges and credentials; never share them. (AC-11; IA-5; PR.AA-01; C-NUCLEAR-S06)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Compliance is checked through the annual independent assessment (P07), quarterly access reviews, the metrics reported to the audit committee, and, for anything in 73.54 scope, the 73.55(m) program review. Violations are handled under POL-01 4.13.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 4.8: written, risk-rated, approved by the right authority under POL-01 4.5, recorded in the risk register, and limited to 12 months. No exception may permit a known regulatory noncompliance.
