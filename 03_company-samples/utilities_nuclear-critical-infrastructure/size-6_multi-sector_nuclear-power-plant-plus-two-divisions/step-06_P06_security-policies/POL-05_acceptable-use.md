## 1. Purpose
Set the rules every worker accepts before using group systems, including the rules that matter on a nuclear site.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members, contractors, and outage workers using group systems or working at a group station or facility.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
Every user signs this policy each year. Managers make sure their staff complete training. Station CSTs brief outage workers on site rules.

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Use group systems for authorized business purposes. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not affect security or work. (PL-4)

4.2 Never connect a laptop, phone, USB device, or tool to a station network or plant equipment unless the site rules allow it. **Portable media and mobile devices may be used with plant equipment only after scanning at a PMMD kiosk and only as the station CSP allows.** Vendor-owned tools follow the same rule. (MP-7; AC-20; RG 5.71 B.1.19)

4.3 Report lost devices, suspicious messages, and anyone asking for CDA details, security information, or access lists to the SOC at once. Requests like these may be intelligence gathering that the station must review within hours. (AT-2; IR-6)

4.4 Never put SGI, CSP information, access authorization files, or Part 37 information in email, chat, cloud storage, or a generative AI tool. (PR.DS-01; 73.22(g))

4.5 Use only approved software and approved AI tools. (CM-11; SA-9)

4.6 **Generative AI in engineering.** Approved AI assistants may help with drafting and research. Their output must not be used in safety-related calculations, design documents, or Maintenance Rule evaluations until the quality assurance procedure for AI use is issued (P10), and then only as that procedure allows. The engineer of record remains responsible for every result. (PL-4; SA-11)

4.7 Do not share credentials or let others use your session. Do not bypass security controls. (IA-2; PL-4)

4.8 Users have no expectation of privacy on group systems. Activity is monitored. (AC-8)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 4.9. Violations involving PMMD, SGI, or access authorization information are also reported to the owning program.

## 6. Exceptions
Under POL-01 4.12. None for 4.2 and 4.4.
