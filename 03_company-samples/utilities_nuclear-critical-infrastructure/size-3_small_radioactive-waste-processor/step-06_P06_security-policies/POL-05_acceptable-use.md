## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how the workforce uses company systems, plant equipment, devices, and data.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (owners, employees, contractors, and temporary staff) at the Plant, in company vehicles, and at customer sites. Covers all company systems and data, including systems that vendors operate for the company, the plant OT network, and the physical security systems. It applies to Part 37 security-related information and all other company information.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |

| IT Manager | Security awareness content, reminders, and phishing exercises |
| Radiation Safety Officer | Part 37 training content, including information handling and cyber indicators |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems are for company business. Limited personal use (for example, checking personal email on a break) is allowed if it does not involve company data or create risk. (PL-4)

4.2 Every workforce member must sign this policy before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4(1))

4.3 **Training.** Security awareness training is required at hire and every year. The IT Manager sends monthly security reminders and runs phishing exercises at least quarterly. Approved individuals also complete the Part 37 training and refresher, which covers handling of security-related information and cyber indicators at the security systems. (AT-2; PR.AT-01; 37.43(c))

4.4 Lock your screen when you step away. (AC-11)

4.5 Do not install software or browser extensions unless IT approves them. (CM-10; CM-11)

4.6 **Removable media.** Do not connect USB storage to any company computer unless IT approves it. **Never** connect USB storage or a laptop to an HMI, PLC, engineering workstation, or security system device unless the Maintenance and Controls Supervisor or RSO approves it for a specific task. (MP-7; CM-11)

4.7 **At customer sites.** Use only company field laptops. Follow the site's portable media and device rules, including scanning at reactor site kiosks. Never connect company devices to a customer's plant network. (MP-7; PL-4)

4.8 **Generative AI.** Use only tools on the approved list kept by the IT Manager. Never paste Security-Related, Restricted, or Confidential information into a tool that is not approved for that level. Public chatbots are not approved for any company data. (PL-4)

4.9 Do not photograph or describe the vault, security devices, alarm layouts, or shipment schedules on social media or in personal messages. (PL-4; 37.43(d))

4.10 Report lost devices, lost badges, suspicious messages, and anything unusual about cameras, doors, or plant screens immediately (POL-03 4.2 and 4.3). (AT-2)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement (POL-01 section 4.8). Sanctions range from retraining to termination, depending on intent and harm. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), the Part 37 program reviews, and the access reviews in this policy set.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the President for High risk), and expire within 12 months.
