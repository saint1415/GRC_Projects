- POL-01 8.2 and 8.5: Client B security information only in Client B's share; named-person sharing only.
- POL-01 6.4 and 6.6: watched remote support sessions; telling Client B when access is no longer needed (CSIA-B (6)).
- POL-01 section 12: duties of an individual with unescorted access at Client A (10 CFR 73.56(f)-(g)).
- SP 800-53 AC-1 and IA-1 (policy and procedures) are met by POL-01 as a whole.
