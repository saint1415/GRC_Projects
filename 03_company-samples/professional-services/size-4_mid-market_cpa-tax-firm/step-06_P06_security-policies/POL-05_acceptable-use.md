## 1. Purpose
Tell every workforce member what they may and may not do with Company systems and client information, and how to spot and stop the frauds that target tax and accounting firms.

## 2. Scope
Every workforce member of the Company and the Attest Firm, including seasonal staff, interns, contractors, and offshore vendor staff, on every Company system and device, at the office or remote.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Chief People Officer | Acknowledgments, training records, training gate for new and seasonal staff |
| Director of Information Security | Training content, phishing simulations, approved-tools list |
| Managers | Make sure their staff complete training and follow this policy |
| All workforce | Follow this policy and report concerns |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Every workforce member must sign this policy and the confidentiality agreement at hire and each year. The agreement explains that misuse of tax return information is a federal offense (26 U.S.C. 7216) with civil penalties (26 U.S.C. 6713). (PL-4; GV.RR-04)

4.2 Background checks must be completed before any access, including for seasonal staff and interns. (PS-3)

4.3 Security training must be completed **before** access is granted and every year after. The identity provider grants access to client systems only after training is recorded. Phishing simulations run at least quarterly, plus a simulation for seasonal staff each January. Training covers token-theft phishing pages, MFA prompts the user did not start, help desk impersonation, refund and payment change fraud, IRC 7216, and AI use. (AT-2; AT-2(2); AT-2(3); 314.4(e)(1); 164.308(a)(5))

4.4 **Call-back verification.** Any request to change a refund bank account, address, email, or phone on a return, or a bank account or payee in a CAS client's bill pay or payroll, must be verified by calling the client on the number already on file, never a number in the request. The call is logged before the change is made. (AT-3; PR.AT-02)

4.5 Client data may be handled only on Company-managed devices and approved services. Personal email, personal cloud storage, and unapproved SaaS must never hold client data. Phones used for Company email must be enrolled in device management. (AC-19; AC-20)

4.6 Returns and source documents go to clients through the portal. If a client insists on email, the enforced-encryption option must be used. (SC-8)

4.7 Keep a clean desk. Collect printouts immediately; at the office use pull printing where available. At home, print client documents only if approved, and shred them with a cross-cut shredder. (PE-5; 164.310(b))

4.8 Report suspected incidents immediately under POL-03 4.2.

4.9 **AI tools.** Use only the AI tools on the approved list, and only with the data classes approved for each (STD-05). Never paste client data into a public AI chatbot. Every AI output used in client work must be checked by the professional who signs it, including every cited authority and amount. (PL-4; SA-9)

4.10 Open client files only as your work requires. Access is logged and reviewed. (AU-6)

4.11 Do not take a Company device outside the United States, or sign in from outside the United States, without approval from the Director of Information Security. (AC-17)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 4.14. The acknowledgment report, training records, and phishing results are reviewed monthly by the Chief People Officer and the Director of Information Security.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 4.8.
