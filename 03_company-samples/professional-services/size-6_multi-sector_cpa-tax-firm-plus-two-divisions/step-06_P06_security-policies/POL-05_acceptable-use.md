## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how the workforce uses group systems and information.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members in every division, CPA Partners, and corporate, including seasonal staff, on any device used for group work.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow these rules; complete training; report incidents |
| Managers and office managers | Make sure staff complete training and acknowledgments before access |
| Group HR and the seasonal workforce team | Track acknowledgments and training completion |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Workforce members must access client and customer information only as needed for their job. Looking up returns or accounts out of curiosity, including those of family, friends, or public figures, is prohibited and is a disclosure or use that IRC 7216 forbids. (PL-4; AC-8; 301.7216-1(a))

4.2 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy, an IRC 7216 and 6713 confidentiality statement, and their division supplement at hire and every year. (PL-4; PR.AT-01)

4.3 Every workforce member must complete security and privacy training before receiving access and every year after. Training covers business email compromise, refund and payroll change fraud, and IRC 7216. The group runs quarterly phishing exercises, including fake bank-change requests. (AT-2; AT-2(3); PR.AT-01; 314.4(e)(1))

4.4 Reviewers, e-file operators, integrated planning coordinators, Wealth operations staff, and Practice Cloud support engineers must complete role training before access. (AT-3; PR.AT-02)

4.5 **Call-back rule.** No one may change a refund bank account, payroll deposit account, client address or email, or Wealth transfer instruction based on an email, text, or chat request, even from an internal sender, without calling the client at a number of record. (PL-4; 314.3(b)(3); 248.201)

4.6 Suspected incidents must be reported to the group SOC within 1 hour (POL-03 4.1). (IR-6)

4.7 Restricted information may be used only on group-managed devices and approved services. It must not be sent to personal email or stored on personal devices. Wealth business communications must use archived channels only. (AC-19; AC-20; 275.204-2(a)(7))

4.8 **Generative AI.** Only AI tools on the group approved-tools list may be used for group work. Restricted information must never be entered into public AI tools. CPAs and enrolled agents remain responsible for every figure and statement in a return or a client communication drafted with AI assistance. (PL-4; PL-4(1); 31 CFR 10.22)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
