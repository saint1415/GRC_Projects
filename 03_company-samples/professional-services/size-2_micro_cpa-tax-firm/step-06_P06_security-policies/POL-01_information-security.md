At the Micro tier the firm keeps three core policies: access control (POL-02), incident response (POL-03), and data classification and handling (POL-04). A separate Information Security Policy would add little for a 7-person firm, so its essential rules live in **POL-02 Part A. Program governance**. Together with POL-03, POL-04, the SSP (P02), the risk register (P01), and the runbook (P08), they form the firm's written information security program (its WISP) under 16 CFR 314.3(a):
