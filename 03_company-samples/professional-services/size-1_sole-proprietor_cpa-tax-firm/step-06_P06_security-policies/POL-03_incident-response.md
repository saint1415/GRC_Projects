At the Sole Proprietorship tier the practice keeps **one** consolidated policy, because one person writes, follows, and checks it (`01_company-sizes/tier-project-scaling.csv`, t1 P06). This file is kept so the folder has the standard policy set names. It holds no separate rules.

**Where the incident response rules are:**

- POL-01 section 10 (Incident response): runbook and printed contacts (10.1), same-day incident log with discovery and confirmation times (10.2), IRS report through the Stakeholder Liaison by the next business day after confirmation (10.3), FTC, Florida, and other notices per the matrix (10.4), no payment without counsel and an OFAC check (10.5), annual walkthrough and lessons learned (10.6).
- The procedure itself is the P08 runbook (`../step-08_P08_incident-response-runbook/ir-runbook.md`) and its notification matrix. A written incident response plan is not required under 16 CFR 314.6, but the owner keeps one.
- SP 800-53 IR-1 is met by POL-01 as a whole.

Traceability for every statement is in `policy-control-map.csv`.
