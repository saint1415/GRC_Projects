## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the practice's own records before the self-assessment began on 2026-07-27. A one-person practice has no HR system, asset database or internal audit, so the systems of record are the vendors' admin settings and reports, the IRS PTIN, e-file and Tax Pro Account records, bank and card statements, the email inbox, the signed agreements folder, the laptop, phone and router settings, a walk-through of the home office, and the insurance agent's portal. Intake covers the organization, the systems that hold client tax return information and other customer information, the vendors that touch them, and the rules that may bind the practice. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (self-reviews, walk-throughs, tests) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analysis (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Tax software and client portal.** The tax software vendor enforces MFA on every account. The owner's second factor is an authenticator app on the owner's phone, and no second authenticator is registered. The activity log records sign-ins, return access and bank account changes, and no saved or exported activity report appears. The AI document extraction feature is turned off (EV-001). In the 2025 season the firm filed about 380 individual returns covering about 600 individual consumers and about 45 business, trust and estate returns. The client database holds records from 2014 onward on about 1,450 consumers. The acknowledgment report shows a status for every 2026 return, and the filing-season checklist includes a daily acknowledgment check (EV-002). Client MFA on the portal is optional, about 35% of individual clients used the portal in 2026, signed Forms 8879 are stored back to 2019, and an IRC 7216 lender consent is set up as a signing template (EV-003).

**Email, practice management and accounting.** The owner's mailbox is the suite's only administrator account. Two-step verification is off, basic sign-in methods are allowed, sign-in history and file versions are kept for 30 days, no backup service is connected, and client folders go back to 2014 (EV-004). The business plan terms include a data protection addendum (EV-005). Practice management has MFA available and turned off. Each 2026 client has a signed engagement letter, the engagement types include no attest work, the letter template declines work that requires protected health information, and card payments are taken on the vendor's hosted page (EV-006). The owner is an accountant user on 12 clients' own accounting files, each added by client invitation, and those platforms require a second factor (EV-007).

**Devices, home office and network.** On the laptop, full-disk encryption (since 2023), automatic updates, built-in antivirus and the host firewall are on. No backup is configured, download folders hold client documents, the browser has the same saved password for the email suite and practice management plus the accountant logins, and the IT consultant's remote-support tool is installed (EV-008). The phone is encrypted, has no remote wipe set up, syncs its camera roll to a personal photo backup account, and holds client document photos, the tax software authenticator, the email app and the AI assistant app (EV-009). The home office is a separate room with a keyed lock, a locked file cabinet and a cross-cut shredder. Paper source documents go back to 2019, and the printer-scanner stores the mailbox password for scan-to-email (EV-010). One network serves the office devices, family devices and smart-home devices, a guest network is not in use, and the router admin password is the default on its label (EV-011).

**Vendors and contracts.** The IT consultant's invoices show remote sessions on the laptop from 2023, and a 2024 invoice records mailbox MFA turned on and then off the next day when scan-to-email stopped working. No invoice refers to an agreement (EV-012). A services agreement with confidentiality and security terms and the signed IRC 6713 and 7216 notice are dated 2026-07-23, and no earlier one was found (EV-013). The agreements folder holds the tax software contract, the suite terms, click-through terms for practice management and accounting, the AI assistant's individual plan terms and the 2025 contract preparer's agreement. No vendor list or record of a provider review was found (EV-014). Statements show recurring payments to 8 suppliers, hourly payments to the IT consultant, per-return payments to the contract preparer in early 2025, and no payment for a cyber policy, a backup service, a password manager or a security course (EV-015).

**Business volume.** Gross receipts were about $180,000 in 2025 with no wages paid (EV-016). About 60% of fees were billed from February to April, about $1,700 per business day in season and about $400 per business day in the rest of the year (EV-017).

**IRS records.** The PTIN was renewed for 2026, and the 2024 to 2026 renewals each answered Yes to the Form W-12 line 11 question on a written information security plan. The e-file application lists the firm as an ERO with one EFIN and the owner as Responsible Official, at a Florida address (EV-018). About 40 active IRS authorizations belong to taxpayers who are no longer clients (EV-019).

**Documents, training and inbox.** The Pub. 5708 WISP template downloaded in 2024 has no sections filled in. No written security program, Qualified Individual designation, risk assessment, data inventory, vendor list, retention schedule, incident plan or contact list, call-back rule, continuation agreement, or stored recovery codes were found, and the IRS Stakeholder Liaison number is not recorded (EV-020). CPE covers ethics, with no security course or security alert subscription (EV-021). In the 2026 season client requests to change a refund account or address were answered by email with no record of a call. The 2026-02-09 request was stopped after the owner phoned the client. A 2026-03-09 phishing message links to a look-alike sign-in page that was opened, and no report was sent. Clients send documents as attachments, and completed returns were sent as attachments on request (EV-022).

**Vendor assurance and insurance.** The declarations page lists a data breach expense endorsement and no standalone cyber policy; its sublimit was requested (EV-023). The tax software vendor's SOC 2 Type 2 report covers Security and Availability for the 12 months ending 2026-03-31 with one exception, includes the portal module but not the AI extraction feature, states RTO 4 hours and RPO 1 hour, and states no number of days for customer incident notice (EV-024).

**AI tools.** The owner holds an individual paid plan for a generative AI assistant from 2026-02. Its model-improvement setting is on, and the chat history from 2026-02-02 includes uploaded documents and pasted text (EV-025). The plan terms allow use of chats to improve models unless turned off and make no commitment about where data is processed (EV-026). The tax software's AI extraction feature is off (EV-001).
