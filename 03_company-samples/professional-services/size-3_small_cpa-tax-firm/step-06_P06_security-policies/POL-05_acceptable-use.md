## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how the workforce uses firm systems, devices, and client data.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (partners, employees, seasonal preparers, interns, and contractors), on firm devices and on any personal phone used for firm email.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR and Payroll Specialist | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and annually; tracks training |
| IT Manager | Security awareness content, reminders, and phishing exercises |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Firm systems are for firm business. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not involve client data or create risk. (PL-4)
4.2 Every workforce member must sign this policy, and an acknowledgment of the IRC 7216 and 6713 penalties, before receiving access, and again each year. (PL-4; PS-6)
4.3 **Training before access.** Security awareness training is required before system access (including for seasonal preparers) and every year before the filing season. Training covers phishing, business email compromise, refund diversion, and IRC 7216. The IT Manager sends monthly reminders from January to April and runs phishing exercises at least quarterly. (AT-2; PR.AT-01; 314.4(e)(1))
4.4 **Verify by phone.** Never change a client's refund bank account, address, or email, or a vendor's payment details, because of an email or portal message alone. Call the phone number already on file to confirm. (AT-3)
4.5 Do not open unexpected attachments or links. Report suspicious messages with the Report Phishing button. Never approve an MFA prompt you did not start; report it. (AT-2)
4.6 Lock your screen when you step away. At seasonal stations, sign out at the end of each session. (AC-11)
4.7 Do not install software, browser extensions, or connect USB storage unless IT approves it. Do not set up email forwarding to outside addresses. (CM-11)
4.8 Report lost or stolen devices, misdirected returns, and anything suspicious to IT immediately (POL-03 4.2).
4.9 **Generative AI.** Use only the AI tools on the approved list, and only for their approved purposes. Never paste, upload, or dictate client names, SSNs, tax return information, or client documents into a public or unapproved AI tool. AI output must be checked by a preparer or reviewer before it is used in a return, a letter, or advice (see P10). (PL-4)
4.10 Open client files only when your work requires it. Access to the tax software and DMS is logged and reviewed. (AU-6)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under the sanctions statement in POL-01 section 4.11. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07), log reviews, and phishing exercise results.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.8. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the Managing Partner for High risk), and expire within 12 months.
