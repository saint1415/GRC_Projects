## 1. Purpose
Set clear rules for how workforce members use group systems, devices, and OT, written so that seasonal workers, crew leads, plant operators, and office staff can all follow them.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members, including seasonal and H-2A workers, and contractors using group systems, harvest tablets, or OT. Issued in English and Spanish.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce | Follow these rules; acknowledge them at hire and each season |
| Supervisors and crew leads | Explain the rules at pre-season meetings |
| Group HR director | Keeps acknowledgments |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Use only your own account and never share passwords, PINs, or badges. Crew leads must not let others enter tally under their login. (IA-2; PL-4; PR.AA-01)

4.2 Report suspicious messages, lost tablets or badges, and any pump, pivot, valve, or line that behaves unexpectedly, at once, to your supervisor and the SOC. (IR-6; PL-4; RS.MA-02)

4.3 Do not connect personal devices, USB drives, or unapproved equipment to HMIs, controllers, or plant or farm networks. (MP-7; SC-41; PR.PS-05)

4.4 Do not change setpoints, schedules, recipes, or limits except through your role and the approved change process. (CM-3; CM-5; PR.PS-01)

4.5 Complete security awareness training at hire and each year (seasonal workers at each pre-season meeting), in English or Spanish. (AT-2; PR.AT-01)

4.6 Drone imagery and telematics data must not be used to watch or evaluate individual workers. (PT-2; PL-4; GV.OC-03)

4.7 Use only approved AI tools, and treat AI outputs (yield estimates, prescriptions, drafts) as advice that a person must check before acting. (PL-4; AC-20; GV.RM-01)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Compliance is checked through the annual common control assessment and division samples (P07), quarterly access certifications, the annual supplement attestations, and OT change reviews. Violations are handled under POL-01 4.14.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 4.11. OT exceptions also need the division OT security manager's written safety reasoning.
