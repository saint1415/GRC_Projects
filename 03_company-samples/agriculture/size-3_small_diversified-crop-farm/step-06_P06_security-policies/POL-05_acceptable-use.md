## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how everyone at the farm uses farm computers, tablets, phones, drones, and data. A Spanish-language summary is given to every field worker.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (owners, year-round and seasonal employees including H-2A workers, and contractors such as the MSP and the irrigation integrator when they work on farm systems) at the Home Block and the North Block. Covers all farm systems and data, including the irrigation, pump, fertigation, and cooler operational technology (OT) and the systems vendors operate for the farm.

| Crew Leads | Explain the rules to their crews in English or Spanish |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Farm systems and devices are for farm business. Limited personal use (for example, checking personal email on a break on an office computer) is allowed if it does not involve farm data or create risk. (PL-4)
4.2 Every workforce member must sign this policy, in English or Spanish, before receiving access and again at each pre-season meeting. (PL-4(1))
4.3 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year at the pre-season meeting, in English and Spanish. Office staff also receive monthly tips and at least quarterly phishing exercises. The Irrigation Technician and security lead receive OT security training. (AT-2; AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02)
4.4 Harvest tablets are for tally and farm apps only. Sign in with your own PIN; never let someone else enter tally under your name. Lock or hand back the tablet when you leave it. (AC-11; AC-19)
4.5 Do not install software or apps, or plug USB drives into the HMI or office computers, unless the security lead approves it. (CM-11)
4.6 Do not open unexpected attachments or links. Any email or text asking to change bank details or payment instructions must be confirmed by calling the known phone number on file, never a number in the message. (AT-2)
4.7 Report lost devices, strange messages, and any pump, pivot, or cooler behavior nobody scheduled immediately (POL-03 4.2).
4.8 **Drones:** fly only planned missions over crop blocks. Drone imagery must not be used to watch or evaluate individual workers. Delete images that show people when they are not needed for the crop analysis. (PL-4)
4.9 **AI tools:** use only tools on the approved list. Never paste worker personal information into any AI tool. Yield estimates from AI tools are advice only; they must be checked against field counts before they are used for crew or sales commitments (see P10). (PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.9. Consequences range from retraining to termination, depending on intent and harm, and are applied the same way to every worker regardless of visa status. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07) and the access reviews in this policy set.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.8. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the majority owner and General Manager for High risk), and expire within 12 months.
