## 1. Purpose
Make sure the farm detects, contains, reports, and recovers from security incidents quickly and lawfully, **keeps crops watered and people safe while it does**, and meets notice deadlines.

## 2. Scope
All Cris Santos Company workforce members (owners, year-round and seasonal employees including H-2A workers, and contractors such as the MSP and the irrigation integrator when they work on farm systems) at the Home Block and the North Block. Covers all farm systems and data, including the irrigation, pump, fertigation, and cooler operational technology (OT) and the systems vendors operate for the farm.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Operations and Technology Manager | Incident commander for security incidents; coordinates the MSP, integrator, and insurer-appointed responders |
| Irrigation Technician | Safety and OT lead: moves pumps, pivots, and fertigation to safe manual or local control |
| Farm Manager | Keeps harvest and packing running on paper; decides crew changes |
| Majority owner and General Manager | Engages the insurer and counsel; approves notices and any ransom decision |
| Office and HR Manager | Worker and employee notices; payroll continuity |
| All workforce | Report suspected incidents immediately |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 The farm must keep an incident response plan and a runbook for its most likely serious incident, ransomware on the farm management and irrigation systems (P08). (IR-8; RS.MA-01)
4.2 Workforce members must report any suspected incident **immediately**, and within 1 hour at most, by calling the incident number on their badge card or telling their crew lead. Examples: a strange email or text, a lost tablet, a pump or pivot doing something nobody scheduled, a ransom message. Reports can be made in English or Spanish. Good-faith reporting is never punished. (IR-6; RS.MA-02)
4.3 **Safety first.** If an incident could affect irrigation, fertigation, or the cooler, the Irrigation Technician must first put the affected equipment in a safe state (manual or local control; fertigation off) before any IT action. (IR-4; RS.MI-01)
4.4 Every incident must be logged, categorized, and tracked to closure, with times of discovery and determination recorded. (IR-5)
4.5 The majority owner and General Manager, with counsel, must decide whether an incident is a breach of personal information under Fla. Stat. 501.171 and document the decision, including any written no-harm determination under 501.171(4)(c), which must be kept for 5 years. (IR-6)
4.6 Notices to individuals, the Florida Department of Legal Affairs, consumer reporting agencies, law enforcement, the insurer, and buyers must meet the deadlines in the P08 notification matrix. Counsel confirms each notice. (IR-6; RS.CO-02)
4.7 No ransom may be paid without approval from the majority owner and General Manager, counsel, and the cyber insurer, and an OFAC sanctions check. (IR-4)
4.8 The farm must be able to produce Produce Safety records within the time FDA allows (21 CFR 112.166(a)) and H-2A earnings records within 72 hours of a DOL request (20 CFR 655.122(j)(2)) during and after an incident, from backups or paper. (CP-2)
4.9 The incident plan must be tested at least annually by tabletop exercise with the integrator and MSP, and the manual irrigation procedure must be drilled each November before freeze season. (IR-3; CP-4; ID.IM-02)
4.10 Lessons learned must be written up within 30 days of closing an incident and added to the risk register. (IR-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.9. Consequences range from retraining to termination, depending on intent and harm, and are applied the same way to every worker regardless of visa status. Compliance is checked through the annual control assessment (P07) and the access reviews in this policy set.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.8. They must be written, risk-rated, approved by the policy owner (or by the majority owner and General Manager for High risk), and expire within 12 months.
