## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the company's own records before any assessment work began on 2026-07-06. It covers the organization, the systems that grow, protect, pack and ship the crops and hold worker, grower and customer data, the suppliers that touch them, and the rules that may bind the company. At this size the sources are systems of record: the identity provider, the HR system, the CMDB and endpoint consoles, the OT inventory and installer invoices, the SCADA, pivot cloud and dealer portal account lists, the cloud organization console, the SIEM, the accounts payable vendor master, the contract register, prior risk assessments and internal audit reports, scan results and the incident queue. Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (interviews, samples, tests) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement or the CSF 2.0 benchmark is decided in the gap analysis (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Users and access.** The identity provider holds named accounts for the office, management, IT and settlement users on the HR roster, plus vendor and service accounts, and every active user account has MFA registered (EV-001). Conditional access requires push MFA with number matching for office users, cloud administrators, the VPN and the SYS-01 web console; accounts inactive for 45 days are disabled (EV-002). HR recorded 214 terminations (160 seasonal), 41 transfers and 388 H-2A arrivals in the 12 months to 2026-06-30 (EV-003). The HR feed creates and disables identity provider accounts; SYS-01 mobile accounts, SCADA and HMI accounts, the pivot cloud, grower portal staff roles, badges and pump-station keys change through separate manual requests (EV-004). The last access review was the annual campaign in February 2026, and it did not include SCADA or SYS-01 mobile accounts (EV-005). 38 accounts hold privileged roles across the administrative planes; just-in-time elevation through the access broker covers the cloud accounts only (EV-006). Crew lead tablets at Farms 1 and 2 use 6 shared SYS-01 logins (EV-007). The SCADA integrator signs in with one shared account through an always-on remote access tool on a SCADA server, with no MFA; SCADA audit logging is off (EV-008). The pivot manufacturer has a support account with standing access to all 46 pivots (EV-009), and dealer technicians have standing access to the telematics portal (EV-010). About 140 grower users have portal accounts, and 31% have enrolled in the optional MFA (EV-011).

**Devices and network.** The CMDB and endpoint consoles list 210 laptops and desktops with EDR in blocking mode, 160 tablets and phones in mobile device management, and 20 packinghouse line PCs with EDR in detect-only mode, 390 in total (EV-012). The OT and IoT inventory lists about 1,600 devices (EV-013); installer invoices and purchasing records account for about 2,900 (EV-014). IT/OT firewalls were installed at headquarters and the packinghouse in 2025; Farm 1 and Farm 2 pump-station and fertigation devices share one network with the farm office devices (EV-015). Farm 2 has a single internet circuit, and each of Farms 1 and 2 has one licensed radio link to its pump stations (EV-016). Quarterly authenticated scans of IT systems found 50 Critical findings in Q1 and Q2 2026; OT is outside the scan scope (EV-017). 82% of IT patches were applied within 30 days in Q2 2026, and SCADA servers and HMIs were last patched in 2025-03 (EV-018). The 2025-11 grower portal penetration test reported 2 medium findings, closed at the 2026-01 retest (EV-019). Baselines exist for IT endpoints and cloud images, and none were provided for SCADA servers, HMIs, PLC configurations or the grower portal build (EV-020). The IT change log holds 168 changes and no OT changes (EV-021).

**Cloud, backups and monitoring.** The landing zone has 5 accounts with federated sign-in, guardrails, company-managed keys and logging to a write-once archive (EV-022). Daily backups of the operations and Grower Services accounts go to the backup account in a second region with 35-day write-once retention and separate administrator credentials; SCADA servers are backed up weekly to a storage device on the SCADA network (EV-023). Restore tests cover file services only (EV-024). Of 68 PLC and HMI programs, 31 are held only by the integrator (EV-025). The SIEM receives identity provider, cloud, firewall, EDR, SYS-03 and SYS-12 logs and nothing from SCADA, PLCs, HMIs, the pivot cloud, packinghouse controls or SYS-01 (EV-026). The MSSP monitors IT 24x7 and must call the Security Manager within 30 minutes of a high-severity alert (EV-027). The incident queue holds 22 incidents in 12 months (EV-028). SCADA servers, the local SCADA backup device and the line PCs report no disk encryption (EV-029). Monthly security metrics cover IT only (EV-030).

**Programs and documents.** Five security policies were approved in 2024; an encryption standard and a vulnerability and patch standard date from 2024, and the request for OT security, configuration, logging, vendor risk, secure development and AI standards returned none (EV-033). The Security Manager has been the designated security lead since 2024, with a part-time vCISO (EV-034). The last risk assessment is dated July 2025, and 5 of its 12 actions were open in June 2026 (EV-035). The 2025 internal IT audit did not cover OT (EV-036). Annual training and quarterly phishing simulations reach office users in English and Spanish; module completion was 94%, the June 2026 click rate was 9.1%, and crew leads and Irrigation Technicians are not enrolled (EV-037). The 2024 IT disaster recovery plan covers SYS-02, SYS-03 and the cloud workloads, and the 2024 incident response plan covers IT incidents only (EV-038). Farm 3 has a written manual irrigation procedure and every pump and skid has Hand-Off-Auto switches; the request for a Farm 2 freeze-night manual start procedure, packinghouse downtime procedures and an OT restore procedure returned none (EV-039). Freeze and after-hours alarms page the on-call Irrigation Technician through one SCADA alarm dialer (EV-040). The retention schedule covers finance records only, and the request for a data inventory returned none (EV-041). 14 finger time clocks store templates with no retention setting (EV-042). Food safety audits were passed, and the 2026-04 mock recall took 9 hours, with data assembled by hand from 3 systems (EV-043). Pump stations and fertigation skids use padlocks, and cameras cover headquarters and the packinghouse (EV-044). Replaced HMIs, line PCs and 2 time clocks were returned to vendors with no wipe record (EV-045). The sponsor's growth plan includes acquiring neighboring farms (EV-046).

**Suppliers and contracts.** Accounts payable paid about 110 vendors in the 12 months (EV-047). The contract register flags 38 vendors with access to company data or systems; the SCADA integrator, refrigeration contractor and development firm contracts contain no security terms, the pivot manufacturer and the agronomy analytics vendor are on click-through terms, and vendor files hold onboarding reviews only (EV-048). The FMIS vendor's SOC 2 Type 2 report (12 months ending 2026-03-31) states RTO 8 hours and RPO 1 hour, and its disaster recovery is tested annually (EV-049). SOC 2 reports are on file for the identity provider, cloud provider and MSSP (EV-050). Records live in SYS-01 under the subscription, and no full record set export is on record (EV-051). Purchasing has required a security review for new SaaS since 2025; OT equipment, graders, sprayers and the agronomy service were bought without one (EV-052). The development firm can deploy to production with no company approval step, and 10 settlement service changes in 12 months have no company approval record (EV-053). Grade data from the optical graders is not reconciled to packed cases before settlement (EV-054). The cyber insurance policy has a $10 million limit and a $250,000 retention, with notice through the carrier hotline first (EV-055). Retail agreements require notice within 24 hours of events affecting product safety, traceability or volumes (EV-056); grower agreements promise 99.5% alert availability in season and notice within 72 hours, and the growers' association and two lenders ask for a SOC 2 Type 2 report (EV-057). The credit agreement requires notice of material disruptions (EV-058). Licenses, permits and USDA program records show a PACA license, H-2A certifications, 54 permitted wells, Part 107 pilots and no food facility registration or federal procurement contract (EV-059). Card data stays in the processor's P2PE terminals and the hosted payment page (EV-060). ACH release needs a second approver, and bank detail changes arrive by email (EV-061).

**Business volume.** Receipts were about $100 million in FY2025: crop sales about $88 million, Grower Services fees about $10 million and direct retail about $2 million; about $490,000 of produce shipped per shipping day on average (EV-062). About 30 contract growers supplied about 22% of packinghouse volume, and weekly settlements averaged about $900,000 in season (EV-063). The online store holds about 9,000 customer accounts and no card data (EV-064).

**AI tools.** Five AI tools or features are in use: yield prediction from the agronomy analytics vendor, the SYS-01 irrigation scheduling model that sets schedules on 12 Farm 3 pivots automatically, camera-based targeted spraying on 6 sprayers, optical grading on 2 packing lines, and public generative AI chatbots used by office staff. No security or privacy review record and no approved-tools list exist (EV-065).

**Reconciliation across sources.**
- **Workforce.** The HR roster has about 230 year-round employees and up to 370 H-2A workers in season (EV-003). Office, management, IT and settlement users have identity provider accounts (EV-001); crew leads use 6 shared SYS-01 logins, and field workers have no system accounts (EV-007). The 214 terminations and 41 transfers are the sampling populations for P03 and P07.
- **Endpoints.** The CMDB total (210 laptops and desktops, 160 tablets and phones, 20 line PCs) matches the SSP boundary and the BIA resource table (EV-012).
- **OT devices.** The OT inventory (about 1,600) and the installer invoices and purchasing records (about 2,900) differ by about 1,300 devices (EV-013, EV-014). The difference stays an open request.
- **PLC and HMI programs.** The program list accounts for all 68 programs: 37 with company copies and 31 held only by the integrator (EV-025).
- **Vendors.** About 110 vendors in accounts payable, 38 of them flagged with data or system access (EV-047, EV-048). The named vendors and two summary rows in [`vendor-register.csv`](vendor-register.csv) add up to the 38 and the 110.
- **AI tools.** One AI vendor in accounts payable (the agronomy analytics service), plus the scheduling model inside SYS-01, the vision models bought with the sprayers and graders, and public chatbots found only through the survey, give the 5 tools in [`asset-inventory.csv`](asset-inventory.csv) (EV-047, EV-065).
- **Revenue.** The crop mix in the revenue report (about $41 million, $27 million, $11 million and $9 million) adds up to the about $88 million of crop sales (EV-062).
