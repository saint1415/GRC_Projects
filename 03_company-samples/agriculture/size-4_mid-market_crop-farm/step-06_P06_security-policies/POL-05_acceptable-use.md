## 1. Purpose
Set clear, plain-language rules for how everyone at the company uses computers, tablets, phones, drones, AI tools, and data.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members, including seasonal and H-2A workers who use company tablets, time clocks, or Wi-Fi, and anyone else given access to company systems or data, at every site and when working remotely.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Director | Collects signed acknowledgments at hire and annually (at pre-season meetings for seasonal staff) |
| Security Manager | Training content, monthly reminders, and quarterly phishing simulations |
| Farm Managers and crew leads | Explain the rules to their crews in English or Spanish |
| Precision Agriculture Manager | Drone and AI tool rules |
| All workforce | Follow these rules |

## 4. Policy statements
4.1 Company systems and devices are for company business. Limited personal use is allowed if it involves no company data and creates no risk. H-2A housing Wi-Fi is separate from company networks and may be used for personal purposes. (PL-4)
4.2 Every workforce member must acknowledge this policy, in English or Spanish, before receiving access and again each year. (PL-4(1))
4.3 Security awareness training is required at hire and every year, in English and Spanish, and includes how to recognize and report insider threats such as unexplained setpoint or settlement changes. Office users also receive monthly reminders and quarterly phishing simulations. Crew leads and Irrigation Technicians receive a security segment at each pre-season meeting. SCADA engineers, Irrigation Technicians, settlement analysts, and IT administrators must complete role-based training. (AT-2; AT-2(2); AT-3; PR.AT-01; PR.AT-02)
4.4 Lock your screen or hand back the tablet when you step away. Crew tablets are for tally and farm apps only; sign in with your own account and PIN and never let someone else enter tally under your name. (AC-11; AC-19)
4.5 Do not install software or apps, or plug USB drives into SCADA servers, HMIs, packinghouse line PCs, or office computers, unless IT approves it. Vendors may connect laptops or media to OT only through the process in POL-02 4.9. (CM-11)
4.6 Company data may be used on personal devices only for email and chat through the managed apps. Contractors must use company-managed devices or the company's virtual desktop to reach company systems. (AC-19; AC-20)
4.7 Any email, text, or call asking to change bank details or payment instructions must be confirmed by calling the known number on file (POL-02 4.14). (AT-2)
4.8 Report lost devices, strange messages, and any pump, valve, pivot, injection, ripening room, or cooler behavior that nobody scheduled immediately (POL-03 4.2).
4.9 **Drones.** Only the company's certificated remote pilots may fly company drones, under 14 CFR Part 107 and the flight plan. Imagery is used only for crop analysis. It must not be used to watch or evaluate individual workers or neighbors, and images showing people must be deleted when not needed. Imagery is kept for 3 seasons. (PL-4)
4.10 **AI tools.** Use only AI tools on the approved list (STD-05; P10). Never paste worker personal information, grower prices or yields, or the food defense plan into a tool that is not approved; the enterprise AI assistant is the approved tool for office drafting. AI outputs (yield estimates, irrigation recommendations, grades, drafts) are advice to a person unless P10 has approved automatic action with its controls, and must be checked before they are used for crew, sales, irrigation, or settlement decisions. (PL-4)

## 5. Compliance and enforcement
Violations are handled under POL-01 section 4.8, applied the same way to every worker regardless of visa status. Compliance is checked through the annual independent assessment (P07), phishing results, and acknowledgment records.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions follow POL-01 section 4.7.
