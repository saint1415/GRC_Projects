**Where the access control rules are:**

- POL-01 section 7 (Access control): own accounts (7.1), MFA (7.2), passphrases and password manager (7.3), no default passwords on any device (7.4), account review each July and November (7.5), sealed emergency access (7.6), monthly and freeze-season log review (7.7), irrigation change log (7.8), device lock (7.9).
- POL-01 section 5.1: family members use their own standard laptop account.
- POL-01 section 6.3: irrigation dealer access only in owner-opened service windows.
- SP 800-53 AC-1 and IA-1 (policy and procedures) are met by POL-01 as a whole.
