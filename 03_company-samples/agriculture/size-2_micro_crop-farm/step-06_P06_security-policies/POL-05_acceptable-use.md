At the Micro tier the farm keeps three core policies. The workforce use rules that an Acceptable Use Policy would hold are short enough to sit with the access rules staff already read, so they live in **POL-02 Part C. Workforce use rules**, with the AI tool and drone imagery rules in POL-04. A one-page Spanish summary of Part C is given to every field worker.

| Essential rule | Where it lives | Benchmark or rule |
|---|---|---|
| Use farm systems for farm work; look only at the records your job needs | POL-02 C.1 | CSF 2.0 PR.AT-01 |
| No Restricted information in personal accounts, messaging apps, or public AI chatbots | POL-02 C.2 | Fla. Stat. 501.171(2) |
| Approved AI tools only; no farm data to tools that reuse it for others | POL-04 4.6 | CSF 2.0 GV.SC-05 |
| Drone images for crops only; never to watch or evaluate workers | POL-04 4.10 | CSF 2.0 PR.DS-01 |
| Lock screens; never share passwords, PINs, or MFA codes | POL-02 C.3 | CSF 2.0 PR.AA-03 |
| Training at hire and yearly, in English or Spanish; check payment-change requests by phone | POL-02 C.4 | CSF 2.0 PR.AT-01 |
| Report suspected incidents at once, including a pump or pivot acting on its own | POL-02 C.5 and POL-03 4.2 | CSF 2.0 RS.MA-02 |
| Signed acknowledgment at hire, at each season start, and after each annual update | POL-02 C.6 | CSF 2.0 PR.AT-01 |

Traceability for these rules is in `policy-control-map.csv` under POL-02 and POL-04.
