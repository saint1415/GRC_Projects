## 1. Purpose and scope
Intake collected the company's own records before the enterprise risk analysis, the BIA and the gap analysis began on 2026-06-01, and before Internal Audit's control assessment fieldwork began on 2026-07-13. It covers the organization (48 farms in 6 regions, 17 packing sites, the control centers and data centers, and the acquired operations AQ-01 and AQ-02), the IT and OT systems that hold worker, grower or customer information or support tier-1 processes, the vendors that touch them, and the rules that may bind the company and its two external service lines (SL-1 and SL-2). Each item has an ID in [`evidence-register.csv`](evidence-register.csv). Later steps add their own fieldwork evidence (BIA interviews, risk workshops, gap analysis samples, Internal Audit tests, AI committee records) to the same register, so one list backs every deliverable.

At this size the sources are enterprise systems of record across business units: the identity platform and HR system, the CMDB and the OT asset inventory, the OT monitoring platform, the OT remote access gateway, the cloud consoles and SIEM, the third-party risk register and accounts payable vendor master, the contract repository, the FMIS and payroll records that hold the regulated farm records, board and committee records, prior Internal Audit and SOX workpapers, and the SEC filings.

Each register row names the owning business unit's system of record and owner. The [asset inventory](asset-inventory.csv) reconciles the CMDB with the OT asset inventory, the cloud account inventory, the EDR and MDM consoles and the AI governance committee register; the [vendor register](vendor-register.csv) reconciles the third-party risk register with the accounts payable vendor master and the contract repository.

This report records **observations, not findings**. Whether an observation meets a requirement is decided in the gap analysis (P03) and the control assessment (P07).

## 3. Observations by area
**Identity and access.** Year-round workforce accounts are federated through SSO with MFA registered; workforce at AQ-01 and AQ-02 sign in through 2 legacy directories that are not federated and are run by local IT firms, with disable dates entered by hand (EV-001, EV-006). MFA is required for every SSO application, with phishing-resistant keys for privileged roles and number-matching push for most users (EV-002). About 290 privileged accounts are vaulted and elevated just in time with session recording (EV-003). Joiner, mover and leaver events flow from the HR system, and quarterly certification covers 46 corporate applications; seasonal FMIS accounts expire on the contract end date and are not in the inactivity job (EV-004). HR terminations at AQ-01 and AQ-02 go to local IT as emailed lists (EV-005). The FMIS has about 2,900 year-round users and 3,840 seasonal accounts from the 2025-26 season; 19 users hold the irrigation planner role, which includes recipe editing and release to SCADA (EV-007). The SCADA role matrix was approved 2026-03-09 (EV-008). AQ-01 crews sign in to tally tablets with shared crew logins, so their tally entries carry a crew login rather than a named person (EV-009).

**Endpoints, OT and network.** About 6,500 rugged tablets and phones are enrolled in MDM; the about 260 AQ-01 tally tablets are not (EV-010, EV-009). EDR reports on 97% of about 6,200 IT laptops, desktops and servers, and on SCADA servers, engineering workstations and the 17 supported HMIs; the 31 HMIs on an operating system past vendor support run no agent (EV-011, EV-014). The OT inventory lists about 45,500 OT and IoT devices; the Director of OT Security estimates the field device inventory at about 78% complete, and AQ-02 pivots are not in it (EV-013). About 1,300 pivot panel modems run firmware with published vulnerabilities (EV-015). Passive OT monitoring covers the 6 control centers and 120 of 410 pump stations (EV-017). OT changes follow PRC-01.4 with bench tests and regional approval (EV-016). The SD-WAN reaches about 64 sites; OT DMZs and OT firewalls sit at the 6 control centers; the 6 AQ-01 farm offices are flat networks on legacy firewalls joined by a legacy site VPN (EV-019). One carrier's private APN carries about 80% of field OT devices, and licensed radio covers 140 pump stations (EV-020). INT-1 to INT-3 use the OT remote access gateway with MFA and recording; INT-4 and INT-5 connect through their own remote tools, and 2 of 4 packing line vendors' sessions are not recorded (EV-022). About 180 AQ-02 pivots are commanded through the manufacturer's cloud service with shared logins and no MFA (EV-023). Technicians at the 3 equipment dealers hold standing remote diagnostic access to about 1,450 machines (EV-024). Monthly PLC program comparison runs at 290 of 410 stations, not at the INT-4 and INT-5 stations (EV-025). Fertigation interlocks are verified each season on paper checklists (EV-026).

**Cloud, logging, backup and recovery.** Both clouds use account vending, guardrails as code and daily posture scans (EV-029), with customer-managed keys and SL-1 tenant isolation tests in the pipeline (EV-030). Cloud backups are immutable in separate accounts, and the SCADA configuration and PLC library replicate hourly between DC-1 and DC-2 (EV-032). AQ-01 farm servers back up nightly to an external disk and a shared cloud folder, with no restore record (EV-033). In the 2026-05-19 DR test the SCADA masters restored in 9.5 hours against a 6-hour RTO, with 2 more hours to a verified known state (EV-034). The FMIS is one tenant for all 48 farms, with a contract RTO of 8 hours and RPO of 1 hour and a vendor failover test observed on 2026-04-22 (EV-035). The SIEM has about 1,420 log sources; the 6 AQ-01 farm servers and the AQ-02 pivot cloud service are not among them (EV-036). The SOC runs 24x7 with MSSP overflow (EV-037).

**Governance, prior assurance and disclosure.** The policy set in force has 5 policies and 20 standards with procedures and an exception register (EV-041). The enterprise risk register and the 2025 risk analysis are on file, and the board approved the risk appetite in 2026-02 (EV-042, EV-043). The Chief Compliance Officer keeps an obligations register mapped to systems (EV-093). Internal Audit's charter and plan, and the FY2025 SOX IT general control workpapers, are on file (EV-046). The disclosure committee has 8 members, 3 of whom joined after 2026-02; playbook v2 does not name OT outage, crop loss or worker safety among its materiality factors, and the minutes record no exercise with an OT outage scenario (EV-047). The FY2025 Form 10-K includes Item 1C (EV-048). The 2026-03-24 tabletop used an IT ransomware scenario without an OT outage or the disclosure committee (EV-049). Written manual irrigation and freeze-protection procedures exist for every region and were drilled in 2025-11; no drill assumed SCADA and alarms were lost to a cyber event (EV-050).

**Workforce and privacy.** Annual awareness training in English and Spanish, monthly phishing exercises and role-based OT training are recorded; no integrator attestations are on file for INT-4 or INT-5 staff (EV-051). The Privacy Office keeps a data inventory, a sanctions log and a manual state-by-state notice tracker (EV-053). 58 remote pilots hold certificates (EV-054).

**Third parties, contracts and service lines.** About 1,100 vendors, 190 with system access or sensitive data, are tiered; the cold-chain monitoring SaaS has no SOC report, questionnaire or security terms on file (EV-055). Standard security terms appear in tier-1 and tier-2 contracts since 2025; the INT-4 and INT-5 agreements, the pivot manufacturer's terms and the dealers' portal terms do not carry them (EV-057). Retail and foodservice agreements require 24-hour notice of events affecting product safety, traceability or volumes (EV-058). SL-1 issued its 2025 SOC 2 Type 2 report; SL-2 has no SOC report (EV-060). The PCI DSS questionnaire was submitted 2026-03 (EV-061).

**Facilities and disposal.** Badge access with two-factor entry protects data halls and control rooms, with quarterly access list reviews; cameras cover 96 freeze pump stations (EV-063). Generators serve DC-1, the control centers, the freeze stations and the packing sites (EV-064). Certificates of destruction are on file (EV-065).

**Business volume and structure.** FY2025 revenue was about $4.8 billion, about $13.2 million per calendar day, with peak harvest weeks above $25 million a day (EV-066). The company farms about 310,000 acres, about 205,000 irrigated, and ships about 1,900 truckloads a week at peak (EV-067). AQ-01 (acquired 2025-09) and AQ-02 (acquired 2026-03) are due to complete integration on 2027-03-31 and 2027-06-30 (EV-069).

**Food safety, labor and traceability records.** About 5,600 H-2A workers work under about 60 labor certifications a year; tally feeds payroll, and a 2026-04 record production drill took 6 hours (EV-070). Produce Safety records sit in the FMIS with an edit audit trail and 3-year retention (EV-071). The 2026 mock FDA request produced the 24-hour sortable spreadsheet for 2 of the 4 FTL commodity groups, and key data elements are split across the FMIS, packing line software and EDI (EV-072). Hub 3 keeps paper receiving logs (EV-073). The third-party share of produce packed is 22%, 31% and 18% at Hubs 1 to 3, tracked in a spreadsheet; the grower services plan would take Hub 2 above a majority in 2027 (EV-074, EV-075).

**AI.** The AI governance committee register lists 11 use cases; AI-004, AI-009, AI-010 and AI-011 entered through vendor feature releases and have no committee review. AI-001 was reviewed at Medium in 2025-11, and its forecasts now feed crew planning and draft H-2A job orders; no test of crew plan outcomes by worker group is recorded (EV-076).

**Obligations.** The company is an SEC registrant (EV-048), a covered farm that grows and packs Food Traceability List foods (EV-075), an H-2A employer (EV-070), and a drone operator under Part 107 (EV-054). The employee health plan is a separate plan (EV-078). Counsel's view on each candidate rule is in EV-077; the results are in [`obligations-register.csv`](obligations-register.csv).
