Obligations that apply **regardless of industry**, plus the US AI governance landscape used by P10. Vertical-specific rules live in `02_verticals/`.

- **Last full verification:** 2026-09-25, against primary sources (.gov, eCFR, Federal Register, official legislature sites).
- **Verified column:** Yes = key facts read from the cited primary source; Partial = some facts confirmed; No = not confirmed.
- **Machine-readable subset:** [`notification-baseline.csv`](notification-baseline.csv) feeds each scenario's P08 notification matrix.
- **Status-uncertain items** are listed at the end. Re-check them first at each review.
